# Replace your lost, stolen or damaged domestic certificate of competency

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-certificates-competency-cocs/replace-your-lost-stolen-or-damaged

If you have lost your certificate of competency, or it has been damaged or stolen you can apply to get it replaced.

## How to replace your certificate of competency

The documents you will need to replace your certificate of competency depend on the type of certificate you hold.

### Prepare your documents

#### Medical fitness

To replace a Master <45m, Master <100m, MED 1, or Engineer Class 3 you must submit a Certificate of medical fitness.

If your previous Certificate of medical fitness has expired, download the [Certificate of medical fitness for domestic seafarers](https://www.amsa.gov.au/form1850) (AMSA form 1850) and follow the instructions on the form.

For all other certificates, you will need to either:

- declare that your medical fitness has not changed, or
- provide a Certificate of medical fitness.

#### First aid certificate

To replace any certificate you must submit a current 'Provide first aid' certificate (HLTAID011), or [equivalent](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/equivalent-qualifications-and-skill-recognition/equivalent-first-aid).

### Apply online

- Log in to myAMSA using your AMSA ID and [Australian Government myID](https://www.myid.gov.au/).
- In myAMSA:
- Open the Certificates tab.
- Find the certificate you want to replace and select Replace.
- Fill in the application form online and upload copies of your documents.
- Pay the [required fee](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/fees-services-domestic-commercial-vessel-safety) and submit your application online.

[Apply now](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/myAMSA)

## After you apply

We will confirm receipt of your application, and you can track your application in myAMSA.

We will also contact you if:

- your application is incomplete
- the documents you provided are not suitable
- we need more information to make our assessment.

See our [incomplete application policy](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/incomplete-application-policy-international-and-near-coastal-seafarer).
