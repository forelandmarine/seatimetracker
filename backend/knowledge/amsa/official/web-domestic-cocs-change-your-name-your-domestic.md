# Change your name on your domestic certificate of competency

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-certificates-competency-cocs/change-your-name-your-domestic

If you have recently changed your name, you can apply to get it changed on your certificate of competency and receive a replacement.

If you hold a near coastal certificate of competency you must let AMSA know if you change your name.

You can then apply to have your name changed on your certificate.

## Notify AMSA

To notify AMSA:

- email amsaconnect@amsa.gov.au
- attach a certificate or document attesting to the requested name change.

This can be a marriage certificate, change of name certificate issued by a state or territory authority, divorce certificate, or similar.

After we receive your email we will:

- update your name in myAMSA, our online application portal
- give you instructions for replacing your certificate.

## Replace your certificate

After we have updated your name in myAMSA, you can [apply to replace your certificate](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/replace-your-lost-stolen-or-damaged-domestic).
