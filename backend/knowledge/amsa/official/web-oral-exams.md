# Oral exams

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/oral-exams

Some near coastal and STCW seafarer certificates require you to pass an oral exam before your certificate can be issued. Find out how to book, what to expect and how to prepare.

Download the syllabus and checklist, understand the assessment process, and find out what examiners will assess.
