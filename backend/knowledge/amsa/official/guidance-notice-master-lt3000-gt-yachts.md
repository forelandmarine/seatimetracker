Marine Order 74 - Certificate of Competency
Master <3000 GT (Yachts)
The following provides a summary of the requirements for a Master <3000 GT (Yachts)
CoC. This certificate is issued under Marine Order 70 (Seafarer certification) 2014 and
Marine Order 74 (Master and deck officers - yachts) 2015, made under the Navigation Act 2012.

### How do I get this certificate?

To obtain a Master <3000 GT (Yachts) Certificate of
Competency (CoC) you will need to:
- Hold:
- A Master <500 GT (Yachts) CoC, or
- Any STCW certificate AMSA considers equivalent to
the above.
- Complete at least 24 months sea service as an officer
in charge of a navigation watch on vessels ≥12 metres,
while holding any of the following certificates:
- Watchkeeper Deck <3000 GT (Yachts).
- Master <500 GT (Yachts).
- Chief Mate <3000 GT (Yachts).
- Any certificate AMSA considers equivalent to the
above of which
- At least 8 months served on vessels ≥20 metres
- Complete an AMSA approved course of study from an
AMSA approved registered training organisation for a
Master <500 GT (Yachts).
- Complete the following approved STCW short courses:
- Basic Safety Training course, which includes
Security Awareness training.
- Advanced Fire Fighting.
- Medical Care on board vessels.
- Hold the following valid certificates:
- Certificate in Proficiency in Survival Craft and
Rescue Boats other than Fast Rescue Boats.
- Certificate of Proficiency as ECDIS trained.

Yacht means a vessel that:
- Is less than 3000GT; and
- Is commercial use for sport or pleasure; and
- Does not carry cargo; and
- Does not carry more than 12 passengers.
A yacht need not be designed to carry sail and may be a
training  vessel, a sail training vessel or a short ranger
yacht provided they are not designed to carry cargo.

### How do I keep this certificate?

To revalidate your certificate you must:
- Hold a valid:
- AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness.
- GMDSS Radio Operators Certificate or GMDSS CoR.
- Have seagoing service performing functions
appropriate to the certificate you hold, for a period of at
least:
a) 12 months in total during the preceding 5 years
before application, or
b) 3 months in total during the preceding 6 months
prior to revalidating, or
c) Have completed an approved revalidation course
appropriate for your certificate within 5 years
before applying to revalidate, or
d) Have completed an oral exam and approved short
courses that AMSA considers equivalent; or
 Master on yachts of <3000 GT in any operating area
 Watchkeeper Deck Officer on yachts of <3000 GT in any operating area
 Able Seafarer - deck duties or functions

On completion of the seagoing service, you should:
- Obtain a letter(s) from your employer(s) confirming sea
service and stating that you have actively participated
in the mandatory abandon ship and fire drills required
by SOLAS as well as scheduled drills required by the
on board Safety Management System). A copy of the
letter should be submitted with the application (you
should keep the original).
- Provide written evidence of performing radio service in
a seagoing ship fully fitted with GMDSS equipment for
sea area A3 of at least 12 months in total within the
preceding 5 years, or 3 months in total during the
preceding 6 months prior to revalidation. This should
be written on company letterhead paper and includes
the following details:
- Ship particulars - the name, IMO number, MMSI
number, ship type(s), Gross Tonnage.
- The rank served on board.
- The dates served on board the ship.
- That the ship was fitted with a full GMDSS station
(sea area A3).
- That the applicant operated the equipment on a
regular basis.
- Or have performed functions relating to the category of
GMDSS Certificate held which are considered by
AMSA to be at least equivalent to the seagoing service
required.
- Complete continued competence training for the
STCW short courses in Advanced Fire Fighting and
Fire and Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Craft
other than Fast Rescue Boats in the previous 5 years
(refer to MO 74 Schedule 4).
Please note: There are three types of continued
competence courses. Please ensure you complete the
continued competence for deck / engineer officers and
chief integrated ratings.
If you DO NOT HAVE at least the specified seagoing
service mentioned above, you will be required to
complete the STCW short courses Advance Fire
Fighting and Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue
Craft other than Fast Rescue Boats in full and one of the
revalidation options c, d or e shown above.
How to apply for a CoC - Initial Issue /
Revalidation (select as required)
Lodge an application at Australia Post:
- Complete a CoC application form 419 online or
download a pdf version and then complete it.
- Complete all sections of the form.
- Download a pdf version of the form to generate your
barcode.
- Print the form.
- Attach copies of all required documents.
- Sign the declaration.
- Submit your application and pay a fee at an AMSA-
contracted Australia Post outlet.
- Provide proof of identity when you lodge your
application.
AMSA may request reasonable evidence to confirm or
verify any of the information provided as part of the
application.
Please note:
- All certificates and training must comply with STCW
- All training courses and training programs must be
approved by AMSA.
- All sea service must be documented qualifying sea
service.
