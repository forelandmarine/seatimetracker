# Use an overseas certificate in Australia

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/use-overseas-certificate-australia

Find out whether your overseas STCW certificate can be recognised in Australia, then identify the pathway you need to work here or apply for skilled migration.

## Can you use your overseas certificate or experience?

STCW certificates issued by other countries are not automatically valid in Australia and cannot be converted into an Australian STCW certificate.

If you hold an overseas STCW certificate, the pathway you need depends on where your certificate was issued and what you want to do in Australia.

If you can't use your overseas certificate in Australia, you'll [need to apply for an Australian STCW certificate](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/get-new-stcw-certificate/how-to-get-stcw-certificate).
