# Approval of registered training organisations as final assessors

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/registered-training-organisations-delivering

How to become an AMSA approved final assessor for low complexity certificates of competency.

AMSA 132

24 September 2018

This information is for registered training organisations (RTOs) on how to become an AMSA approved final assessor for low complexity certificates of competency issued under the national law.

Detail on the criteria, conditions and term of approvals are available in the attached document.

## Applications

An RTO can apply for:

- Approval to be a final assessor.
- Variation to an existing approval.

An application for an approval, or variation of an approval, must be in writing using the [Application for approval as a final assessor](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/application-approval-final-assessor).
