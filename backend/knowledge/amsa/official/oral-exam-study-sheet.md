Get ready with AMSA
Oral Exam Resources.
Preparing for your AMSA Oral Exam
Using the AMSA Syllabus and AMSA Examiner Checklist to guide your study.
The AMSA Oral Exam forms part of your assessment and must be passed before you can be
issued your Certificate of Competency. The exam assesses how you think, decide and explain
safe actions in real situations. It is not a memory test.
Start with these two documents
AMSA Syllabus
The syllabus sets out the knowledge and skills
required for the certificate you are applying for.
Use it to understand the scope and level of
knowledge expected.
AMSA Examiner Checklist
The Examiner Checklist shows what examiners
will assess during your oral exam.
Each topic is prioritised:
A) Safety‑critical (must be demonstrated
to succeed)
B) Operational knowledge
C) General knowledge
The checklist is your study roadmap.
How to prepare effectively
Start with A‑topics on the AMSA Examiner Checklist.
Use the AMSA Syllabus to understand the depth of
knowledge required.
Practise explaining:
What you would do
Why it matters
What you would check next
Practise speaking your answers out loud ‑ clear
verbal explanations are essential.
Before you book your exam
Only book your oral exam once AMSA has
confirmed you are eligible and you feel ready
to demonstrate your competence.
Good preparation helps you:
Succeed the first time
Avoid repeat exam fees and long wait times
Get certified and be able to work sooner
