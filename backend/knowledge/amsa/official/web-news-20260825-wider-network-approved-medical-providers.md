# AMSA news: Wider network of AMSA-approved medical providers is now available

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/news-community/news-and-media-releases/wider-network-amsa-approved-medical-providers-now-available

Published: Tuesday 25 August 2026

If you need an AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness (form 303), you now have more providers to choose from.

## What’s changing

Using our new medical provider search you can:
- choose from more AMSA-approved medical providers
- search for providers by state or territory
- sort results by suburb to find a provider nearby
- view provider contact details.

Note: The change is about where you can have your medical examination. It does not change the medical fitness requirements.

## Find an AMSA-approved medical provider

Choose a provider that suits your location and availability.

Check back regularly as more provider locations are added.
