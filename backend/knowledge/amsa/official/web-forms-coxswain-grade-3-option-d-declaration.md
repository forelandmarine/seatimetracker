# Coxswain grade 3 declaration (AMSA form 1882)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/coxswain-grade-3-option-d-declaration

## Overview

Use this form if you are applying for a Coxswain Grade 3 using a recreational boat licence. If you aren’t sure whether you need this form, [check the pathways to becoming a Coxswain Grade 3](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/10473).

You will need to complete specific on water tasks witnessed by an observer. Make sure the observer meets the requirements for the duties you want to qualify for.

## How to complete this form

[Print AMSA form 1882](https://www.amsa.gov.au/sites/default/files/mo505-grade-3-nc-declaration_0.pdf)

- Fill in the Applicant details.
- Give the form to an observer who meets the requirements.
- Complete the practical on water tasks witnessed by the observer.
- If appropriate, the observer signs the declaration on the form.
- You sign the declaration on the form.

### Observer requirements

To qualify for all Coxswain Grade 3 duties, your observer must hold one of the following:

- Coxswain Grade 2 or 1
- Master <24m, <45m, or <100m

If you want to work in a marina or mooring area only your observer must:

- be eligible for a Coxswain Grade 3
- have held a recreational boating licence for at least two years
- have at least six months experience operating similar kinds of vessels in a marina or mooring area.

Note: If you are looking to operate as a Coxswain grade 3 limited to marina/mooring area operations, AMSA cannot issue you with a physical Coxswain grade 3 NC card.

The observer will need to assess you using our [guide for completing the Coxswain Grade 3 declaration](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/230).

### Vessel requirements

You will need to complete the practical on water tasks on a vessel that meets the following requirements:

| Length | Less than 12 metres. |
|---|---|
| Propulsion power | Inboard engine: under 500 kilowattsOutboard engine: any |

## Submitting the form

Submit a copy of this form with your Coxswain Grade 3 application.

To work without the certificate or work only in a marina or mooring area, you do not need to submit the form. Keep a copy of this form on board along with your other evidence.

[Find out how to qualify as a Coxswain Grade 3](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/10473).
