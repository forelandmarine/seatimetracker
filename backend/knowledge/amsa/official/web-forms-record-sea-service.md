# Record of sea service (form 771)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/record-sea-service

## Overview

Use this form as evidence of sea service when you apply for a near coastal certificate of competency.

This form cannot be used to apply for an STCW certificate.

## How to complete this form

You can either:

- download a blank copy of this form, or
- fill in the form online and download it as a PDF.

### Before you start

- [Check the type and amount of sea service required for the certificate you want](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/50).
- Read about [how to calculate and record your sea service](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/704).

On each form you can only list one vessel, one mode of operation, and one type of duty. Use multiple forms as needed.

### To complete this form:

- Fill in your details.
- Add the operational and vessel details.
- Sign and date the form.
- Have your supervisor sign and date the form. The signature field can be electronically or physically signed. It cannot be typed.

Note: anything crossed out or whited out must be signed and dated by your supervisor.
