# Master less than 100 metres near coastal

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/master-less-100-metres-near-coastal

This is a domestic Certificate of Competency for operating in Australian waters.

Learn about the Master less than 100 metres certificate, including the vessels it lets you operate, how to qualify, apply, or renew.

A Master less than 100 metres near coastal can work as:

- master, chief mate or deck watchkeeper on commercial vessels up to 100 metres long within the exclusive economic zone (EEZ)
- a General Purpose Hand, Master less than 24 metres, and Master less than 45 metres.

When you're ready to apply:

[Apply online in myAMSA](https://my.amsa.gov.au/)

New certificate Renewal Equivalence

## New certificate

To obtain this certificate you will need to:

Check your eligibility

To be eligible for this certificate you must be:

- at least 16 years old on the certificate issue date
- able to speak, read and understand English
- medically fit for the duties required.

You must also have completed sea service while holding either:

- a Master less than 45 metres near coastal
- a Master less than 35 metres near coastal (phased out 1 January 2023)
- a Mate less than 80 metres near coastal (phased out 1 January 2023)

Complete medical and eyesight test

You will need to see a medical practitioner to check that you are medically fit for working at sea. They will assess you in line with [AMSA’s medical requirements](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/eyesight-and-medical-requirements-domestic).

Download the [Certificate of medical fitness for domestic seafarers (AMSA form 1850)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/form1850) and follow the instructions on the form.

If you have a current [Certificate of medical fitness (AMSA form 303)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/certificate-medical-fitness-international-certificates) you can use this instead.

If you have colour deficient vision, you will be restricted to working only in daylight hours.

Provide evidence of sea service

#### Type of sea service required

You need to show you have experience working at sea:

- in charge of the navigational watch
- on commercial vessels at least 24 metres long

#### Amount of sea service required

The amount of sea service you need will depend on which certificate you held while completing the sea service.

You can reduce the amount of sea service you need by completing an [AMSA task book](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/order-task-books-record-books-and-log-books).

With a Master less than 45 metres or Master less than 35 metres:

| With an AMSA task book | Without an AMSA task book |
|---|---|
| 180 days including 120 days in charge of the navigational watch | 360 days including 240 days in charge of the navigational watch |

With a Mate less than 80 metres or other non-command certificate:

| With an AMSA task book | Without an AMSA task book |
|---|---|
| 360 days including 120 days in charge of the navigational watch | 720 days including 240 days in charge of the navigational watch |

#### Recording your sea service

Use at least one of the following as proof of qualifying sea service:

- [Record of sea service form 771](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/record-sea-service)
- the on board sea service record from your [task book](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/order-task-books-record-books-and-log-books)
- a letter from the vessel operator, owner, master or chief engineer detailing your sea service
- an approved sea service log book.

[Read more about qualifying sea service](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/sea-service-and-task-books/qualifying-near-coastal-sea-service).

Complete a training course

Complete a Diploma of Maritime Operations at a [registered training organisation](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/registered-training-organisations-approved-conduct) (RTO).

Submit your course completion certificate with your application.

Obtain first aid and marine radio certificates

You must also hold both:

- a current 'Provide first aid' certificate (HLTAID011), or [equivalent](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/equivalent-qualifications-and-skill-recognition/equivalent-first-aid).
- a [marine radio 'Long range operator certificate of proficiency' (LROCP)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/marine-radio-certificates) card or higher.

If your first aid or medical certificate expires within 90 days of submitting your application, we may request an updated certificate.

Submit your application

- Gather the required documents:
- a signed and current certificate of medical fitness (AMSA form 1850)
- evidence of your sea service
- your Diploma of Maritime Operations course completion certificate
- a current first aid certificate (HLTAID011 'Provide first aid' certificate, or equivalent)
- a marine radio 'Long range operator certificate of proficiency' (LROCP)
- a colour passport-sized photograph that meets [our requirements](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/completing-your-domestic-application/photo).
- Log in to myAMSA using your [Australian Government myID](https://www.myid.gov.au/).
- Fill in the application form online and upload copies of your documents.
- Pay the [required fee](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/fees-services-domestic-commercial-vessel-safety) and submit your application online.

[Apply online](https://my.amsa.gov.au/)

#### If you cannot use myAMSA

Complete and print the [Certificate of competency application (AMSA form 426)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/certificate-competency-application). Submit the form with copies of the required documents at a [participating Australia Post outlet](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/who-we-are/australia-post-retail-outlets). You will also need to provide [proof of your identity](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/completing-your-domestic-application/proof-identity).

Pass a final assessment

You need to pass a final oral exam before we will issue your certificate of competency. An AMSA examiner will ask you to answer questions verbally to test your skills and knowledge.

#### Booking your exam

When we've assessed your application we will invite you to book the exam (if you haven't already passed an AMSA exam at your training organisation).

You will need to:

- confirm your booking
- pay an exam fee.

If you don't pass, you can try again. You must pay the fee each time you sit the exam.

[Prepare for your oral exam](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-certificates-competency-cocs/preparing-near-coastal-oral).

## Renewal

A Master less than 100 metres certificate is valid for 5 years.

If you apply before your certificate expires, you can continue working for 90 days.

To renew your certificate you need to:

Complete medical and eyesight test

You will need to see a medical practitioner to check that you are medically fit for working at sea. They will assess you in line with [AMSA’s medical requirements](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/eyesight-and-medical-requirements-domestic).

Download the [Certificate of medical fitness for domestic seafarers (AMSA form 1850)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/form1850) and follow the instructions on the form.

If you have a current [Certificate of medical fitness (AMSA form 303)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/certificate-medical-fitness-international-certificates) you can use this instead.

Provide evidence of sea service

You must have completed at least 120 days of qualifying sea service on commercial vessels in the 5 years before you apply.

Your sea service must include work performing officer of the watch duties.

#### Recording your sea service

Use at least one of the following as proof of qualifying sea service:

- [Record of sea service form 771](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/record-sea-service)
- the on board sea service record from your [task book](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/order-task-books-record-books-and-log-books)
- a letter from the vessel operator, owner, master or chief engineer detailing your sea service
- an approved sea service log book.

[Read more about qualifying sea service](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/sea-service-and-task-books/qualifying-near-coastal-sea-service).

#### If you don't have the required sea service

You must provide evidence that you have (any one of the following):

- completed an approved final assessment
- at least 60 days of sea service in the last 6 months, performing officer of the watch duties relevant to your certificate
- worked in a seasonal fishery for at least 120 days in the last 5 years
- other approved industry experience (e.g. state or territory marine safety agency assessor, fleet manager, shore-side servicing, maritime studies teacher at a registered training organisation, marine surveyor)
- completed a Master less than 100 task book in the 5 years before your application.

Complete first aid training

You must hold a current 'Provide first aid' certificate (HLTAID011), or [equivalent](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/equivalent-qualifications-and-skill-recognition/equivalent-first-aid).

If your first aid or medical certificate expires within 90 days of submitting your application, we may request an updated certificate.

Submit your application

- Gather the required documents:
- your existing Master less than 100 metres (if you are applying online, there will be a copy in your myAMSA account)
- a signed and current certificate of medical fitness (AMSA form 1850)
- evidence of your sea service
- a current first aid certificate (HLTAID011 'Provide first aid' certificate, or equivalent)
- a colour passport-sized photograph that meets [our requirements](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/completing-your-domestic-application/photo).
- Log in to myAMSA using your [Australian Government myID](https://www.myid.gov.au/).
- Fill in the application form online and upload copies of your documents.
- Pay the [required fee](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/fees-services-domestic-commercial-vessel-safety) and submit your application online.

[Apply online](https://my.amsa.gov.au/)

#### If you cannot use myAMSA

Complete and print the [Certificate of competency application (AMSA form 426)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/certificate-competency-application). Submit the form with copies of the required documents at a [participating Australia Post outlet](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/who-we-are/australia-post-retail-outlets). You will also need to provide [proof of your identity](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/completing-your-domestic-application/proof-identity).

## Equivalence

If you hold any of the following certificates, you can work as a Master less than 100 metres near coastal:

- Chief Mate <3000 GT with Master <500 GT unlimited and <3000 GT NC endorsement
- Chief Mate with Master <500 GT unlimited and <3000 GT endorsement
- Master <500 GT
- Master <3000 GT
- Master

You do not need to hold a Master less than 100 metres near coastal to work while holding these certificates, but you can choose to apply for one as equivalent.

If you hold a [perpetual certificate of competency or a domestic certificate of competency that expired more than 5 years ago](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/perpetual-certificates-competency-domestic-seafarers), you can also apply for an equivalent domestic certificate.

To get a Master less than 45 metres near coastal:

Complete medical and eyesight test

You will need to see a medical practitioner to check that you are medically fit for working at sea. They will assess you in line with [AMSA’s medical requirements](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/eyesight-and-medical-requirements-domestic).

Download the [Certificate of medical fitness for domestic seafarers (AMSA form 1850)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/form1850) and follow the instructions on the form.

If you have a current [Certificate of medical fitness (AMSA form 303)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/certificate-medical-fitness-international-certificates) you can use this instead.

Complete first aid training

You must hold a current 'Provide first aid' certificate (HLTAID011), or [equivalent](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/equivalent-qualifications-and-skill-recognition/equivalent-first-aid).

If your first aid or medical certificate expires within 90 days of submitting your application, we may request an updated certificate.

Submit your application

- Gather the required documents:
- a signed and current certificate of medical fitness (AMSA form 1850)
- a current first aid certificate (HLTAID011 'Provide first aid' certificate, or equivalent)
- your existing certificate
- a colour passport-sized photograph that meets [our requirements](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/completing-your-domestic-application/photo).
- Log in to myAMSA using your [Australian Government myID](https://www.myid.gov.au/).
- Fill in the application form online and upload copies of your documents.
- Pay the [required fee](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/fees-services-domestic-commercial-vessel-safety) and submit your application online.

[Apply online](https://my.amsa.gov.au/)

#### If you cannot use myAMSA

Complete and print the [Certificate of competency application (AMSA form 426)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/certificate-competency-application). Submit the form with copies of the required documents at a [participating Australia Post outlet](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/who-we-are/australia-post-retail-outlets).
