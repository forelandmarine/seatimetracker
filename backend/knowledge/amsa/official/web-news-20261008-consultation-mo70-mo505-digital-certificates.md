# AMSA news: Consultation now open on proposed minor changes to the look of seafarer certificates (MO 70 and MO 505)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/news-community/news-and-media-releases/consultation-now-open-proposed-minor-changes-look-seafarer

Published: Thursday 8 October 2026

We are seeking feedback on proposed minor changes to Marine Order 70 (Seafarer certification) (MO70) and Marine Order 505 (Certificates of competency - National law) (MO505). These are regulatory changes that support the issuing of digital certificates.

## Key proposed changes

- Removing photograph and signature requirements for digital certificates issued under Marine Order 70
- Removing prescriptive certificate format requirements from Marine Order 505
- Correcting outdated or incorrect references
- Making other minor editorial changes

**Note:** The changes will not affect seafarer qualification requirements or obligations. There are no changes or actions required from certificate holders.

## Why we’re proposing these changes

We introduced digital Certificates of Safety Training (CoST) in August 2026. Building on the success of this initiative, we plan to make more AMSA-issued seafarer certificates available digitally from early-to-mid 2027.

Currently, certificates of competency are required to have a photograph and signature under MO70, and a photograph under MO505. Digital certificates will not have a photograph or signature. We are proposing minor amendments to these marine orders to facilitate this change.

## Have your say

Consultation opens on **6 October 2026** and closes on **3 December 2026**.

Learn more about the proposed changes and submit your feedback.
