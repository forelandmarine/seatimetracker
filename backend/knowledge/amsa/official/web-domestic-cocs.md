# Domestic Certificates of Competency (COCs)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-certificates-competency-cocs

You must hold a near coastal certificate of competency to operate a domestic commercial vessel (DCV) in Australia. Find the right certificate for you.
