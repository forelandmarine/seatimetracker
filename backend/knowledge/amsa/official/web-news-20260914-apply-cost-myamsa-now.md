# AMSA news: Apply for your CoST in myAMSA now (digital Certificate of Safety Training)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/news-community/news-and-media-releases/apply-your-cost-myamsa-now

Published: Monday 14 September 2026

Skip the paperwork and post office. Apply online, track your application and access your Certificate of Safety Training (CoST) - all in one place, with myAMSA.

## What you can do

You can now apply for, or revalidate, your Certificate of Safety Training (CoST) using our secure online application portal.

In myAMSA you can:
- apply for or revalidate a CoST online
- upload supporting documents
- pay your application fee
- track your application
- access your digital certificate.

## What you need

To apply through myAMSA, you'll need:
- a myID Digital ID (with at least a Standard ID strength)
- an AMSA ID (if you already have one)
- your supporting documents ready to upload.

## Get a digital certificate

Your CoST will be issued as a digital certificate in myAMSA as soon as it’s approved.

This means you won’t need to wait for it to arrive in the post.

With myAMSA, you can:
- view your certificate online
- add it your digital wallet
- save a copy to your device
- print a copy if needed.

## Ready to apply?

Apply for, or revalidate, a CoST in myAMSA now.
