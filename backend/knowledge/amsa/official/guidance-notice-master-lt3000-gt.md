Marine Order 71 - Certificate of Competency
Master <3000GT
The following provides a summary of the requirements for a Master <3000GT Certificate
of Competency (CoC). This certificate is issued under Marine Order 70 (Seafarer
certification) 2014 and Marine Order 71 (Master and deck officers) 2023, made under the
Navigation Act 2012.

### How do I get this certificate?

To obtain a Master <3000 GT CoC you will need to:
- Hold or have held following a Chief Mate or Chief Mate
<3000 GT CoC.
- Have completed either:
OPTION 1: you will need to:
- Have completed 36 months qualified sea service
(QSS) as an officer in charge of a navigation watch on
ships ≥24 metres, while holding a certificate from the
list below:
- Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT CoC (with
Watchkeeper Deck <3000 GT near-coastal
endorsement).
- Mate <500 GT CoC.
- Watchkeeper Deck CoC.
- Chief Mate <3000 GT CoC.
- Master <500 GT CoC.
- Chief Mate CoC.
- A foreign certificate AMSA considers equivalent to
the above.
OPTION 2: you will need to:
- Have completed 24 months QSS as an officer in
charge of a navigation watch on ships ≥24 metres, of
which 12 months was served as Chief Mate or Master
while holding a Mate <500 GT CoC (with Chief Mate
<3000 GT near-coastal endorsement), or AMSA
equivalent.
For both options you will also need to:
- Have completed 24 months QSS on voyages of at
least 24 hour duration.
- Have served at least 12 months QSS while holding a
Chief Mate or Chief Mate <3000 GT CoC.

### How do I keep this certificate?

To revalidate your certificate you must:
- Hold a valid:
- AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness.
- GMDSS Radio Operators Certificate or GMDSS
Certificate of Recognition.
- Have QSS performing functions appropriate to the
certificate you hold, for a period of at least:
a) 12 months in total during the preceding 5 years before
application, or
b) 3 months in total during the preceding 6 months prior
to revalidating, or
c) Have completed an approved revalidation course
appropriate for your certificate within 5 years before
applying to revalidate, or
 Master or Chief Mate on ships <3000 GT in any operating area
 Watchkeeper Deck Officer on ships of any gross tonnage in any operating area
 Able Seafarer Deck - deck duties or functions.

On completion of the seagoing service, you should:
- Obtain a letter(s) from your employer(s) confirming
QSS and stating that you have actively participated in
the mandatory abandon ship and fire drills required by
SOLAS as well as scheduled drills required by the on
board Safety Management System (see sample
template letter). A copy of the letter should be
submitted with the application (you should keep the
original).
- Provide written evidence of performing radio service in
a seagoing ship fully fitted with GMDSS equipment for
sea area A3 of at least 12 months in total within the
preceding 5 years, or 3 months in total during the
preceding 6 months prior to revalidation. This should
be written on company letterhead paper and includes
the following details:
- Ship particulars - the name, IMO number, MMSI
number, ship type(s), Gross Tonnage;
- The rank served on board;
- The dates served on board the ship.
- That the ship was fitted with a full GMDSS station
(sea area A3).
- That the applicant operated the equipment on a
regular basis.
- Or have performed functions relating to the category of
GMDSS Certificate held which are considered by
AMSA to be at least equivalent to the QSS required.
- Complete continued competence training for the
STCW short courses in Advanced Fire Fighting and
Fire and Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Craft
other than Fast Rescue Boats in the previous 5 years
(refer to MO 71 Schedule 4).
Please note: There are three types of continued
competence courses.  Please ensure you complete the
continued competence for deck / engineer officers and
chief integrated ratings.
If you DO NOT HAVE at least the specified
seagoing service mentioned above, you will be required
to complete the Advance Fire Fighting and Proficiency
and Survival Craft in full and one of the revalidation
options c, d or e shown above.
How to apply for a CoC - Initial Issue /
Revalidation (select as required)
Lodge an application at Australia Post:
- Complete a CoC application form 419 online or
download a pdf version and then complete it.
- Complete all sections of the form.
Progress of application
If you submit an incomplete application, AMSA will contact you
for further information. If you fail to provide the requested
information within 60 days, AMSA will consider and decide your
application on the information available. This may result in
AMSA deciding to cancel the application.
More Information
Seafarer Certification Guidance containing additional
information on CoC, sea service requirements and task
books, can be obtained from the AMSA website at
www.amsa.gov.au.
