# Perpetual certificates and domestic certificates that expired more than 5 years ago

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/perpetual-certificates-competency-domestic-seafarers

How to get a National Law certificate if you have a perpetual state or territory-issued certificate, or if your certificate expired more than 5 years ago.

This information is for people who hold either:

- a perpetual certificate of competency, or
- a certificate of competency that expired more than 5 years ago.

You can use either of these to apply for an equivalent National Law certificate of competency.

Note: You can still use your perpetual certificate in the state it was issued as per the conditions on the certificate.

## How to apply

### 1. Find your equivalent certificate

Perpetual or >5 years-expired certificates are equivalent to corresponding National Law certificates.

### 2. Gather supporting documents

You will need:

- a [Certificate of medical fitness for domestic seafarers](https://www.amsa.gov.au/form1850) (AMSA form 1850)
- a current 'Provide first aid' certificate (HLTAID011), or [equivalent](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/equivalent-qualifications-and-skill-recognition/equivalent-first-aid)
- a copy of your expired or perpetual certificate of competency.

If you are applying for a Coxswain Grade 1, Master <24m, Master <45m, or Master <100m you will also need:

- a copy of your current [marine radio certificate](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-qualifications/marine-radio-certificates).

### 3. Sea service

For some certificates you will need to show evidence of sea service in the last 5 years.

This sea service must be relevant to the duties of your certificate (e.g. engineering duties for a Marine Engine Driver Grade 2).

Requirements for sea service

AMSA usually requests the sea service listed below. We may request additional information about sea service as required on a case-by-case basis.

#### Perpetual certificates

| Certificate of competency | Days of sea service in the last 5 years |
|---|---|
| Engineer Class 3Marine Engine Driver Grade 1 NCMaster <45m NCMaster <100m NC | 120 days |
| All other certificates | Not required |

#### Sea service for state or territory, or AMSA-issued certificates expired >5 years ago

| Certificate of competency | Days of sea service in the last 5 years |
|---|---|
| Coxswain Grade 1 NC Engineer Class 3Marine Engine Driver Grade 1 NCMarine Engine Driver Grade 2 NCMaster (inland waters) NCMaster <24m NCMaster <45m NCMaster <100m NC | 120 days |
| Coxswain Grade 2 NCMarine Engine Driver Grade 3 NC | 50 days |
| General Purpose Hand NC | 5 days |

#### Other options to meet sea service requirements

If you cannot provide the required number of sea service days, we may be able to accept the following alternatives:

- 60 days sea service in the last 6 months (instead of the 120 days in the past 5 years)
- completion of a task book
- completion of a final assessment (an AMSA mandated practical assessment (AMPA) or an oral examination)
- other approved industry experience.

### 4. Submit your application

- Contact AMSA Connect on 1800 627 484 to request your AMSA ID.
- Log in to myAMSA using your AMSA ID and [Australian Government myID](https://www.myid.gov.au/).
- In myAMSA:
- Select the corresponding National Law certificate
- Select the Equivalent certificate of competency option
- Select the scenario that applies to you (e.g. I hold a state, territory or AMSA issued certificate of competency that has expired more than 5 years ago)
- Upload copies of your documents.
- Pay the [required fee](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/fees-services-domestic-commercial-vessel-safety) and submit your application online.

[Apply now](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/myAMSA)

## After you apply

We will confirm receipt of your application, and you can track your application in myAMSA.

We will also contact you if:

- your application is incomplete
- the documents you provided are not suitable
- we need more information to make our assessment.

See our [incomplete application policy](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/incomplete-application-policy-international-and-near-coastal-seafarer).
