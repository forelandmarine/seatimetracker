Marine Order 74 - Certificate of Competency
Chief Mate <3000 GT (Yachts)
The following provides a summary of the requirements for a Chief Mate <3000 GT (Yachts)
CoC. This certificate is issued under Marine Order 70 (Seafarer certification) 2014 and Marine
Order 74 (Master and deck officers - yachts) 2015, made under the Navigation Act 2012.

### How do I get this certificate?

To obtain a Chief Mate <3000 GT (Yachts) CoC you
will need to satisfy the eligibility requirements of
Watchkeeper Deck <3000 GT (Yachts) as follows:
- Complete at least 36 months sea service (or at least
18 months if part of an approved and monitored
course) performing watchkeeping duties or functions in
the deck department on vessels ≥12 metres.
Of which:
- At least 8 months served on vessels ≥20 metres on
voyages of at least 24 hours duration, and
- At least 6 months of the 8 months mentioned above
was served as understudy of the officer in charge of
the navigation watch or
- An officer in charge of a navigation watch on a vessel
that AMSA considers equivalent to watchkeeping
service.
You will also need to:
- Complete an AMSA approved course of study from
an AMSA approved registered training organisation
for a Watchkeeper Deck <3000 GT (Yachts) CoC
that includes vocational training.
- Complete the following approved STCW short courses:
- Basic Safety Training course, which includes Security
Awareness training.
- Hold the following valid certificates:
- Certificate in Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue
Boats other than Fast Rescue Boats.
- GMDSS Radio Operators Certificate.
- AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness.

Yacht means a vessel that:
- Is less than 3000GT, and
- Is commercial use for sport or pleasure, and
- Does not carry cargo, and
- Does not carry more than 12 passengers.
A yacht need not be designed to carry sail and may be a
training  vessel, a sail training vessel or a short ranger
yacht provided they are not designed to carry cargo.

### How do I keep this certificate?

- Hold a valid:
- AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness.
- GMDSS Radio Operators Certificate or GMDSS
Certificate of Recognition.
- Have seagoing service performing functions
appropriate to the certificate you hold, for a
period of at least:
a) 12 months in total during the preceding 5 years
before application, or
b) 3 months in total during the preceding 6 months
prior to revalidating, or

On completion of the seagoing service, you should:
- Obtain a letter(s) from your employer(s) confirming
sea service and stating that you have actively
participated in the mandatory abandon ship and fire
drills required by SOLAS as well as scheduled drills
required by the on board Safety Management
System. A copy of the letter should be submitted with
the application (you should keep the original).
- Provide written evidence of performing radio service
in a seagoing ship fully fitted with GMDSS equipment
for sea area A3 of at least 12 months in total within
the preceding 5 years, or 3 months in total during the
preceding 6 months prior to revalidation. This should
be written on company letterhead paper and includes
the following details:
- Ship particulars - the name, IMO number, MMSI
number, ship type(s), Gross Tonnage.
- The rank served on board.
- The dates served on board the ship.
- That the ship was fitted with a full GMDSS station
(sea area A3).
- That the applicant operated the equipment on a
regular basis.
- Or have performed functions relating to the
category of GMDSS Certificate held which are
considered by AMSA to be at least equivalent
to the seagoing service required.
- Complete continued competence training for
the STCW short courses in Advanced Fire
Fighting and Fire and Proficiency in Survival
Craft and Rescue Craft other than Fast Rescue
Boats in the previous 5 years (refer to MO 74
Schedule 4).
Please note: There are three types of continued
competence courses. Please ensure you complete the
continued competence for deck / engineer officers and
chief integrated ratings.
If you DO NOT HAVE at least the specified seagoing
service mentioned above, you will be required to
complete the STCW short courses Advance Fire Fighting
and Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Craft other
than Fast Rescue Boats in full and one of the
revalidation options c, d or e shown above.
How to apply for a CoC - Initial Issue /
Revalidation (select as required)
Lodge an application at Australia Post:
- Complete a CoC application form 419 online or
download a pdf version and then complete it.
- Complete all sections of the form.
- Download a pdf version of the form to generate your
barcode.
- Print the form.
- Attach copies of all required documents.
- Sign the declaration.
- Submit your application and pay a fee at an AMSA-
contracted Australia Post outlet.
- Provide proof of identity when you lodge your
application.
AMSA may request reasonable evidence to confirm or
verify any of the information provided as part of the
application.
Please note:
- All certificates and training must comply with STCW.
- All training courses and training programs must be
approved by AMSA.
- All sea service must be documented qualifying sea
service.
