# Fees for services - domestic commercial vessel safety

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/fees-services-domestic-commercial-vessel-safety

These fees are for certificates of competency, certificates of operation, certificates of survey, the surveyor accreditation scheme, and National Law exemptions.

### Annual fee change

Annual indexation is applied to specified fees. The indexation factor applied to 2026-27 is 4.6%. The indexed fees below came into effect from 1 July 2026.
