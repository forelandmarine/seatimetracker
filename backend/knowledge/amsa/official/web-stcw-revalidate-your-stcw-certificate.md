# Revalidate your STCW certificate

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/revalidate-your-stcw-certificate

You must revalidate your STCW certificate every 5 years to stay eligible to work. Find out what you need to do and how to apply.

### Apply early to avoid delays

You can apply to revalidate up to 6 months before your certificate expires. Apply early to allow time for assessment and avoid a gap in your certification.

[Check how to revalidate your STCW certificate](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/revalidate-stcw-certificate/how-to-revalidate)
