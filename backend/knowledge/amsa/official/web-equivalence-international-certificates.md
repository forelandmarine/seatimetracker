# Working on domestic commercial vessels with STCW certificates

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/equivalent-qualifications-and-skill-recognition/international-certificates

You can use some STCW certificates in place of equivalent near coastal certificates to work on a domestic commercial vessel.

## Check your eligibility

To work on a domestic commercial vessel (DCV) with your STCW certificate, it must be both:

- current, and
- issued by AMSA.

This includes certificates of recognition issued by AMSA.

## Equivalent near coastal certificates

If you hold an STCW certificate in this table you are qualified to work on a DCV in the equivalent near coastal role. You do not need to hold the equivalent near coastal certificate.

## Applying for a near coastal certificate

If you choose to obtain the equivalent near coastal certificate, you can apply for equivalence.

Check the webpage for the equivalent near coastal certificate for instructions on how to apply.

If you hold an AMSA certificate of recognition, your near coastal certificate will only allow you to perform the duties and tasks permitted by your primary certificate of competency.

### If your STCW certificate is expired

You can:

- revalidate your STCW certificate then apply for an equivalent near coastal certificate or
- apply for a new near coastal certificate.

To apply for a new near coastal certificate, you will need to meet all of the requirements for that certificate.
