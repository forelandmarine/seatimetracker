# Replace an STCW certificate

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/international-qualifications/replace-international-certificate

Replace your lost or damaged certificate, or get a new certificate after updating your personal details.

If your certificate has been lost, stolen or damaged you will need to submit a [Seafarer form 419](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/seafarer-form-419) to get a replacement. If you have changed your personal details, such as your name or address, you will need to submit a Seafarer form 419 to get a replacement.

## Lodge from within Australia

You will need to fill out the [Seafarer form 419](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/seafarer-form-419), attach all required supporting documents and lodge it at a [participating Australia Post retail outlet](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/who-we-are/australia-post-retail-outlets).

You will need to pay the fees for your application at the post office at the time of lodgement.

## Lodge from overseas

You will need to fill out the [Seafarer form 419](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/seafarer-form-419), attach all required supporting documents and send the paperwork to us at our postal or courier address:

- Postal address - Australian Maritime Safety Authority Ship Operations and Qualifications GPO Box 2181 Canberra City ACT 2601 Australia
- Courier address - Australian Maritime Safety Authority Ship Operations and Qualifications 18 Marcus Clarke Street, Canberra ACT 2601 Australia

You must include payment with your application.
