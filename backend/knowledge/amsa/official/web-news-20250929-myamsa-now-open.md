# AMSA news: myAMSA now open, apply online for your near coastal ticket

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/news-community/news-and-media-releases/myamsa-now-open

Published: Monday 29 September 2025

myAMSA is here! Save time and apply online for your next near coastal certificate of competency using our new application portal myAMSA. Apply, submit and track the progress of your application online - anywhere, anytime - through myAMSA.

The days of having to travel to a post office to apply are over!

myAMSA uses the Australian Government’s ‘myID’ Digital ID app to securely verify your identity. myID is managed by the Australian Taxation Office (ATO). Don’t have a myID account? View the ATO’s simple guide for [setting up a myID](https://www.myid.gov.au/how-to-set-up-myid).

We are committed to being a modern regulator that offers services that reflect the needs - and meet the expectations - of our customers in this busy, digital age.

myAMSA will reduce the time and effort it takes for seafarers to apply for a near coastal certificate of competency.

Find out more about [myAMSA](https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/myAMSA) or get started straight away by [logging into myAMSA](https://my.amsa.gov.au/).
