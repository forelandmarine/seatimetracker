# Application for a Certificate of equivalence (form 326)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/application-certificate-equivalence

## Overview

You can use this form to apply for a new or re-issued Certificate of equivalence. This is for service on ships registered in the Australian international shipping register (AISR) as required by [Marine Order 2 (Australian International Shipping Register) 2013](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/9690).

## Submitting the form

You can submit your application by mail or in person at AMSA offices.

Post: Australian Maritime Safety Authority Shipping Qualifications GPO Box 2181 Canberra, ACT 2601, Australia

Courier: Australian Maritime Safety Authority Shipping Qualifications 18 Marcus Clarke Street Canberra, ACT 2601, Australia
