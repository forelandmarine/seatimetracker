# AMSA consultation: Proposed minor changes to the look of seafarer certificates (MO 70 and MO 505 amendments, 2026)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/news-community/consultations/open-consultations/proposed-minor-changes-look-seafarer-certificates

We are seeking feedback on proposed minor changes to Marine Order 70 (Seafarer certification) (MO70) and Marine Order 505 (Certificates of competency - national law) (MO505). These are regulatory changes that support the issuing of digital certificates.

## About the changes

In August 2026, AMSA introduced digital certificates for Certificates of Safety Training (CoST). Building on its success, AMSA is working to make more seafarer certificates available digitally from early-to-mid 2027.

Currently, certificates of competency are required to have a photograph and signature under MO70, and a photograph under MO505. Digital certificates will not have a photograph or signature. We are proposing minor amendments to these marine orders to facilitate this change.

## Certificates this applies to

The proposed changes will apply to these AMSA-issued seafarer certificates:
- STCW (international) certificates issued under MO70
- near coastal (domestic) certificates issued under MO505.

**Note:** The proposed changes relate to the details on seafarer certificates. They do not change seafarer qualification requirements or obligations. There are no changes or actions required from certificate holders. The introduction of digital certificates will not affect existing printed certificates while they remain valid.

## Digital certificate rollout

AMSA will provide further information in 2027 about the rollout of digital certificates. This will include what certificate holders can expect during the transition.

## Key dates

**6 October 2026:** Consultation opens

**3 December 2026:** Consultation closes

**1 April 2027:** The amended marine orders are expected to commence

**Early-to-mid 2027:** AMSA plans to make more digital certificates available

## Who the changes apply to

- Seafarers holding AMSA-issued near coastal certificates
- Seafarers holding AMSA-issued STCW certificates
- Vessel owners and operators
- Maritime employers
- Approved training and assessment organisations

## Key proposed changes

Key proposed changes include:
- removing the requirement in MO70 for digital certificates to display the holder's photograph and signature
- removing prescriptive certificate format requirements from MO505 e.g. requirement to include the holder’s photograph
- correcting outdated or incorrect references
- making other minor editorial changes.

### AMSA Regulatory Review - Miscellaneous Amendment Order: Marine Order 70 (Seafarer certification) 2014

Who does this Marine Order apply to? Marine Order 70 (Seafarer certification) 2014 (MO70) applies to: a. a person performing or intending to perform, duties or functions as a qualified seafarer on a regulated Australian vessel, b. a person who holds a seafarer certificate, and c. an owner of a regulated Australian vessel who employs a person who holds a seafarer certificate.

Background: MO70 sets out general provisions that apply for a person to be a qualified master, officer or other seafarer under the Navigation Act 2012, and gives effect to the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers. In early-to-mid-2027, AMSA will commence issuing digital certificates. Digital certificates will not include a photograph or signature of the seafarer and MO70 requires amendments to accommodate the new format. The current form of printed certificate with a photograph and signature also needs to be recognised while physical cards remain valid.

What are the key changes? The proposed amendments are:
- a. Amending the certificate form requirements to remove the photograph and signature requirements on digital certificates,
- b. Clarifying that the requirements for physically replacing, disposal and returning only applies to printed certificates, and
- c. Making minor editorial changes to update incorrect or out of date references.

Commencement: The amended Marine Order 70 (2014) is anticipated to come into effect on 1 April 2027.

### AMSA Regulatory Review - Miscellaneous Amendment Order: Marine Order 505 (Certificates of competency - national law) 2022

Who does this Marine Order apply to? Marine Order 505 (Certificates of competency - national law) 2022 (MO505) applies to: a. a person who has or is intending to obtain a certificate of competency, and b. organisations conducting or seeking approval to conduct final assessments.

Background: MO505 provides for certificates of competency and sets out standards for the qualifications of the crew and masters on domestic commercial vessels. In early-to-mid-2027, AMSA will commence issuing digital certificates. Digital certificates will not include a photograph of the seafarer and MO505 requires amendments to accommodate the new format. The current form of printed certificate with a photograph also needs to be recognised while physical cards remain valid.

What are the key changes? The proposed amendments are:
- a. Removing prescriptive certificate format requirements from MO505,
- b. Clarifying that the return of revoked certificates only applies to printed certificates, and
- c. Making minor editorial changes to update incorrect or out of date references.

Commencement: The amended Marine Order 505 (2022) is anticipated to come into effect on 1 April 2027.
