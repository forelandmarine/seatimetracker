# Incomplete application policy for seafarer certificates and endorsements

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/about/fees-levies-and-payments/incomplete-application-policy-international-and-near-coastal-seafarer

We cannot process or issue any certificate or endorsement if your application is incomplete.

## What is an incomplete application?

Any of the following issues will make an application incomplete:

- required documents are missing, incomplete, expired, or illegible
- the sea service provided does not meet the requirements
- a recent passport-style colour photograph is not included
- any signatures are missing
- the payment is missing or incorrect
- the application form is incomplete.

Note: Incorrect personal details do not usually make an application incomplete, but accurate information is essential for processing.

## If your application is incomplete

If your application is incomplete we will notify you in writing.

We will tell you:

- why your application is incomplete
- what you need to provide
- how to provide the required documents or information.

You have one calendar month to provide the documents or information. Do not submit a new application.

If you do not provide everything within one calendar month:

- your application will be closed
- you will not receive a refund
- if you reapply you will need to pay the fee again.

[Check the requirements before you apply](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/domestic-certificates-competency-cocs).
