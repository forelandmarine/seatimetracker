# Chief Mate less than 3000 GT (STCW II/1 and II/2)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/get-new-stcw-certificate/chief-mate-less-3000gt

For deck officers who want to work as Chief Mate (second in command) on vessels less than 3000 gross tonnes (GT).

## At a glance

- Hold a prerequisite STCW certificate
- Hold a valid AMSA medical (303)
- Hold a valid GMDSS certificate
- Complete approved training
- Complete qualifying sea service

- Pass an oral exam

## About this certificate

A Chief Mate <3000 GT is an STCW certificate of competency (officer-level). It is for experienced deck officers progressing into senior deck officer roles.

With this certificate, you can:

- work as Chief Mate on vessels less than 3000 gross tonnes, in any operating area
- perform watchkeeper deck officer duties on vessels of any size, in any operating area
- perform Able Seafarer  -  Deck duties or functions on vessels of any size, in any operating area.

If endorsed, you can also perform Master duties:

- on vessels less than 500 GT in any operating area
- on vessels less than 3000 GT in near coastal waters.

## Eligibility requirements

To be issued this certificate, you must:

- be at least 18 years old
- hold, or have held, one of these AMSA-issued certificates:
- Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT (with <3000 GT near-coastal endorsement)
- Watchkeeper Deck
- Mate <500 GT
- Master <500 GT
- Master <100 near coastal
- an overseas STCW certificate that AMSA considers equivalent.
- hold a valid AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness (303)
- hold a valid GMDSS radio operator certificate (or GMDSS certificate of recognition).

You must also complete the training, sea service, assessment and exam requirements explained in the steps below.

## How to get this certificate

Follow these steps to get this certificate.

(1) Check your prerequisites

To be eligible for a Chief Mate <3000 GT, you need to hold the required prerequisite and supporting certificates.

#### Prerequisite certificate

You must hold (or have held) one of the following AMSA-issued certificates:

- Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT (with <3000 GT near-coastal endorsement)
- Watchkeeper Deck
- Mate <500 GT
- Master <500 GT
- Master <100 near coastal
- or an overseas STCW certificate that AMSA considers equivalent.

If you do not hold, or have not held, one of these certificates, you are not eligible for a Chief Mate <3000 GT certificate.

#### GMDSS radio operator certificate

You must hold a valid [GMDSS radio operator certificate (or GMDSS certificate of recognition)](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/international-qualifications/global-maritime-distress-and-safety-systems).

Your GMDSS certificate may expire at a different time to your primary certificate. Make sure it is valid when you apply.

#### Certificate of medical fitness

You must hold a valid AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness (303) for STCW seafarers before you apply.

You must complete your medical examination in Australia with an AMSA-approved medical practitioner.

We do not accept:

- overseas medical assessments
- flag state medicals (such as ENG1 or OEUK/OGUK)
- AMSA near coastal medical certificates (1850).

[Find out how to get a medical for an STCW certificate](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/cost-sea-service-and-medical-fitness/certificate-medical-fitness-stcw).

(2) Complete an approved training course

To be eligible for this certificate, you must complete an approved training course and the required STCW short courses with [an AMSA approved training provider](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/international-qualifications/approved-training-courses).

Make sure your training provider is approved You must complete these courses with an AMSA-approved training provider. We do not accept training completed with other providers or overseas. [Find an AMSA approved training provider](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/international-qualifications/approved-training-courses)

#### Certificate course

Complete AMSA-approved Chief Mate <3000 GT training (STCW A-II/1 and parts of A-II/2).

This is typically delivered as part of a Diploma of Maritime Operations (Masters up to 500 GT) course.

#### STCW short courses (if not already held)

You must also have:

- Advanced fire fighting (STCW A-VI/3)
- Medical care on board ship (STCW A-VI/4)(4-6)
- Proficiency in survival craft and rescue boats other than fast rescue boats (STCW A-VI/2)
- Basic safety training (STCW Reg VI/1)
- Security awareness training (STCW VI/6 (4))

#### Recognition of prior learning

Your AMSA-approved training provider can:

- confirm which units are included in your course
- assess whether your existing training meets the requirements
- advise if your previous training may count through recognition of prior learning (RPL).

(3) Meet qualifying sea service requirements

You must complete 36 months of qualifying sea service, including 24-hour voyages and experience as a Master or OICNW.

You must complete:

- at least 1080 days (36 months)
- on vessels 12 metres or more in length
- in the deck department
- performing watchkeeping or other deck duties
- including at least 720 days (24 months) as a Master or Officer in Charge of Navigational Watch (OICNW)
- with at least 360 days (12 months) on vessels 24 metres ore more in length and on voyages of 24 hours or more.

Sea service rules Your sea service only counts while performing duties at sea. Time assigned to a vessel alone will not count as qualifying sea service towards this certificate. Special rules apply for FPSO, FSO, dredger and tug service. [Check the sea service rules for STCW certificates](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/cost-sea-service-and-medical-fitness/calculate-sea-service-stcw)

(4) Apply for a sea service assessment

Once you have completed your qualifying sea service, you need to apply to AMSA for a sea service assessment.

You will need to provide evidence of your sea service.

[Find out how to prepare your evidence and apply for a sea service assessment](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/cost-sea-service-and-medical-fitness/apply-sea-service-assessment).

(5) Pass an oral exam

You must pass an AMSA oral exam before you can apply for this certificate.

Do not book your oral exam until you have received your sea service assessment letter.

[Learn about oral exams and how to book](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/international-qualifications/oral-exams-international-and-domestic).

(6) Prepare your documents

When you apply, you need:

- Proof of identity
- A passport-style photograph (if you live overseas)
- A copy of your prerequisite STCW certificate
- AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness (303)
- GMDSS radio operator certificate or GMDSS certificate of recognition
- Course completion certificate(s) showing the relevant STCW course codes (not transcripts, attendance records or statements of attainment)
- Sea service assessment notice

Do not submit additional documents unless we ask for them.

Find out more about [proof of identity and providing supporting documents](https://www.amsa.gov.au/identity-and-supporting-documents-seafarer-certificates).

(7) Apply for your certificate

Once you have completed all the steps, you’re ready to apply.

Complete Seafarer form 419, then download and print it.

How you submit your application depends on where you live.

[Complete and submit Seafarer form 419](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/seafarer-form-419)

## Keep your certificate valid

You must revalidate this certificate every 5 years to continue working.

Find out how to [revalidate your certificate](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/stcw-international-certificates/revalidate-stcw-certificate/how-to-revalidate).

Apply early to avoid delays You should apply to revalidate your STCW certificate in the 6 months before it expires

## Get a Master <3000 GT near coastal or Master <500 GT endorsement

You can apply for an endorsement on this certificate to perform:

- Master duties on near coastal vessels up to 3000 GT
- Master duties on vessels less than 500 GT.

To be eligible, you must:

- hold (or have held) a Chief Mate <3000 GT certificate
- hold a valid AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness (303)
- meet one of the sea service pathways.

Apply using [Seafarer form 419](https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/seafarer-form-419).

Pathway 1 - with deck officer experience (36 months)

This pathway is for deck officers with qualifying watchkeeping or deck officer experience.

You must have:

- at least 1080 days (36 months)
- on vessels 24 metres or more in length
- as officer in charge of a navigational watch
- while holding one of these certificates:
- Unrestricted Master <45 m (Near Coastal)
- Chief Mate <3000 GT
- Master <500 GT
- Watchkeeper Deck
- Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT (with NC endorsement)
- Mate <500 GT
- another certificate AMSA considers equivalent
- including at least 720 days (24 months) on voyages of 24 hours or more.

Pathway 2 - with Master or Chief Mate experience (24 months)

This pathway is for experienced Chief Mates or Masters who already have senior operational experience.

You must have:

- at least 720 days (24 months)
- on vessels 24 metres or more in length
- as officer in charge of a navigational watch
- including at least 480 days (16 months) on voyages of 24 hours or more
- with at least 360 days (12 months) as a Master or Chief Mate while holding one of these certificates:
- Chief Mate <3000 GT
- Master <500 GT
- Mate <500 GT

## Add an endorsement

You may need additional endorsements depending on the vessel, cargo or duties you perform.

Endorsements available for this certificate include:

- Tanker (oil, chemical, gas)
- Security
- Polar code (basic and advanced)
- IGF code (Basic and advanced)
- Passenger vessel
- High speed craft
- Fast rescue boats
- WIG type A craft
- Dynamic position
- Sailing vessels
- ECDIS trained

[Learn more about STCW endorsements](https://www.amsa.gov.au/qualifications-training/international-qualifications/international-endorsements)

Competencies included with this certificate Training completed for this certificate already meets the requirements for: Proficiency in survival craft and rescue boats other than fast rescue boats (PSCRB) Medical care on board ships You do not need to apply for these endorsements separately.

## Career pathways

With this certificate, you can progress to higher-level deck officer certificates, including:

- Master <3000 GT
- Master (unlimited)
