# Apply for a migration skills assessment (form 12)

Source: https://www.amsa.gov.au/forms/application-migration-skills-assessment-mariners

## Overview

Use this form to apply for a skills assessment if you're a mariner or marine surveyor. You’ll need this assessment if you’re applying for a skilled occupation visa to live and work in Australia.

### Before you start

Make sure you’ve read the full instructions and understand what’s required before submitting your application.

[Go to the step-by-step guide](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/444)

## Eligibility criteria

To apply for a migration skills assessment, you must:

- have a skilled maritime occupation
- hold a valid and eligible STCW certificate of competency or Marine Surveyor qualification
- meet sea service or professional experience requirements.

[Check if you are eligible](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/444#skilled-occupations).

## How to complete this form

You need to gather and certify your documents before applying.

You'll need:

- a valid passport
- certified copies of your certificates and training records
- sea service letters
- any [additional supporting documents listed in the instructions](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/444#2Prepareyourdocuments).

[Find out more about what documents you need to apply](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/444#2Prepareyourdocuments).

## Submitting the form

To submit your application:

- complete AMSA form 12 - Application for migration skills assessment
- attach your certified documents
- email your application to STCW.applications@amsa.gov.au

After we receive your application, we'll email you payment instructions. [Check the application fee](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/330).

### What happens next

Find out about [processing times and what happens after you apply](https://www.amsa.gov.au/node/444#how-long).

We cannot give advice on immigration or visas A successful skills assessment from AMSA does not guarantee you will be issued a visa or get a job in Australia. If you have questions or need help, contact the [Department of Home Affairs](https://www.homeaffairs.gov.au/).
