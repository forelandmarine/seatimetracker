Marine Order 71 - Certificate of Competency
Mate <500 GT
The following provides a summary of the requirements for a Mate <500GT Certificate of
Competency (CoC). This certificate is issued under Marine Order 70 (Seafarer certification) 2014
and Marine Order 71 (Master and deck officers) 2023, made under the Navigation Act 2012.

### How do I get this certificate?

To obtain a Mate <500 GT CoC, you will need to:
- Complete 36 months qualifying sea service (QSS)
performing watchkeeping duties in the deck
department on ships ≥12 metres of which:
- At least 24 months served as master or an officer in
charge of a navigation watch, and
- At least 12 months was served on ships ≥24 metres on
voyages at least 24 hours duration.
- Complete an AMSA approved course of study
from an AMSA approved registered training
organisation for a Mate <500 GT.
- Complete the following approved STCW short
courses:
- Basic Safety Training course, which includes Security
Awareness Training.
- Advanced Fire Fighting.
- Medical Care on board ships.
- Hold or have held the following certificates,
either a:
- Valid Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT or
- Unrestricted Master <24 metres NC CoC, or
- Certificate AMSA considers equivalent to these.
- Hold the following valid certificates:
- Certificate in Proficiency in Survival Craft and

### How do I keep this certificate?

To revalidate your certificate you must:
- Hold a valid:
- AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness.
- GMDSS Radio Operators Certificate or
GMDSS Certificate of Recognition.
- Have seagoing service performing functions
appropriate to the certificate you hold, for a period
of at least:
-
 Chief Mate or Watchkeeper Deck Officer on ships <500 GT in any operating area
 Master on ships <500 GT in near-coastal waters
 Watchkeeper Deck Officer on ships <3000 GT in near-coastal waters
 If endorsed, Chief Mate on ships <3000 GT in near- coastal waters
 Able Seafarer Deck - deck duties or functions

On completion of the seagoing service, you should:
- Obtain a letter(s) from your employer(s) confirming sea
service and stating that you have actively participated in
the mandatory abandon ship and fire drills required by
SOLAS as well as scheduled drills required by the on
board Safety Management System (see sample
template letter). A copy of the letter should be submitted
with the application (you should keep the original).
- Provide written evidence of performing radio service in
a seagoing ship fully fitted with GMDSS equipment for
sea area A3 of at least 12 months in total within the
preceding 5 years, or 3 months in total during the
preceding 6 months prior to revalidation. This should
be written on company letterhead paper and includes
the following details:
- Ship particulars - the name, IMO number,
MMSI number, ship type(s), Gross Tonnage.
- The rank served on board.
- The dates served on board the ship.
- That the ship was fitted with a full GMDSS
station (sea area A3).
- That the applicant operated the equipment
on a regular basis.
- Or have performed functions relating to the category of
GMDSS Certificate held which are considered by
AMSA   to be at least equivalent to the seagoing
service required.
- Complete continued competence training for the
STCW short courses in Advanced Fire Fighting and
Fire and Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Craft
other than Fast Rescue Boats in the previous 5 years
(refer to MO 71 Schedule 4).
Please note: There are three types of continued
competence courses. Please ensure you complete the
continued competence for deck / engineer officers and
chief integrated ratings.
If you DO NOT HAVE at least the specified seagoing
service mentioned above, you will be required to
complete the Advance Fire Fighting and Proficiency and
Survival Craft in full and one of the revalidation options c,
d or e shown above.
How to Apply for CoC Initial
Issue/Revalidation (select as required)
Lodge an application at Australia Post:
- Complete a CoC application form 419 online or
download a pdf version and then complete it.
- Complete all sections of the form.
- Download a pdf version of the form to generate your
barcode.
- Print the form.
- Attach copies of all required documents.
- Sign the declaration.
- Submit your application and pay a fee at an AMSA-
contracted Australia Post outlet.
- Provide proof of identity when you lodge your application.
AMSA may request reasonable evidence to confirm or
verify any of the information provided as part of the
application.
Please note:
- All certificates and training must comply with STCW.
- All training courses and training programs must be
approved by AMSA.
- All sea service must be documented qualifying sea
service.
