# AMSA knowledge library manifest

Built 2026-10-25 for the SeaTime Tracker 'Explain training pathways' assistant.

- `official/`: Australian Government documents. Marine Orders were downloaded as the current compilation (Word version) from the Federal Register of Legislation (legislation.gov.au, CC BY 4.0) and converted to markdown; long Marine Orders are split by Division or Schedule. AMSA web pages were fetched from amsa.gov.au (CC BY 4.0 per https://www.amsa.gov.au/copyright) with navigation, banners and print links removed. AMSA PDFs and Word files (guidance notices, AMPA forms, oral exam syllabi and checklists, medical standards, templates) were converted to text; PDF table columns are read left to right, so a requirement and its standard can appear one after another.
- `providers/`: paraphrased fact notes from Australian training providers and industry bodies (third-party copyright, no verbatim copying). Secondary sources; where they disagree with the official text they are flagged `CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL` and the Marine Order or AMSA page wins.
- Every official file has YAML frontmatter with title, authority, doc_ref, url, version_date, licence, retrieved (2026-10-25) and currency_note (CURRENT / EXPIRED / SUPERSEDED). See COVERAGE.md for which files answer which question for each certificate.

## Currency and supersession summary

- Domestic (National Law) certificates: Marine Order 505 (Certificates of competency - national law) 2022 is CURRENT, compilation No. 2 dated 1 June 2025 (register ID F2025C00646). Its new certificate structure applies to applications from 1 January 2023; it replaced MO 505 (Certificates of competency - national law) 2013 (repealed), which had replaced NSCV Part D. Old grades (Master <35 m NC, Master <80 m NC, Mate <80 m NC) survive only as corresponding certificates (MO 505 s7 and definitions).
- STCW (Navigation Act) certificates were NOT consolidated: the 2014 series is still in force alongside a remade MO 71. Current: MO 70 (Seafarer certification) 2014, compilation No. 11, 1 April 2026; MO 71 (Masters and deck officers) 2023, compilation No. 1, 23 December 2024 (replaced MO 71 2014 from 21 November 2023); MO 72 (Engineer officers) 2014, compilation No. 3, 1 January 2023; MO 73 (Ratings) 2014, compilation No. 2, 1 January 2018; MO 74 (Masters and deck officers - yachts) 2015, compilation No. 4, 1 July 2024.
- Medical: Marine Order 9 (Health - medical fitness) is REPEALED; it was replaced by Marine Order 76 (Health - medical fitness) 2017 from 1 January 2018 (never amended). The STCW medical standard is now "Standards for the medical examination of seafarers and coastal pilots" (AMSA 182), June 2026 edition; the STCW form 303 PDF is dated July 2026. Domestic seafarers use the January 2023 domestic standards and form 1850.
- Australian STCW yacht certificates sit in their own Marine Order (MO 74): Watchkeeper Deck <3000 GT (Yachts) (with an optional Master <200 GT (Yachts) endorsement), Chief Mate <3000 GT (Yachts), Master <500 GT (Yachts), Master <3000 GT (Yachts). There is no Chief Mate <500 GT (Yachts) grade in MO 74.
- Final assessments: lower near coastal grades (GPH, Coxswain 2 and 1, Master Inland Waters, Master <24 m, MED 3 and 2) use the AMSA Mandated Practical Assessment (AMPA) at an RTO; Master <45 m, Master <100 m, MED 1 and Engineer Class 3 NC use an AMSA oral exam, like the STCW certificates.
- Current marine notices on certification (from the index of current marine notices, 2026-10-25): 2025/05 Revalidation of Australian STCW certificates (last updated 21 November 2025) and 2025/03 Watchkeeping and crewing standards; 2021/08 ECDIS is stored for the ECDIS endorsement rules. Cancelled notices that once changed certification rules (2016/14 revalidation of CoCs and CoPs, 2016/07 perpetual ratings certificates, 2020/02 and 2020/09 COVID extensions, 2016/20 STCW amendments) were not stored.
- AMSA guidance notice PDFs for STCW certificates are dated June 2024; where they differ from a newer AMSA web page or the Marine Order, the newer source wins. The STCW web pages were mostly updated in 2025 and 2026.
- Recent changes found (AMSA news, consultations and marine notices 2024 to 2026, checked 2026-10-25): myAMSA opened for near coastal applications on 29 September 2025; online CoST applications and digital CoSTs started in August to September 2026; AMSA widened its approved STCW medical provider network on 25 August 2026 (standards unchanged); new oral exam resources were released on 19 October 2026; AMSA is consulting (6 October to 3 December 2026) on minor MO 70 and MO 505 amendments to allow digital CoCs without a photo or signature, expected to commence 1 April 2027 with digital CoCs from early to mid 2027. These change certificate format only, not qualification rules. Domestic fees were indexed by 4.6% from 1 July 2026 (stored fees page). No consultation on remaking MO 70 to MO 74 or on changing MO 505 eligibility or sea service was found, and no new certification marine notice after 2025/05.
- Exemption 23 (Masters or crew without certificates) 2025 is CURRENT 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2030 but only helps people who worked uncertificated on 30 June 2013 and notified AMSA before 2018.
