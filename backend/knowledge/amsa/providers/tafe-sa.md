# TAFE SA: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of the TAFE SA course page for MAR20324 Certificate II in Maritime Operations (Coxswain Grade 1 Near Coastal). It is not TAFE SA's text. Marine Order 505 and AMSA's pages are the binding rules and win over anything here.

## Q: Where and how long is the TAFE SA Coxswain Grade 1 course?
Four weeks full time, day classes on campus at Port Lincoln. Applications listed on the page were for the 2027 intake, made free of charge through SATAC. TAFE SA warns the course is heavily applied for and places may be ranked by application date.
Source: https://www.tafesa.edu.au/xml/course/aw/aw_TP01459.aspx

## Q: What does it cost?
Indicative, October 2026: about AUD 346 for subsidised or concession places and about AUD 4,240 full fee. Fees are charged per semester and depend on units enrolled.
Source: https://www.tafesa.edu.au/xml/course/aw/aw_TP01459.aspx

## Q: What water and fitness tests apply?
You must be able to stay afloat for at least 5 minutes without a lifejacket, get into a life raft unaided, and do roughly 45 minutes of water activities in a lifejacket. You also complete a language, literacy, numeracy and digital assessment and a water skills self-declaration, and you must be an Australian resident.
Source: https://www.tafesa.edu.au/xml/course/aw/aw_TP01459.aspx

## Q: Does the course alone give me a Coxswain certificate?
No. TAFE SA says the qualification is required for the AMSA Coxswain Grade 1 NC certificate of competency, but the page does not cover sea service, task books, the AMPA or medicals. Those come from Marine Order 505: 30 days with an AMSA task book on commercial vessels of 5 m or more, or 150 days without, plus the AMPA, a medical and a radio certificate.
Source: https://www.tafesa.edu.au/xml/course/aw/aw_TP01459.aspx

## Q: Is first aid included?
Yes, HLTAID011 Provide First Aid is one of the 13 core units. If you do not finish all units you receive a statement of attainment for those completed.
Source: https://www.tafesa.edu.au/xml/course/aw/aw_TP01459.aspx
