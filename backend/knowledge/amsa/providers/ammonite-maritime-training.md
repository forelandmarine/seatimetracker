# Ammonite: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of a private RTO's course booking page. It is not the provider's text. It is useful mainly as a snapshot of private RTO pricing; Marine Order 505 and AMSA pages are the binding rules.

## Q: Can I do just the AMPA if I trained somewhere else?
Yes, at this RTO. It lists an "AMPA only" option for students who trained with another provider (RPL students), indicative AUD 750, October 2026. Not every RTO runs AMPAs, so ask before you book.
Source: https://ammonite.com.au/maritime/coursesbooking

## Q: What do private RTO courses cost for the domestic ladder?
Indicative, October 2026, from this provider's price list (AUD):
- General Purpose Hand NC (MAR10224): 1,800, online plus practical or face to face.
- Coxswain Grade 2 NC (MAR10424): 2,390.
- Coxswain Grade 1 NC (MAR20324): 3,390. A live-aboard sailing version on the vessel Starsand is about 5,500 in total.
- Marine Engine Driver Grade 3 NC (MAR20424): 3,390, face to face.
- Master up to 24 m NC (MAR30924): 5,150, online plus practical.
Course lengths are not given for most of these.
Source: https://ammonite.com.au/maritime/coursesbooking

## Q: What are the add-on tickets and their costs?
Indicative, October 2026 (AUD): Shipboard Safety skill set MARSS00008 about 800; Short Range Operator Certificate of Proficiency (SROCP, listed as suitable for Coxswain 1) 285; Long Range Operator Certificate of Proficiency (LROCP) 350; HLTAID011 Provide First Aid 150 (finish the online theory before the practical).
Source: https://ammonite.com.au/maritime/coursesbooking

## Q: Is there a Coxswain Grade 3 course?
Yes. The provider sells Coxswain Grade 3 NC (three units for vessels up to 12 m) as online theory plus a 1-day practical (about AUD 1,000) or face to face (about AUD 1,200), and a shorter face-to-face option for people who already hold a boat licence (about AUD 870), referencing AMSA form 1882.
Source: https://ammonite.com.au/maritime/coursesbooking

## Q: Does the page explain sea time or task books?
No. The page lists no sea time, task book or location details, so use Marine Order 505 and AMSA's certificate pages for those.
Source: https://ammonite.com.au/maritime/coursesbooking
