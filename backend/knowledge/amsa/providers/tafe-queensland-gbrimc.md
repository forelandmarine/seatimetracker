# TAFE Queensland (GBRIMC): paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of two TAFE Queensland course pages delivered at the Great Barrier Reef International Marine College in North Queensland. It is not TAFE Queensland's text. Marine Order 505 and AMSA's pages win if anything here differs.

## Q: How long is the Master up to 24 m course at TAFE Queensland?
Six weeks full time, five days a week (8:30 am to 4:30 pm), fully on campus with on-campus assessment. Practical training uses a 19 m commercially registered training vessel. Listed starts: February 2027 and July 2027. Shipboard safety units and HLTAID011 first aid run as a separate block.
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/course/17/17859/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal

## Q: What does Master <24m cost there?
Indicative, October 2026 (AUD): concession 3,045, subsidised 3,780, full fee 7,140. Payment plans are offered.
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/course/17/17859/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal

## Q: When does my AMPA expire?
TAFE Queensland issues the AMPA on the last day of the course and says it expires 12 months after the first date shown on the AMPA, not 12 months after the final sign-off. Plan your AMSA application (and remaining sea time) around that date.
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/course/17/17859/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal

## Q: Can I get an interim certificate after the course?
Yes. Once you have applied to AMSA for the certificate of competency, you can ask your assessor for an interim certificate, subject to AMSA's criteria. (Under Marine Order 505 an interim certificate lasts up to 60 days.)
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/course/17/17859/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal

## Q: What water and fitness tests apply to the shipboard safety units?
Swim 50 m in a lifejacket, stay afloat 5 minutes in work clothes with and without a lifejacket, and climb unaided into a life raft wearing a lifejacket. You also do a skills check (English, maths, digital, benchmark level 3), and TAFE recommends being at least 16 at the start.
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/course/17/17859/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal

## Q: Does the TAFE Queensland page tell me the sea time for Master <24m?
No. It does not cover sea service, task books, medicals or radio certificates. Under Marine Order 505: 120 days on commercial vessels of at least 7.5 m with an AMSA task book, or 360 days without (at least half on commercial vessels).
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/course/17/17859/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal

## Q: How long is Marine Engine Driver Grade 2 (MED2) and what does it cost?
Five weeks full time on campus plus first aid. Listed starts: November 2026, February 2027 and August 2027. Indicative, October 2026 (AUD): concession 1,330, subsidised 2,060, full fee 5,425. The AMPA is issued on the last day and follows the same 12-month expiry rule.
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/courses/17870/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-marine-engine-driver-grade-2-near-coastal

## Q: What can I do with MED2, and does it count toward MED1?
After AMSA issues the MED2 certificate: chief engineer up to 750 kW, second engineer up to 1500 kW, and engine room work on vessels up to 80 m and 3000 kW. TAFE Queensland says the course also gives partial completion of the MED1 qualification. Under Marine Order 505, MED2 needs 180 days (task book) or 360 days on vessels of at least 150 kW, and MED1 then needs 120 or 240 days on vessels of at least 375 kW while holding MED2.
Source: https://tafeqld.edu.au/courses/17870/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-marine-engine-driver-grade-2-near-coastal
