# ETO training (MAR50120 Diploma of Marine Engineering): paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of course listings. It is not their text. AMSA's rules for the Electro-Technical Officer certificate are in MO 72, web-intl-electro-technical-officer.md and guidance-notice-eto.md. No Australian provider page describing an ETO-specific stream in detail could be fetched (AMC, TAFE NSW and South Metropolitan TAFE ETO pages were not found or not reachable).

## Q: Which course do I do for an AMSA Electro-Technical Officer certificate?
Course listings name the national qualification MAR50120 Diploma of Marine Engineering as suitable for people seeking AMSA certification as Electro-Technical Officer (STCW Electro-Technical Officer Unlimited), as well as Engineer Watchkeeper and Engineer Class 3 NC. The ETO version is the one that meets STCW A-III/6. AMSA's guidance notice says the course must be an AMSA approved course from an AMSA approved RTO and must include workshop skills training, and that RTOs offer recognition of prior learning to licensed electricians.
Source: https://search.studyaustralia.gov.au/course/diploma-of-marine-engineering-mar50120/9e47fea655ad47ee27528b218df646b0

## Q: Does the diploma alone give me the ETO certificate?
No. The listing says the diploma meets only some of the requirements. You also need qualifying sea service, the STCW short courses, a valid AMSA Certificate of Medical Fitness and a pass in the AMSA final assessment (oral exam), and you should confirm requirements with AMSA.
Source: https://search.studyaustralia.gov.au/course/diploma-of-marine-engineering-mar50120/9e47fea655ad47ee27528b218df646b0

## Q: Where and how long, and what does it cost?
One example: TAFE International Western Australia lists MAR50120 at its Fremantle campus, full time over 2 semesters, with a Semester 1 2026 start and total international tuition of about AUD 12,550. Domestic fees and other campuses differ, so check the provider.
Source: https://search.studyaustralia.gov.au/course/diploma-of-marine-engineering-mar50120/9e47fea655ad47ee27528b218df646b0

## Q: Is MAR50120 listed in other states?
South Australia's skills catalogue lists MAR50120 with the same three AMSA certification outcomes (Engineer Class 3 NC, Engineer Watchkeeper, Electro-Technical Officer) but showed no provider, dates or entry requirements when checked.
Source: https://mytraining.skills.sa.gov.au/courses/mar50120

## Q: The listing mentions "NSCV Part D". Is that still the rule?
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (out of date wording): the Engineer Class 3 NC note in the listing refers to NSCV Part D. Near coastal certificates have been issued under Marine Order 505 since 1 January 2023 (mo-505-div1.md). This does not affect the ETO, which is an STCW certificate under MO 72.
Source: https://search.studyaustralia.gov.au/course/diploma-of-marine-engineering-mar50120/9e47fea655ad47ee27528b218df646b0
