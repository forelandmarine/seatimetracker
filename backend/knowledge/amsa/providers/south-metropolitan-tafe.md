# South Metropolitan TAFE: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of South Metropolitan TAFE course pages for its Fremantle maritime campus. It is not SM TAFE's text. Some details on these pages look dated (flagged below). Marine Order 505, Marine Orders 70 to 76 and AMSA's pages win.

## Q: How long is Master up to 24 m at SM TAFE, and what does it cost?
Options listed: 18 weeks blended (online theory, practicals on campus) or 8 weeks full-time on campus, with about 4 weeks of practical attendance (5 if you also need Elements of Shipboard Safety). Indicative, October 2026: resource fee about AUD 2,870 with no tuition fee; fee free for eligible WA residents. SM TAFE prefers applicants to hold Coxswain Grade 1 and be working in the industry. AMSA also needs an AMPA on top of the course.
Source: https://www.southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/certificate-iii-maritime-operations-master-24-metres-near-coastal-0

## Q: What can a Master <24m holder do, according to SM TAFE?
Master of commercial vessels under 24 m within the EEZ (unlimited outboard power or inboard up to 500 kW), chief mate or deck watchkeeper up to 45 m in the EEZ or up to 100 m inshore, plus GPH and Master Inland Waters duties. Next steps listed: Master <45m, Watchkeeper Deck, Master <500 GT and Master Unlimited.
Source: https://www.southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/certificate-iii-maritime-operations-master-24-metres-near-coastal-0

## Q: How long is Master up to 45 m and what are the prerequisites?
8 weeks full time on campus. Indicative, October 2026: resource fee about AUD 1,775, no tuition, fee free for eligible WA residents. Listed prerequisites: Elements of Shipboard Safety, first aid, and the Certificate III Master <24m.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the page names first aid unit HLTAID003 and Certificate III code MAR30921. The current first aid unit is HLTAID011 and the current Master <24m code is MAR30924.
Source: https://www.southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/certificate-iv-maritime-operations-master-45-metres-near-coastal-0

## Q: What sea time does SM TAFE quote for Master <45m?
It says you need an AMSA task book or qualifying sea service, a long range operator certificate of proficiency, medical and eyesight tests, age 16 or over, and a final assessment. It also says the qualification cannot be used for work without the certificate of competency unless you have served 600 days as Master <24m, or 180 days with an approved task book.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: Marine Order 505 Schedule 3 requires 180 days on vessels of at least 12 m while holding Master <24m, including 120 days as officer of the watch with a task book, or 360 days including 240 days without a task book. The 600-day figure does not match.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the page says the final assessment is at an AMSA office. Under Marine Order 505 the domestic final assessment is the AMPA, done at an RTO.
Source: https://www.southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/certificate-iv-maritime-operations-master-45-metres-near-coastal-0

## Q: How long is the Diploma (Watchkeeper Deck) and who can enrol?
25 weeks full time over two semesters at Fremantle, with basic firefighting and personal survival held over 5 days at a separate centre in Jandakot. Indicative, October 2026: resource fees about AUD 6,510 across both semesters, no tuition for eligible WA residents, VET Student Loan eligible. Entry routes include Master <45m, Integrated Rating with training record books and sea time, or an AMSA-approved trade.
Source: https://www.southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/diploma-maritime-operations-watchkeeper-deck

## Q: What does AMSA require after the Watchkeeper Deck diploma?
Medical and eyesight standards, qualifying sea service as an understudy to the officer of the watch (details on AMSA's site), and a final assessment with AMSA. For STCW certificates under Marine Order 71 this final step is AMSA's oral examination.
Source: https://www.southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/diploma-maritime-operations-watchkeeper-deck

## Q: What is the Master less than 500 GT diploma at SM TAFE?
Two semesters (29 weeks) full time at Fremantle. Indicative, October 2026: resource fees about AUD 7,620 combined, no tuition for eligible WA residents. Entry routes are the same as for Watchkeeper Deck. AMSA then needs medical and eyesight standards, sea service as officer in charge of a navigational watch, and an oral examination. Outcomes listed include master or chief mate under 500 GT in any area and master or chief mate under 3000 GT near coastal. The page does not mention yachts or Marine Order 74.
Source: https://www.southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/diploma-maritime-operations-master-500-gt-0

## Q: I lack sea time to revalidate my STCW deck CoC. What course does SM TAFE run?
Masters Revalidation Part B: 10 days at Fremantle, indicative AUD 3,000 (October 2026), for masters and deck officers with too little sea time in the past five years. Entry needs a valid or expired Australian deck CoC, security awareness endorsement, HLTAID011 done within 12 months of course end, and an AMSA GMDSS certificate or certificate of recognition. Part A is covered by separate survival craft, advanced firefighting and (if needed) GMDSS courses. Officers with enough sea time may only need a short firefighting and sea survival refresher. Without a generic ECDIS certificate you need a full ECDIS course before AMSA will endorse you for ECDIS ships.
Source: https://southmetrotafe.wa.edu.au/courses/masters-revalidation-part-b
