# RYA Australia endorsement and the AMSA Sailing Master route: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of the RYA's endorsement pages. It is not their text, and RYA course content is not reproduced. The binding rules are MO 505 Schedule 3 and AMSA's pages web-domestic-sailing-master-coastal-nc.md and web-domestic-sailing-master-offshore-nc.md. The RYA pages show no date apart from a 2026 copyright line.

## Q: How does an RYA Yachtmaster get me an AMSA Sailing Master certificate?
AMSA does not run its own Sailing Master course or exam. Instead it accepts a commercially endorsed RYA Yachtmaster certificate of competence (or an IYT Master of Yachts certificate) as the core prerequisite, and then adds its own requirements: an engine qualification from AMSA's list, a marine radio certificate, first aid, an AMSA domestic medical (form 1850, or a current form 303) and an application in myAMSA. The RYA describes its side of this as an equivalency arrangement between the RYA and AMSA.
Source: https://www.rya.org.uk/training/careers/commercial-endorsements/australia-endorsements/

## Q: What is the RYA "Australia endorsement"?
An RYA add-on with two uses: validating an RYA cruising or Yachtmaster instructor qualification for instructing in Australia only, and validating an RYA Yachtmaster (Sail) certificate with a full commercial endorsement as a prerequisite for the AMSA Sailing Master certificate. It lets the holder use the Australian domestic medical (AMSA 1850) instead of a UK medical.
Source: https://www.rya.org.uk/training/careers/commercial-endorsements/australia-endorsements/

## Q: What do I send the RYA for the Australia endorsement?
In summary: the original Yachtmaster certificate, proof of sea survival training (STCW personal survival techniques or the RYA basic sea survival course), an AMSA 1850 certificate of medical fitness (the RYA notes it was previously AMSA 559), the RYA professional practices and responsibilities certificate, a passport photo and the RYA fee. Check the RYA form for the current fee.
Source: https://www.rya.org.uk/training/careers/commercial-endorsements/australia-endorsements/

## Q: Does the Australia endorsement let me skipper commercially?
No. The RYA says the endorsement on its own does not allow work on UK or Australian flagged commercial vessels. To work as skipper on an Australian domestic commercial sailing vessel you need the AMSA Sailing Master Coastal or Offshore NC certificate itself, issued by AMSA.
Source: https://www.rya.org.uk/training/careers/commercial-endorsements/australia-endorsements/

## Q: Which RYA level do I need: Coastal Skipper or Yachtmaster Offshore?
The RYA page does not map RYA levels to the two AMSA certificates. Use AMSA's pages: Sailing Master Offshore needs a commercially endorsed RYA Yachtmaster Offshore or Ocean (or IYT Master of Yachts Limited); Sailing Master Coastal needs a commercially endorsed RYA Yachtmaster Coastal (or IYT Master of Yachts Coastal) (web-domestic-sailing-master-coastal-nc.md). Neither AMSA certificate needs AMSA sea service of its own, because the RYA exam already has its own sea time and mileage prerequisites.
Source: https://www.rya.org.uk/training/careers/commercial-endorsements/australia-endorsements/

## Q: What is a "commercial endorsement" in RYA terms?
The RYA's general endorsement page says a commercial endorsement is what makes certain RYA certificates usable for professional work on UK-flag vessels under the MCA small commercial vessel codes. It needs a UK medical (ML5, or ENG1 for the most exposed waters), sea survival training, a marine radio certificate and the online professional practices and responsibilities course, and it is revalidated every 5 years with at least 150 days of relevant sea service in that period. Yachtmaster Offshore and Ocean can also carry an STCW endorsement. Crew using the certificate for AMSA's Sailing Master are pointed to the separate Australia endorsement above, which accepts the AMSA 1850 medical.
Source: https://www.rya.org.uk/training/careers/commercial-endorsements/
