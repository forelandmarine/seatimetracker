# TAFE NSW: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of two TAFE NSW course pages. It is not TAFE NSW's text. When fetched, neither page showed any current study or location options, fees or durations, so check with a campus. Marine Order 505 and AMSA's pages win if anything here differs.

## Q: What do I need before starting Master up to 24 m at TAFE NSW?
The Certificate II in Maritime Operations (Coxswain Grade 1 NC) and an AMSA Coxswain Grade 1 certificate of competency, plus relevant industry experience. TAFE NSW also recommends physical fitness, resistance to chronic seasickness, the ability to work in all weather and meeting AMSA's medical standard.
Source: https://www.tafensw.edu.au/course-areas/maritime/courses/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal--MAR30924-01

## Q: What happens after I finish the Master <24m course?
You apply to AMSA and meet its other conditions, such as sea time and the medical standard. TAFE NSW says relevant industry experience can count as qualifying sea service toward the AMSA Master <24m NC certificate. (Marine Order 505: 120 days with a task book on commercial vessels of at least 7.5 m, or 360 days without, at least half commercial; plus the AMPA.)
Source: https://www.tafensw.edu.au/course-areas/maritime/courses/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal--MAR30924-01

## Q: Where does Master <24m lead at TAFE NSW?
Earlier steps listed: General Purpose Hand NC and Coxswain Grade 1 NC. Next steps: Certificate IV in Maritime Operations (Master up to 45 m NC) or the Diploma of Maritime Operations (Watchkeeper Deck). The course has 15 units (12 core and 3 specialty), and not every specialty unit runs at every campus.
Source: https://www.tafensw.edu.au/course-areas/maritime/courses/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-master-up-to-24-metres-near-coastal--MAR30924-01

## Q: How do I get into Marine Engine Driver Grade 2 (MED2)?
Either hold Certificate I in Maritime Operations (General Purpose Hand NC) plus the AMSA General Purpose Hand NC certificate, or hold a mechanical trade qualification plus maritime experience. Certificate II (MED3) is recommended first.
Source: https://www.tafensw.edu.au/course-areas/maritime/courses/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-marine-engine-driver-grade-2-near-coastal--MAR30824-01

## Q: What sea time counts for MED2?
TAFE NSW says the sea service must be on vessels with inboard diesel engines of at least 150 kW, which matches Marine Order 505 (180 days with a task book, 360 without, or 90/180 with workshop skills).
Source: https://www.tafensw.edu.au/course-areas/maritime/courses/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-marine-engine-driver-grade-2-near-coastal--MAR30824-01

## Q: Is there a time limit after the MED2 course?
TAFE NSW says you must apply to AMSA within 12 months of finishing the course or AMPA, and that all the AMSA sea time must be completed within that same 12-month period.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: Marine Order 505 Schedule 3 sets no 12-month window for earning MED2 sea service. The 12-month limit we can confirm is the validity of the AMPA. Check with AMSA before relying on this.
Source: https://www.tafensw.edu.au/course-areas/maritime/courses/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-marine-engine-driver-grade-2-near-coastal--MAR30824-01

## Q: What engines can a MED2 holder run?
Per TAFE NSW: chief engineer on domestic vessels with inboard propulsion under 750 kW, second engineer under 1500 kW, chief or second engineer with outboards of unlimited power, and engine room work up to 3000 kW. The course has 17 core units including HLTAID011 first aid.
Source: https://www.tafensw.edu.au/course-areas/maritime/courses/certificate-iii-in-maritime-operations-marine-engine-driver-grade-2-near-coastal--MAR30824-01
