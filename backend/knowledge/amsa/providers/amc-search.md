# AMC Search: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of AMC Search pages on revalidating Australian STCW certificates. It is not AMC Search's text. The main amc.edu.au course pages (Master <24m, Master <45m, MED) refused automated access, so they are not covered here. Marine Order 70, Marine Notice 2025/05 and AMSA's revalidation pages win.

## Q: Do I need a refresher or a full revalidation course for my STCW CoC?
It depends on sea time. AMC Search quotes AMSA's test: at least 12 months in total in the 5 years before you apply, or 3 months in total in the 6 months before you revalidate. With that sea time, masters, deck and engineer officers only need the continued competence refresher (sea survival and firefighting). Without it, you need the revalidation parts for your role. Ask AMSA if unsure.
Source: https://www.amcsearch.com.au/revalidate-coc-stcw

## Q: Which revalidation parts apply to me?
Masters and deck officers: Part A and Part B. Pilots: Part A only. Engineer officers: Part A and Part C (AMC Search does not run Part C).
Source: https://www.amcsearch.com.au/revalidate-coc-stcw

## Q: How long are the AMC Search refresher and revalidation courses?
CoC continued competence refresher: about 5 hours online. Revalidation Part A: about 12 hours online. Revalidation Part B: about 10 hours online plus 2 days in the simulator at Launceston. Related 4-day courses: advanced firefighting, proficiency in survival craft, and GMDSS renewal (for GMDSS holders without enough sea time to revalidate the radio certificate).
Source: https://www.amcsearch.com.au/revalidate-coc-stcw

## Q: What does Revalidation Part B cost and what is in it?
Indicative, October 2026: about AUD 2,915 for the November 2026 class and about AUD 3,030 for 2027 classes (GST exempt). Online modules cover bridge resource management, buoyage, COLREGs, ECDIS refresher, electronic positioning and electronic passage planning, finished at least two working days before the 2-day simulator block. You need 100% attendance. Entry needs a valid STCW master or deck officer CoC. On-campus rooms were about AUD 100 a night.
Source: https://www.amcsearch.com.au/course/certificate-of-competency-coc-revalidation-1-1

## Q: What else does AMSA need when a deck officer revalidates?
AMC Search lists, as a guide: a valid AMSA seafarer medical from an AMSA-approved doctor (our note: the STCW medical under Marine Order 76, form 303), a valid AMSA GMDSS GOC or GMDSS certificate of recognition, and an ECDIS endorsement if you will serve on ECDIS-fitted ships. It says several other flag states (for example the Bahamas, Cayman Islands, Marshall Islands and Liberia) also accept the Part B course.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the page says the course meets Marine Order 71 (Masters and Deck Officers) 2014. The current order is Marine Order 71 (2023).
Source: https://www.amcsearch.com.au/course/certificate-of-competency-coc-revalidation-1-1

## Q: Where is refresher training done?
At the Launceston campus of the Australian Maritime College, with an indoor survival pool (ship mock-up, liferaft launching, simulated weather) and an on-site firefighting ground. AMC Search runs refreshers for the certificate of safety training, rating CoPs, chief integrated rating and CoCs.
Source: https://www.amcsearch.com.au/stcw-refresher
