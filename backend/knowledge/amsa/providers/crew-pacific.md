# Crew Pacific: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of Crew Pacific's web pages. It is not their text. Crew Pacific is one of two Australian training providers named in Superyacht Australia's 2023 crew guide. Marine Orders 70, 73, 74 and AMSA's recognition pages are the binding rules.

## Q: How long is the STCW basic safety course in Cairns, and what does it cost?
Five days, Monday to Friday, covering personal survival techniques, personal safety and social responsibility, and basic fire prevention and firefighting. Security awareness is done online and included. Indicative, October 2026: about AUD 2,830 for 2026 dates. You must also hold a senior first aid certificate (about AUD 145 to 195 extra).
Source: https://crewpacific.com.au/jobcourse/stcw95-10-course/

## Q: Where is it held and who delivers it?
At the Great Barrier Reef Maritime College campus of TAFE Queensland (RTO 0275) in Manunda, Cairns, where Crew Pacific also has its office. Roughly monthly dates are listed for 2026.
Source: https://crewpacific.com.au/jobcourse/stcw95-10-course/

## Q: How do I turn the course into an AMSA certificate?
Crew Pacific mentions an AMSA fee of about AUD 112 for applying for the AMSA certificate of safety training, using AMSA form 419. Check the current fee with AMSA.
Source: https://crewpacific.com.au/jobcourse/stcw95-10-course/

## Q: Is AMSA or MCA basic training better for Australian yachts?
Crew Pacific says AMSA-endorsed training is accepted on both Australian-flagged and foreign-flagged superyachts, while MCA-endorsed courses are only accepted on foreign-flagged (offshore) yachts because AMSA does not recognise MCA training.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: this is a broad provider claim. AMSA does issue certificates of recognition for some foreign STCW certificates of competency, so check AMSA's recognition guidance for your exact certificate before assuming it is unusable on an Australian-flagged yacht.
Source: https://crewpacific.com.au/

## Q: What entry-level yacht courses does Crew Pacific run?
Steward/ess and deckhand courses in 2, 5, 8 and 10-day formats (including a 2-day induction), plus the STCW course. It also says it can place trained crew on superyachts worldwide, typically within 1 to 3 months. The site does not cover officer certificates, sea time or Marine Order 74 yacht certificates.
Source: https://crewpacific.com.au/
