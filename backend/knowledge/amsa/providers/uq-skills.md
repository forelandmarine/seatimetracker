# UQ Skills: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of the UQ Skills page for MAR20324 Certificate II in Maritime Operations (Coxswain Grade 1 Near Coastal). It is not UQ's text. Marine Order 505 and AMSA's pages win if anything here differs.

## Q: How is the UQ Skills Coxswain Grade 1 course structured?
Ten weeks of blended study: online learning plus in-person sessions at the St Lucia campus and days at sea. It runs in four study periods: shipboard safety and first aid, coxswain deck (including two full days at sea), coxswain engineering (including a half day at sea) and coxswain navigation (including a full day trip to Seaworld). The page advertises 10 days in person, but the dated blocks shown do not obviously add up to that, so confirm the timetable.
Source: https://uqskills.uq.edu.au/programs/maritime/certificate-ii-maritime-operations

## Q: What does it cost?
Indicative, October 2026: full fee AUD 4,150 (a lower rate applies to UQ staff).
Source: https://uqskills.uq.edu.au/programs/maritime/certificate-ii-maritime-operations

## Q: Are there entry requirements?
No formal ones. UQ recommends fitness for coxswain duties on a commercial vessel under 12 m, the ability to swim 25 m fully clothed, a computer with Office and Acrobat Reader, a language, literacy and numeracy test at enrolment, and a Unique Student Identifier. RPL may be possible with recent relevant work experience.
Source: https://uqskills.uq.edu.au/programs/maritime/certificate-ii-maritime-operations

## Q: When is the AMPA done?
Alongside the coursework. UQ issues the AMPA together with the course completion certificate once every component is done.
Source: https://uqskills.uq.edu.au/programs/maritime/certificate-ii-maritime-operations

## Q: What do I send to AMSA after the course?
The course completion certificate and the AMPA, plus a certificate of medical fitness, a record of sea service, a first aid certificate equivalent to HLTAID011 (included in the course) and a radio operator certificate if you do not already hold one. UQ says you can apply for Coxswain Grade 1 or Grade 2 NC. UQ notes the course covers only two of the compulsory parts of licensing, so sea service is still needed: under Marine Order 505 that is 30 days with a task book or 150 without for Grade 1, and 10 or 50 days for Grade 2.
Source: https://uqskills.uq.edu.au/programs/maritime/certificate-ii-maritime-operations

## Q: Does UQ mention task books or interim certificates?
No. The page does not cover task books or interim certificates. Under Marine Order 505 the RTO's assessor can issue an interim certificate for up to 60 days once you have applied.
Source: https://uqskills.uq.edu.au/programs/maritime/certificate-ii-maritime-operations
