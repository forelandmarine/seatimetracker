# Yachties of Australia: MCA to AMSA guide, paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of the "MCA to AMSA" section of the Yachties of Australia page. It is not their text. This is the only post-2019 crew-facing explanation found of how an Australian holding a UK MCA yacht CoC gets working rights on Australian-flagged yachts.

Reliability: MEDIUM-LOW. It is an unnamed community author, not AMSA, an RTO or a crew agency. Most of its steps match AMSA's current pages, but some details have moved on (see the notes after each answer). Use it only to explain the practical sequence, and quote AMSA's rules from the official files.

## Q: I hold an MCA yacht CoC (for example OOW <3000 GT Yachts). Can I use it on an Australian-flagged yacht?
The guide says yes, through an AMSA certificate of recognition (CoR) at the matching yacht level, for example MCA OOW <3000 (Yachts) recognised as the AMSA Watchkeeper Deck <3000 GT (Yachts) equivalent.
Check against official: matches AMSA. A CoR for an equivalent yacht certificate is valid only on Australian-registered vessels that meet the MO 74 yacht definition (web-intl-yachting-qualifications-essentials.md). The UK is on AMSA's recognised-country list (web-equivalence-country-eligibility.md). The guide's "31 countries" figure is a snapshot; use the stored list.
Source: https://yachtiesofaustralia.com/life-after-yachting/

## Q: What are the steps, in order?
The guide gives: sea service assessment on AMSA form 419, then pass an AMSA oral exam in Australia, then apply for the CoR on form 419 again.
Check against official: matches the current AMSA CoR page, which also requires an AMSA form 303 medical done in Australia (ENG1 not accepted) and the right to work in Australia (web-stcw-overseas-cert-get-stcw-certificate-recognition.md). AMSA's 2026 video confirms the order: assessment, eligibility notice, oral, then the form 419 application with the pass result (amsa-video-20260909-oral-exams-prepare-and-what-to-expect.md).
Source: https://yachtiesofaustralia.com/life-after-yachting/

## Q: Will AMSA accept my MCA sea service testimonials or PYA-verified service?
The guide does not say testimonials are accepted. It likens AMSA's sea service assessment to the PYA or Nautilus testimonial check, and says AMSA wants "Confirmation of Sea Service" letters from the employing company, not signed by the master, using AMSA's template.
Check against official: consistent with AMSA, which will not accept discharge certificates or letters from the vessel without supporting letters from employers or owners (web-intl-yachting-qualifications-essentials.md) and supplies its own sea service letter template (template-stcw-sea-service-letter.md). No AMSA page names MCA testimonials or PYA verification as acceptable on their own, so tell users to get employer or owner letters on AMSA's template as well.
Source: https://yachtiesofaustralia.com/life-after-yachting/

## Q: Does AMSA count yacht sea days the same way as the MCA?
The guide says no and that you may need to recalculate: under MO 74 Schedule 3, 8 or more hours of deck officer duty in 24 hours is one day (never more), and under 8 hours counts as the hours worked plus 25%.
Check against official: matches mo-74.md Schedule 3 clauses 3.2 and 3.3. AMSA also refuses service on vessels that spend a lot of time in port (web-intl-yachting-qualifications-essentials.md).
Source: https://yachtiesofaustralia.com/life-after-yachting/

## Q: Does a recognised MCA yacht CoC give me a domestic (near coastal) ticket too?
The guide says not automatically. You apply separately on form 426 for the domestic equivalent, and yacht CoCs convert poorly: Master or Chief Mate <3000 (Yachts) maps only to Coxswain Grade 1 NC (under 12 m), while unlimited or commercial <3000 GT CoCs map to much higher domestic grades.
Check against official: matches MO 505 Schedule 2, which maps all four yacht grades to Coxswain Grade 1 NC (mo-505-sch1-2.md), and AMSA's equivalence page, which includes CoRs and limits the result to the duties of the primary certificate (web-equivalence-international-certificates.md).
Source: https://yachtiesofaustralia.com/life-after-yachting/

## Q: How long does a certificate of recognition last?
The guide says up to 5 years or the expiry of the MCA CoC, whichever is first, and you must renew the MCA CoC to renew the CoR.
Check against official: AMSA's current page adds a third limit, the expiry of your Australian visa if you hold one, and requires the overseas certificate to stay valid (web-stcw-overseas-cert-get-stcw-certificate-recognition.md).
Source: https://yachtiesofaustralia.com/life-after-yachting/

## Q: Can I swap my MCA yacht CoC for a full AMSA yacht CoC instead?
The guide says yes but it is harder: get recognition of prior learning (RPL) from an Australian RTO for each course, sit the oral, then apply for the AMSA CoC.
Check against official: AMSA says holders of a recognised foreign yacht CoC who want an AMSA CoC must complete the full approved diploma course, with any prior learning granted by an AMSA-approved provider recognised (web-intl-yachting-qualifications-essentials.md). So RPL is the mechanism, but through the full course, not a shortcut around it.
Source: https://yachtiesofaustralia.com/life-after-yachting/
