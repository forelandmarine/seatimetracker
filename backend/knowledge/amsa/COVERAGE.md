# AMSA library coverage checklist

Checked 2026-10-25 against the AMSA certificates SeaTime Tracker covers. All file names are in `official/` unless marked `providers/`.

Columns: (a) sea service, (b) training and units of competency, (c) assessments and oral exams, (d) medical, (e) application process, (f) revalidation or renewal. Ratings: GOOD = the binding rule and AMSA's plain-English guidance are both stored; PARTIAL = something is stored but a piece is thin, old or indirect; MISSING = nothing usable stored.

## Remaining gaps and why

1. Yacht oral exam checklists: AMSA links only a syllabus (Appendix 11 or 12) and the study sheet for the four MO 74 grades. AMSA 479 (2017, withdrawn) is now stored as background on how deck orals are run and passed. AMSA's 2026 examiner video and audio brief are now stored as full transcripts, but neither uses a yacht example. After a wide search (forums, Reddit, trade press, crew agencies, LinkedIn, RTOs, FOI, Hansard) no candidate report, provider page or examiner briefing describing what is actually asked in the four yacht orals was found. It does not appear to exist publicly. Answers must rely on Appendices 11 and 12, AMSA 479, the general deck checklists as a guide, and the 2026 transcripts.
2. Chief Mate <500 GT (Yachts): no such certificate. The assistant should say the yacht ladder runs Watchkeeper Deck <3000 GT (Yachts), Chief Mate <3000 GT (Yachts), Master <500 GT (Yachts), Master <3000 GT (Yachts).
3. Sailing Master training content: comes from RYA Yachtmaster courses (RYA material, not AMSA), so it was not stored. How the RYA route works is now covered in providers/rya-australia-endorsement.md.
4. Foreign yacht sea service: no AMSA guidance specific to MCA testimonials, PYA verification or foreign-flag superyachts exists publicly. The only post-2019 explanation found is an unofficial crew community guide (providers/yachties-of-australia-mca-to-amsa.md), which agrees with AMSA's pages. No FOI release, Right to Know request, Senate Estimates answer or Hansard item on the topic from 2020 to 2026 was found. AMSA's statistics exclude certificates of recognition, so how many MCA yacht CoC holders have one is unknown. Answers must rely on the general STCW evidence rules, the yacht essentials page and the certificate of recognition pages; the old press claims are stored only with out-of-date flags.
5. STCW guidance notice PDFs are dated June 2024. The newer web pages (mostly 2025 and 2026) should be preferred where they differ.
6. Provider coverage is thin for yachts, oral exams and MCA recognition because the AMC, Superyacht Crew Academy and most MUA pages could not be fetched, and no Australian RTO page for the MO 74 yacht courses was found (see MANIFEST.md). Superyacht Australia's 2023 booklet, the RYA endorsement pages, MAR50120 listings, old trade press and the Yachties of Australia guide were added. The 2026-10-25 wide search of unofficial sources (Superyacht Crew Academy's reachable pages, Australian Marine, crew agencies, Reddit, trade press, union bulletins, LinkedIn) added nothing else usable after 2019; see MANIFEST.md Known gaps for the list of sources tried.
7. Fees are stored as published on 2026-10-25 and change each financial year.
8. National System reform: the Department of Infrastructure's independent review of domestic vessel safety law could not be fetched, and no 2024 to 2026 change to MO 505 qualification rules or remake of MO 70 to MO 74 was found. The only live change is the 2026 digital certificate consultation.
