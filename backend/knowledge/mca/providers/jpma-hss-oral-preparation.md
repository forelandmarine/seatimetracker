# JPMA / HSS oral preparation: paraphrased notes

JPMA runs oral preparation at Hoylake Sailing School for yacht, workboat and small vessel candidates, a course first set up by Captain John Percival in the 1990s. These notes cover how it prepares candidates and its tips on the NoE and booking process. Paraphrased only; pass rates are the provider's own figures.

## Q: Which MCA orals does JPMA prepare candidates for?
Deck: OOW (Yachts) <3000 GT and OOW Unlimited, Master (Code Vessels) <200 GT, Master (Workboats) <500 GT, Master (Near Coastal) <500 GT, Master (Yachts) <500 GT and <3000 GT. Engineering: MEOL (Y) and the old Y4 to Y1 yacht engineer tickets, SV MEOL, SV EOOW <3000 GT/<9000 kW (formerly SV Second Engineer), and SV Chief Engineer <500 GT/<3000 kW and <3000 GT/<9000 kW. Merchant candidates can ask for courses too.
Note: no new Y4 to Y1 yacht engineer NoEs have been issued since 31 December 2022; that route was replaced by the MSN 1904 Small Vessel route.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/oral-preparation/

## Q: What formats of oral preparation are there?
Three main options: timetabled classroom courses (a 5-day yacht deck oral prep course for OOW <3000, Master <500 and <3000); online, candidate-led study sessions booked by the hour (a single hour as a quick check, or a block in the months before the exam); and a bundle combining both. There are separate engineering oral prep courses. The classroom uses physical kit (chart table work, sextants, publications, model buoys, engine parts), which the school sees as an advantage over purely online study. Engineer candidates said the course helped them simplify complex subjects and drawings into something they could explain under exam conditions.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/oral-preparation/

## Q: What pass rates does JPMA report?
Its own figures: in 2024, 100% of its OOW (Yachts) <3000 GT candidates passed first time and 80% of its Master (Yachts) candidates; in 2025, 100% of its SV MEOL, SV EOOW, SV Chief Engineer <500 GT and EOOW Unlimited candidates passed first time, with 96% first-time passes across all its engineer orals. These are self-reported and cannot be checked against MCA data.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/oral-preparation/

## Q: What documents does JPMA say I need for my first NoE, and can I keep my passport?
JPMA says a first UK CoC application must include original documents and certificates sent by post, but that because seafarers need their passports to travel, the MCA accepts a photocopy of the passport signed, dated and stamped by the yacht's Captain with the Captain's CoC number. A birth certificate can also be sent as ID. If you already hold a UK CoC, later applications can be emailed.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (possible): MIN 690 Amendment 6 says only that NoE applications are made with the form and guidance for each certificate, and MSF 4343 asks for a copy of the passport attested by a responsible person, with original documents needed only if you are picked for random screening. Follow the current application form rather than this summary.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/oral-preparation/

## Q: What happens to my Training Record Book when I apply?
If your CoC needs a TRB, you post the original book to the MCA but keep scans of the overview signature pages, because you may need them in the oral. This matches MIN 690 Amendment 6, which also says OOW candidates should have the TRB to hand to show sections on camera if the examiner asks.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/oral-preparation/

## Q: How do I book the oral once I have the NoE?
JPMA describes the April 2022 online booking system: you can only book after the NoE arrives (by email); you send the electronic NoE, a scan of your government ID and your discharge book (and some TRB pages) to onlineoralexams@mcga.gov.uk; the team checks them and sends a link to the Online Booking Service; the oral is held on Microsoft Teams.
Note: MIN 690 Amendment 6 says booking details now come in the NoE email itself for most certificates, and OOW Unlimited and Master 200/OOW 500 candidates must pass the online Part A test before booking the Part B oral. Check your NoE email and MIN 690 for the current steps.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/oral-preparation/
