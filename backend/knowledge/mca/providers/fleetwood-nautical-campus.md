# Fleetwood Nautical Campus: paraphrased notes

Fleetwood Nautical Campus (Blackpool and The Fylde College) runs cadetships, pre-cadetship courses, ratings apprenticeships and senior officer orals preparation. Paraphrased notes only.

## Q: What does a Chief Mate or Master orals preparation course look like?
Fleetwood runs a three-week intensive Mates and Masters Orals Preparation course (the weeks can vary but taught hours stay the same). It focuses on the knowledge expected of senior officers and on how to put that knowledge across and show management skills in the interview. There are no modules or assessments. Listed fee was about £1,730, excluding the MCA exam fee, accommodation and meals.
Source: https://fleetwoodnautical.blackpool.ac.uk/course/mx1ec255

## Q: What do I need before attending Chief Mate or Master orals prep?
For Chief Mate, a UK OOW Unlimited (II/1) CoC; for Master, a UK Chief Mate Unlimited (II/2) CoC. For both, the right sea service, all ancillary and safety certificates and an ENG1, as set out in MSN 1856. Bring your passport and your discharge book (original or attested copy) on day one.
Source: https://fleetwoodnautical.blackpool.ac.uk/course/mx1ec255

## Q: When should I apply for my Notice of Eligibility before a prep course?
Fleetwood advises applying six to eight weeks before the course starts. The MCA wants an attested passport copy, your original discharge book, your CoC and sea service testimonials.
Source: https://fleetwoodnautical.blackpool.ac.uk/course/mx1ec255

## Q: What syllabus does the Chief Mate/Master oral follow?
The international STCW standard, with the detailed UK syllabus published by the MCA in MIN 653 (now Amendment 1, April 2026).
Source: https://fleetwoodnautical.blackpool.ac.uk/course/mx1ec255

## Q: How do I book the oral exam?
Fleetwood says students book their own oral once they hold a valid NoE, at an MCA Marine Office (Liverpool being nearest).
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: under MIN 690 Amendment 6 MCA oral exams are online and the booking details are sent with the NoE email. The candidate still books the exam personally.
Source: https://fleetwoodnautical.blackpool.ac.uk/course/mx1ec255

## Q: Can I start studying before I find a sponsor?
Fleetwood is listed as offering a pre-cadetship course of about a year for people without the entry grades or a sponsor, and it is an approved provider for rating apprenticeships.
Source: https://www.careersatsea.org/careers/
