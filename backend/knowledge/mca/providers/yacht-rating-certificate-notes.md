# Yacht Rating Certificate: paraphrased notes

In our own words. The binding rules are MSN 1862 Amendment 1 sections 3 and 12 (official/msn-1862-amendment-1-deck-ratings.md), MSF 4340 (official/msf-4340-apply-yacht-rating-certificate.md) and the MCA list of approved issuers (official/list-yacht-rating-certificate-approved-providers.md). See also providers/pya.md for the PYA's requirements summary.

## Q: Does my Yacht Rating Certificate expire or need revalidating?
No expiry. The PYA, which issues YRCs under MCA approval, says the certificate is issued to the crew member, not the vessel, and is valid for life, so you keep using it when you change yachts. This fits the official text: the MSN 1862 Annex F certificate template has a date of issue but no expiry date, and MSN 1862 sets no revalidation process for the Yacht Rating CoP. What does lapse is your supporting paperwork: STCW Personal Survival Techniques and Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting need updating every five years (MSN 1865), and your ENG1 has its own expiry date.
Source: https://www.pya.org/news/our-guide-to-yacht-rating-certificates

## Q: Who needs a Yacht Rating Certificate?
The PYA says yachts, usually those over 200 GT, need YRC holders to meet their safe manning document, and that captains and recruiters also ask for it for more senior deck roles. The PYA goes further and says all deck crew on yachts over 200 GT, including officers and engineers, need one.
Note: the stored MCA text does not set a 200 GT rule. Whether a rating certificate is needed depends on the yacht's Safe Manning Document; officers who hold a CoC are not normally asked for a separate YRC. Treat the PYA's wider statement with care.
Source: https://www.pya.org/news/our-guide-to-yacht-rating-certificates

## Q: How long does the PYA take to issue a YRC and what does it cost?
The PYA's 2021 guide said it aimed to issue within about two weeks, applications are online, and members got the certificate at no extra cost, with a fee for non-members (then €80, or €40 for shore-based members). OUT OF DATE (possibly): these fees are from 2021; check the PYA site for current prices. The MCA statutory fee for the Yacht Rating Certificate is in official/fees-regulations-2018-seafarer-certification.md.
Source: https://www.pya.org/news/our-guide-to-yacht-rating-certificates

## Q: Does a Yacht Rating course cut the sea time I need?
Yes. PYT says that completing an approved Yacht Rating course reduces the service needed from 6 months to 2 months. This matches MSN 1862 and MSF 4340: with an approved Yacht Rating course completion certificate (or certain sailing qualifications) you need 2 months' seagoing service on vessels of 15 m or more and no Training Record Book; otherwise you need 6 months' onboard yacht service including 2 months at sea plus the completed Yacht Rating TRB. PYT describes its own course as five days covering watchkeeping, safety equipment, seamanship, the Code of Safe Working Practices, pollution prevention, ropework, chartwork, the Collision Regulations and buoyage.
Source: https://professionalyachttraining.com/working-on-a-yacht/the-mca-yacht-rating-certificate-and-training-record-book

## Q: When can I do the EDH after getting my Yacht Rating?
PYT says you can take the Efficient Deck Hand course once you have held the Yacht Rating for 6 months. MSN 1862 section 4.2 puts it slightly differently: entry to the EDH course needs a Yacht Rating CoP with at least 6 months' onboard yacht service and a steering certificate (or another listed entry route, such as Yachtmaster Offshore or an NWR), and the EDH CoP itself is only issued after at least 6 months' seagoing service on vessels of 15 m or more. PYT also notes the EDH must be held for 18 months before an OOW (Yachts) <3000 GT CoC can be issued.
Note: the official rule is about 6 months of onboard yacht service plus a steering certificate, not simply holding the YRC for 6 months.
Source: https://professionalyachttraining.com/working-on-a-yacht/the-mca-yacht-rating-certificate-and-training-record-book

## Q: How do I apply for a Yacht Rating or Navigational Watch Rating?
Bluewater points crew to MSN 1862 sections 2 and 3 and its annexes, and to form MSF 4340, and summarises the need as 6 months' yacht service with 2 months at sea, a valid STCW basic training certificate and an ENG1.
Source: https://www.bluewateryachting.com/faq

## Q: I read that Yacht Rating Certificates stopped being issued in 2013. Is that true?
OUT OF DATE. An old MCA notice, MIN 471 (which expired in October 2018), said Yacht Rating Certificates would stop being issued after 31 December 2013 in favour of the Navigational Watch Rating, and that existing holders did not need to upgrade. That is no longer the position: MSN 1862 Amendment 1 (2022) sets out the Yacht Rating CoP, the MCA's MSF 4340 form was updated in April 2025, and the MCA list of approved Yacht Rating issuers is dated October 2026.
Source: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7cc37240f0b6629523baa4/min471.pdf
