# City of Glasgow College: paraphrased notes

City of Glasgow College runs one of the largest UK nautical faculties (cadetships, officer and short courses) from its Riverside campus on the Clyde. Paraphrased notes only, from archived copies of the college's own pages.

## Q: Does City of Glasgow College run MCA oral preparation for engineers?
Yes. It launched a management level oral preparation course suitable for both Second Engineer and Chief Engineer orals. It is taught by chief engineers who have also worked as classification society surveyors, and uses feedback from earlier oral candidates.
Source: https://www.cityofglasgowcollege.ac.uk/work-with-us/nautical-training/engineering-courses/mca-management-level-oral-preparation-courses (archive copy, July 2025)

## Q: How is the engineer oral preparation course delivered?
Remotely, with live lectures every Friday from 9am to 4pm through the academic year. One fee covers as many Friday sessions as you need for up to a year, email access to lecturers between sessions, college systems and course material, and two one-to-one mock orals with feedback, booked when you are ready. If you enrol within six weeks of the end of the academic year you are carried over into the next year's course.
Source: https://www.cityofglasgowcollege.ac.uk/work-with-us/nautical-training/engineering-courses/mca-management-level-oral-preparation-courses (archive copy, July 2025)

## Q: What is the Engineering Knowledge (EK) Management Level course?
A 7-week course, on campus or online, that prepares you for the MCA/SQA Engineering Knowledge management level (Motor and General) written exam for a specific exam sitting. It includes notes, slides and past papers with examiners' feedback, plus a weekly guidance session. The on-campus course uses the college's workshops and a working engine room. Lectures run 9am to 5pm UK time, which matters if you join remotely from another time zone.
Source: https://www.cityofglasgowcollege.ac.uk/work-with-us/nautical-training/engineering-courses/engineering-knowledge-management-level-course (archive copy, April 2024)

## Q: What attendance is needed on the EK management course?
80%, or no course completion certificate is issued. This matches MSN 1857 Amendment 1, which requires at least 80% attendance on approved programmes (colleges and companies may set a higher figure).
Source: https://www.cityofglasgowcollege.ac.uk/work-with-us/nautical-training/engineering-courses/engineering-knowledge-management-level-course (archive copy, April 2024)

## Q: Who can enrol on the EK management course?
Holders of an EOOW (III/1) or Second Engineer (III/2) CoC, or equivalent, who also have the sea service needed to apply for a Second or Chief Engineer NoE. The college points to MSN 1857 Amendment 1 sections 3.5 and 3.6.
Source: https://www.cityofglasgowcollege.ac.uk/work-with-us/nautical-training/engineering-courses/engineering-knowledge-management-level-course (archive copy, April 2024)

## Q: How do deck officers progress from OOW to Chief Mate at Glasgow?
The college listed several routes: MCA/SQA Chief Mate written exam preparation (navigation, and ship stability and structure); a full-time Chief Mate HND course for officers who have held an OOW CoC and served at least 12 months as OOW, which includes HELM (Management) and NAEST (Management) and leads to an HND in Nautical Science and the Chief Mate CoC; the same HND by distance (blended) learning; a Chief Mate/Master post-HND course only for officers who already hold a UK HND in Nautical Science (not for SPD or Foundation Degree holders); and evening tutorials before the written exams.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: this list is from a December 2020 copy of the page, before MIN 720 (2026) began modernising the officer syllabus and the MCA moved some safety exams to digital assessments. Check MSN 1856 Amendment 1, MIN 720 Amendment 1 and the college before relying on it.
Source: https://www.cityofglasgowcollege.ac.uk/work-with-us/nautical-training/navigation-deck/mates-and-masters (archive copy, December 2020)

## Q: Can simulator time at Glasgow replace sea time?
The college says its expanded simulation hub (13 simulators including three 360-degree bridges, opened 2025) lets cadets gain bridge watchkeeping training without extra sea time, and claims it trains over half of UK Merchant Navy officers and cadets.
Note: the official limit is in MSN 1856 Amendment 1. For a first CoC on a UK cadetship, at most 2 months of seagoing service can be credited for a structured Bridge Watchkeeping Skills simulator course (5 simulator days for 15 sea days, 10 for 30, 20 for 60), and it cannot replace the 6 months of bridge watchkeeping duties.
Source: https://www.cityofglasgowcollege.ac.uk/work-with-us/nautical-training/maritime-simulation-hub (archive copy, January 2026)
