# Bluewater: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of Bluewater crew training pages. Bluewater appears on the MCA list of approved providers for yacht OOW Navigation and Radar (Antibes). Check MSN 1858 Amendment 2 for the binding rules.

## Q: Which courses does Bluewater group under the OOW (Yachts) pathway, and what do they cost?
Efficient Deck Hand, ECDIS, General Ship Knowledge (OOW) Yacht, GMDSS GOC, Navigation and Radar (OOW) Yacht and OOW Oral Prep, run in Antibes and Palma. Indicative prices shown in October 2026 (euros): EDH about 1,115; ECDIS about 1,620; GSK about 1,345 plus MCA exam fee; GMDSS GOC about 1,970; Nav and Radar about 3,570 plus exam fee; Oral Prep about 1,600. Both centres have navigation simulators. The page does not set out a recommended order.
Source: https://bluewateryachting.com/crew-training/courses/mca-oow-13

## Q: What is the OOW oral prep course like?
An intensive 7-day revision week in Antibes with around six candidates, so candidates practise questioning each other in exam conditions. European classes mix OOW and Master candidates in work groups. It is optional. Bluewater notes it prepares candidates for exams taken with the UK MCA.
Source: https://www.bluewateryachting.com/training-course/oow-oral-prep-57

## Q: What should I have done before the oral prep course?
Bluewater lists GMDSS GOC, General Ship Knowledge, Navigation and Radar, Yachtmaster Offshore practical preparation and theory, Advanced Sea Survival, STCW basic safety, EDH and HELM (Operational). Entry conditions it states: age 19 or over; since age 16, at least 36 months' yacht service on vessels of any size including at least 12 months' sea service on yachts of 15 m or more; and a completed MCA approved Training Record Book.
Note: MSN 1858 expresses the sea service as 365 days' seagoing service on yachts of 15 m or more, of which at least 250 days must be actual sea service and up to 115 days can be stand-by or yard service. Bluewater's "12 months' sea service" is a simplification of this. The TRB can be skipped with 36 months' actual sea service on yachts of 24 m or more.
Source: https://www.bluewateryachting.com/training-course/oow-oral-prep-57

## Q: Can I use Yachtmaster Coastal instead of Yachtmaster Offshore to get to OOW?
Bluewater's 2018 article reported the plan to accept the RYA Yachtmaster Coastal CoC (with an RYA Coastal Skipper practical course) instead of Offshore for OOW candidates, because crew on large yachts struggled to log the Offshore qualifying miles on boats under 24 m. A commercially endorsed Coastal CoC lets you skipper commercial vessels up to 24 m within 20 miles of a safe haven, and can later be upgraded to Offshore and Ocean by exam.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the article (July 2018) said this was awaiting approval. The MCA later set out the route in MIN 580 (which expired 31 March 2026) and it now sits in MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 3.3, which accepts Yachtmaster Coastal plus a Coastal Skipper practical course completion certificate, or IYT Master of Yachts Coastal/Mate <200 GT. Under MIN 580, Coastal holders could not get the Master (code vessels <200 GT) CoC.
Source: https://www.bluewateryachting.com/blog/yachtmaster-coastal-instead-of-yachtmaster-offshore-256

## Q: Why did the old Yachtmaster Offshore requirement block so many superyacht crew?
According to Bluewater, the Offshore exam required half of the qualifying sea time and all qualifying passages to be on boats under 24 m and did not count time on yachts over 500 GT, which crew who started on 60 to 70 m yachts rarely had.
Source: https://www.bluewateryachting.com/blog/yachtmaster-coastal-instead-of-yachtmaster-offshore-256

## Q: Are exam fees included in Bluewater module prices?
No for the written OOW modules: GSK and Navigation and Radar are listed as price plus exam fee. Budget separately for the MCA written exams, the NoE and the oral exam.
Source: https://bluewateryachting.com/crew-training/courses/mca-oow-13
