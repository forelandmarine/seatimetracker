# MPT Fort Lauderdale: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of MPT course pages. MPT publishes little career guidance online, so this file is short. Coverage note: we could not confirm MPT on the MCA's list of approved yacht OOW module providers; another Fort Lauderdale school, Professional Yacht Training (PYT USA), does appear on MCA approved provider lists for yacht OOW Navigation and Radar and General Ship Knowledge. Check gov.uk "MCA approved nautical colleges, yacht deck and small vessel engineer training providers" before booking modules abroad.

## Q: Can I prepare for and sit an MCA oral exam in the USA?
MPT runs a 5-day MCA oral prep class in Fort Lauderdale for operational and entry management level deck CoCs, including OOW (Yachts) 3000 and Master (code vessels) 200. MPT says it sponsors an MCA examiner to visit roughly twice a year to hold exams there. Exams are booked separately from the course (MPT course 454) and the exam fee is extra. Since the MCA moved to online orals (see MIN 690), most candidates can also sit the oral remotely.
Source: https://mptusa.com/course-details/mca-oral-exam-prep-course-deck-officer-operational-level-course-453

## Q: Do I need my Notice of Eligibility before the prep course?
MPT says candidates must already have applied for their NoE and must have it before the exam. Under MIN 690 you cannot book an MCA oral until the NoE is issued, so apply early (expect several weeks for processing).
Source: https://mptusa.com/course-details/mca-oral-exam-prep-course-deck-officer-operational-level-course-453

## Q: Is there an equivalent for engineers?
Yes. MPT lists an MCA oral prep course for engine operational level candidates as well (course 456). For yacht engineers the relevant oral is the Small Vessel EOOW under MSN 1904; the old Y4 to Y1 engineer tickets closed to new NoEs on 31 December 2022.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/mca-oral-prep-course-engine-operational-level-course-456

## Q: Does US sea time count towards an MCA yacht CoC?
MSN 1858 says qualifying service can be on yachts of any flag, so time on US-based or US-flagged yachts counts if it is deck department yacht service and properly evidenced (testimonial signed by the Master and verified by PYA or Nautilus). This point comes from MSN 1858, not from MPT's page.
Source: https://mptusa.com/course-details/mca-oral-exam-prep-course-deck-officer-operational-level-course-453

## Q: What does the MPT prep course cost?
The course page does not show tuition; the separate exam fee is also not stated. Contact MPT directly.
