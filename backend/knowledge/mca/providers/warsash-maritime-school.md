# Warsash Maritime School (Solent University): paraphrased notes

Warsash is one of the main UK nautical colleges, now part of Solent University in Southampton. It runs merchant cadetships, experienced seafarer routes, Chief Mate/Master packages and the MCA yacht deck modules. These are paraphrased notes, not the school's own wording.

## Q: What does Warsash offer for deck officers, from cadet to Master?
Cadetships leading to an OOW Unlimited CoC (BSc Hons, FdSc or HND in Nautical Science), an HNC/exam route for experienced seafarers, Chief Mate/Master packages (post-cadetship, and an exam route for experienced officers), yacht OOW and Master (Yachts) modules, small commercial vessel courses and Nautical Institute DP courses.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/masters-orals

## Q: I am an experienced rating. How do I get to OOW Unlimited without a cadetship?
Warsash runs a two-term HNC Nautical Science (Part 1) course for senior seafarers. Entry needs at least 36 months' sea service including 6 months of bridge watchkeeping under a qualified officer, plus an MCA-recognised medical. The school stresses that sea service and watchkeeping service are not the same thing. Holders of an OOW (Yachts) 3000GT or higher CoC do not need separate evidence of watchkeeping duties. Before the oral you also need Medical First Aid, Advanced Fire Fighting, PSCRB, GMDSS GOC and EDH. This matches the MSN 1856 "experienced seafarer route" (36 months with 6 months bridge watchkeeping).
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the listing describes the written exams as SQA papers. MIN 720 Amendment 1 says new OOW Unlimited starters from August 2025 follow the modernised syllabus and sit the MCA Deck Operational Level Digital Assessment, and the old written exams (now run by Qualifications Scotland) stop in their current form from April 2028.
Source: https://www.stcwdirect.com/stcw-course/328/officer-of-the-watch-hnc-exam-route-for-experienced-seafarers/

## Q: I finished a UK cadetship and hold OOW Unlimited. What is the route to Chief Mate?
The post-cadetship package (competency assessment preparation, NAEST(M) and HELM(M)) prepares you for the IAMI-administered MCA digital assessment and the MCA oral, leading to Chief Mate Unlimited and Master under 3000 GT under the MIN 720 modernised syllabus. You need a valid OOW Unlimited CoC, an MCA-recognised medical and the Proficiency in Medical Care certificate before the orals. NAEST(M) needs at least 12 months' watchkeeping on a II/1 CoC and evidence of NAEST(O) or ECDIS completed on or after 1 January 2005. Listed price for the full package was about £6,200 (assessment preparation alone about £4,900), excluding external exam fees.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/chief-mate-master-post-cadetship-route

## Q: I came up through the experienced seafarer route. How do I get Chief Mate and Master?
The exam-route package bundles an HND in Nautical Science (modernised syllabus), assessment preparation for the IAMI-run MCA assessment and oral, NAEST(M) and HELM(M). Entry needs an OOW II/1 CoC, at least 12 months' sea service while holding it, a recognised medical and the Medical Care certificate before orals. From 1 August 2026 all Chief Mate candidates follow the modernised syllabus. Listed self-funded package price was about £9,000 excluding exam fees.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/master-chief-mate-exam

## Q: How much sea time do I need before the Master Unlimited oral?
36 months' watchkeeping while holding OOW Unlimited, cut to 24 months if at least 12 months was served as Chief Mate holding a Chief Mate II/2 CoC. You also need Proficiency in Medical Care, HELM (Management) and a recognised medical. This matches MSN 1856 Amendment 1. The Warsash Masters orals course is four weeks of tuition and guided study.
Source: https://www.stcwdirect.com/stcw-course/323/masters-orals/

## Q: How long is a Notice of Eligibility valid and how long does it take?
Warsash says to allow at least six weeks for processing, and that an NoE lasts five years from issue (and three years after a successful oral). MSN 1858 Amendment 2 confirms the 5-year NoE and the 3-year validity of an oral pass.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/oow-yachts-general-ship-knowledge

## Q: Do I need an NoE before taking the yacht OOW General Ship Knowledge course?
No. You can attend the module without an NoE; you need the NoE only for the MCA oral. Apply once you have enough sea time, sending testimonials, STCW certificates, exam passes and ENG1.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the page says to book the oral at an MCA office. Under MIN 690 (Amendment 6) MCA orals are online, and the booking details come in the NoE email (with Part A computer-based exams first for some grades).
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/oow-yachts-general-ship-knowledge

## Q: Is ECDIS required for OOW (Yachts)?
Warsash's yacht page says ECDIS is not required for OOW (Yachts) and that without it your CoC carries a limitation for ECDIS-fitted ships.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: MSN 1858 Amendment 2 (May 2026), section 6.11, says ECDIS is now mandatory for OOW (yachts <3000 GT), Chief Mate (yachts <3000 GT) and Master (yachts <500 GT and <3000 GT). The optional/limitation approach applies only to Master Code Vessel <200 GT.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/oow-yachts-general-ship-knowledge

## Q: Should I do full PSCRB or the yacht-restricted survival craft course?
Warsash advises the full PSCRB because the yacht-restricted version is not recognised by every flag and puts a limitation on your CoC.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (partly): the page says the restricted course has no updating course. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 (6.7) says holders of the restricted CoP must update it every five years and a refresher exists. The advice to prefer full PSCRB is consistent with MSN 1858 6.4, which "strongly recommends" the full course.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/oow-yachts-general-ship-knowledge

## Q: How do I book written exams at Warsash?
Through the Warsash exams team using the relevant form (IAMI forms for yacht deck and small vessel engineering, SQA forms for merchant deck and engine). Applications must arrive by the advertised closing date, invoices must be paid within seven days, a third or later attempt costs more, and candidates who use Warsash only as an exam centre pay a higher admin fee.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/course-info/exams-application-information

## Q: What are tips for the oral exam from Warsash?
Read the published oral syllabus early, use the pre-study notes sent on booking, apply for the NoE well before the planned exam date, and get optional courses such as ECDIS and full PSCRB to avoid limitations.
Source: https://maritime.solent.ac.uk/courses/deck/oow-yachts-general-ship-knowledge
