# Maritime Skills Academy: paraphrased notes

The Maritime Skills Academy (MSA) is an MCA-approved short course centre in Dover, with a simulator centre in Portsmouth and a Gibraltar site. It is part of the Viking Maritime Group. Paraphrased notes only.

## Q: What is in STCW Basic Safety Training and how long is it?
MSA runs it as five days covering Personal Survival Techniques, Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting, Elementary First Aid, Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities, and Security Awareness. Each element gives its own MCA-approved certificate after a short exam and practical exercises. Listed price in Dover was about £875 including VAT, with weekly Monday starts.
Source: https://www.maritimeskillsacademy.com/catalogue/13-stcw-basic-safety-training-course-bst/region-DV/location-Dover/

## Q: Are there entry requirements for basic training?
Minimum age 16, you must be able to wear an 18kg breathing apparatus set during firefighting, and you fill in a fitness declaration on the first day. Bring swimwear and a towel for the pool session.
Source: https://www.maritimeskillsacademy.com/catalogue/13-stcw-basic-safety-training-course-bst/region-DV/location-Dover/

## Q: Who legally needs STCW basic training?
MSA describes it as a legal requirement for anyone working on a commercial vessel over 24 metres.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (scope): MSN 1865 Amendment 1 ties the requirement to being employed or engaged on a seagoing ship, not to a 24 metre threshold. Small commercial (coded) vessels have their own crew training rules in the relevant code.
Source: https://www.maritimeskillsacademy.com/which-course/

## Q: How often do I need to refresh my STCW courses?
Every five years for the courses that carry an updating requirement. Refreshers are shorter than the originals: about half a day for Personal Survival Techniques, one day for Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting, one day for Advanced Fire Fighting, half to one day for PSCRB and Fast Rescue Boat, and three days for Proficiency in Medical Care. Some updating can be met by declared onboard training. This matches MSN 1865, which lists 5-yearly updating and includes onboard training declaration templates.
Source: https://www.maritimeskillsacademy.com/which-course/

## Q: Do I need to redo PSSR because of the new harassment rules?
MSA says that if your PSSR was done before 1 January 2026 you must complete an update by 31 March 2027. The update covers preventing, recognising and reporting violence and harassment, understanding its effect on safety and wellbeing, and responding with trauma-informed support. The MCA offers a free online module, and MSA is adding PSSR dates. MSA links this to an MCA guidance note (MGN 718) on the 2026 IMO amendment.
Note: MGN 718 is not yet in the official library; verify the deadline against the MCA notice.
Source: https://www.maritimeskillsacademy.com/important-updates-to-stcw-pssr/

## Q: What is the Approved Engine Course (AEC 1) for?
A five-day course on diesel engine basics (two- and four-stroke cycles, fuel, cooling, lubrication, electrical and transmission systems). MSA presents it as the entry point for engineering roles on smaller commercial vessels and a required step towards an engineer CoC. MSN 1904 confirms AEC I and II are entry requirements for the Small Vessel engineer routes.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (wording): MSA's suggestion that the Engineer Officer CoC lets holders serve as chief or first engineer on vessels of any size is misleading. Small Vessel CoCs under MSN 1904 are limited by kW and GT, and unlimited engineer CoCs come under MSN 1857.
Source: https://www.maritimeskillsacademy.com/which-course/

## Q: Is GWO sea survival the same as STCW?
No. GWO Basic Safety Training is a separate wind-industry standard (fire awareness, first aid, manual handling, sea survival, working at heights) required for offshore wind workers. It does not replace STCW basic training for seafarers.
