# Chieftain Training: paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of Chieftain Training's OOW 3000 oral prep page. Included as an oral exam focused provider (its lead deck instructor is described as a former MCA Chief Examiner). Check MSN 1858 Amendment 2 and MIN 690 for binding rules.

## Q: What does an OOW 3000 oral prep course look like?
Chieftain offers a five-day Monday to Friday course in Southampton, or bespoke one-to-one sessions in the classroom or on Zoom. Most one-to-one students buy 12 or 16 hours, often taken as three or four four-hour sessions, ending with a one-to-one mock exam. The NoE, the MCA exam fee, meals and accommodation are not included.
Source: https://chieftain.training/oow-3000-yacht-certificate-of-competence/

## Q: What must I hold before sitting the OOW (Yachts) <3000 GT oral?
Chieftain lists: Yachtmaster Coastal or Offshore CoC or IYT Master of Yachts Limited, with the Coastal Skipper/Yachtmaster Offshore shore-based certificate (unless the Yachtmaster CoC predates 31 July 2003); MCA General Ship Knowledge and Navigation and Radar (OOW yachts); ECDIS; GMDSS GOC; STCW basics including security awareness; PSCRB; first aid; HELM (Operational); EDH; ENG1; and an MCA Notice of Eligibility. This matches MSN 1858 section 3.3.
Source: https://chieftain.training/oow-3000-yacht-certificate-of-competence/

## Q: What age and service do I need for the OOW (Yachts) oral?
At least 19 years old; 36 months' yacht service since age 16; at least 365 days' seagoing service on yachts of 15 m or more load line length, including at least 250 days of actual sea time; and either a completed Yacht Training Record Book or 36 months' actual sea service on yachts of 24 m or more. Matches MSN 1858.
Source: https://chieftain.training/oow-3000-yacht-certificate-of-competence/

## Q: What topics does the OOW (Yachts) oral cover?
Chieftain's syllabus outline: navigation (passage planning, IALA buoyage, electronic aids and their errors, radar/ARPA, compass, tides, weather, charts); watchkeeping (COLREGs, bridge equipment, steering, AIS, handovers); manoeuvring (preparing for sea and port, mooring, helm orders, steering failure, anchoring); emergencies (man overboard, collision, grounding, flooding, distress and SAR, fire, life-saving appliances, survival); communications (distress signals, International Code of Signals, GMDSS); operations (MARPOL, watertight integrity, heavy weather, stability); and law and business (MSNs, health and safety, IMO conventions, ISM, ISPS, yacht codes, drills, manning, hours of rest, crew agreements). The official list is the MCA Deck Officer Yacht Oral Examination Syllabus on gov.uk.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: Chieftain still names the LY3 Large Commercial Yacht Code; UK commercial yachts are now covered by the Red Ensign Group Yacht Code (MSN 1895).
Source: https://chieftain.training/oow-3000-yacht-certificate-of-competence/

## Q: Is the OOW (Yachts) <3000 GT oral split into Part A and Part B?
Not according to the MCA. MIN 690 says the Part A online test currently applies only to OOW Unlimited and Master (code vessels <200 GT)/OOW (yachts <500 GT). The OOW (yachts <3000 GT) is a single online oral. Chieftain's page does not describe the exam format.
Source: https://chieftain.training/oow-3000-yacht-certificate-of-competence/

## Q: Is an oral prep course compulsory?
No. MSN 1858 does not require one. Chieftain presents its course as revision and mock-exam practice for deck crew stepping up to officer roles on yachts up to 3000 GT. (Another provider, Hoylake Sailing School, states plainly that oral prep is optional and not MCA approved: https://www.hss.ac.uk/?p=4107)
Source: https://chieftain.training/oow-3000-yacht-certificate-of-competence/
