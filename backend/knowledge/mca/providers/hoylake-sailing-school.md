# Hoylake Sailing School (JPMA): paraphrased fact notes

Our own summary of HSS/JPMA pages. HSS is an MCA and RYA training provider that specialises in oral exam preparation. Check MSN 1858 Amendment 2, MSN 1904, MIN 642 and MIN 690 for the binding rules.

POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the HSS deck pages still link to and cite MSN 1858 Amendment 1. Amendment 2 replaced it on 18 May 2026 (adding the Yacht Unlimited CoCs). The service rules HSS quotes match Amendment 2, but always check the current notice.

## Q: What are the three building blocks of the OOW (Yachts) <3000 GT?
HSS describes it as classroom training, yacht and sea service, then the MCA oral exam. Typical certificates collected along the way: STCW basic safety, Yachtmaster Offshore shore-based plus a commercially endorsed Yachtmaster Offshore or Coastal CoC (or IYT equivalent), PSCRB or the restricted Advanced Sea Survival, GMDSS GOC, OOW Navigation and Radar (course, exam and simulator), OOW General Ship Knowledge, Efficient Deck Hand, HELM (Operational), ECDIS and an ENG1.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-oow-2/

## Q: How is the 365 days of sea service for OOW made up?
You need 36 months of yacht service in total (any size of yacht), including 365 days of seagoing service on yachts of 15 m or more load line length. At least 250 of those days must be actual sea service; the other 115 can be a mix of more sea service, stand-by service (no more than 14 consecutive days at a time) and yard service (up to 90 days). This matches MSN 1858 section 3.3.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-oow-2/

## Q: How are days counted?
Service runs from the date you join (engagement) to the date you leave (discharge), in the deck department. Four hours of working duty in 24 hours counts as one day. A month is a calendar month, or 30 days when made up of part months. At least 6 months of the qualifying sea service must fall in the 5 years before the MCA receives your application. Any flag counts. Dual deck and engine roles count at half rate. Stand-by can never exceed the sea service of the voyage before it, and time at anchor at the end of a passage counts as stand-by, not sea time. Watchkeeping is cumulative: every 4 hours in charge of the watch is a day, but more than 4 hours in one 24-hour period is still only one day.
Note: HSS says leave and other non-working time are excluded from yacht service. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 defines onboard yacht service simply as time signed on a yacht whatever the vessel is doing and does not mention leave; confirm with the MCA if leave periods are in question.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-oow-2/

## Q: Does my yacht service have to be verified?
Yes. HSS states that all yacht service must be verified by Nautilus or the PYA before you apply (this reflects MIN 543).
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-oow-2/

## Q: What proof do I send with the NoE application?
Two forms of evidence per vessel, usually a testimonial plus a certificate of discharge, or a discharge book plus a testimonial for each yacht. PYA members can use their attested PYA service record. Include ID: a passport copy signed by your Captain with their CoC number is accepted, as is a birth certificate or discharge book. Deck candidates use form MSF 4343. HSS says to send originals. NoE processing takes roughly 28 days, and you cannot book the oral until the NoE is issued.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-oow-2/

## Q: What timing traps catch people out?
- The EDH certificate must have been issued at least 18 months before the OOW CoC is issued (MSN 1858 footnote 7).
- Written module pass certificates are valid for 3 years; older passes must be retaken before the CoC is issued.
- PST, PSCRB, fast rescue boat, basic and advanced fire fighting must have been completed or updated within the last 5 years.
- You can apply for the NoE before you hold every certificate, but they must all reach the MCA before the CoC is issued (OOW and Master <500 GT).
- The yacht TRB takes at least 6 months to complete and each task needs a Captain, Chief Mate or Chief Engineer sign-off. No TRB is needed if you can prove 36 months' actual sea service on yachts of 24 m or more load line length.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-oow-2/

## Q: What is Master (Code Vessels) <200 GT and who is it for?
A yacht CoC for holders of a commercially endorsed Yachtmaster Offshore or Ocean (or IYT equivalent) who want an MCA Master qualification for small vessels. Offshore holders get a version limited to 150 miles from a safe haven; Ocean holders get unlimited area. It needs at least 6 months of actual sea service in the deck department while holding Yachtmaster Offshore (or IYT Master of Yachts Limited), STCW basics, GMDSS (ROC for Offshore, GOC for Ocean), HELM (Operational) and ENG1. It is not a step to Master <500 GT: for that you must first hold the OOW (Yachts) <3000 GT.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/what-is-master-200/

## Q: What is the oral exam format for Master 200 and OOW <500 GT?
Two parts. Part A is an online written test (HSS describes 15 questions each with four test points, in 40 minutes). Part B is an online oral, usually a few weeks after passing Part A, and must be passed within 12 months of Part A. Candidates with dyslexia can ask the MCA for extra time on Part A if they send evidence first. HSS recommends an 8-day prep course, sitting Part A straight after it, then a short refresher just before Part B. MIN 690 confirms Part A applies only to OOW Unlimited and Master (code vessels <200 GT)/OOW (yachts <500 GT); the OOW (yachts <3000 GT) oral has no Part A.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/what-is-master-200/

## Q: What service do I need for Master (Yachts) <500 GT and <3000 GT?
Counted from when your OOW CoC was issued. Master <500 GT: 12 months as a deck officer on yachts, including at least 120 days of watchkeeping, on yachts of 15 m or more. Master <3000 GT: 24 months as a deck officer including at least 240 days of watchkeeping, all on yachts of 15 m or more, including either 12 months on yachts of 24 m or more or 6 months on yachts of 500 GT or more. Matches MSN 1858 sections 3.5 and 3.6.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-master-yachts-less-than-500gt-or-3000gt-2/

## Q: Which modules and certificates are needed for the Master tickets?
Master Seamanship and Meteorology, Master Stability, Master Business and Law, Master Navigation Radar and ARPA Simulator (NARAS), the MCA Celestial Navigation exam, Proficiency in Medical Care, HELM (Management), ECDIS if not already submitted, plus Advanced Fire Fighting, Medical First Aid and Yachtmaster Ocean (or IYT Master of Yachts Unlimited) if you did not submit them for Chief Mate. HSS warns that a Master <3000 GT candidate who does not already hold Master <500 GT must have passed all modules before the oral or will fail automatically.
HSS says the Celestial Navigation pass expires after one year. This agrees with MSN 1858 Amendment 2 (section 7.5): Celestial Navigation passes are valid for 1 year, all other yacht module and exam passes for 3 years.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-deck-courses/requirements-for-master-yachts-less-than-500gt-or-3000gt-2/

## Q: What is a deck oral prep course like and what does it cost at HSS?
HSS runs small groups (maximum four, occasionally five), 0900 to 1700, in Hoylake or online. OOW <3000 GT and Master <500/<3000 GT courses are 6 days (5 teaching days plus a self-study day); Master 200 and Workboat Master courses are 8 days. Prices stated in 2026: about 1,630 GBP (5-day, shared) or 2,599 GBP (8-day, shared), more for one-to-one. HSS advises finishing the written modules before the oral and booking the course to end close to the exam date. Prep courses are optional and are not MCA approved. HSS's own pass-rate claims are marketing figures and are not verified.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/?p=4107

## Q: (Engineers) What routes lead to the Small Vessel EOOW CoC, and which one gives a yachts-only ticket?
Three routes under MSN 1904: Standard (cadet style, section 4.3), Experienced Seafarer (section 4.4) and Alternative (for people with relevant degrees, HND/HNC or apprenticeships, section 4.7). The yachts-only EOOW CoC is reached only through the Experienced Seafarer route and needs less actual sea service. It can later be upgraded to the full certificate with more sea service.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-engineering-courses/small-vessel-2nd-engineer-unlimited-coc/

## Q: (Engineers) What do I need before I can get the SV Training Record Book on the Experienced Seafarer route?
24 months' onboard service on vessels of at least 200 kW including 6 months' actual sea service (yacht service verified by Nautilus or PYA), AEC 1 and 2, and the four basic STCW certificates (in date). You buy the TRB from an MCA-approved provider (HSS charges more if your yacht service is not verified), then can study at any approved provider. You must hold the TRB before booking the SV written modules.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/?p=3211

## Q: (Engineers) How much service is needed after getting the TRB?
Per HSS: non-yacht engineers need 11 months' sea service on 350 kW plus vessels including 6 months watchkeeping or UMS. Yacht engineers aiming for the full certificate need 12 months' yacht service on 350 kW plus vessels including 7 months' sea service on watch. Yacht engineers aiming for the yachts-only certificate need 12 months' yacht service including 4 months' sea service on watch and 6 months' total watchkeeping, where up to 2 months can be at anchor or alongside with the yacht on its own power. Then 2 weeks' further sea service or 2 weeks of additional workshop training.
Note: MIN 642 Annex A shows the yacht-restricted route as a further 12 months' yacht service including at least 5 months' seagoing service; HSS's "4 months" figure may reflect the overall summary box (4 further months plus 2 months fast to shore). Check MIN 642 before relying on either number.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-engineering-courses/small-vessel-2nd-engineer-unlimited-coc/

## Q: (Engineers) Can yard or refit time count?
On the Experienced Seafarer route up to 6 months of yard or dry dock time can count: the first 3 months towards total service (before or after the TRB), and a further 3 months only towards pre-TRB onboard service with MCA approval requested in advance by email. Yacht engineers can only count yard time as yacht (onboard) service, never as sea service. Yard work must be written up and signed by the Master, Chief Engineer or company. This matches MSN 1904 section 5.5.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-engineering-courses/small-vessel-2nd-engineer-unlimited-coc/

## Q: (Engineers) I am the only engineer on board. Who signs my TRB?
The Master can sign tasks they saw you complete, but that does not assess technical competence unless they hold an engineering CoC. A qualified engineer officer with a CoC (for example a training provider lecturer, a former engineer officer you sailed with, or a superintendent) must then review the TRB and your evidence file and give a signed cover letter quoting their CoC. Build a strong evidence file with photos and videos.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/?p=3211

## Q: (Engineers) I have a degree, HND or old qualifications. Can I get exemptions?
Degree, HND/HNC or NVQ Level 3 apprenticeship holders can apply for an Action Plan under the Alternative route (HSS says Warsash is currently the only provider issuing these). Experienced Seafarer route candidates with other relevant training can apply to IAMI for exemption from AEC 1 and 2 and Workshop Skills Training. At Chief Engineer level there are no sea service exemptions, but a relevant degree or HND/HNC can exempt General Engineering Science I and II. Qualifications from outside the UK need an ENIC comparability letter.
Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/2025/08/06/how-can-engineers-with-previous-qualifications-apply-for-exemption-from-sv-courses-service-requirements/

## Q: (Engineers) What if I am stuck on the TRB or written exams?
HSS suggests applying for the SV Marine Engine Operator Licence (MEOL) on the basis of the 24 months' service and 6 months' sea service held at TRB stage plus STCW basics and AEC 1 and 2. You keep building EOOW service meanwhile, but the MEOL does not reduce EOOW service requirements.

Source: https://www.hss.ac.uk/course-details/yacht-engineering-courses/small-vessel-2nd-engineer-unlimited-coc/
