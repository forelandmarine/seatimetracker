# Nautilus International: paraphrased notes

Nautilus is the UK-based union for maritime professionals. For yacht crew its main certification role is verifying sea service under the MCA arrangement set out in MIN 543. These are paraphrased notes.

## Q: Why does my yacht sea service have to be verified by Nautilus or the PYA?
The MCA moved verification of superyacht sea service testimonials away from its own staff and delegated it to the PYA and Nautilus. Under MIN 543, yacht crew should have service verified by one of these bodies before sending it to the MCA; unverified testimonials are checked in detail by the MCA and can add very long delays (MIN 543 quotes around 160 days). The arrangement took effect from 1 January 2017.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the MIN 543 text on gov.uk states that it expired on 28 March 2022, yet it is still published and MSN 1858 Amendment 2 does not restate the delegation. Check with the MCA whether verification is still formally required.
Source: https://www.superyachtnews.com/operations/new-arrangements-for-sea-service-verification

## Q: Do I have to be a Nautilus member to get sea service verified?
Yes for the Nautilus service. Verification was described as free for full members, and a one-year "Friend of Nautilus" membership was offered so yacht crew could access the record book and verification. The PYA offers the equivalent for its members and a pay-as-you-go option for non-members.
Source: https://www.superyachtnews.com/operations/new-arrangements-for-sea-service-verification

## Q: What is the Nautilus Service Record Book and does the MCA accept it?
It is a book in which crew log and calculate qualifying yacht service. The MCA had approved it, and once Nautilus verifies the entries the MCA generally accepts it as evidence of service without separate testimonials or certificates of discharge, usually without further checking. This fits MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 5.4, which allows proof of service in a "similar MCA approved service record book".
Source: https://www.superyachtnews.com/operations/new-arrangements-for-sea-service-verification

## Q: Is there a digital version of the record book?
Nautilus describes a digital Sea Service Record (SSR) inside its Yacht Hub, for members to record, calculate and review qualifying service online. The paper book continues alongside it. Non-members can register as authorisers (for example a captain signing off service).
Source: https://www.nautilusint.org/en/our-union/where-we-work/yachts/sea-service-verification (Internet Archive copy of 2026-01-06) and https://nautilusint.org/yachts

## Q: Does someone signing off my sea time on the Nautilus SSR need to be a member?
No. You must be a member to use the SSR yourself, but the person authorising your sea time (for example the captain) does not need to be a Nautilus member. Members can also keep using the paper Service Record Book (SRB), which runs alongside the digital SSR.
Source: https://www.nautilusint.org/en/our-union/where-we-work/yachts/sea-service-verification (Internet Archive copy of 2026-01-06)

## Q: How did the Nautilus online testimonial form work?
From June 2020 members could fill in a Sea and Onboard Service form online. The completed testimonial was emailed back to the member to print, get signed and stamped by the Master, and return to Nautilus for verification.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: this describes the 2020 beta form; the SSR platform appears to have replaced or extended it.
Source: https://www.nautilusfederation.org/en/news/nautilus-launches-beta-online-yacht-sea-service-verification-form/

## Q: Can the Master sign sea time digitally?
In March 2020 Nautilus reported that the MCA had approved digital sea time verification for members as a temporary Covid-19 measure.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: it was described as temporary. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 still says testimonials must be signed by the Master (or by the owner or management company for service as Master).
Source: https://www.nautilusfederation.org/en/news/mca-approves-digital-sea-time-verification

## Q: What categories of yacht service does Nautilus ask me to separate?
Its guidance splits yacht service into onboard service, sea (seagoing) service and watchkeeping service, and notes that harbour watches do not count as watchkeeping. This matches MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 5.2, which also defines stand-by and yard service.
Source: https://www.nautilusint.org/en/our-union/where-we-work/yachts/sea-and-onboard-service-form/

## Q: Where do I find the Nautilus testimonial template?
The Sea Service Testimonial template and verification guide sit behind the member login (My Nautilus / Yacht Hub). Nautilus says using its template can speed up the MCA's online process.
Source: https://www.nautilusint.org/en/our-union/where-we-work/yachts/sea-and-onboard-service-form/

## Q: Why does Nautilus say unverified yacht testimonials cause delays?
In 2018 Nautilus said that since early 2017 the MCA had required superyacht crew applying for an NoE or a CoC revalidation to have their sea service testimonials verified by an approved body such as Nautilus, and that verification avoided MCA processing delays of up to 160 days. It also issued a template testimonial (members-only) and encouraged those who sign testimonials on board to use it so the whole crew benefits.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: this rests on MIN 543, which expired on 28 March 2022 by its own text. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 5.4 still accepts an MCA approved service record book as proof of service.
Source: https://www.nautilusfederation.org/en/news/union-updates-guidance-on-sea-service-verification/

## Q: If my certificate is investigated, can Nautilus help?
Nautilus offers members "certificate protection": representation if a CoC is investigated or at risk of suspension, and a financial benefit if a member's certificate is cancelled, suspended or downgraded after a formal inquiry.
Source: https://nautilusint.org/protect-your-career

## Q: What is an MCA deck oral exam like, according to a former examiner?
A 2021 opinion piece in the Nautilus Telegraph by Ulrich Jurgens, a marine consultant who was an MCA examiner for about 12 years, made these points (opinion and candidate anecdotes, not MCA policy):
- There is no set length; candidates reported orals lasting from about 30 minutes to two hours (one reported 105 minutes and found it exhausting).
- There are no published, measurable pass or fail criteria. The examiner decides on "professional judgment" against the competences in MIN 653, which copy the STCW Code tables.
- The MCA told him there is no leeway on safety-critical topics such as the Colregs, IALA buoyage, emergencies and enclosed spaces; on other topics the examiner judges whether you know enough for the grade.
- Candidate feedback on examiners ranged from practical, thorough and fair to unclear questions, being stopped from writing questions down, and examiners who seemed disengaged. One tip passed between candidates was to speak up, because an examiner who is writing may not be listening closely.
- Examiners train by watching and then conducting supervised exams at each level before examining alone.
- He warned that some unregulated private courses sell preparation aimed at particular examiners' favourite questions, and argued that orals should be recorded so that disputes could be checked.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the article says a resit cost £220. Under the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 2018 (still in force) a resit of the oral is £159; £220 is the fee for the full CoC application, first oral and issue. The article also notes MIN 653 replaced MGN 69 from 1 November 2021; MIN 653 is now at Amendment 1 (April 2026).
Source: https://www.nautilusfederation.org/en/news/mca-deck-oral-exams---to-fail-or-not-to-fail/ (Internet Archive copy)

## Q: Can the MCA speed up my oral exam or NoE if I have a job waiting?
In December 2020 Nautilus reported that MIN 620 moved orals to video conference and that the MCA might expedite candidates with a confirmed job offer who had met every other CoC requirement, if the employer confirmed the offer and joining date.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: MIN 620 has been replaced (now MIN 690 Amendment 6, which does not repeat the job-offer priority). For CoC revalidation the current MCA guidance lets your company email revalidation@mcga.gov.uk with "Fast Track" in the subject line; a premium service fee also exists under the Fees Regulations.
Source: https://www.nautilusfederation.org/en/news/mca-updates-guidance-on-oral-examinations/ (Internet Archive copy)
