# Scottish Maritime Academy (NESCol): paraphrased notes

The Scottish Maritime Academy in Peterhead, part of North East Scotland College, runs a blended HNC in Nautical Science aimed at deck ratings working towards OOW Unlimited. Paraphrased notes only.

## Q: Can a deck rating study for OOW Unlimited part time?
Yes. The academy's blended HNC Nautical Science is aimed at ratings. It takes about 18 to 24 months of self-study with face-to-face tuition and supervised assessment, with at least 50 days' attendance at the academy. Enrolment is rolling and applications are unit by unit.
Source: https://nescol.ac.uk/?p=8959

## Q: How much sea time do I need for the experienced seafarer OOW route?
The college asks for at least 36 months' sea service in an acceptable deck capacity on ordinary trading vessels, with testimonials showing at least 6 months of bridge duties in the last 12 months of service.
Note: MSN 1856 Amendment 1 requires 36 months' seagoing service with 6 months engaged in bridge watchkeeping; it does not say the 6 months must fall in the last 12 months. The college's timing rule may be its own entry condition.
Source: https://nescol.ac.uk/?p=8959

## Q: Do I need an NoE before starting?
The college puts the onus on the candidate to meet the MCA entry requirements and obtain an NoE before starting the full OOW Unlimited CoC programme. MSN 1856 notes that no college letter is needed for an NoE on the experienced seafarer route.
Source: https://nescol.ac.uk/?p=8959

## Q: Does the HNC alone give me the CoC?
No. It leads to the OOW Unlimited CoC only together with the STCW short courses, the external written exams and the MCA oral.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the page describes SQA written exams. MIN 720 Amendment 1 says new OOW Unlimited starters from August 2025 sit the MCA Deck Operational Level Digital Assessment, and the old written exams end in their current form from April 2028.
Source: https://nescol.ac.uk/?p=8959

## Q: What subjects are covered?
Ten units plus a graded unit: celestial navigation, meteorology, bridge watchkeeping, navigational maths and science, ship construction, chartwork and tides, stability, cargo operations, emergency response and communications, and marine law and management.
Source: https://nescol.ac.uk/?p=8959

## Q: Does the academy also run cadetships?
Yes, it is listed among the UK colleges delivering officer cadet training.
