# Yacht Unlimited route: paraphrased notes from the MCA forum talk and providers

These notes are in our own words. The binding rules are in MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 4 (official/msn-1858-amendment-2-yacht-deck-officers.md) and the MCA news release of 18 May 2026 (official/news-2026-05-yacht-unlimited-coc-launch.md). Where a provider says something the MSN does not, it is flagged.

## Q: Has the MCA said anything about Yacht Unlimited since the May 2026 launch?
Yes. At the PYA Sea Changes Forum in Monaco on 24 September 2026, Captain Javed Munir (MCA Assistant Director, Seafarers Training, Safety and Health) presented the route. His slides repeat the MSN 1858 Amendment 2 structure: two STCW Regulation II/2 certificates, Chief Mate and Master (Yachts) Unlimited, valid on any size of yacht worldwide but not on cargo ships or other vessel types. The steps he showed were: an existing yacht or OOW Unlimited qualification, verified yacht service, nine approved management-level modules (unless an eligible MCA-approved management qualification already covers them), the MCA Chief Mate Navigation and Stability assessments, a valid ENG1, ancillary certificates, and the MCA oral exam. He said the yacht-limited route was notified to the IMO and circulated to STCW Parties as STCW.2/Circ.148, using the STCW Article IX and Chapter VII alternative arrangements. He asked industry to promote the route accurately and to help officers understand the service evidence needed.
Source: https://www.pya.org/news/sea-changes-forum-2026-highlights-navigating-the-future-of-yachting

## Q: What are the three service routes to Master (Yachts) Unlimited?
The MCA slides list them the same way as MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 4.4: (A) 12 months onboard as Chief Mate including 6 months seagoing, all on yachts of 500 GT or more; (B) for an existing Master (Yachts) <3000 GT holder, 6 months onboard as Master including 3 months seagoing, all on vessels of 500 GT or more; (C) from OOW Unlimited, 36 months onboard as OOW including 15 months seagoing, under the service conditions in MSN 1858. Every route also needs the ancillary certificates, a valid ENG1 and the Master Unlimited GT Yacht oral. Providers stress that you must hold, or meet the requirements for, Chief Mate (Yachts) Unlimited first, so a Master 3000 holder cannot skip the Chief Mate modules and assessments.
Source: https://www.pya.org/news/sea-changes-forum-2026-highlights-navigating-the-future-of-yachting ; https://www.fredsoralprep.com/blog/yacht-unlimited-cocs

## Q: Is there an engineer Yacht Unlimited route yet?
Not published as of 25 October 2026. The May 2026 MCA release said an engineer route was in development and due in 2026. The MCA's September 2026 forum slides gave the latest status of the engineering review: a working group of fourteen organisations was set up in January 2026; feedback supported keeping about 88% of the existing learning outcomes unchanged; the main concern was the steam plant and turbines outcome in Applied Thermodynamics (equipment rarely found on yachts). On 28 August 2026 the MCA recommended keeping the current engineering modules unchanged, with members given until 30 September 2026 to object. If there was no objection, candidates would top up to an MCA-approved marine engineering academic qualification at an MCA-approved college. No engineer MSN or amendment to MSN 1904 had been published by 25 October 2026, so engineers should use MSN 1904 (Small Vessel route) or MSN 1857 (merchant route) for now.
Source: https://www.pya.org/news/sea-changes-forum-2026-highlights-navigating-the-future-of-yachting ; https://blog.ardent-training.com/new-route-to-the-top-for-yacht-professionals-mca-launches-yacht-unlimited-certificate-of-competency/

## Q: Can I actually start the Yacht Unlimited route now? Are colleges running the modules?
Whitehorse Maritime Academy (31 August 2026) said the MCA had published oral syllabuses for both new CoCs and that candidates who already meet the requirements were starting to receive NoEs for the Yacht Unlimited orals, after some early applicants had been told they could not yet be issued. It also said it had not found any MCA-approved nautical college offering the full Chief Mate (Yachts) Unlimited module and assessment programme yet. Under MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 4.2, only colleges approved under Annex G of MSN 1856 may run these modules (they may partner with yacht training providers under MCA-approved oversight), and the tailored 'Management of Yacht Operations' and 'Ship Stability' modules need separate approval; colleges ask exams@mcga.gov.uk for the approval details. So in practice, candidates who are exempt from the modules (see below) can move first, and others depend on college approvals.
Source: https://whitehorsemaritime.academy/blogs/blogs/beyond-3000gt-mca-new-yacht-unlimited-certificates-of-competency

## Q: I hold Chief Mate Unlimited (merchant) and work on yachts. What do I still need?
Whitehorse reports candidates in this position having applications processed without repeating the management academic modules and Navigation and Stability assessments. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 4.4 lets Chief Mate Unlimited service count, and footnote 10 exempts Chief Mate Unlimited holders (merchant or yachts) from the Chief Mate Yacht Unlimited oral. In practice the main remaining items are 12 months onboard yacht service as Chief Mate (6 months seagoing) on yachts of 500 GT or more, the ancillary certificates, ENG1 and the Master Unlimited GT Yacht oral.
Note: the point that the academic and assessment items are not repeated is the provider's report of current MCA practice, not wording in MSN 1858 Amendment 2. Confirm with the MCA Deck team.
Source: https://whitehorsemaritime.academy/blogs/blogs/beyond-3000gt-mca-new-yacht-unlimited-certificates-of-competency

## Q: I hold OOW Unlimited with a Foundation Degree or HND. Am I exempt from everything except the oral?
No. An MCA-approved management-level qualification (Honours Degree, Foundation Degree, Scottish Professional Diploma, DipHE, HND or Advanced Diploma from an approved nautical college) exempts you from the nine academic modules (MSN 1858 Amendment 2 footnote 8), but providers point out you still need the management-level Chief Mate Navigation and Stability (Yacht Unlimited) assessments, because the OOW Unlimited Navigation and Stability exams are operational level. An OOW Unlimited holder who came through an HNC route does not get the module exemption. OOW Unlimited holders also need 12 months onboard yacht service as OOW (6 months seagoing) on yachts of at least 500 GT, NAEST (Management), and the Chief Mate (Yachts) Unlimited oral.
Source: https://whitehorsemaritime.academy/blogs/blogs/beyond-3000gt-mca-new-yacht-unlimited-certificates-of-competency

## Q: As a Master (Yachts) <3000 GT holder, do I sit two orals?
No, one. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 footnote 10 says Master (Yachts) <3000 GT holders are exempt from the Chief Mate Yacht Unlimited oral but must pass the Master Unlimited GT Yacht oral. Whitehorse and YachtSync read it this way. Fred's Oral Prep (25 May 2026) said it was checking this footnote with the MCA because the main text of section 4.3 lists an oral; the footnote is the MCA's own wording, so follow it unless the MCA tells you otherwise.
Source: https://whitehorsemaritime.academy/blogs/blogs/beyond-3000gt-mca-new-yacht-unlimited-certificates-of-competency ; https://yachtsync.uk/guides/yacht-unlimited-certificate-shortcuts ; https://www.fredsoralprep.com/blog/yacht-unlimited-cocs

## Q: Is NAEST new for yacht officers on this route?
Fred's Oral Prep notes that NAEST (Navigation Aids, Equipment and Simulator Training) is a normal unlimited requirement but new to yacht officers. YachtSync says it is unclear whether NAEST (M) applies on every entry route, because section 4.3 names it only for OOW Unlimited entrants. CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (YachtSync): the ancillary table in MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 6.2 ('Ancillary course certificates required, Yachts Unlimited') lists NAEST (Management) as required for both Chief Mate Yacht Unlimited and Master Yacht Unlimited, so every entrant needs it.
Source: https://www.fredsoralprep.com/blog/yacht-unlimited-cocs ; https://yachtsync.uk/guides/yacht-unlimited-certificate-shortcuts

## Q: Is there a new application form for Yacht Unlimited?
None found. The MSF 4343 page on gov.uk was last changed on 12 March 2024 (form Rev 12/23), before the route existed, and no provider article names a different form. Providers say NoEs are being issued, so applications appear to be going through the existing deck yacht process (MSF 4343). Check with deck@mcga.gov.uk before applying.
Source: https://whitehorsemaritime.academy/blogs/blogs/beyond-3000gt-mca-new-yacht-unlimited-certificates-of-competency

## Q: Why was the route created, and when was it first announced?
Fred's Oral Prep reports that the MCA Chief Examiner, Captain Ajit Jacob, first presented the plan at the PYA Sea Changes Forum during the Monaco Yacht Show on 25 September 2025, as part of the MCA's Cadet Training and Modernisation programme, and that an industry working group would finalise it. The post said amendments to MSN 1858 and MSN 1859 were targeted for April 2026. OUT OF DATE: MSN 1858 Amendment 2 was in fact published on 18 May 2026, and no engineer amendment has appeared; MSN 1859 itself was replaced by MSN 1904 for new yacht engineer applicants from January 2023.
Source: https://www.fredsoralprep.com/blog/mca-yacht-unlimited-pathway

## Q: Does Yacht Unlimited let me command any ship over 3000 GT?
No. Both the MCA and providers stress the certificates are limited to yachts. Ardent describes the new CoC as applying to large yachts above 3,000 GT; more precisely, MSN 1858 Amendment 2 and the MCA slides say the certificates cover yachts of any size worldwide, so they can also be used below 3,000 GT. They give no Chief Mate or Master status on cargo ships or other vessel types.
