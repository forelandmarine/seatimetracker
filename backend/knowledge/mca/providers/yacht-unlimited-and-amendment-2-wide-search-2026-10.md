# Yacht Unlimited and pending MSN amendments: industry sources (October 2026)

Reliability: these are second-hand. British Marine and the PYA report what the MCA said at meetings they attended, which is fairly reliable for dates and intentions but not for final rules. College and press articles mostly restate MSN 1858 Amendment 2. Where they differ from the MSN, the MSN wins. Paraphrased in our own words.

## Q: When did the MCA first tell industry about a Yacht Unlimited route?
At the PYA Sea Changes Forum in Monaco on 25 September 2025, followed by a large-yacht qualification panel at MCA headquarters in Southampton on 29 September 2025. British Marine, which attended the panel, reported that the MCA had an outline pathway for Masters, Chief Mates and engineers holding 3000 GT yacht CoCs, had not finalised it, would use industry working groups, and expected to implement it in the first half of 2026. The MCA's own slides from the forum are stored in official/mca-slides-2025-09-pya-yacht-coc-modernisation.md.
Source: https://www.britishmarine.co.uk/news/2025/september-1/british-marine-attend-mcas-large-yacht-qualification-pannel ; https://www.pya.org/news/summary-sea-changes-forum

## Q: Which colleges are running the nine Yacht Unlimited modules, when do they start and what do they cost?
No source found names a college that is running, or has announced dates or prices for, the management-level Yacht Unlimited modules. A YachtSync comparison of UK nautical colleges, last reviewed 14 October 2026, lists yacht provision at Warsash (OOW and Master yacht modules), Fleetwood (OOW Unlimited for the superyacht sector) and City of Glasgow (Superyacht Academy) but no Yacht Unlimited module course anywhere. Bluewater (July 2026) said it would share guidance but gave no course. A PYA-written explainer on SuperyachtNews (6 October 2026) simply tells candidates to contact an MCA-approved college. Whitehorse (31 August 2026, in providers/yacht-unlimited-route-notes.md) found none offering the full programme. So, as of 25 October 2026, there is no public course listing.
Note: 'Master of Yachts Unlimited' courses advertised by IYT schools (for example in Phuket or Palma) are a different, private IYT certificate for small yachts, not the MCA Yacht Unlimited CoC. Do not confuse them.
Source: https://yachtsync.uk/guides/uk-nautical-colleges-compared ; https://www.superyachtnews.com/crew/the-new-mca-yacht-unlimited-coc-is-here ; https://theislander.online/2026/07/industry-insider/an-amendment-to-msn-1858-means-a-new-unlimited-qualification-pathway-for-yacht-deck-officers-bluewater/

## Q: Is there a syllabus for the Yacht Unlimited modules?
Not published. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 lists the nine module titles and says 'Management of Yacht Operations' and 'Ship Stability' need separate MCA approval, with details from exams@mcga.gov.uk. The 2025 MCA slides said the Chief Mate Yacht Unlimited syllabus would follow the modernised syllabus (see official/min-720-amendment-1-modernised-officer-syllabus.md for the merchant management-level modules). No press or college source has published module learning outcomes.
Source: https://www.pya.org/news/summary-sea-changes-forum

## Q: Is there an application form or MCA FAQ for Yacht Unlimited?
None found. The PYA explainer of 6 October 2026 lists only three steps (verify sea service, contact an approved college, update your CV) and names no form. Beyond the Sea (24 October 2026) refers readers to MSN 1858 Amendment 2 section 4 only. MSF 4343 on gov.uk is still Rev 12/23.
Source: https://www.superyachtnews.com/crew/the-new-mca-yacht-unlimited-coc-is-here ; https://www.beyondthesea.gr/mca-yacht-unlimited-certification/

## Q: What happened to the engineer Yacht Unlimited route after 30 September 2026?
Nothing public. The most recent article found (Beyond the Sea, 24 October 2026) still says the engineer route is planned and needs confirming in MCA documents. No press, provider, union or Parliamentary source reports the result of the MCA working group's objection window that closed on 30 September 2026. Earlier plans, in order: a 'yacht restricted Unlimited kW' CoC with yacht-tailored syllabuses and digital exams (MCA, Sept 2025); then a proposal to keep the existing merchant engineering modules and add an academic top-up at an approved college (MCA, Sept 2026).
Source: https://www.beyondthesea.gr/mca-yacht-unlimited-certification/

## Q: Has anyone published drafts or briefings on MSN 1856, 1857, 1862, 1864 or 1865 Amendment 2?
No. Searches of trade press, college and provider sites, union sites, GOV.UK consultations, Parliament written questions and Hansard found no draft, consultation, briefing or announcement of these amendments. The only references to them anywhere are inside MSN 1858 Amendment 2, and even there the notice is inconsistent: section 4.2's heading cites Annex G of MSN 1856 (Amendment 1) while its text cites Amendment 2, and footnotes 12 to 14 and 16 tell readers to refer to MSN 1864, 1862 and 1865 (Amendment 1) with links to the Amendment 1 pages. The safest reading is that Amendment 1 of each notice applies until the MCA publishes an Amendment 2.
Source: MSN 1858 Amendment 2 (official/msn-1858-amendment-2-yacht-deck-officers.md), sections 4.2, 6.2, 6.8, 6.9 and footnotes 12 to 16.
