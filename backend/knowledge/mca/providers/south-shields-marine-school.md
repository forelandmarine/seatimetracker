# South Shields Marine School: paraphrased notes

South Shields Marine School (part of Tyne Coast College, formerly South Tyneside College; founded 1861) trains cadets through to senior officers and runs MCA short courses including Efficient Deck Hand. Paraphrased notes only.

## Q: What is the EDH course and how long is it?
A five-day MCA-approved practical seamanship course and examination. Passing EDH is the qualifying exam for the Able Seafarer Deck certificate (MSN 1862, section 4.1). Listed price was about £565. Bring your discharge book or record of sea service.
Source: https://stcwdirect.com/stcw-course/228/efficient-deckhand-mca-approved

## Q: What do I need to get onto an EDH course on the merchant route?
The school's own EDH page (2026) lists the MSN 1862 entry routes: an MCA-accepted Yachtmaster Offshore; a Yacht Rating with at least 6 months' seagoing service and a steering certificate; an NWR certificate on its own; a Boatmasters' Licence Tier 1 Level 2; or a Tug Rating with 6 months' seagoing service and a steering certificate (or an NWR). So a merchant rating with an NWR can enter without extra conditions. Before the EDH certificate is issued you also need at least 6 months' seagoing service on vessels of 15 metres or more (yachts, fishing vessels, tugs, workboats and Royal Navy vessels count), with category C and D waters counted at full rate.
Note: the older STCW Direct listing asked merchant candidates for age 17, 6 months' service and an NWR or steering certificate; the school's own page has replaced that and now matches MSN 1862 Amendment 1 section 4.2.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/efficient-deckhand/

## Q: How is the EDH assessed at South Shields?
Students are assessed continuously over the 5 days, with a final practical and oral exam for everyone on the last day. The school describes EDH as the qualifying exam for the AB (Able Seafarer Deck) certificate and a prerequisite for a first deck CoC. It also lists fishing routes in: a UK Class 1 or Class 2 fishing CoC (deck or engineering), or a Seafish 16.5 m skipper's certificate with 6 months' seagoing service.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: fishing certificates are not among the EDH entry routes in MSN 1862 Amendment 1 section 4.2. Check with the MCA before relying on a fishing route.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/efficient-deckhand/

## Q: Can yacht crew do EDH?
Yes. Yacht entry is an MCA-accepted Yachtmaster Offshore, or a Yacht Rating certificate with at least 6 months' service plus a steering certificate. This matches MSN 1862 section 4.2. The listing adds detail about the Yachtmaster shore-based course for certificates issued after July 2003, which is not in MSN 1862.
Source: https://stcwdirect.com/stcw-course/228/efficient-deckhand-mca-approved

## Q: Can tug, inland or fishing crew do EDH?
Tug ratings with 6 months' sea time (which may be in categorised waters) plus a steering or NWR certificate, and holders of a Boatmasters' Licence Tier 1 Level 2, can enter, as MSN 1862 allows. The listing also accepts fishing Class 2 CoC holders and the Seafish watchkeeping course.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: fishing qualifications are not among the EDH entry routes listed in MSN 1862 Amendment 1 section 4.2.
Source: https://stcwdirect.com/stcw-course/228/efficient-deckhand-mca-approved

## Q: Is sea time on small boats or in sheltered waters accepted for EDH?
Service in category C and D waters counts at full rate, but at least 6 months' sea service on vessels of 15 metres or more is needed before the EDH certificate is issued. Both points match MSN 1862.
Source: https://stcwdirect.com/stcw-course/228/efficient-deckhand-mca-approved

## Q: What syllabus does the EDH course follow?
The listing cites the EDH syllabus in the old MGN 10 and MGN 97 annexes.
POSSIBLY OUT OF DATE: the current requirements are in MSN 1862 Amendment 1, with course approval under MSN 1865 Annex F and the MNTB EDH course criteria.
Source: https://stcwdirect.com/stcw-course/228/efficient-deckhand-mca-approved

## Q: Does South Shields run cadetships and pre-cadetship courses?
Yes. It is one of the UK colleges delivering officer cadet training and offers a pre-cadetship course for applicants who do not yet meet entry grades.
Source: https://www.careersatsea.org/careers/

## Q: What is the OOW (Unlimited) experienced seafarer route at South Shields?
An MCA-approved programme of about 32 weeks for experienced deck ratings. It covers the academic knowledge for an OOW (Unlimited) CoC as a Certificate of Higher Education (operational navigation, bridge operations, stability and operations, navigational maths and science, ship construction and meteorology, operational management), then the MCA digital safety assessment in navigation and stability, the MCA signals exam, and the EDH, NAEST (Operational, including an approved ECDIS course) and HELM (Operational) short courses. It ends with two weeks of oral preparation before the MCA oral. GMDSS GOC, STCW Advanced Fire Fighting and Medical First Aid are not included but are needed before the CoC is issued, and the school advises doing them before starting.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/officer-of-the-watch-unlimited-experienced-seafarer-route/

## Q: What sea time and age does South Shields say I need for OOW (Unlimited) by the experienced route?
At least 36 months of qualifying deck sea service, with testimonials showing at least 6 months on bridge duties, and discharge book entries (or company documents if you have no discharge book). It also says candidates must be at least 19. The candidate is responsible for meeting the NoE conditions before starting.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: MSN 1856 Amendment 1 sets a minimum age of 18 for the OOW (Unlimited) CoC, not 19. The 36 months with 6 months of bridge watchkeeping matches MSN 1856.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/officer-of-the-watch-unlimited-experienced-seafarer-route/

## Q: How long is the OOW (Unlimited) oral preparation course, and how is the oral held?
Two weeks, covering the whole OOW syllabus in MIN 653 Amendment 1, taught topic by topic by specialist lecturers, with half a day at the college's offshore safety training centre. The school recommends finishing the academic (underpinning knowledge) part first. It says the MCA oral is held over Microsoft Teams with an MCA examiner. A deposit is needed to reserve a place (listed as £500 for UK and £1,000 for non-UK students).
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/oow-unlimited-oral-preparation/

## Q: What does a Second or Chief Engineer oral preparation course involve?
A rolling programme you can join any week (September 2026 to July 2027 intake listed), taught on campus or remotely over Teams. It mixes tutor-led teaching with candidate-led question and answer sessions using recent oral questions, and a one-to-one mock oral on request. Topics: engineering knowledge and systems, legislation (ISM, MARPOL, SOLAS, MLC, surveys and class), operations and safety (permits to work, enclosed spaces, bunkering, emergencies), leadership and management, and oral exam technique such as structured answers and handling "what if" follow-up questions. Listed fee £1,540; applications close 2 weeks before your chosen start.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/second-and-chief-engineer-oral-preparation/

## Q: Is there oral preparation for the ETO CoC?
Yes. A one-week course for candidates on the experienced ETO route or the ETO alternative route, with tutor-led teaching, question and answer sessions using recent oral questions, and a mock oral on request. It can be taken on campus or remotely.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/eto-oral-preparation/

## Q: How can an experienced electrician at sea get an ETO CoC through South Shields?
The school is MCA-approved for the experienced ETO route with study and assessment in the UK. People who have served as an ETO (or similar) without STCW certification can take the required ETO short courses there. Part of the academic requirement can be met with the SQA Chief Engineer Electro-Technology single subject, on campus or by distance learning. The MCA documents to read are MSN 1860 (requirements) and MSF 4259 (application form).
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/short-courses-for-electro-technical-officer-certificate-of-competency-eto-coc-experienced-and-alternative-routes/

## Q: Which ETO record book do I need?
The school says a UK ETO CoC needs a completed record book. For sea time gained before 2018 it is the MNTB Electro-Technical Officer Competency Record Book, bought directly from Witherby Seamanship; for later sea time the school supplies the Training Record Book when you apply for an ETO action plan. Check MSN 1860 Amendment 1 for the record book the MCA expects on your route.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/short-courses-for-electro-technical-officer-certificate-of-competency-eto-coc-experienced-and-alternative-routes/

## Q: Can I get a Senior ETO (SETO) certificate?
The school notes that SETO is not a rank recognised by STCW, and says the MCA was not issuing NoEs for SETO while it reviewed procedures (the page gives no date for this).
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: MSN 1860 Amendment 1 section 5 sets out a UK SETO endorsement on an ETO CoC (ETO CoC held, HND or degree in electrical and electronic engineering, the section 5.2 sea service, HELM management and High Voltage management certificates, and a SETO MCA exam). Ask the MCA whether SETO NoEs are being issued now.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/course/short-courses-for-electro-technical-officer-certificate-of-competency-eto-coc-experienced-and-alternative-routes/

## Q: What does the pre-cadetship cost?
Free for 16 to 18 year olds; £3,295 for those 19 and over (no upper age limit). It is a one-year programme at Level 2 or Level 3, with help applying to shipping companies for a sponsored cadetship. Cadetship fees are paid by the sponsoring company.
Source: https://www.southshieldsmarineschool.com/support/faqs/
