# Online UK CoC/FSE/GMDSS checker

Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/online-uk-coc-fse-checker

> How to verify UK Certificates of Competency (CoC), Flag State Endorsements (FSE), GMDSS endorsements and other MCA-issued documents online.

## Change history (gov.uk)

- 2026-07-24: Our online document verification service has been extended to include other MCA-issued documents including GMDSS endorsements.
- 2022-02-21: The alternative process for verifying a CoC has been added.
- 2015-08-23: First published.
## Online verification

Use the [online document verification service](https://coccec.mcga.gov.uk/) to verify an MCA-issued certificate or endorsement.

## If you are unable to verify a document online

Email: [exams@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:exams@mcga.gov.uk)

You must provide:

- a full scan (all pages) of the certificate or endorsement
- the most recent version of the certificate or endorsement (we cannot verify superseded documents)

Please note we cannot provide verification to individuals.
