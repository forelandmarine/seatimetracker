# Boatmasters' licence

Source: https://www.gov.uk/boatmasters-licence

> Apply for a boatmasters' licence to run a passenger-carrying or non-passenger carrying commercial vessel - apply, replace, renew or upgrade your licence

## Check if you need a licence

You must have a boatmasters’ licence if you’re the master of certain vessels on:

  * inland waters in [categories A to D](/inland-waterways-and-categorisation-of-waters), such as canals, rivers, lakes and some estuaries
  * ‘limited’ coastal area operations - no more than 5 miles from the land and 15 miles from where your vessel sets off or arrives



You may be prosecuted and fined for not having the right boatmasters’ licence.

Find out [what types and class of vessels can operate on UK inland waters](/inland-waterways-types-of-vessels).

## When you do not need a licence

You do not need a boatmasters’ licence if you’re in charge of:

  * a pleasure vessel, including hire boats used as pleasure vessels
  * fishing vessels



## Using alternative certificates

Some certificates can be used instead of a boatmasters’ licence, including STCW certificates of competency and certificates awarded by the Royal Yachting Association.

If you have an alternative certificate, you must:

  * check if you need a paper endorsement or a boat handling test - read sections 3 and 4 of the [boatmasters’ qualifications and hours of work](/government/publications/msn-1853-m-boatmasters-qualifications-crew-and-hours-of-work) notice
  * download and fill in the [application form](/government/publications/msf-4398-application-for-a-boatmasters-licence) and send it to your nearest [marine office](/government/organisations/maritime-and-coastguard-agency/about/access-and-opening)
  * pay the [right fees](/government/publications/maritime-coastguard-agency-mca-fees)


  *[STCW]: Standards in Training Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers

## Before you apply

Read the [boatmasters’ qualifications and hours of work](/government/publications/msn-1853-m-boatmasters-qualifications-crew-and-hours-of-work) notice to find out:

  * which vessels you need a boatmasters’ licence for
  * what kinds of boatmasters’ licence you can apply for
  * what you need to apply for a boatmasters’ licence
  * what you need to revalidate a boatmasters’ licence
  * alternative qualifications you can have instead of a boatmasters’ licence
  * information on local knowledge requirements



You should also:

  * keep records of [relevant work](/government/publications/boatmasters-licence-work-record-book-msf-4366) and [training](/government/publications/new-boatmasters-licence-task-record-book-msf-4367) for your licence application
  * [check for local regulations](/government/publications/min-495-m-the-merchant-shipping-boatmasters-qualifications-crew-and-hours-of-work-regulations-2015-local-regulations), such as byelaws that you may need to know
  * check that you have safety training from an [approved training provider](/guidance/boatmasters-approved-training-providers-atp)


  *[STCW]: Standards in Training Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers

## How to apply

You’ll need to:

  * read the [boatmasters’ qualifications and hours of work](/government/publications/msn-1853-m-boatmasters-qualifications-crew-and-hours-of-work) notice
  * download the [application form for a boatmasters’ licence](/government/publications/application-for-a-boatmasters-licence-new-entrants-msf-4364)
  * pay the [right fees](/government/publications/maritime-coastguard-agency-mca-fees)



## Replacing your licence

Download the [application form for a replacement boatmasters’ licence](/government/publications/application-for-a-replcement-of-a-bml-licence-msf-4390).

Replacing your licence costs £23.

## Renewing your licence

Your boatmasters’ licence usually lasts 5 years.

Renewing your licence costs £31.

Download the [boatmasters’ licence renewal application form](/government/publications/revalidation-of-a-boastmasters-licence-msf-4365). Send it to the address on the form.

## Upgrading your licence

Download the [form to upgrade your licence, or add another area to your licence](/government/publications/apply-for-a-bml-upgrade-msf-4370).

The fee for upgrading your licence can vary.
  *[STCW]: Standards in Training Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers

## Exemptions under the 2006 Boatmasters' Regulations

You need to apply for a ‘Tier 2 Level 2’ licence before your exemption expires if you want to keep working. This will cover all the areas described on your exemption certificate.

You may be prosecuted and fined for not having the right boatmasters’ licence when your exemption expires.

## How to apply

If you have an exemption certificate and want to continue working, you must:

  * check if you can apply for a Tier 2 boatmasters’ licence by reading section 22 of the [boatmasters’ qualifications and hours of work](/government/publications/msn-1853-m-boatmasters-qualifications-crew-and-hours-of-work) notice
  * download and fill in the [application form](/government/publications/msf-4399-application-for-a-boatmasters-licence) and send it to your [nearest marine office](/government/organisations/maritime-and-coastguard-agency/about/access-and-opening)
  * pay the [right fees](/government/publications/maritime-coastguard-agency-mca-fees)


  *[STCW]: Standards in Training Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers
