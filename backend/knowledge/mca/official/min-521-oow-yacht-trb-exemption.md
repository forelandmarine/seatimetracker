> Amendments to MSN 1858 yacht requirements to gain an exemption from completing the training record book 

## Change history (gov.uk)

This notice is designed advise all Owners, Masters, Deck Officers and Crews of Commercially and Privately

### **MARINE INFORMATION NOTE**

# **MIN 521 (M)**

## **Amendments to MSN 1858 OOW Yacht Requirements** **to gain an Exemption from Completing the Training** **Record Book**

**Notice to all Owners, Masters, Deck Officers and Crews of Commercially and Privately**
**Operated Yachts and Sail Training Vessels, and those concerned with Maritime**
**Training.**

_This notice should be read with MSN 1858_
_This MIN expires 06 February 2021_

**Summary**
This MIN amends the “Note” from line **(f)** of section **3.3** of MSN 1858. This MIN details the
seagoing service requirement that enables an exemption from the Officer of the Watch Yacht
(OOW) Training Record Book.

**1. The Amendment**

1.1 The note in line **(f)** of section **3.3** in MSN 1858 states that there is no requirement to
complete an MCA Officer of the Watch (OOW) Training Record Book if you have
completed 36 months **onboard yacht service** . This has been amended to ensure a
seafarer has adequate experience to gain the exemption from the completion of the TRB.

1.2 To obtain the exemption from completing a TRB for OOW yachts, less than 3000 GT,
unlimited area, regulation II/1 you must demonstrate 36 months’ **actual sea service** in
vessels of at least 24 metres in load line length.

1.3 The definition of actual seagoing service is taken from MSN 1858: **Actual sea service**
is time spent at sea, which may include time at anchor or river and canal transits
associated with a passage.
