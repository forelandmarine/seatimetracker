# The Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 2018, as amended: seafarer certification fees

Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1104/contents

> Library note: this file holds the parts of the Fees Regulations that set fees for seafarer certificates, examinations, ratings certificates, ENG1 medicals, boatmasters' licences and discharge books. Parts on ship surveys, registration and other vessel matters were left out on purpose. The text below is the revised (consolidated) version from legislation.gov.uk, including its amendment notes.

## Quick answer (library summary of the tables below)

- Certificate of Competency (deck or engineer officer, including yacht and small vessel CoCs): £220 covers the assessment of the application, the oral exam and the issue of the CoC. Oral resit: £159. Premium service for either: £150 extra.
- Refund if you cancel an oral exam application: £152 before a Notice of Eligibility is issued, £130 after.
- Revalidation of a CoC: £54. Certificate of Proficiency (except able seafarer): £34; its revalidation £54.
- CoC not needing an oral: £77. Lost certificate replacement: £77. Adding, removing or amending an endorsement: £73.
- Initial assessment for a merchant marine engineer officer: £121.
- Certificate of Equivalent Competency (CEC) application including issue: £109; CEC revalidation £109 (see Part 3 for the other CEC items).
- Able Seafarer Deck or Engine CoP: £34. EDH certificate: £34. Yacht Rating Certificate: £57. Watch Rating Certificate (NWR or EWR): £57. MEOL or Senior MEOL exam: £215.
- ENG1 medical examination by an MCA approved doctor: £115 (for exams on or after 13 November 2020).
- Boatmasters' licence: combined practical and oral assessment £241; issue £23; revalidation £31.
- Discharge book or British Seaman's Card: £55 (premium service £50 extra); free replacement if lost or destroyed through no fault of the holder.

### **PART 12** U.K.Seamen's Documents

#### InterpretationU.K.

1. In this Part, references to a British Seaman's Card or to a discharge book are references to the documents of those names issued pursuant to the Merchant Shipping (Seamen's Documents) Regulations 1987 M1.U.K.

Marginal Citations

M1S.I. 1987/408, to which there are amendments not relevant to these Regulations.

#### Fees for issue of documentsU.K.

2. Subject to paragraph 4, the fee for the issue, or replacement, of a British Seaman's Card or a discharge book is £55.U.K.

3. Where a person requests and receives premium service, a fee of £50 is payable in addition to the aggregate of the fees for documents issued on the same occasion.U.K.

#### Lost documents, etc.U.K.

4. No fee is payable in respect of a replacement British Seaman's Card or discharge book where the document has been lost, destroyed or defaced through no fault of the holder.U.K.
