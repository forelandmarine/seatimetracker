# How to apply for a revalidation of a boatmasters' licence

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/revalidation-of-a-boatmasters-licence-msf-4365

> MSF 4365: Use this form if you already have a boatmasters' licence and you would like to revalidate it.

## Change history (gov.uk)

- 2025-11-11: Payment link has been updated. The telephone number and email address for the Registry of Shipping and Seamen has also been changed.
- 2025-06-12: Updated form for the application for the revalidation of a boatmasters' licence (MSF 4390).
- 2020-11-05: Updated form (payment section)
- 2020-04-14: Payment corrected to £31
- 2018-05-23: Application form updated
- 2018-05-18: Application form updated
- 2018-01-19: Application, title and summary updated
- 2017-12-21: Application form updated
- 2017-11-30: Application form has been updated
- 2014-04-16: First published.

This form contains 8 sections.

Please ensure that you:

  * read the form carefully and complete all applicable sections
  * complete the form in block letters and in black ink
  * have read and understood [MSN 1853 (M) Amendment 1: Boatmasters’ Qualifications, Crew and Hours of Work](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/msn-1853-m-boatmasters-qualifications-crew-and-hours-of-work) before completing the form



Failure to complete this form in full may mean we will have to return your application to you and will result in a delay to your application being processed.

Further guidance notes can be found from page 7 of MSF 4365.

## Submit your application form

Return your completed application form by post to:

Maritime and Coastguard Agency  
Registry of Shipping and Seamen  
Anchor Court  
Keen Road  
Cardiff  
CF24 5JW 

We are unable to accept applications by fax or email.

You should allow at least 10 days for us to process your application, plus postage.

## Further information

Please address any queries about your application to the Registry of Shipping and Seamen by post or the following contact details:

  * Telephone: +44 (0) 2920 448844
  * Fax: +44 (0) 2920 448820
  * Email: [seafarers_registry@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:seafarers_registry@mcga.gov.uk)

1.  PERSONAL DETAILS  
 
Enter your personal details in the boxes provided. Your name should be given IN FULL, and should be given in the 
same format as appears in your passport or driving licence. 
 
Your date of birth should be given in the format DD/MM/YYYY, e.g. 18 February 1960 would be written 18/02/1960.  
 
You should give your permanent home address, where you are normally resident. You may also provide an alternative 
address for return of documents or correspondence relating to this application. 
 
Your documents will be returned by Recorded Delivery and will need to be signed for. Please ensure you provide us 
with the full address details you would like your documents sent to. You must include a contact telephone number and 
email address should there be any queries with your delivery. 
 
 
 
 
Personal information which you supply to us will be used to process your application to meet the applicable Standards 
of Training Certification and Watchkeeping (STCW) requirements. To check and report on how effective the MCA and 
the services it commissions have been. To make sure that the MCA gives value for money. We may use your 
information to investigate complaints, legal claims or important incidents. We may use your information for crime 
prevention and prosecution of offenders.  
The information you provide is primarily used for issuing your UK Certificate of Competency (CoC)/Certificate of 
Equivalent Competency (CEC) but may also be used for other related purposes, and shared with other statutory 
bodies/organisations to enable them to fulfil their statutory obligations.  
We will not disclose any financial details you provide to us. Once your payment has been processed all financial details 
are securely destroyed.  
For more information on how we use your information, and your rights to access information we hold on you, please 
see our full privacy policy available on our website: 
https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/maritime-and-coastguard-agency
