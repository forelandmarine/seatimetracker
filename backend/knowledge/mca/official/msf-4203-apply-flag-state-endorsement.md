You should use this form whether you are applying for your first FSE, revalidation or upgrade.

Please email your completed application form (including seafarer signature page) and supporting documents to: [fse@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:fse@mcga.gov.uk)

If you have any queries, please see our [detailed guidance on how to apply for a Flag State Endorsement](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-a-FSE-for-deck-and-engineer-officers).

We aim to process FSE applications within 20 working days (roughly 4 weeks) subject to receiving timely CoC verification from the other flag state.

Please note that **we are unable to provide a counter service**.
