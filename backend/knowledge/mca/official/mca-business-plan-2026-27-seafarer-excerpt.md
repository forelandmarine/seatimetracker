# MCA Business Plan 2026 to 2027: seafarer-related objectives

Verbatim rows from the objectives tables, fetched from the HTML version via the GOV.UK Content API. "Milestones" and "Deadlines" are given in the order the plan lists them.

## Why this is in the library
It is the MCA's own plan for the year to March 2027 and the most recent official statement of what will change for seafarer exams and fees. It does NOT mention Yacht Unlimited, an engineer yacht route, or any planned amendment of MSN 1856, 1857, 1862, 1864 or 1865 (whole plan text searched on 2026-10-25).

## P3: Continue implementation of Survey and Inspection 3 to 5 year plan
Milestones:
- Review entry requirements for new entrant surveyors providing options for developing more surveyor resource in-house.
- The phased withdrawal of surveyor involvement in seafarer oral examinations for non-management grade Certificates of Competency will continue with a transition to full digital examinations for the Officer of the Watch.
- Implementation of a risk-based approach for reapproval and mid-term audits for Standard of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping (STCW) course to focus surveyor resource on higher risk providers.

Deadlines: 30 September 2026; 31 March 2027; 31 March 2027.

## E1: Support industry adoption of Alternative Fuels/ Decarbonisation, including the development of new regulatory frameworks
Milestones (seafarer-relevant part): Lead the development and implementation of UK seafarer regulations, training, and certification frameworks, and actively support the development of international standards to enable the safe and effective adoption of maritime decarbonisation technologies and alternative fuels.

## E2: Support industry adoption of Maritime Autonomy (seafarer-relevant part)
Milestones: Develop standardised training and certification for remote operators of MASS, creating employment opportunities and career pathways, and ensuring consistent, high quality training standards across the sector.

## E3: Introduce enhanced digital solutions to modernise our customer-facing transactional services and business processes, including Seafarer Certification, Vessel Survey, and Vessel Registration.
This will see the use of Field Service Management by Marine Offices and Surveys for the management and administration of Survey and Inspection bookings. These improvements will provide better self-service and automated customer services.

Milestones: Deliver the Maritime Customer Platform Project as part of the realisation of the Maritime Digital Platform concept:
- Foundation for Survey and Inspection's field service management for Maritime Offices
- Discovery and design of: Customer Identity and Access Management; Customer Portal for Seafarers; Customer Portal for Ship Owner/Operators

Deadline: 31 March 2027.

## E4: Implement a programme of work to improve seafarers' health and wellbeing through enhanced processes (seafarer certification part)
Milestones include: Complete a review of the MCA Approved Doctor audit programme.

## E5: Commence implementation of accepted Support for Maritime Training (SMarT) Review Recommendations.
Milestones: Begin implementation of the approved recommendations relating to the MCA, with the aim of enhancing recruitment and retention of UK seafarers.
Deadline: 31 March 2027.

## E6: Update MCA's Fees, allowing MCA to recover costs incurred in providing statutory services.
Milestones: Agree revised fees with cross-government stakeholders and complete public consultation ready for implementation in 2027-28.
Deadline: 31 March 2027.

## KPI EK2
We will commence the appeal process relating to ENG1 Seafarer Medical Certificate appeals within 2 working days. Target: 90%.

## Notes for the assistant
- Fees: the current fees (official/fees-regulations-2018-seafarer-certification.md, official/mca-fees.md) still apply. The plan says revised fees will go to public consultation by 31 March 2027 for use in 2027-28. No fees consultation had been published by 25 October 2026. Tell users fees may rise from 2027-28 but no figures exist yet.
- Orals: the plan confirms the MCA is moving non-management CoC orals away from surveyor examiners, with OOW moving to full digital examination. It gives no date for when this reaches yacht OOW or Master <3000 GT candidates; check MIN 690 and MIN 710 for what is in force.
