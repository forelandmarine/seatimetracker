# How to apply using an alternative route for a CoC revalidation

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/alternative-route-for-a-coc-revalidation-msf-4258

> MSF 4258 provides an alternative route for the revalidation of a certificate of competency.

#### Application Information

Please email your application to [deck@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:deck@mcga.gov.uk) for those holding Deck CoC’s or [engineering@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:engineering@mcga.gov.uk) for those holding Engineering CoC’s.

#### Other information

If you have been away from the industry and wish to revalidate your expired certificate of competency, you are required to pass an oral examination.

Before completing this form, please ensure you have read [MSN 1861 Amendment 1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/msn-1861-m-amendment-1-uk-procedure-for-the-revalidation-of-certificates-of-competency-and-tanker-endorsements) and the guidance notes and instructions on pages 6 and 7.

#### The MCA is unable to provide a counter service.

**[This procedure is outlined in MSN 1861 Amendment 1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/msn-1861-m-amendment-1-uk-procedure-for-the-revalidation-of-certificates-of-competency-and-tanker-endorsements)**

[Please read these notes carefully before completing this form. Section 9 (pages 9 to 13) of Marine Shipping Notice (MSN)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/msn-1861-m-amendment-1-uk-procedure-for-the-revalidation-of-certificates-of-competency-and-tanker-endorsements)
[1861 Amendment 1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/msn-1861-m-amendment-1-uk-procedure-for-the-revalidation-of-certificates-of-competency-and-tanker-endorsements) will provide further guidance and details of requirements.

(1)  The STCW regulations have a provision for the revalidation of a Certificate of Competency by the completion of at
least 3 months sea service in a certified capacity lower than that of the certificate held. However, as you have been
away from the industry for a long time you may not be familiar with the latest developments. The MCA must check to
ensure that you have the appropriate knowledge and competence to serve in a lower than certified capacity. This is
achieved by an oral examination conducted by an examiner of the relevant discipline. **Your oral exam will be at the**
**lower capacity.** On passing the oral examination you will be issued with a temporary CoC for service at a lower rank.
Temporary Certificates will be issued for a period of 6 months; they may be extended at the discretion of the Chief
Examiner. **(Deck Officers of the Watch and Engineering Officers of the Watch should refer to point 4)**

(2) You may wish to serve on one particular type of vessel, in which case the oral examination may be restricted to that
type of vessel.

(3) Once you have completed three months sea service in an appropriate capacity you should submit your temporary
Certificate of Competency (CoC), current ENG 1, Discharge Book **or** Certificates of Discharge **and** Sea Service
Testimonials with a covering letter to the Seafarer Training and Certification branch. Once the sea time has been
checked the MCA will issue you with a full UK CoC.

(4) If you are either a Deck Officer of the Watch or Engineering Officer of the Watch who cannot revalidate through
paragraph **(1) above**, you may sit a **FULL** Deck Officer of the Watch or Engineering Officer of the Watch oral exam.
On successful completion of the oral examination and providing you meet the other revalidation requirements, your
certificate will be revalidated in full.

(5) If your Certificate of Competency expired over 10 years ago you maybe be required complete additional training
and/or examinations. This will be indicated on your NOE. This is summarised on pages 8, 9 and 10 of this
application form.

Enter your personal details in the boxes provided. Your name should be given IN FULL, and should be given in the same

Personal information which you supply to us will be used to process your application to meet the applicable Standards of

The information you provide is primarily used for issuing your UK Certificate of Competency (CoC)/ Flag State
