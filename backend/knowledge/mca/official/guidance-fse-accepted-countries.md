# List of countries accepted towards issuing a UK FSE

Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/list-of-countries-accepted-towards-issuing-a-uk-fse

> List of administrations whose Certificate of Competency (CoC) are accepted towards issuing a UK Flag State Endorsement (FSE)

In accordance with STCW Convention Regulation I/10 and MSN 1867 Amendment 1, the UK requires foreign seafarers to obtain a UK Flag State Endorsement (FSE) to work onboard a UK registered vessel. Please see the table below for merchant STCW CoCs that can be used to apply for a UK FSE.

We only accept yacht CoCs issued by France, Italy, Netherlands and New Zealand - please see Annex A of [MSN 1867 Amendment 1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/msn-1867-m-amendment-1-recognition-of-non-uk-certification-for-the-issue-of-a-flag-state-endorsement) for more details. We will also accept some yacht CoCs issued by Malta. Please email [fse@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:fse@mcga.gov.uk) for further details.

We only accept fishing CoCs issued by EU Member States, Iceland and Norway. We do not accept substitute skippers.

Where a CoC contains near-coastal limitations, these are included on the FSE as near-coastal to the country that issued the CoC (in line with the terms of the STCW Convention).
