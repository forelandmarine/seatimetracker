# How to apply for a master workboat less than 500 GT CoC

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/certificate-of-competency-workboats-msf-4257

> Complete form MSF 4257,  for an oral examination leading to the issue of a certificate of competency (STCW) for master workboat less than 500 GT unlimited.

For all applications the sea service declaration (page 2) must be completed in full. 
 
Service should be in the deck department. 
 
Sea service is time spent at sea, at anchor, on river or canal transits and time in port associated with the work on a Workboat. 
 
Watch Keeping Service is sea service spent as a Watch  Keeping Officer in full charge of a  navigational watch for not less than four out 
of every 24 hours. Shorter periods may be aggregated together. 
 
Length of Voyage:  this must be given in calendar months and days, e.g. from 3 January to 5 March = 2 months and 3 days.  Odd days 
should be added together and reckoned at 30 days to the month. Please specify under voyage the area worked e.g. unlimited area or 
categorised waters.    
 
For all sea service that is declared, you must submit two forms of evidence. One  form of evidence  can be Discharge B ook entries or 
Certificates of Discharge, and the other  form of evidence can be sea service testimonia ls; testimonials must be count ersigned by the 
Master or an authorised company representative. Testimonials need to state t he sign on and sign off dates and watch keeping service 
undertaken.  
 
Masters should obtain a company letter providing confirmation of  their sea service signed by an authorised official within the 
company. We will not accept self certified sea service. 
 
 
Sea service requirements:  
 
From Master (Code vessel < 200 GT) to Master Workboats < 500 GT 
 
Complete 36 months sea service on vessels of any size, including not less than 12 months sea service and 120 days watch keeping 
service in vessels 15 metres or over in load line length, whilst holding a Master (Code vessel 200 GT) CoC. You must have 
completed 6 months sea service on a workboat of 15m or over in load line length. Note: Your 6 months service onboard a workboat 
can be completed within the 12 months specified above, although this is not mandatory. 
 
From OOW unlimited to Master Workboats < 500 GT 
 
Complete 12 months sea service holding an OOW unlimited CoC, including not less than 120 days watch keeping service in vessels 
of 15m or over in load line length. You must have completed 6 months sea service on a workboat of 15m or over in load line length.  
Note: Your 6 months service onboard a workboat can be completed within the 12 months specified above although, this is not 
mandatory.    
 
From OOW (Yachts < 3000 GT) to Master Workboats < 500 GT 
 
Complete 12 months sea service since gaining OOW (yachts < 3000 GT)  CoC, including not less than  120 days watch  keeping 
service on vessels of 15m or over in load line length. You must have completed 6 months sea service on a workboat of 15m or over 
in load line length.  Note: Your 6 months service onboard a workboat can be completed within the 12 mont hs specified above , 
although this is not mandatory. 
 
From Fishing Class 1 to Master Workboat < 500 GT 
 
Complete 6 months sea service o n a workboat of15m or over in load line length including not less than 120 days watch  keeping 
service in vessels of 15m or over in load line length.

Your photographs must be taken full face without a hat and must be UK approved passport photographs, measuring a maximum of 50mm

Medical Fitness Certificates 
 
All candidates for any CoC must produce a valid medical fitness certificate, either: 
 
• The UK medical fitness certificate, currently known as an ENG 1, issued by an MCA approved medical practitioner; or 
• A certificate issued by the Administration of any country whose medical fitness certificate is recognised as equivalent to the UK 
ENG 1. 
 
Medical Fitness Certificates must be valid at the time of issue of the CoC.

ECDIS 
 
The STCW Convention 1978 has been amended by the 2010 Manila Amendments and contains new requirements for all seafarers. 
Seafarers applying for a new CoC or revalidating an existing CoC will be required to submit additional evidence to ensure their certificate 
is valid for service on certain types of ships after 31 December 2016.  There is no additional action required for seafarers who do not work 
on and do not intend to work on ships fitted with ECDIS.  These seafarers will receive the following limitation: 
 
“From the 1 January 2017 this certificate is not valid for service on ships fitted with ECDIS.” 
 
For new CoC’s the above limitation will apply unless you submit one of the following: 
 
• MCA approved ECDIS course completed on or after 01 January 2005; or 
• MCA approved NARAS/ NAEST* (Operational Level or Management) course completed on or after 01January 2005
