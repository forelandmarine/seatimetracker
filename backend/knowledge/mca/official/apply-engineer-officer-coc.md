#### Application Information

If you are applying for III/1 EOOW, **please post your Training Record Book** to UK Seafarer Services (Training & Certification), Maritime and Coastguard Agency, Spring Place, 105 Commercial Road, Southampton SO15 1EG. All other supporting documents including the application form can be emailed to [engineering@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:engineering@mcga.gov.uk)

For all other applicants, please email your application and scanned documents to [engineering@mcga.gov.uk](mailto:engineering@mcga.gov.uk)

#### Other information

It is important that the guidance notes and instructions on pages 7, 8 and 9 are read when completing the application.

Please provide an email address in block capitals for us to inform you that we have received your application.

#### The MCA is unable to provide a counter service.

Enclose all documents necessary to establish your eligibility for examination for an STCW Certificate of Competency.
A document checklist is on pages 4 & 5 to help you.

If eligible you will be issued with a Notice of Eligibility (NoE) enabling you to sit an MCA oral examination. The NoE will
be valid for 5 years from the date of issue. The NoE will advise you how to apply for an appointment for the
examination and what you should do once you have taken the examination. Oral examination passes for each level of
certification are valid for three years from the date of the oral exam.

## Engineer OOW

### Cadetship

- Completed a HNC/HND and Sea Service within 5 years of enrolling on the course or 6 years for those that are studying Foundation Degree/Scottish Professional Diploma/Bachelor Degree
- During which time an approved MNTB Training Record Book (TRB) must be completed, this should be completed after registering with the college.
- 12 months combined seagoing service and workshop skills.
  - With a minimum of 6 months seagoing service engaged in watchkeeping or UMS duties on ships of at least 750kW
- Minimum of 3 month workshop skills (should be confirmed in college letter or certificate)
- Remaining 3 months can be
  - Seagoing service
  - Combined workshop/industrial training or
  - 1 month MCA approved Lab time and 2 months seagoing/industrial training

### Experienced Seafarer

- Seagoing service of no less than 24 months in merchant ships of at least 350 kW (to be completed prior to seafarer registering with the college)
- 9 months’ seagoing service in the engine room of vessels of at least 750 kW.
  - Including 6 months’ service undertaking watchkeeping or UMS duties,
    during which time an approved MNTB Training Record Book (TRB) must be completed, this should be completed after registering with the college

### Alternative Route

- Seagoing service must be performed on merchant ships proceeding to sea. Proceeding to sea means, beyond the categorised waters around the coast of the United Kingdom or beyond the harbour limits for waters outside the United Kingdom.
- At least 75% of the required seagoing service must be performed while the vessel is underway on sea passage.
  - Seagoing service (steaming time) will be calculated from the seagoing service testimonials.
  - During which time an approved MNTB Training Record Book (TRB) must be completed, this should be completed after registering with the college

### Combined Motor and Steam EOOW CoC

- Completion of 4 months’ watchkeeping or UMS on each type of ship (motor and steam)
- Completion of all sections in the MNTB TRB (motor and steam)

### Important TRB Information

Page i & ii – Contact Details – Must be fully completed

Section 1 – Guidance – Nothing to be completed

Section 2 – Record of Progress and Achievement - Must be fully completed

Section 3 – Ship and Voyage Details - Must be fully completed

Section 4 – Marine National Occupational Standards – Nothing to be completed

Section 5 – Training Tasks – The relevant tasks must be fully completed.

Section 6 – Engineering and Operations Workbook Guidance – Nothing to be completed

- Requirements for MNTB TRB – All sections must be completed and if left incomplete will delay your application. If there are any sections incomplete then please speak to your Training Provider/Nautical College.

### Additional Endorsements to a current CoC (Motor or Steam)

- Complete at least 4 months’ watchkeeping or UMS duties as a trainee/junior engineer officer under the supervision of qualified engineer officers, on the type of ship for which you do not hold an endorsement.
- Two forms of evidence are required in the forms of Discharge Book or Certificates of Discharge and Sea Service Testimonials
- Complete the motor or steam section of the TRB (whichever one you do not currently hold an endorsement in);

## Marine Engineer Operator Licence (MEOL)

For All MEOL Route Applications
\* Copy of passport, attested by a responsible person
\* An attested passport photograph, cropped to 4:3

Seafarer must show either:
###An apprenticeship in one of the following:
- Diesel Engine Maintenance
- Heavy Vehicle Maintenance,
- Agriculture Machinery Maintenance or
- A relevant (marine and mechanical engineering) apprenticeship acceptable to the MCA.
\* Seagoing service, the amount specified by the MCA after an examiner assessment, as a trainee marine engine operator on vessels of at least 200 kW; and
\* Complete the Approved Engine Course (AEC)

### or

- A Diploma (level 2) in Maritime Studies Able Seafarer Engine (for information please contact Merchant Navy Training Board, [enquiry@mntb.org.uk](mailto:enquiry@mntb.org.uk)) and
- 6 months’ seagoing service as a trainee engine operator or as a rating forming part of the engineering watch while holding an ERWRC

### or

Hold an Able Seafarer Engine Certification

### or

- 24 months’ shore employment in an engineering background acceptable to the MCA and 3 months’ qualifying seagoing service as a trainee marine engine operator on a vessel of at least 200 kW and an Approved Engine Course (AEC)

### or

- If a EOOW Unlimited CoC is held (UK CoC or Non-UK FSE Country CoC). Sea Service evidence of 6 months in the last 5 years
