# Maritime and Coastguard Agency fees

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/maritime-coastguard-agency-mca-fees

> The MCA provides a range of services to vessel owners and operators, seafarers and other individuals and organisations in the maritime industry.

## Change history (gov.uk)

- 2018-10-22: First published.
The Maritime and Coastguard Agency’s (MCA) fees changed in November 2018 (following an [MCA fees consultation](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/mca-fees-consultation)) and are listed in the document above.

To confirm the exact fee for a specific item, please check with your nearest MCA contact.

A list of marine office contact details can be found on our [Office access and opening times](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/maritime-and-coastguard-agency/about/access-and-opening) page.

## Registration of Hovercraft

The fee payable on an application for the registration of a hovercraft under article 5
of the Hovercraft (General) Order 1972[[footnote 7]](#fn:7) is £153.

## Copies of, and Extracts from, Documents

1. 1. In this part, “copy” means a certified copy of—

   1. (a) a declaration or other document, a copy of which is made evidence by virtue
      of the Act, or
   2. (b) an extract from a document declared by the Act to be admissible in evidence.
2. 2. The fee for a copy of a document is £15 for each hour or part of an hour required
   for copying.
3. 3. Where a person requests and receives premium service, a fee of £50 is payable in
   addition to the aggregate of the fees for copying done on the same occasion.

## Seamen’s Documents

### Fees for issue of documents

The fee for the issue, or replacement, of a British Seaman’s Card or a discharge
book is £55. If you request and receive premium service, a fee of £50 is payable in
addition to the aggregate of the fees for documents issued on the same occasion.

### Lost documents

No fee is payable in respect of a replacement British Seaman’s Card or discharge
book where the document has been lost, destroyed or defaced through no fault of the
holder.

## Compulsory Insurance Certificates

The fee payable on an application for the issue of a certificate under section 164 of
the Act (which relates to certificates of compulsory insurance against liability for
pollution) is £31.

The fee payable on an application for the issue of a certificate under section 255N[[footnote 8]](#fn:8)
of the Act (which relates to wreck removal insurance satisfying the requirements of
the Nairobi International Convention on the Removal of Wrecks 2007[[footnote 9]](#fn:9)) is £31.

## Additional Charges (Waiting Time, Abortive Visits and Attendance at Unusual Hours)

Where an inspection is delayed or is disrupted during its course or a visit to carry out an inspection is abortive, a fee calculated at the standard hourly rate for the delay caused or time wasted is chargeable.

1. The fee is not chargeable where—

   1. (a) the delay or time wasted is less than one hour in total or is due to circumstances over which the applicant has no control; or
   2. (b) the person carrying out the inspection is reasonably able to perform other work in the vicinity for which a fee has been paid.

Where any part of an inspection is carried out between 6pm and 8am on a weekday night or at any time on a Saturday, an additional fee of £74 per hour is chargeable for that part.

Where any part of an inspection is carried out in Scotland at any time on a Sunday or a Bank Holiday, an additional fee calculated at the standard hourly rate is chargeable for that part.

Where any part of an inspection is carried out in England and Wales or in Northern Ireland at any time on a Sunday or a Bank Holiday, or on Good Friday or Christmas Day, an additional fee calculated at the standard hourly rate is chargeable for that part.

Only one additional fee is charged to cover attendance at unusual hours, if a part of an inspection covers two or more of the periods described above.

## Waste Management

The fee for the Secretary of State’s consideration and, if appropriate, approval or re- approval of a waste management plan prepared and submitted by a harbour authority or terminal operator in respect of a harbour or terminal pursuant, as the case may be, to regulation 6(1) or (2) or 8(1) of the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Port Waste Reception Facilities) Regulations 2003[[footnote 10]](#fn:10) (“the Regulations”) is charged at the standard hourly rate.
The fee for the preparation by the Secretary of State of a waste management plan in respect of a harbour or terminal pursuant to regulation 9 of the Regulations is charged at the standard hourly rate.

The fee for the issue by the Secretary of State of an exemption in respect of a harbour authority or terminal operator pursuant to regulation 15(1) and (2) of the Regulations, or in respect of a ship pursuant to regulation 15(3) of the Regulations, is
£147.

## Strategic Ships

The fee for the issue by the Secretary of State of an exemption, pursuant to section 48 of the Act (power to exempt from manning requirements), from the requirements in regulation 3 of the Merchant Shipping (Officer Nationality) Regulations 1995[[footnote 11]](#fn:11) relating to a strategic ship is £250.
