**5 PRIVACY STATEMENT**

Personal information which you supply to us will be used to process your application to meet the applicable Standards of Training
Certification and Watchkeeping (STCW) requirements. To check and report on how effective the MCA and the services it commissions
have been. To make sure that the MCA gives value for money. We may use your information to investigate complaints, legal claims or
important incidents. We may use your information for crime prevention and prosecution of offenders.

The information you provide is primarily used for issuing your UK Certificate of Proficiency (CoP) but may also be used for other
related purposes, and shared with other statutory bodies/organisations to enable them to fulfil their statutory obligations.

We will not disclose any financial details you provide to us. Once your payment has been processed all financial details are securely
destroyed.

For more information on how we use your information, and your rights to access information we hold on you, please see our full privacy
