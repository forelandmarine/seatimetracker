# Yacht Rating Certificate: MCA Approved Training Providers

Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/mca-approved-training-providers-atp (PDF: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6ac7a54bb016250bca013a84/YRC_08.10.2026.pdf)

Library note: these are the companies the MCA lists as approved for the Yacht Rating Certificate. Under MSN 1862 Amendment 1 section 12, approved companies issue the Yacht Rating Certificate of Proficiency under the MCA's authority, using the Annex F template (official/msn-1862-amendment-1-deck-ratings.md). There are no UK-based providers on this list. Full text of the list follows.

On validity (library note): the Annex F template in MSN 1862 has a date of issue but no expiry date, and MSN 1862 sets no revalidation requirement for the Yacht Rating CoP (it says only, at 5.7, that the Able Seafarer Deck CoP needs no revalidation). The PYA describes the YRC as valid for life (providers/yacht-rating-certificate-notes.md). The STCW basic safety training refreshers in MSN 1865 still apply to the holder.
