# MCA knowledge library manifest

Built 2026-10-23 for the SeaTime Tracker 'Explain training pathways' assistant. Second, third and fourth (wide off-gov.uk search) fill-in passes 2026-10-25 (coverage audit in COVERAGE.md).

- `official/`: UK government (MCA) documents fetched from gov.uk via the GOV.UK Content API. Full text under the Open Government Licence v3.0. PDFs were converted to markdown; where a PDF page held a table that did not convert cleanly, the full raw text of that page is kept in a fenced block directly after the converted text, so nothing is lost. Key tables in MSN 1858 Amendment 2, MSN 1904 and MIN 642 were also rebuilt by hand as markdown tables.
- `official/` also holds two non-gov.uk items: UK legislation from legislation.gov.uk (OGL v3) and a paraphrase of the Red Ensign Group Yacht Code crew sections (no open licence, so not verbatim).
- `providers/`: paraphrased fact notes from training providers and industry bodies (third-party copyright, no verbatim copying). These are secondary sources; where they disagree with the official text they are flagged `CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL` and the MSN/MIN wins.

## Currency and supersession summary

- MSN 1858 (yacht deck): CURRENT is Amendment 2, published 18 May 2026, replacing Amendment 1. Adds Yacht Unlimited Chief Mate and Master CoCs (section 4). Amendment 1 is superseded and was not stored.
- MSN 1904 (Engineer Officer Small Vessel): current (Dec 2022). Replaced MSN 1859 (Amendment 1) yacht engineer route (Y4 to Y1), MIN 524 and MIN 594 from 10 January 2023; no new yacht engineer NoEs after 31 December 2022. MCA news (May 2026) says an engineer Yacht Unlimited pathway is in development.
- MSN 1856, 1857, 1861, 1862, 1863, 1864, 1865, 1867, 1868: Amendment 1 (Dec 2022) is the latest on gov.uk. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 refers to Amendment 2 of MSN 1862, 1864 and 1865, which could not be found on gov.uk on 2026-10-23.
- MSN 1866 is at Amendment 2 (Dec 2025). MSN 1815 is at Amendment 7 (Aug 2026). MSN 1887 is at Amendment 1 (Nov 2023).
- Expired by their own text but still on gov.uk: MIN 580 (31 Mar 2026; route now in MSN 1858 Am 2 s3.3), MIN 642 (1 Dec 2025), MIN 716 Am 1 (31 May 2026), MIN 543 (28 Mar 2022), MIN 521 (6 Feb 2021), MIN 503 (9 Apr 2019).
- MIN 690 (oral exams and NOEs) is at Amendment 6 (Apr 2026, edited May 2026). MIN 720 Amendment 1 (30 Sep 2026) sets out the modernised officer syllabus.
- Fees (checked 2026-10-25): the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 2018 are still in force with unchanged amounts (CoC application and oral £220, oral resit £159, CoC revalidation £54, ENG1 £115). S.I. 2025/1103 only extended them to 12 November 2028. MIN 690 adds £84 for third and later resits.
- MSN 1860 (ETO) Amendment 1 (Dec 2022) and MSN 1853 (Boatmasters) Amendment 1 (Dec 2021) are the latest versions. MSN 1862, 1864 and 1865 Amendment 2 were still not on gov.uk on 2026-10-25 (MSN collection checked).
- REG Yacht Code: the July 2024 Edition is current (Part A, Part B, Common Annexes). Part A covers yachts of 24 m and over carrying up to 12 passengers; Part B covers passenger yachts carrying 13 to 36 passengers (it is not a code for small yachts under 24 m). MSN 1895 (Apr 2025) gives Part A effect in the UK. Crew and manning parts are stored as a paraphrase because no open licence was found.
- 2026 notices added in the 2026-10-25 pass: MGN 718 (PSSR violence and harassment training, Sep 2026, replaces MIN 729), MSF 4203 Rev 05/26 (FSE form), FSE accepted countries list (Oct 2026), SSNR course criteria 2nd edition (Jul 2026), news on the Workboat Code Edition 3 deadline (Feb 2026) and the rolling five-year syllabus review (Aug 2026).
- MIN 690 Amendment 6 still links MSN 1858 Amendment 1 in its form table; MSN 1858 Amendment 2 is the current notice.
- Third pass (2026-10-25): MSN 1856, 1857, 1862, 1864 and 1865 Amendment 2 are still not published (gov.uk MSN list checked by date), and no consultation or announcement was found. MSF 4343 is unchanged (Rev 12/23, page last changed 12 Mar 2024); no Yacht Unlimited form exists yet. The engineer Yacht Unlimited route is not published; the MCA's 24 Sept 2026 forum slides (paraphrased in providers/yacht-unlimited-route-notes.md) give its status.
- Third pass: the Sport or Pleasure Vessel Code (in force 12 Dec 2025, with MIN 724 amended Mar 2026) replaced the Yellow, Blue and Red Codes and MGN 280 for small commercial vessels in sport or pleasure use. Its manning section and Appendix 5 are stored; they make RYA commercial endorsements mandatory and still cite MGN 496 for Master (Workboat), confirming MGN 496 has not been replaced. Workboats stay under the Workboat Code Edition 3.
- Third pass: MIN 471 (2013) said Yacht Rating Certificates would stop being issued; it expired in October 2018 and is out of date. Yacht Rating CoPs are issued under MSN 1862 Amendment 1 by approved companies (list dated 8 Oct 2026).
- Fourth pass (2026-10-25, wide search beyond gov.uk: WhatDoTheyKnow, Parliament written questions and Hansard, GOV.UK consultations, PYA forum slides, trade press, college and provider sites, Reddit): MSN 1856, 1857, 1862, 1864 and 1865 Amendment 2 have no draft, consultation, briefing or announcement anywhere. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 is itself inconsistent (section 4.2 heading and footnotes 12 to 16 cite Amendment 1; body text cites Amendment 2), so Amendment 1 of each applies until an Amendment 2 is published. No Yacht Unlimited form, FAQ, module syllabus or college course listing exists. Engineer route: no outcome published after the 30 Sept 2026 objection deadline. Added the full text of the MCA's 2025 and 2026 PYA forum slides, an excerpt of the MCA Business Plan 2026 to 2027 (fees consultation planned by 31 Mar 2027 for 2027-28; OOW orals moving to full digital), and 2026 Parliamentary answers on seafarer training.
