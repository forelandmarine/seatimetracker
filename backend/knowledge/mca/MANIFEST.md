# MCA knowledge library manifest

Built 2026-10-23 for the SeaTime Tracker 'Explain training pathways' assistant.

- `official/`: UK government (MCA) documents fetched from gov.uk via the GOV.UK Content API. Full text under the Open Government Licence v3.0. PDFs were converted to markdown; where a PDF page held a table that did not convert cleanly, the full raw text of that page is kept in a fenced block directly after the converted text, so nothing is lost. Key tables in MSN 1858 Amendment 2, MSN 1904 and MIN 642 were also rebuilt by hand as markdown tables.
- `providers/`: paraphrased fact notes from training providers and industry bodies (third-party copyright, no verbatim copying). These are secondary sources; where they disagree with the official text they are flagged `CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL` and the MSN/MIN wins.

## Currency and supersession summary

- MSN 1858 (yacht deck): CURRENT is Amendment 2, published 18 May 2026, replacing Amendment 1. Adds Yacht Unlimited Chief Mate and Master CoCs (section 4). Amendment 1 is superseded and was not stored.
- MSN 1904 (Engineer Officer Small Vessel): current (Dec 2022). Replaced MSN 1859 (Amendment 1) yacht engineer route (Y4 to Y1), MIN 524 and MIN 594 from 10 January 2023; no new yacht engineer NoEs after 31 December 2022. MCA news (May 2026) says an engineer Yacht Unlimited pathway is in development.
- MSN 1856, 1857, 1861, 1862, 1863, 1864, 1865, 1867, 1868: Amendment 1 (Dec 2022) is the latest on gov.uk. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 refers to Amendment 2 of MSN 1862, 1864 and 1865, which could not be found on gov.uk on 2026-10-23.
- MSN 1866 is at Amendment 2 (Dec 2025). MSN 1815 is at Amendment 7 (Aug 2026). MSN 1887 is at Amendment 1 (Nov 2023).
- Expired by their own text but still on gov.uk: MIN 580 (31 Mar 2026; route now in MSN 1858 Am 2 s3.3), MIN 642 (1 Dec 2025), MIN 716 Am 1 (31 May 2026), MIN 543 (28 Mar 2022), MIN 521 (6 Feb 2021), MIN 503 (9 Apr 2019).
- MIN 690 (oral exams and NOEs) is at Amendment 6 (Apr 2026, edited May 2026). MIN 720 Amendment 1 (30 Sep 2026) sets out the modernised officer syllabus.
