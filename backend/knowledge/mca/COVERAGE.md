# MCA library coverage audit

Audit date: 2026-10-25 (after the third fill-in pass; fourth pass, a wide search beyond gov.uk, added notes but changed no rating). Scope: the certificate families the SeaTime Tracker 'Explain training pathways' assistant covers. File names are relative to this folder; `official/` files are MCA or UK legislation text, `providers/` files are paraphrased third-party notes.

Ratings:
- GOOD: the official text answers the usual questions on its own.
- PARTIAL: some official text exists, but it is old, expired, incomplete, or the detail sits in a non-MCA document that is not stored.
- MISSING: nothing usable in the library.

Columns: (a) sea service, (b) training and courses, (c) exams and orals, (d) medical, (e) application process, (f) revalidation.

## Known remaining gaps
- MSN 1856, 1857, 1862, 1864 and 1865 Amendment 2: still not published on gov.uk on 2026-10-25 (MSN list checked by date: the latest seafarer-training MSN is MSN 1858 Amendment 2 of 18 May 2026). No MCA consultation or announcement about them was found on gov.uk, and no provider or press article explains what they will change. MSN 1858 Amendment 2 already refers to Amendment 2 of MSN 1856 (Annex G), 1862, 1864 and 1865, but inconsistently: the section 4.2 heading cites MSN 1856 (Amendment 1), and footnotes 12 to 14 and 16 cite and link MSN 1864, 1862 and 1865 (Amendment 1). Treat Amendment 1 as current. Fourth pass (wide search beyond gov.uk, 2026-10-25): no draft, consultation, industry briefing, union bulletin, college news, Parliamentary question or WhatDoTheyKnow FOI about these amendments exists. The nearest official signals are the Ratings Review (4 of 16 recommendations implemented, no end date: official/parliament-written-answers-2026-seafarer-training.md) and the MCA Business Plan 2026 to 2027 (official/mca-business-plan-2026-27-seafarer-excerpt.md), neither of which mentions the amendments.
- No MCA page on what to expect in an oral, or a list of common reasons applications are refused, exists on gov.uk. The library uses MIN 690 and the warnings in each application form, plus provider notes (now including providers/oral-exam-and-noe-application-tips.md). No reported examiner talk on oral performance from 2025 to 2026 was found.
- RYA scheme documents (Yachtmaster and Powerboat exam prerequisites, syllabuses) are RYA copyright and not stored; RYA commercial endorsement rules are paraphrased only.
- Yacht Unlimited: no new or updated application form, no MCA FAQ, no published module syllabus and no approved-college list yet. Engineer Yacht Unlimited route not published (status as of the MCA's 24 Sept 2026 forum slides is in providers/yacht-unlimited-route-notes.md and, in full, official/mca-slides-2026-09-pya-yacht-unlimited-route.md). Fourth pass: nothing found anywhere (press, providers, unions, Parliament, FOI, Reddit, LinkedIn) on college module start dates or prices, an application form, or the engineer route outcome after 30 Sept 2026; see providers/yacht-unlimited-and-amendment-2-wide-search-2026-10.md.
- Fees: current fees stand, but the MCA Business Plan 2026 to 2027 plans a public consultation on revised fees by 31 March 2027 for use in 2027-28 (official/mca-business-plan-2026-27-seafarer-excerpt.md). No consultation published by 2026-10-25.
- No separate Master (Workboat) oral syllabus exists on gov.uk; MGN 496 (2013) has not been replaced.
- Workboat Association: no public guidance page on skipper qualifications was found.
- Not stored: MSN 1886 Amendment 3 (Oct 2025, medical examination system standards for approved doctors); the library's ENG1 coverage relies on MSN 1887, the medical guidance pages and MSF 4112.
