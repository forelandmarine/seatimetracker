# USCG exams in practice: paraphrased fact notes

These are our own summaries of public pages from a captain's license school (MLS) and a nautical chart and publication seller (Amnautical), read in October 2026. The official sources are 46 CFR 10.205 and 11.201, 46 CFR Part 11 Subpart I, the NMC Examinations page and exam FAQ (nmc-examinations-and-exam-modules.md, nmc-faq-exams.md), the NMC REC/MU FAQ, the sample approval letter (nmc-sample-original-raise-of-grade-letter.md) and the Marine Safety Manual Vol III chapter on exams. Where anything here differs, the official text wins.

## Q: What modules are on the OUPV or Master 100 exam and what score do I need?
Both sources describe four modules for OUPV: Rules of the Road and Chart Plotting at 90 percent, and Navigation General and Deck General/Safety at 70 percent. All questions are multiple choice with four options, and a failed module can be retaken on its own. The full Master (25/50/100 ton) exam adds a fifth module, the Master upgrade, which MLS describes as 70 questions at 70 percent covering firefighting, ship business and law, survival, trim and stability and pollution rules.
Note: the official module lists and passing scores for each exam code are in nmc-examinations-and-exam-modules.md and on the NMC approval to test letter (see nmc-sample-original-raise-of-grade-letter.md).
Source: https://www.amnautical.com/blogs/the-mariners-blog/how-to-get-a-captains-license
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/oupv-six-pack-upgrade-to-25-50-100-ton-masters-online-testing

## Q: If I already hold OUPV, do I resit everything to get Master?
MLS says no. An OUPV holder takes only the Master upgrade module (Rules of the Road and Chart Plotting are not repeated), then sends an updated application. Sea time still has to meet the Master requirement for the route and tonnage.
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/oupv-six-pack-upgrade-to-25-50-100-ton-masters-online-testing

## Q: Can I take the exam at a school instead of a Regional Exam Center?
Yes, for many entry-level deck credentials. MLS explains that a Coast Guard approved course with its own proctored final exam can replace the Coast Guard exam, so you never visit an REC; you send the course completion certificate with your application instead. MLS gives one year from passing to submit the application. Some schools hold the exam in a classroom; MLS proctors it online.
Note: the approved course list says exactly which exam each course replaces and for how long, usually "within one year of application" (see nmc-approved-course-list-how-to-check.md). The NMC courses FAQ says a certificate that substitutes for an exam is valid for one year; other course certificates for five years.
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/oupv-six-pack-upgrade-to-25-50-100-ton-masters-online-testing
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/captains-application-process-overview

## Q: If I pass a course exam, do I still pay a Coast Guard exam fee?
MLS says no separate Coast Guard examination fee applies when an approved course replaces the exam; you pay only the evaluation and issuance fees, which Navita calculates, and MLS puts the typical total for an original captain's license at about 145 dollars.
Note: fees are in 46 CFR 10.219 and nmc-fees-page.md. Check the fee table for your transaction.
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/captains-application-process-overview

## Q: What reference books and tools may I take into the exam room?
Amnautical reproduces the NMC list of materials allowed in the exam room (dated March 2020): the Light Lists, Coast Pilots and tide table reprints, Publication 102 (International Code of Signals), Publication 117, Publication 229, Bowditch, the training charts 12354TR and 13205TR, the deck and engineer illustration books, the MODU operating manuals, plotting tools, and a calculator. Nothing is allowed for the Rules of the Road module. Pre-printed forms are not allowed.
OUT OF DATE: the calculator rules on that page (no programmable calculators) were replaced by the NMC calculator policy of 3 Dec 2024, which allows a programmable calculator at an REC if its programs and memory are cleared in front of the examiner; otherwise a TI-30XIIS is provided (nmc-calculator-policy-exams-2024.md). Check the current list with your REC.
Source: https://www.amnautical.com/blogs/the-mariners-blog/17664800-uscg-exam-study-materials

## Q: How long does the whole captain's license process take?
MLS suggests allowing three to six months from starting the course: most students finish its online course within 90 days, gathering the paperwork takes two to four weeks, and it quoted four to eight weeks for Coast Guard processing (another MLS page says four to six weeks, allow up to three months). It warns that medical waivers can add weeks or months and that TWIC timing is unpredictable.
OUT OF DATE: NMC processing estimates in 2026 were much longer after the government shutdown backlog (about 4 months for a complete application as of July 2026; see nmc-announcements-2026.md).
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/captains-license-resources/captain-license-timeline-process
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/captains-application-process-overview

## Q: What recent sea time do I need before I can test and apply?
MLS's timeline page and Amnautical both say 90 days in the last three years. MLS's upgrade page instead says 90 days in the last seven years under a September 2025 policy letter.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the three-year figure is out of date. CG-MMC Policy Letter 02-25 (September 2025) accepts 3 months of service in the last 7 years for national officer endorsements.
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/captains-license-resources/captain-license-timeline-process
Source: https://www.amnautical.com/blogs/the-mariners-blog/how-to-get-a-captains-license

## Q: Is there a grace period if my license expires?
Amnautical says there is a one-year grace period to renew without retaking the full exam, during which you cannot work, and that after that you start again.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-24 extended the renewal grace period from 1 year to 6 years. You still cannot sail on an expired credential.
Source: https://www.amnautical.com/blogs/the-mariners-blog/how-to-get-a-captains-license

## Q: What does Amnautical say about the physical for a captain's license?
It says vision must be correctable to 20/40 in each eye and that color testing checks red, green and white.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: for a national deck endorsement, 46 CFR 10.305(a)(1) requires 20/40 correctable in one eye (both eyes only for STCW endorsements), and color vision is tested with the approved methods listed in 10.305 rather than a three-color check.
Source: https://www.amnautical.com/blogs/the-mariners-blog/how-to-get-a-captains-license
