# Navita launch and the 2026 application process: paraphrased fact notes

These are our own summaries of trade press, a union reprint and a captain's license school page, read in October 2026. The official sources are the NMC announcements (nmc-announcements-2026.md), the Navita page, FAQ and user guide (navita-page.md, nmc-faq-navita.md, navita-mariner-user-guide.md) and the Maritime Commons articles (maritime-commons-credentialing-articles-2025-2026.md). No press report found by 25 Oct 2026 described problems after launch or gave post-launch processing times. Where anything here differs, the official text wins.

## Q: When did Navita start and what changed on day one?
gCaptain reported that Navita went live at 8 a.m. Eastern on Monday 14 Sep 2026 as the main electronic route for MMC and medical certificate applications. The ASAP upload portal closed, and the IASKNMC email stopped being used for questions; mariners now open a case inside Navita instead. Paper applications by mail or in person were still accepted but would be phased out within 12 months, and the Coast Guard warned they would take much longer. RECs still review documents for completeness at appointments and answer Navita questions, but do not provide computers for applying.
Source: https://gcaptain.com/coast-guards-new-digital-mariner-credentialing-platform-set-to-go-live/

## Q: Will Navita make my application faster?
Not straight away, according to gCaptain. Applications sent before 14 Sep and those filed in Navita are processed together, first in, first out. Mariners who already applied should not resubmit in Navita, since duplicates add delay. Expedited processing is only considered with a documented national defense link. The Coast Guard still advised applying well before expiry.
Source: https://gcaptain.com/coast-guards-new-digital-mariner-credentialing-platform-set-to-go-live/

## Q: Why was there such a backlog in 2026?
gCaptain explained that normal NMC work stopped during the 2026 funding lapse and more than 19,000 applications built up. When work resumed in May the NMC warned of 8 to 12 months; the estimate fell to about 8 months in June and about 4 months by July, with volumes still high. It also noted the August 2026 relief letters for some mariners whose renewals were stuck, and that Navita replaced a set of interim tools used since HOMEPORT was shut down in April 2025.
Source: https://gcaptain.com/coast-guards-new-digital-mariner-credentialing-platform-set-to-go-live/

## Q: What does Navita do for me?
Ocean News carried the Coast Guard's launch release of 14 Sep 2026. Current and planned features: a guided application that flags missing information as you go, automatic fee calculation, Pay.gov payment, online course completion records from schools, and electronic sea service records. Mariners create a profile and submit applications and documents online.
Note: course completion data from schools will not be enforced before 25 Nov 2028 (Federal Register 91 FR 65694, stored in federal-register-recent-credentialing-rules.md).
Source: https://oceannews.com/news/milestones/us-coast-guard-launches-navita-digital-credentialing-platform-for-merchant-mariners/

## Q: What gets an application rejected outright rather than held?
The AMO union reprinted the NMC's October 2024 notice: from 1 Dec 2024, applications missing a complete current-edition CG-719B, evidence of a TWIC (held or applied for), proof of the evaluation fee, or a valid drug test are rejected at initial screening. You get an email listing what is missing and must resend the entire package; the NMC keeps nothing from a rejected submission.
Note: the NMC notice and sample rejection form are stored in nmc-application-rejection-and-top-ai-reasons.md.
Source: https://www.amo-union.org/nmc-to-reject-incomplete-merchant-mariner-credential-mmc-applications/

## Q: How do I apply for a captain's license in Navita, step by step?
MLS describes two separate applications in Navita: the MMC (CG-719B) application, where you answer the 719B questions online and upload your sea service forms, drug test, TWIC copy, first aid and CPR card and course certificate, and the medical certificate (CG-719K or 719K/E) application. Navita calculates fees; you upload the official Pay.gov receipt PDF, not a screenshot. The Submit button does not appear until every checklist item is complete, and Navita saves drafts. If the NMC asks for something, you open a case under Contact Us and upload the file there. Character reference letters are no longer needed. Medical certificates are emailed (since 1 Apr 2026) and the MMC is mailed.
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/captains-application-process-overview

## Q: Can I still send my application by email, fax or post?
MLS states that since 14 Sep 2026 all applications must go through Navita and email or fax submissions are rejected.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: email submission did end with the ASAP and IASKNMC shutdown, but the NMC Navita announcement of 11 Sep 2026 says paper applications by mail or in person are still accepted for now (to be phased out within 12 months), only slower.
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/captains-application-process-overview

## Q: How long does processing take?
MLS says four to six weeks for a complete original application and advises allowing up to 90 days.
OUT OF DATE: the latest NMC estimate in this library (21 Jul 2026) is about 4 months for a complete application, because of the 2026 backlog.
Source: https://marinerslearningsystem.com/blogs/knowledge/captains-application-process-overview
