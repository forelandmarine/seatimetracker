# Maritime Institute: paraphrased fact notes

These are our own summaries of Maritime Institute's published pages. 46 CFR and current National Maritime Center (NMC) policy are binding, and the school itself refers students to the NMC checklists.

## Q: Where is Maritime Institute and what does it teach?
Maritime Institute has campuses in San Diego, Norfolk and Everett (Washington), with exam proctoring also offered in places such as Alameda and Honolulu. It covers professional deck and engine courses, government vessel training, recreational and online courses, and an entry-level bootcamp in Norfolk and Everett. The Norfolk campus accepts the GI Bill.
Source: https://www.maritimeinstitute.com

## Q: How much sea time do I need for OUPV or Master up to 100 GT?
Maritime Institute lists OUPV at age 18 with 360 days total, of which 90 must be near coastal or the licence is limited to inland waters. Master up to 100 GT inland or Great Lakes is age 19 with 360 days, and Master near coastal is 720 days with 360 of them near coastal or ocean. A sail endorsement needs 180 days on sail vessels for inland and 360 for near coastal.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/u-s-coast-guard-captains-license-credential-requirements-oupv-up-to-100-gt/

## Q: What recency rule does Maritime Institute quote?
It states 90 days within the last 7 years, and stresses that this replaces the older 3-year wording. That matches CG-MMC Policy Letter 02-25 (Sep 2025), which accepts 3 months of service in the last 7 years for all national officer endorsements, although the CFR text still says 3 years.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/u-s-coast-guard-captains-license-credential-requirements-oupv-up-to-100-gt/

## Q: What decides whether I get 25, 50 or 100 tons?
Maritime Institute says the tonnage on a Master licence follows the size of the vessels in your sea service and points to the NMC checklist for the exact thresholds. Non-US citizens can hold an OUPV, but only for undocumented vessels and with tonnage limits.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/u-s-coast-guard-captains-license-credential-requirements-oupv-up-to-100-gt/

## Q: Does the Master 100 Tons course replace the Coast Guard exam?
Yes. The course and its proctored final exam satisfy the written exam for Master under 100 GRT or OUPV, near coastal, Great Lakes and inland, or inland. The certificate is valid for one year and can be used for only one application that results in an endorsement, so upgrading later (for example OUPV to Master) means new exams. The online version gives 12 weeks of access (about 80 hours of work, no extensions), with the final exam sat in person; in-person classes were listed at about 1,600 USD.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/course/master-100-tons-online/

## Q: Can I do a course to upgrade Master 100 to Master or Mate 200 tons?
Maritime Institute has an online 100 to 200 ton upgrade course whose certificate would satisfy the exam for Master or Mate under 200 GRT, valid for one year and one application. However, the page says it has been under NMC reapproval since January 2023 and was not open for registration in October 2026. It also does not cover upgrading from OUPV.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/course/upgrade-master-100-tons-to-master-200-tons-near-coastal-online/

## Q: What does the Apprentice Mate of Towing Vessels upgrade course do?
It is a 24-hour course, listed at 500 USD in Norfolk (dates on request). Completing it within one year of submitting a complete application can satisfy the exam for an original Apprentice Mate (Steersman) of Towing Vessels endorsement, near coastal, Great Lakes and inland, or inland. You must also hold, or have passed within the last 12 months, the Master or Mate under 200 GRT exams for the same scope.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/course/apprentice-mate-steersman-upgrade/

## Q: Can Maritime Institute prepare and file my Coast Guard application?
Yes, through a paid service it calls Stress-Free MMC: staff build the application, check documents against Coast Guard requirements, submit it, track it and deal with Coast Guard follow-up, with support until 30 days after issue. In October 2026 it was 349 USD per application for students (normally 399 USD), excluding federal fees paid on Pay.gov. Its MM-SEAS tool calculates sea days and lets you create and sign digital CG-719S forms for vessels under 200 GRT; a guidance-only tier is 99 USD a year.
Note: the page does not mention Navita. Since 14 Sep 2026 all applications go through Navita, and the NMC estimate (Jul 2026) is about 4 months for complete applications.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/mariner-credential-consulting-services/

## Q: What do I need to keep Basic Training valid?
Maritime Institute's STCW FAQ says you need qualifying sea service plus approved refresher (revalidation) training; without qualifying service you must take the fuller refresher course. The same rule applies to Survival Craft and Advanced Firefighting, with NVICs 08-14, 04-14 and 09-14 cited.
Note: from 1 Oct 2029 (CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-26) Basic Training original and renewal also needs an approved violence and harassment prevention course.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/faqs-stcw/

## Q: Do I need STCW if I only hold a domestic licence?
The FAQ says national (domestic) officer endorsements do not require STCW. STCW endorsements are only needed for service on voyages covered by STCW, where for example ABs and QMEDs need Able Seafarer Deck or Engine, and everyone needs at least Security Awareness.
OUT OF DATE: the FAQ refers to NVIC 03-14 for course validity. NVIC 01-26 (Jun 2026) replaced NVIC 03-14 for course approval.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/faqs-stcw/

## Q: What does Maritime Institute say an AB needs?
Every AB rating needs a lifeboatman qualification, usually through a Proficiency in Survival Craft course. For international voyages you also need Basic Training, RFPNW and Able Seafarer Deck. The page does not give day counts and refers to the NMC deck ratings checklist.
Note: AB sea service is temporarily reduced under Policy Letter 02-24 for applications submitted before 22 Dec 2026; the page does not mention this.
Source: https://maritimeinstitute.com/requirements-able-seaman/
