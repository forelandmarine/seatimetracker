# MODU, fishing and Rivers credentials in practice: paraphrased notes

Our own summaries. Where these notes and official text differ, the official text wins. For which courses are Coast Guard approved today, use nmc-approved-courses-rivers-fishing-modu-extract-2026.md (30 Sep 2026 list). For the fishing operator training law, use fishing-vessel-operator-training-4502g-status.md.

What genuinely does not exist (searched Oct 2026): no NVIC, policy letter or NMC guidance page specific to Rivers, fishing officer or MODU officer training; no Coast Guard statement accepting IADC or OPITO certificates for a US endorsement; no exam-substitute course for Master or Mate of Rivers, for an original Master or Mate of uninspected fishing industry vessels, or for MODU engineers; no school program built around a Rivers deck officer endorsement.

## MODU (OIM, Barge Supervisor, Ballast Control Operator)

### Q: What courses do people actually take for BCO or Barge Supervisor?
In a 2009 forum thread a mariner listed what he took: a 5-day MODU stability course, a 5-day BCO course, a 5-day firefighting course and a 2-day survival suit course, using a Louisiana school for the stability and BCO courses and a university survival center for the suit training. Others said employers favored that school because its course exam replaced the Coast Guard exam. This matches the current NMC list, where the Louisiana and Texas schools offer separate stability courses and exam-substitute courses for OIM, BS and BCO. Reliability: old first-hand account, consistent with the 2026 course list.
Source: https://forum.gcaptain.com/t/bco-barge-supervisor/1935

### Q: If I hold an unlimited license, do I still have to test for BCO, Barge Supervisor or OIM?
Forum posters disagreed. In 2009 some said unlimited Masters were given OIM without a test, while others reported NMC making them test for Barge Supervisor and concluded that all BS and BCO applicants test because the MODU exam modules are not covered elsewhere. A 2012 poster claimed an unlimited Master needs no OIM test. Reliability: low, old and contradictory. Check the current 46 CFR 11.470 to 11.474 and the MODU checklists (nmc-checklists-modu-osv.md); an approved exam-substitute course is the usual way to avoid the REC exam.
Sources: https://forum.gcaptain.com/t/bco-barge-supervisor/1935 and https://forum.gcaptain.com/t/oim-very-similair-job-to-master-of-ocean-freighter/7338

### Q: How do people become an OIM on a drillship or semi-submersible?
Posters in 2012 described the usual path on dynamically positioned rigs as DPO, senior DPO, Chief Mate, then Master/OIM, with an unlimited Master license as the base. They said some companies require the OIM endorsement on top of the Master license and some do not, depending on the flag state manning certificate, and that an OIM endorsement without a Master license rarely gets you hired as OIM on DP rigs. A 2023 thread pointed to 46 CFR 15.520, which in some cases requires the Master and OIM to be the same person. Reliability: career anecdote, plausible but old.
Sources: https://forum.gcaptain.com/t/oim-very-similair-job-to-master-of-ocean-freighter/7338 and https://forum.gcaptain.com/t/are-separate-master-and-oim-no-longer-allowed/66689

### Q: Does the Coast Guard accept IADC or OPITO training?
We found no evidence that it does for US endorsements. IADC reported in 2006 that its Ballast Control and Stability accreditation system had approvals from the Vanuatu and Marshall Islands registries and a provisional approval from Liberia, which are foreign flag states. The US NMC course list (Sept 2026) names neither IADC nor OPITO. MERPAC in 2014 noted that offshore workers take industry courses such as RigPass, SafeGulf, HUET and BOSIET, but those are for persons other than crew (see merpac-task-86-modu-officer-training-2014.md). Reliability: the IADC point is old trade press; the absence from the NMC list is official. Answer: for a US endorsement, take a Coast Guard approved course; an IADC or OPITO certificate may help with a foreign-flag employer or flag state, not with NMC.
Source: https://iadc.org/dcpi/dc-septoct06/Sept06-kropla.pdf

## Uninspected fishing industry vessels (Master, Mate, Engineers)

### Q: Is there a course I can take instead of the Coast Guard exam for Master UFIV?
In a June 2024 thread an experienced poster said there are no approved courses for that exam, so it must be taken at an REC, and no extra courses are required beyond those for Mate UFIV. Others mentioned that a Washington fishing school (Crawford Nautical Training) and a Louisiana technical college used to run courses; one said the Louisiana course was no longer on the NMC list, and posters disagreed on whether Crawford was still operating. One suggested studying with Master 1600 material because the exams are similar. Reliability: forum, but it matches the 2026 NMC list, where the only Crawford fishing course is for renewal refresher or sea time credit.
Source: https://forum.gcaptain.com/t/upgrade-to-masters-question/69748

### Q: Does any college offer a fishing officer course?
Southwestern Oregon Community College listed a course "Master/Mate Fishing Vessel Coastal" (MARI 0295) for Jan to Mar 2024, covering stability and fishing vessel safety regulations for mariners who hold a near coastal officer endorsement of at least 150 GRT and want to add Master or Mate of uninspected fishing industry vessels. The listing does not say it is Coast Guard approved, and it does not appear on the Sept 2026 NMC approved course list. Reliability: school listing; may not be current.
Source: https://mylakerlink.socc.edu/ICS/Academics/MARI/MARI_0295/2023_WI-MARI_0295-65

### Q: Can a Chief Engineer UFIV license be converted to Chief Engineer OSV or Chief Engineer Limited?
In a 2024 thread an experienced poster said there is no specific crossover: you must meet the normal requirements and take the full exam, though sea service already used may count if it fits. Others warned that horsepower limits (for example 4,000 HP on a UFIV chief license) tend to carry over and limit work on larger OSVs, and suggested Chief Engineer Limited over Chief OSV for wider use. Reliability: forum, consistent with the CFR having no UFIV crossover rule.
Source: https://forum.gcaptain.com/t/ufiv-to-chief-osv-or-chief-limited/69025

### Q: What fishing safety training can a skipper actually take?
AMSEA runs a Fishing Vessel Stability class (for example free, about six hours, Sitka, 14 Nov 2024) that it describes as Coast Guard accepted, saying the Coast Guard recommends stability training for commercial fishermen; bring the vessel's stability book if you have one. AMSEA's two-day Fishing Vessel Drill Conductor course (for example Astoria, 16 to 17 Jul 2026, 50 dollars for commercial fishermen) meets the drill conductor training rule in 46 CFR 28.270(c). Reliability: provider listings; the acceptance claims match the NMC list (AMSEA stability course accepted under 46 U.S.C. 4502(g)(2)(A)).
Sources: https://amsea.org/classes/1086711 and https://www.amsea.org/classes/1087091

## Rivers (Master or Mate of Rivers)

### Q: How are the Rivers exams organized?
An exam prep site (last updated Oct 2019) groups the river deck exams into five sets: Master Rivers unlimited tonnage, Master Rivers under 500 to 1600 GRT, Mate Rivers under 500 to 1600 GRT, Master or Mate Rivers under 200 GRT, and Master Rivers under 100 GRT. It says moving from Master or Mate under 500 GRT to under 1600 GRT on the same route needs no further exam, and Master under 100 GRT to under 200 GRT needs only the navigation and deck general/safety module. Reliability: low to moderate, third-party and dated; the official exam modules are in nmc-examinations-and-exam-modules.md (RVR01 to RVR05).
Source: https://www.edumaritime.net/uscg-exams/deck-officer-exams-rivers

### Q: Is there a school course for Master or Mate of Rivers?
None was found. Searches of schools and the forum turned up Western Rivers towing courses (Apprentice Mate (Steersman) and route additions), Radar Observer (Rivers) courses and the Louisiana river port pilots' apprentice program, but nothing built for the Rivers deck officer endorsement itself. Forum threads about "rivers" are almost all about towing on Western Rivers, which is a different endorsement. Reliability: absence after a wide search; the NMC course list (Sept 2026) agrees.
Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/courses/courses.pdf (list check, see nmc-approved-courses-rivers-fishing-modu-extract-2026.md); the NMC checklist page says only to use the Great Lakes and inland checklists for Rivers (per 46 CFR 11.459).
