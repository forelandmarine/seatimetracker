# MPT (towing and workboat): paraphrased fact notes

These are our own summaries of MPT's published course pages, chosen because MPT runs towing officer and TOAR-related courses. Houston Marine Training Services (houstonmarine.com) now redirects to RelyOn Nutec and had no towing course pages we could read. 46 CFR, NVIC 03-16 Change 7 and current NMC policy are binding.

## Q: What does MPT's Apprentice Mate (Steersman) upgrade course give me?
Course 120 is a 2-day, Coast Guard approved class in Fort Lauderdale. Completing it within one year of applying satisfies the exam for an original Apprentice Mate (Steersman) of Towing Vessels endorsement, near coastal, Great Lakes and inland, or inland, if you already hold Master under 200 GRT for that scope or passed that exam in the last 12 months. It does not cover Western Rivers.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the page describes the course as taken in place of an exam at a Regional Exam Center. That is still the effect, but applications are now filed through Navita (since 14 Sep 2026), not at an REC.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/apprentice-mate-steersman-master-of-towing-crossover-upgrade-apx-course-120

## Q: How does the TOAR fit in after the steersman course?
MPT gives students a personal copy of the Towing Officer Assessment Record to complete on their own vessel toward Mate (Pilot) or Master of Towing Vessels, and says no further exam is then needed. The TOAR tasks must be signed by a designated examiner on board. Current Coast Guard guidance on towing endorsements and the TOAR is NVIC 03-16 Change 7 (Sep 2024).
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/apprentice-mate-steersman-master-of-towing-crossover-upgrade-apx-course-120

## Q: Is there a one-stop MPT package from no licence to towing officer?
Course 121 is an 18-day (about 3.5 week) combination of the Master 100 ton course, the 200 ton upgrade and the steersman crossover, all with exams at MPT rather than the Coast Guard. MPT says the outcome is Apprentice Mate (Steersman), Mate (Pilot) or Master of Towing, depending on what your sea service qualifies you for. If you already hold 100 tons you only take the other two parts.
OUT OF DATE: the course 121 page and the program 27 page number the parts differently (course 120 versus 107 for the 200 ton step), so confirm with MPT which course is which.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/apprentice-mate-steersman-of-towing-vessels-course-121

## Q: What is in MPT's Master of Towing, Mate Pilot, Apprentice Mate program?
Program 27 bundles Master 100 Tons (11 days), the 100 to 200 GRT upgrade (no Coast Guard test), the Apprentice Mate (Steersman) upgrade and Radar Observer Unlimited. In October 2026 the page said a new updated program was coming soon, package prices require payment in full, and enquiries go to student services.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/program-details/master-of-towing-mate-pilot-apprentice-mate-program-27

## Q: How much towing sea service do I need for Apprentice Mate (Steersman)?
MPT's towing pages do not state the day counts. Under 46 CFR 11.466 (as commonly summarised from the NMC checklist) the route is 540 days of total service, including 360 on towing vessels and 90 on the route sought, plus the exam or approved course. Check the current NMC checklist before advising a user.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/program-details/master-of-towing-mate-pilot-apprentice-mate-program-27

## Q: Do 12-hour days on a towboat count as 1.5 days?
MPT pages do not cover this. Under 46 CFR 10.107 a day is 8 hours, but on vessels authorised to run a two-watch system a 12-hour working day may be credited as 1.5 days, and vessels under 100 GRT may accept not less than 4 hours as a day. One month is 30 days. Many towing vessels on shorter routes run two watches, but the credit depends on the vessel's authorisation.
Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/46/10.107

## Q: How much sea time and recency does MPT quote for OUPV and Master?
OUPV near coastal: 360 days with 90 offshore. OUPV inland and Master inland: 360 days. Master near coastal: 720 days with 360 offshore. Each needs 90 days within the last 7 years, which matches CG-MMC Policy Letter 02-25. MPT notes you can go straight to Master without holding OUPV first.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license

## Q: How does MPT explain tonnage limits on a Master licence?
Tonnage is based on the documented GRT of vessels in your sea service. MPT lists, for example, 25 GRT for 180 days on vessels of 6 to 25 GRT, 50 GRT for 180 days on vessels of 26 GRT or more (or 360 days on 17 GRT or more), and 100 GRT for 180 days on vessels of 51 GRT or more (or 360 days on 34 GRT or more).
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license

## Q: What paperwork does MPT say goes into a captain licence application?
The CG-719B application, sea service letters or CG-719S forms signed by the owner or master (plus proof of ownership if you sign for your own boat), a CG-719K medical within the last year, a CG-719P drug test within 6 months or proof of a random testing programme, a TWIC, the course certificate (within one year), First Aid and CPR or valid Basic Training, and Coast Guard fees.
OUT OF DATE: the page does not mention Navita. Since 14 Sep 2026 these documents are uploaded through Navita and fees are paid on Pay.gov; NMC estimated about 4 months for complete applications in Jul 2026.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license

## Q: Does the MPT DDE course replace the exam or give sea time?
Course 267 (DDE Unlimited Horsepower) is 4 weeks (160 hours, about 22 days). It is approved either to satisfy the exam for an original DDE less than 4000 HP motor endorsement, or to give 45 days of sea service credit toward DDE unlimited HP, for one transaction only, completed within one year of applying. It does not meet any STCW requirement. A DDE may serve as Chief Engineer on domestic vessels up to 500 GRT. The price has been listed around 3,999 USD.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/designated-duty-engineer-dde-unlimited-horsepower-course-267

## Q: When do I need the Basic Training refresher instead of revalidation at MPT?
MPT's refresher (course 165) is for mariners who do not have one year of sea time in the last five years; the revalidation course (164) is for those who do. Original Basic Training is five days covering survival, firefighting, first aid and CPR, and personal safety, with security training sold separately.
Note: from 1 Oct 2029 (CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-26) Basic Training original and renewal also needs an approved violence and harassment prevention course.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/safety-training

## Q: Is there a grace period if my credential expires?
MPT's FAQ says you can renew up to 12 months after expiry, but cannot work on an expired credential.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-24 extended the renewal grace period from 1 year to 6 years. You still cannot sail on an expired credential during that time.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/faq
