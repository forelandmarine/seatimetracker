# Chapman School of Seamanship: paraphrased fact notes

These are our own summaries of Chapman School pages, read in October 2026. Chapman is a non-profit training school; 46 CFR and current National Maritime Center (NMC) guidance are binding where anything here differs.

## Q: What counts as a sea day according to Chapman?
Chapman describes a documented sea day as at least 4 hours on the water operating a vessel of 16 feet or more, counted from age 16. Time on your own boat and recreational time (rivers, the Intracoastal, offshore fishing, coastal cruising) all count if documented. Crew, galley and other assisting roles need no licence but still build sea time.
Note: 46 CFR 10.107 defines a day as 8 hours, with the Coast Guard able to accept not less than 4 hours on vessels under 100 GRT.
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/
Source: https://chapman.org/maritime-training-school-in-stuart-fl

## Q: How do I record sea time on my own boat?
Use Coast Guard form CG-719S with the vessel details, approximate dates and the owner's signature, which is your own if you own the boat. Chapman says photos and logbooks are useful backing evidence and that the form works on an honour system. You do not have to finish your sea time before starting the course.
Note: if you sign as owner, the NMC also needs proof of ownership with the form.
Source: https://chapman.org/maritime-training-school-in-stuart-fl

## Q: What sea time does Chapman list for OUPV and Master?
OUPV: 360 documented days, 90 of them near coastal for a Near Coastal route and 90 for a Great Lakes endorsement. Master under 100 GRT Inland: 360 days. Master Near Coastal: 720 days with 360 near coastal. Chapman states 90 days must fall in the 3 years before applying.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: Policy Letter 02-25 (September 2025) accepts 90 days in the last 7 years for all national officer endorsements. Also, Chapman's career steps page gives 760 days for Master Near Coastal; the requirement is 720 days, as its own course page says.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: What do Chapman's licence courses cost and how long are they?
Prices seen October 2026: OUPV 8 days (64 hours), $930 total including books and Coast Guard testing fees; OUPV to Master upgrade 3 days, $370 tuition; combined OUPV plus Master under 100 GRT about 11 days, $1,200; Master 100 to 200 GRT upgrade 4 days, $890; one-day renewal course $260. Assistance towing is included with OUPV and Master. Full attendance is required to sit the exams.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: Who can get a Master licence rather than an OUPV?
Chapman requires age 19 and US citizenship for Master. OUPV needs age 18, and non-citizens need a Permanent Resident card and are limited to undocumented vessels. Master is issued at 25, 50 or 100 GRT according to the sea service forms.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: How long is my course certificate valid?
Chapman's OUPV, Master upgrade, towing, sail, 200 ton and AB certificates are valid for one year, so you have 365 days after passing to submit your application. STCW Basic Training certificates last five years, except First Aid, which Chapman lists at one year.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: What else do I need besides the course?
Chapman lists a medical exam, a drug test, a TWIC, adult First Aid and CPR, and a signed, notarised application, all submitted to the Coast Guard with your certificates. Drug testing is available walk-in at a local urgent care clinic.
OUT OF DATE: the oath no longer needs notarising (since December 2024) and applications are filed online in Navita since 14 September 2026. The drug test must be within 185 days, and the medical is on form CG-719K. TWIC is still required unless you qualify for the Policy Letter 11-15 exemption.
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: Can Chapman check my application before I send it?
Yes. Chapman offers a licence package review: $150 for a simple application and $250 for a complex one on the programs page, while the career steps page says the service helps assemble and submit applications electronically from $125 (October 2026). Fees depend on complexity.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: How long does the Coast Guard take to issue my credential?
Chapman estimates 3 to 6 months from enrolling to holding your credential, and 4 to 8 weeks of processing once the application is submitted. Those with sea time already documented move faster.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the NMC's July 2026 estimate was about 4 months for a complete application, after the 2026 shutdown backlog.
Source: https://chapman.org/maritime-training-school-in-stuart-fl

## Q: How do I renew a licence up to 100 GRT?
Chapman runs a one-day, open-book renewal course (8 hours) covering navigation and deck safety plus Rules of the Road, at $260 in October 2026. The credential is valid for five years.
Note: Policy Letter 01-24 gives a 6-year administrative grace period after expiry to renew, though you cannot sail on an expired credential.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: What is the Professional Mariner Training (PMT) program?
A 9-week, 360-hour full-time course, $10,280 tuition including books (October 2026), with housing extra. Graduates who pass the exams can obtain Master or Mate under 100 GRT, OUPV, auxiliary sail and towing endorsements, STCW Basic Training, Security Awareness, Able Seafarer and an FCC radio permit, provided they have enough sea time. Boat-handling in the course is credited as deck sea service. The program targets commercial and yacht work, and PMT graduates within 5 years may enter the Coast Guard at petty officer level, subject to Coast Guard approval.
Source: https://chapman.org/pmt/

## Q: I have little sea time. Is there a shorter route into yacht or deck work?
Chapman's 5-week Deckhand/Mate course ($6,360 tuition, October 2026) covers STCW Basic Training, Security Awareness, Able Seafarer, an FCC radio permit and NASBLA safe boating, with boat-handling credited as deck sea service. Once you have built the required sea time, Chapman lets you return for the last two weeks of a PMT class to do the licence prep and exam. The stand-alone AB course needs no prior sea time, but you have one year to complete the service.
Source: https://chapman.org/deckhand-mate/
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: Do yacht crew need STCW Basic Training?
Chapman says STCW Basic Training is required for internationally flagged vessels and large commercial yachts, and most crew agencies ask for it. Its 5-day course covers personal survival, firefighting (with Port Canaveral firefighters), first aid and personal safety, for $1,360 in October 2026.
Source: https://chapman.org/maritime-training-school-in-stuart-fl
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: Do I need a USCG licence to work as yacht crew?
Chapman says a Merchant Mariner Credential (often called a captain's licence) is needed to be in charge of a vessel, especially one carrying paying passengers. Deck, galley and other support roles do not need one, but the time spent in them builds sea service toward a future credential. Some employers still ask for an MMC for certain jobs.
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: How much sea time do I need for an OUPV (six-pack) Near Coastal licence?
Chapman lists 360 documented sea days, with 90 of them on near coastal waters and 90 within the last 3 years, and a minimum age of 18. The OUPV Inland version needs 360 days on any waters.
OUT OF DATE: Policy Letter 02-25 (September 2025) accepts 3 months of service in the last 7 years for all national officer endorsements, so the "90 days in the last 3 years" recency rule on Chapman's pages is no longer the official position.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: How much sea time do I need for Master under 100 GRT Near Coastal?
Chapman's approved-programs page lists 720 documented days, with at least 360 on near coastal waters, and 360 days for the Inland version. Its "steps for a maritime career" page gives 760 days instead. The 720 figure is the one that matches the official requirement, so treat 760 as a typo. Chapman also gives the old 90 days in 3 years recency rule, which is OUT OF DATE since Policy Letter 02-25 (3 months in the last 7 years).
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: Do I have to be a US citizen?
For a Master licence, yes: Chapman says the applicant must be a US citizen and at least 19. A non-citizen with a green card (lawful permanent resident) can hold an OUPV, but only for undocumented vessels. The PMT page adds that foreign students can enrol but would also be limited to undocumented vessels.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: How is the tonnage on a Master licence decided?
Chapman says the Master Near Coastal licence is issued at 25, 50 or 100 GRT, depending on the tonnage of the vessels shown on your sea service forms. Chapman does not discuss gross tonnage (GT ITC) versus gross register tons. Officially, CG-543 Policy Letter 11-12 says there is no conversion factor between GRT and GT ITC, so superyacht time recorded only in GT needs checking with the NMC.
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: Can I go above 100 GRT after a 100-ton Master?
Chapman runs a 200T course to upgrade a Master under 100 GRT to Master under 200 GRT. The page does not set out the extra sea service needed for that upgrade, which comes from 46 CFR and NMC checklists.
Source: https://chapman.org/uscg-approved-programs/

## Q: What else goes in a first licence application?
Chapman lists a medical exam and physical, a drug test, a TWIC and an adult First Aid/CPR certificate. It says drug test evidence is needed for both original and renewal applications, and that the application must be completed, signed and notarised.
OUT OF DATE: since 14 September 2026 applications go through the Navita system with fees on Pay.gov, so check the current NMC instructions for how the signature and oath are now handled.
Source: https://chapman.org/steps-for-a-maritime-career/

## Q: Does time on a school course count as sea service?
Chapman says the boat handling parts of PMT are credited as deck department sea service under a master or qualified officer, and the programme includes about 21 days of sea time. That is far short of the 360 days needed for an OUPV, so most of the time must come from working afloat.
Source: https://chapman.org/pmt/

## Q: What credentials does the PMT yacht-sector programme lead to?
Chapman says passing the PMT exams can qualify graduates for Master or Mate up to 100 GRT (Inland or Near Coastal) or OUPV, with Auxiliary Sail, Commercial Assistance Towing, STCW Basic Training, Security Awareness and Able Seafarer endorsements. The credential is only issued if the graduate also has enough sea time, and Chapman says the applicant is responsible for checking their own eligibility.
Source: https://chapman.org/pmt/
