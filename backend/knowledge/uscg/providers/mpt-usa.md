# Maritime Professional Training: paraphrased fact notes

These are our own summaries of MPT's public web pages, written in October 2026. MPT is a training school, not the regulator: the text of 46 CFR and current National Maritime Center (NMC) guidance is binding wherever it differs.

Access note: MPT's program-package pages (for example the "Mate (OICNW) with STCW Less than 500GT" and "Master with STCW 100Ton or 200Ton" packages) returned server errors (HTTP 520) on 2026-10-25, so they are not summarised here. No page describing a dedicated MPT application-filing service was found; the site only says counselors and student services will help with career planning.

## Q: How much sea time does MPT say I need for OUPV or Master under 100 GRT?
MPT lists OUPV Near Coastal as 360 days of boating experience including 90 days offshore, and OUPV Inland or Master Inland as 360 days. Master Near Coastal is listed as 720 days with 360 of them offshore. For every level MPT states that 90 days must fall within the past 7 years. This matches the current official position on recency (CG-MMC Policy Letter 02-25 accepts 90 days in the last 7 years).
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license

## Q: How does MPT say the Coast Guard decides between 25, 50 and 100 ton Master?
The tonnage is set by the size of the boats in your documented sea time. MPT's table: 25 GRT needs, for example, 180 days on boats of 6 to 25 GRT or 360 days on boats of 1 to 16 GRT; 50 GRT needs 180 days on 26 GRT or more, or 360 days on 17 GRT or more; 100 GRT needs 180 days on 51 GRT or more, or 360 days on 34 GRT or more. Uninspected vessels up to 100 GRT are covered automatically by a Master licence whatever the tonnage on it. If you do not know your boat's tonnage, the documentation certificate shows it or MPT's flyer has a formula.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/captains-license-uscg-master-or-mate-100-ton-course-101

## Q: Do I have to hold an OUPV before going for Master?
No. MPT points out that the Coast Guard does not require a six-pack first, so if your sea time already qualifies you can take the full Master course and go straight to Master or Mate. MPT says most of its students qualify for Master. A Master Near Coastal licence also reaches 200 miles offshore, against 100 miles for OUPV Near Coastal.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/captains-license-uscg-master-or-mate-100-ton-course-101

## Q: How long is the MPT captain's course and is the exam taken at the Coast Guard?
The OUPV level is 8 days of instruction (MPT also describes it as 11 days in total including review and two testing days), and the Master level adds three more days. The course is Coast Guard approved, so the written exams are taken at MPT at the end of the course rather than at a Coast Guard exam centre. The training certificate stands in for the Coast Guard exam if presented within one year, and can only be used for one application. MPT did not show a price on these pages when checked in October 2026.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/captains-license-uscg-master-or-mate-100-ton-course-101
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license

## Q: What documents does MPT list for an original captain's application?
MPT lists the CG-719B application, sea service on CG-719S forms or letters, proof of ownership if you sign off your own sea time, a CG-719K medical exam, a drug test on a CG-719P (or a company letter showing you are in a random testing programme), a TWIC, First Aid and CPR (or STCW Basic Training within 5 years), the course certificate, proof of residency for non-citizen six-pack applicants, and the Coast Guard fees. MPT says the drug test should be within 6 months and the physical within 1 year.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the drug test must be within 185 days of application. Since 14 September 2026 the CG-719B is completed online in Navita rather than as a paper form, and fees are paid on Pay.gov.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license

## Q: How long after the course do I have to send in my application?
MPT says you have up to one year from finishing the course to submit your paperwork. The 200 ton upgrade page repeats the one-year rule for its certificate. Plan your sea service forms, medical and drug test so they are all ready inside that year.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/master-or-mate-200-ton-upgrade-course-107

## Q: How do I upgrade from Master 100 ton to Master 200 ton?
MPT runs a 5-day Coast Guard approved upgrade course (Course 107) with the exam taken at the school. It covers compasses, ocean track plotting, ship construction, stability, damage control, cargo, maritime law and lifesaving. You need a Master 100 ton licence, or proof you met the 100 ton exam requirements, or a 100 ton course certificate. It only changes tonnage, not route: a 100 ton Inland master gets 200 ton Inland, and changing route needs a further course. You still need the extra sea service on larger vessels, which the page does not spell out, and MPT suggests career counselling because some candidates qualify for more than 200 tons.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/master-or-mate-200-ton-upgrade-course-107

## Q: What does MPT offer toward Master or Mate 500/1600 tons?
MPT's 10-day Terrestrial and Coastal Navigation course (Course 200) is exam preparation for the Master or Mate 500, 1600, 3000 and unlimited navigation problem modules (near coastal and chart plot). It also meets the terrestrial navigation training standard for STCW OICNW on ships of 500 GT or more, and the navigation standards for STCW Master and OICNW on vessels under 500 GT near coastal. It does not cover electronic or celestial navigation. Sea service for 500/1600 is not set out on this page.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/terrestrial-coastal-navigation-200

## Q: I am US yacht crew. Which MPT courses help toward STCW Master or OICNW under 500 GT near coastal?
MPT's 5-day Electronic Navigation course (Course 206) meets the electronic navigation training for STCW Master under 500 GT near coastal (46 CFR 11.317) and OICNW under 500 GT near coastal (46 CFR 11.321), as well as OICNW 500 GT or more. Radar Observer Unlimited is a prerequisite. The practical assessments done in class count, so you do not need separate signed task sheets for those tasks. These STCW endorsements sit on top of a national licence and need extra sea service and training.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/electronic-navigation-oicnw-course-206

## Q: Is an OUPV recognised under STCW for foreign voyages?
MPT notes that the OUPV is a national licence only and has no STCW equivalent. Master and Mate licences can be issued as national credentials or with an STCW endorsement if you complete additional training and service. Crew who want to work on larger or foreign-flag yachts usually need the STCW route.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/captains-license-uscg-master-or-mate-100-ton-course-101

## Q: Can a six-pack captain carry 12 passengers in the US Virgin Islands?
MPT explains that US law lets certain uninspected charter boats in the USVI carry up to 12 passengers if the boat meets the UK MCA small vessel codes. An OUPV holder may operate those boats in those waters only. Any inspected vessel still requires a Master licence.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/course-details/captains-license-uscg-master-or-mate-100-ton-course-101

## Q: What does MPT say about the TWIC and background checks?
MPT says a TWIC is required and that the Coast Guard runs criminal record and National Driver Register checks, where some recent offences can disqualify you. Certain medical conditions or medications may also need a waiver.
Note: TWIC remains required for most applicants, but Policy Letter 11-15 exempts mariners who serve only on vessels without a security plan. Medical waivers follow the Merchant Mariner Medical Manual (COMDTINST M16721.48).
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/captains-license

## Q: My credential expired. How long do I have to renew?
MPT's FAQ says you can renew during a grace period of up to 12 months after the five-year expiry, but you cannot work on the credential during that time.
OUT OF DATE: Policy Letter 01-24 extended the administrative grace period from 1 year to 6 years. You still cannot sail on an expired credential.
Source: https://www.mptusa.com/faq
