# USCG knowledge library manifest

Built 2026-10-25 for the SeaTime Tracker 'Explain training pathways' assistant. Equivalent in structure to the MCA library in ../mca/.

- `official/`: US government documents (public domain, 17 U.S.C. 105). eCFR text came from the eCFR versioner API (point in time 2026-10-22, the latest available). NMC web pages, checklists, FAQs, forms, NVICs and policy letters came from dco.uscg.mil and media.defense.gov. PDFs were converted to text with pypdf; six scanned policy letters were converted with OCR (noted in their currency_note). Long PDFs keep `[p. N]` page markers. Each file's `currency_note` starts with CURRENT, EXPIRED or SUPERSEDED.
- `providers/`: paraphrased Q&A notes from training providers, unions, industry bodies, trade press and mariner forums (third-party copyright, no verbatim copying). Files built from second-hand material (forum posts, press, anecdotes, school claims) have a currency_note starting `UNOFFICIAL:` and say how reliable each item is. Where they disagree with official text they are marked `CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL` or `OUT OF DATE`, and the official text wins.

## Currency and supersession summary (as of 2026-10-25)

- 46 CFR Parts 10, 11, 12, 13, 15: eCFR current to 2026-10-22. Parts 11 and 12 include the passenger-ship training rule (91 FR 60846, effective 26 Oct 2026). Part 10 last changed by the Jan 2025 rules (electronic applications and Pay.gov fees, "Mariner Credentialing Program Transformation"; electronic course completion data, now not enforced before 25 Nov 2028).
- Policy letters that change how the CFR is applied today: CG-MMC PL 02-25 (recency: 3 months in the last 7 years for everyone, Sep 2025); PL 01-24 (renewal grace period 6 years instead of 1); PL 02-24 and 04-24 (reduced AB sea service and concurrent RFPNW/AB-Deck credit, written as temporary to 22 Dec 2026, but Pub. L. 119-60 of 18 Dec 2025 made the AB reductions permanent in 46 U.S.C. 7307 to 7309; the concurrent credit in PL 04-24 is policy and still shows the end date); PL 01-26 (violence and harassment training in STCW Basic Training from 1 Oct 2029).
- Navita, the online credentialing system, launched 14 Sep 2026. It replaced the ASAP upload portal and the IASKNMC email; paper applications are being phased out. Medical certificates are e-mailed since 1 Apr 2026.
- The 2026 government shutdown: credential extensions expired 31 Aug 2026; a 120-day relief letter applies to some pending renewals; latest processing estimate is about 4 months (21 Jul 2026).
- NVIC 01-26 (2 Jun 2026) cancelled NVIC 03-14 (course approval); NVIC 03-14 not stored. NVIC 01-24 superseded NVIC 04-14 (lifeboatman/PSC). NVIC 04-08 (medical) and NVIC 01-14 were superseded by the Merchant Mariner Medical Manual COMDTINST M16721.48 (Aug 2019); neither stored. NVIC 03-16 is at Change 7 (Sep 2024), though the NMC MMC Evaluation page still links Change 6.
- Expired but stored for history: CG-MMC PL 03-20 (COVID renewals). Partly out of date but stored: Marine Safety Manual Vol III (CH-2, 2017), NMC military recency sheet (2019), NMC guidance documents index (2019).
- Marine Safety Manual Vol III: Part A (credentialing), B2 section R (oceangoing yachts), B5 (working conditions), B7 (uninspected vessels) and Part C stored; B1 to B4 (other manning scales) and B6 (automated vessels) left out as not crew-facing.
- Not stored in full: the 1,047-page weekly approved course list (courses.pdf); only the MODU, fishing and Rivers entries are extracted. nmc-approved-course-list-how-to-check.md explains its layout with page 1 of the 30 Sep 2026 edition; the courses FAQ and NVIC 01-26 explain how to use it. Search the latest list at https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/courses/courses.pdf .
- Proposed, not yet law: NPRM 91 FR 62383 (1 Oct 2026) would remove LMS (STCW Master under 500 GT near-coastal), BRM and LTW (STCW OICNW under 500 GT near-coastal), ETSO (OICEW and DDE at 750 kW or more) and CSM and HVPS (ETR). Comments close 30 Dec 2026.
- Exam calculators: the NMC policy of 3 Dec 2024 allows programmable calculators at RECs if cleared in front of the examiner, replacing the Aug 2023 TI-30XIIS-only rule; the Jan 2026 REC/MU FAQ still says non-programmable.
- Third pass (2026-10-25, unofficial and advisory sources): an extract of every MODU, fishing and Rivers entry in the 30 Sep 2026 approved course list is now stored (nmc-approved-courses-rivers-fishing-modu-extract-2026.md); the full list still is not. 46 U.S.C. 4502(g) fishing operator training: law since 2010, no implementing rule (2016 NPRM left it out; no final rule by 25 Oct 2026), but three courses are NMC-accepted for parts of it. GAO-23-106629 recommendations on measuring overall processing time were still open in June 2026 (target Sept 2028 and March 2029). No official processing figures after the Navita launch were published by 25 Oct 2026; mariner reports are in providers/navita-processing-reports-after-launch-2026.md and are UNOFFICIAL. Advisory items are marked "CURRENT (advisory, not binding)"; second-hand items are marked "UNOFFICIAL:".
- Second pass (2026-10-25): several dco.uscg.mil and news.uscg.mil files returned HTTP 403 and were taken from web.archive.org captures, noted in each currency_note.
