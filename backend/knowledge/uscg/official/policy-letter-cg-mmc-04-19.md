  c.  National Endorsement. To be eligible for a limited STCW endorsement as OICNW,
  mariners must hold any national officer endorsement other than Operator of Uninspected
  Passenger Vessels (OUPV) that authorizes service as Mate on near coastal routes. For an
  endorsement as Master, mariners must hold any national officer endorsement, other than
  OUPV, authorizing service as Master valid for near coastal routes.

  d.  Service.

  1)  For OICNW, applicants must provide evidence of either:

  i)  Twenty-four months of service in the deck department on vessels operating on
  oceans, near-coastal waters and/or the Great Lakes. Service on inland waters,
  bays, or sounds that are navigable waters of the United States may be substituted
  for up to 50 percent of the required service. Experience gained in the engine
  department may be creditable for up to 3 months of the required service (46 CFR
  11.321(a)(1)(i)); or

  ii)  Successful completion of an approved OICNW training program that includes 12
  months of seagoing service (46 CFR 11.321(a)(1)(ii) and (iii)).

  2)  For Master, applicants must provide evidence of meeting the service for an STCW
  endorsement as OICNW above, and an additional 12 months of service as mate or
  other officer in charge of a navigational watch on vessels operating in oceans, near-
  coastal waters, and/or Great Lakes. Service on inland waters, bays, or sounds that are
  navigable waters of the United States may be substituted for up to 50 percent of the
  total required service. Experience gained in the engine department on vessels may be
  creditable for up to 1 month of the service requirement (46 CFR 11.317(a)(1).

  It is not necessary to have held an STCW endorsement as OICNW at the time service
  as the officer in charge of a navigational watch was obtained.

  3)  For the purposes of crediting sea service, a day will be as specified in 46 CFR
  10.232(h)(3) and should be at least 4 hours. A creditable day of service may include
  time spent on vessel maintenance and repair, time preparing for and securing from a
  voyage, and may include more than one voyage in a single day.

  e.  Training. Evidence of successful completion of the following training must be submitted
  to qualify for a limited endorsement as OICNW or Master:

  1)  Basic Training (BT) [46 CFR 11.302(a)]. This training may be tailored to vessels of
  less than 500 GT (200 GRT) on near-coastal voyages and the BT will be limited to
  vessels of less than 500 GT (200 GRT). If BT was completed more than 5 years

[p. 3]

  before the date of application, the applicant must provide evidence of maintaining the
  standard of competence as specified in 46 CFR 11.302(b);

  2)  Medical First Aid Provider, if not completed previously [46 CFR 11.317(a)(3)(i) and
  11.321(a)(3)(i)];

  3)  Proficiency in Survival Craft (PSC) Limited, if not completed previously [46 CFR
  11.317(a)(3)(iii) and 11.321(a)(3)(iii)]. This training may be tailored to vessels of less
  than 500 GT (200 GRT) that do not carry davit launched survival or rescue craft and
  the PSC endorsement will be limited to vessels of less than 500 GT (200 GRT) that
  do not carry davit launched survival or rescue craft;

  4)  Electronic Chart Display Information Systems (ECDIS), for the endorsement to be
  valid on vessels with this equipment [46 CFR 11.317(a)(3)(v) and 11.321(a)(3)(v)];

  5)  Radar Observer, for the endorsement to be valid on vessels with this equipment [46
  CFR 11.317(a)(3)(vi) and 11.321(a)(3)(vi)]; and;

  6)  Automatic Radar Plotting Aids (ARPA), for the endorsement to be valid on vessels
  with this equipment [46 CFR 11.317(a)(3)(vii) and 11.321(a)(3)(vii)].

  Approved training for Advanced Firefighting and Leadership and Managerial Skills is not
  required for these limited endorsements.

  f.  Assessments of Competence. To qualify for a limited endorsement as OICNW or Master,
  mariners should submit evidence of successful completion of the assessment tasks in

## Enclosure (2) of Navigation and Vessel Inspection Circular (NVIC) 13-14 (or a Coast

  Guard approved alternative), with the exception of the following tasks that do not need to
  be completed:

  Task No.  Task Description
  1.5.A  Navigate in a VTS
  1.10.B  Set auto pilot weather controls
  1.10.C  Change from auto pilot to hand steering
  1.10.D  Change from hand steering to auto pilot
  2.4.A  Comply with VTS procedures
  6.1.A  Cargo handling, stowage, and securing
  6.1.B  Carriage of dangerous cargo

  Tasks noted in NVIC 13-14 as only being required for Master do not need to be
  completed to qualify for endorsement as OICNW. Mariners seeking a raise in grade from
  OICNW to Master with the same limitation need only submit the assessment tasks noted
  as being only for Master. Mariners seeking an endorsement as Master who have not held
  an STCW endorsement as OICNW must submit evidence of completion of all assessment

[p. 4]

  tasks for both OICNW and Master described in NVIC 13-14, except those noted above as
  not required for these limited endorsements.

  The assessments from NVIC 13-14 may be tailored to the equipment and practices
  aboard the vessel(s) on which the endorsement will be valid, provided that approval for
  alternative assessments is received from the National Maritime Center prior to their use.
  Assessments may also be embedded in Coast Guard approved training for these
  endorsements. Guidance on Coast Guard approved training may be found in NVIC 3-14,
  Guidelines for Approval of Training Courses and Programs. This NVIC and others are
  available at https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/NVIC/.

  g.  Renewal of Endorsements. To renew this limited endorsement, mariners should meet the
  applicable requirements in 46 CFR 10.227 to renew their national endorsement(s) and
  provide evidence of maintaining the standard of competence for Basic Training as
  specified in 46 CFR 11.302(b). Mariners may meet this requirement with courses that
  were approved for vessels of less than 200 GRT or 500 GT on near-coastal voyages and
  have the BT endorsement limited to vessels of less than 500 GT (200 GRT).

  h.  Removal of Limitation.

  1)  To remove the limitation to vessels on voyages from the port of San Diego,
  California, and to qualify for an STCW endorsement as Master of Vessels of Less
  Than 500 GT Limited to Near-Coastal Waters, mariners should meet all requirements
  specified in 46 CFR 11.317 that were not met previously, including, but not limited to
  completion of the assessments noted in paragraph 4.f, above, and approved training
  for Advanced Firefighting.

  2)  If the mariner had qualified for this limited endorsement by completing Basic
  Training that was limited to vessels of less than 200 GRT or 500 GT, and is applying
  for an endorsement valid for vessels of 500 GT or more, the mariner must provide
  evidence of having completed approved original BT that was not limited. If this
  training was completed more than 5 years before the date of application, the applicant
  must provide evidence of maintaining the standard of competence as specified in 46
  CFR 11.302(b).
