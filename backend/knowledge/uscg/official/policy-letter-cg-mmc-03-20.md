### 5. DISCUSSION.

  a.  The extension of the MMC expiration described in MSIB 08-20 Change 3 was intended to
  allow mariners to continue to work in their current capacities until they are able to meet all
  of the requirements for renewal of their credential.

  b.  Although the expiration of MMCs has been extended, the Coast Guard has become aware
  that some vessel owners and operators will not engage a mariner if the expiration date
  printed in their MMC will occur during a voyage.

  c.  The Coast Guard recognizes that some STCW courses, including BT and AFF
  “revalidation” courses, may not be available for the near future. The Coast Guard also
  recognizes that some mariners may have been planning to take courses to renew their
  STCW endorsements at the time courses became unavailable. In order to allow mariners to
  renew their MMC, the following temporary guidance will apply until July 1, 2021. This
  guidance is applicable for renewals of MMCs that have an expiration date between January
  1, 2020, and June 30, 2021, and for mariners who have at least one year of relevant service
  during the previous five years. It does not apply to MMC renewals when the mariner does
  not have at least one year of relevant service during the previous five years.

### 6. ACTION.

  a.  For applications for renewal of MMCs expiring from March 1, 2020 to June 30, 2021, that
  are received by the Coast Guard before July 1, 2021, the Coast Guard will accept one year
  (360 days) of relevant service within the past five years as meeting the requirements for
  continued competence for all of the components of PSC, FRB, PSC-Ltd, BT, and AFF. All

[p. 3]

  mariners currently holding endorsements as PSC, PSC-Ltd, FRB, BT, and/or AFF will be
  considered for this waiver.  The endorsements combined with the relevant service will be
  considered as having demonstrated continuing competence for all of the PSC, PSC-Ltd,
  FRB, BT and/or AFF components required to renew their STCW endorsements. Relevant
  service will be determined as it is described in NVICs 04-14 (PSC and PSC-Ltd), 05-14
  (FRB), 08-14 (BT), and 09-14 (AFF).

  b.  Mariners who do not have at least one year (360 days) of relevant service in the past five
  years can only renew their STCW endorsements by demonstrating continued competence
  for PSC, PSC-Ltd, FRB, BT, and/or AFF as specified in 46 CFR 12.613(b)(4) or 46 CFR
  12.615(b)(4) and NVIC 04-14 (for PSC and PSC-Ltd); 46 CFR 12.617(b)(4) and NVIC 05-
  15 (for FRB); 46 CFR 11.302(e), 12.602(e), and NVIC 08-14 (for BT); and/or 46 CFR
  11.303(e) and NVIC 09-14 (for AFF). These mariners will need to complete original or
  “refresher” training for PSC, PSC-Ltd, FRB, BT, and/or AFF, as appropriate. Shorter and
  less comprehensive “revalidation” courses are not acceptable.

  c.  NMC will issue the credential and/or endorsements in the following manner:

  i.  Applicants requesting raise of grade, new endorsement, or modification of
  endorsements on an extended MMC, will be issued a new MMC with an expiration
  date of March 31, 2021.

  ii.  Applicants who request renewal and meet all renewal requirements will be issued a
  new MMC with an expiration date five years from the date of issuance in
  accordance with 46 CFR 10.205(a).

  iii.  Applicants renewing MMCs where all renewal requirements for specific
  endorsements are not met will result in those endorsements becoming invalid in
  accordance with 46 CFR 10.205(d) and those endorsements will not be retained on
  the renewed MMC.  These applicants may request continuity for specific national
  endorsements in their application or may request continuity after receiving an
  Awaiting Information letter from the NMC.

  d.  The guidance in this policy letter will expire on July 1, 2021.
