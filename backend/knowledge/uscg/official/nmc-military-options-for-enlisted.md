# NMC: Options for enlisted personnel

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/professional_qualifications/enlisted_personnel.pdf

> Military enlisted pathways to MMC endorsements.


[p. 1]

National Maritime Center
Providing Credentials to Mariners

  Enlisted Personnel

•  The Marine Safety Manual, Volume III, Chapter 2 contains a table (Table 2-1, see
  Attachment A, Suggested Acceptance of Military Sea Service by Rating for License
  Qualifications) with all military ratings.  The table shows which ratings may be used
  towards deck, engineer, or staff officer endorsements.

•  Notes at the  end of the table may be applicable to your position.  If so, ensure additional
  information is not required, such as a Professional Qualification Standard or
  documentation of engine room Watchstanding for Hull Technicians.

•  Due to the great variety of specialized duties that enlisted personnel perform, the following
  guidelines are provided for sea service evaluation:

  o  Service as a seaman apprentice  (SA) or seaman (SN)  is equivalent to sea service as an
  ordinary seaman or deckhand.

  o  Service as fireman apprentice  (FA) or fireman (FN) is equivalent to sea service as a
  wiper or coal passer.

  o  Service as a petty officer (E-4 and above) in the deck department is considered
  equivalent to that of an able -bodied seaman.

  o  Service as a petty officer (E-4 and above) in the engineering department is considered
  equivalent to that of a qualified member of the engine department.

  o  Deck rating of E-4 and above with qualifications as deck watch officer-underway is
  considered equivalent to licensed mate time.

  o  Service experience obtained as a junior officer of the deck is considered equivalent to
  able seaman time.

  o  Engine rating of E-  4 and above with qualifications as engineering watch officer -
  underway is considered equivalent to licensed assistant engineer time.
