# NMC FAQ: Contacting the NMC

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/contact_faq.pdf

> National Maritime Center frequently asked questions on contacting the nmc.

  Frequently Asked Questions:  Contact

### 1. I need to send information to my e valuator. Where do I send it?

When sending information to National Maritime Center ( NMC),  you must submit all
information via our online application portal, except applications for First Class Pilot and Local
Limited applications, which will continue to be sent to the respective Regional Exam Centers
(RECs).

•  If the information is related to Courses/Programs/Instructors/Proctors/
  Assessors, Designated Examiners,  or Qualified Assessors, please send it to the
  attention of the Training & Assessment division.  It can be sent via e-mail or mail:
  o  E-mail - NMCCourses@uscg.mil
  o  Mail - Commanding Officer (NMC-21)
  USCG National Maritime Center
  100 Forbes Drive
  Martinsburg, WV 25404

### 2. How do I obtain or submit my open book renewal examination?

As of May 1, 2020, open book renewal examinations are no longer sent from the Regional
Exam Center (REC)  or Monitoring Unit (MU).  Now, open book renewal examinations are
issued from the NMC.

To obtain:  First, you must receive an Approval to Test (ATT) letter detailing the renewal
modules required.  ATT letters will:  (1) direct you to pay your exam fee at pay.gov  , and then
(2) send an e-mail directly to NMCRenewalExams@uscg.mil  .

To submit a completed renewal examination:  If you obtained a renewal examination
electronically, instructions were on the electronic answer sheet.  If you obtained a paper
renewal examination by mail, you can mail back to:

•  Commanding Officer (NMC-22)
  USCG National Maritime Center
  100 Forbes Drive
  Martinsburg, WV 25404

[p. 2]

### 3. I need to send information to my local REC. Where do I send it?

When sending information to your local REC, please ensure you include your name and
mariner reference number.  To determine which REC to send your information to, visit the
REC webpage.  Once there, select the desired REC.  This will give you their contact
information.

### 4. I need copies of information that is contained in my file. How do I get them?

To receive copies of information contained in your file, you will need to submit  a request for
your records.  The request should include the following information:

•  A detailed statement of what you need

•  Mariner name

•  Current contact information (address and phone number)

•  Social Security number or Mariner Reference Number

•  Date of birth

•  Signature

•  Date.

The request must be sent electronically via the DHS Online Porta  l.  Please refer to the Federal
Register Doc. 2025 -23783.pdf for more information.

5.  I need to get a DD-214 for time served during World War II (  WWII).  What do I need to
  do?

In order to receive  a DD-214 for time served during WWII, the individual who served must
meet the eligibility requirements listed in Public Laws 95-  202 or 105-368, and meet the
eligibility criteria.  If these requirements are met, a request to receive the DD-214 will need to
be submitted.  To request this, the requestor should use DD Form 2168 .

The requestor can  also submit a handwritten request that includes the following information:

•  A detailed statement of what is needed

•  Name

•  Current contact information (address and phone number)

[p. 3]

•  Social Security number

•  Date of birth

•  Signature

•  Date.

If the request  is from someone other than the individual, it must include one of the following:

•  A release from the individual giving consent for the request

•  Power of Attorney

•  Death Certificate

•  Letter from the individual’s doctor saying the individual  is incapacitated.

The request can  be sent via e -mail or mail:

•  E-mail  - OSC-SMB-NMC-  4-  Correspondence@uscg.mil

•  Mail - USCG National Maritime Center
  Attention:  Records Management
  100 Forbes Drive
  Martinsburg, WV 25404

### 6. How do I contact the NMC?

If you have a question  and would like to speak with one of our highly trained Customer Service
Representatives, contact our Customer Service Center  .
