1.  PURPOSE. This policy letter provides guidance for qualifying for STCW endorsements
  limited to vessels of less than 500 GT for operations on near-coastal voyages in the vicinity
  of the Virgin Islands.

2.  ACTION. The Coast Guard will use this policy to evaluate applications for STCW
  endorsements as Officer  in Charge of a Navigational Watch (OICNW) and Master of Vessels
  of Less Than 500 GT that will be limited to near-coastal waters in the Virgin Islands.

3.  DIRECTIVES AFFECTED. CG  -MMC Policy Letter 03-18 is cancelled.

4.  BACKGROUND. On June 26, 2018, the Coast Guard published CG-  MMC Policy Letter
  03-18, STCW Endorsements as Master of Vessels of Less Than 500 GT Limited to Near
  Coastal Waters in the Virgin Islands. That policy letter provided guidance for mariners to
  qualify for endorsements as Master that are limited to near-coastal voyages on vessels
  carrying not more than 12 passengers operating between the United States Virgin Islands and
  British Virgin Islands, not to exceed four hours and 12 miles from shore. This policy letter
  retains the guidance in CG-MMC Policy Letter 03-18, expands it to include guidance for
  endorsements as OICNW, and provides new guidance for qualifying for endorsements for
  voyages of greater  duration and distance from shore.

### 5. DISCUSSION.

  a.  As specified in 46 CFR 11.301(f), 11.317(c), and 11.321(c)  , the Coast Guard may exempt
  mariners from meeting any individual knowledge, understanding and proficiency (KUP)
  specified in Table A-II/3 of the STCW Code. We have determined that a number of the
  KUPs in STCW Code Table A-II/3 are not applicable to certain vessels operating on
  near-coastal international voyages in the vicinity of the USVI . Accordingly, we will not
  require mariners to submit training and assessments associated with these KUPs for
  endorsements limited to vessels  of less than 500 GT operating in the Virgin Islands.

## Enclosure (1) to CG-MMC Policy Letter 05-23

#### QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS FOR OFFICER IN CHARGE OF A

  NAVIGATIONAL WATCH AND MASTER ON VESSELS OF LESS THAN 500 GT
  LIMITED TO NEAR-  COASTAL VOYAGES NOT TO EXCEED FOUR HOURS AND
  12 MILES FROM SHORE

1.  SCOPE OF ENDORSEMENT. The STCW endorsement will be for a capacity as Officer  in
  Charge of a Navigational Watch (OICNW) or Master under 46 CFR 11.321 (for OICNW) or
  46 CFR 11.317 (for Master) and Section A-II/3 of the STCW Code and include the following
  limitations:

  Not Valid on Vessels 500 Gross Tons (GT) or More. Limited to Near-Coastal
  Voyages on Vessels Carrying Not More Than 12 Passengers Operating Between
  the United States Virgin Islands and British Virgin Islands, Not to Exceed Four
  Hours and 12 Miles from Shore.

2.  NATIONAL ENDORSEMENT. To be eligible for a limited STCW endorse  ment as OICNW,
  mariners must hold any national officer endorsement other than Operator of Uninspected
  Passenger Vessels (OUPV) that authorizes service as Mate on near coastal routes. For an
  endorsement as Master, mariners must hold any national officer endorsement, other than
  OUPV, authorizing service as Master valid for near coastal routes.

### 3. SERVICE.

  a.  For OICNW, applicants must provide either:

  1)  Twenty-four months of service in the deck department on vessels operating on
  oceans, near-coastal waters and/or the Great Lakes. Service on inland waters, bays, or
  sounds that are navigable waters of the United States may be substituted for up to 50
  percent of the required service. Experience gained in the engine department may be
  creditable for up to 3 months of the required service (46 CFR 11.321(a)(1)(i)); or

  2)  Successful completion of an approved OICNW training program that includes 12
  months of seagoing service (46 CFR 11.321(a)(1)(ii) and (iii)).

  b.  For Master, applicants must provide evidence of meeting the service for an STCW
  endorsement as OICNW above, and an additional 12 months of service as mate or other
  officer in charge of a navigational watch on vessels operating in oceans, near-coastal
  waters, and/or Great Lakes. Service on inland waters, bays, or sounds that are navigable
  waters of the United States may be substituted for up to 50 percent of the total required
  service. Experience gained in the engine department on vessels may be creditable for up
  to 1 month of the service requirement (46 CFR 11.317(a)(1).

  c.  It is not necessary to have held an STCW endorsement as OICNW at the time service as
  the officer in charge of a navigational watch was obtained.

  d.  For purposes of crediting sea service, a day will be as specified in 46 CFR 10.232(h)(3)
  and should be at least 4 hours. A creditable day of service may include time spent on
  vessel maintenance and repair, and time preparing for and securing from a voyage, and
  may include more than one voyage in a single day.

4.  TRAINING. Evidence of successful completion of the following training must be submitted
  to qualify for this limited endorsement:

  a.  Basic Training. This training may be tailored to vessels of less than 500 GT (200 GRT)
  on near-coastal voyages. If basic training was completed more than 5 years before the
  date of application, the applicant must provide evidence of maintaining the standard of
  competence as specified in 46 CFR 11.302(b);

  b.  Medical First Aid Provider, if not completed previously [46 CFR 11.317(a)(3)(i)];

  c.  Electronic Chart Display Information Systems (ECDIS), for the endorsement to be valid
  on vessels with this equipment [46 CFR 11.317(a)(3)(v)];

  d.  Radar Observer, for the endorsement to be valid on vessels with this equipment [46 CFR
  11.317(a)(3)(vi)]; and

  e.  Automatic Radar Plotting Aids (ARPA), for the endorsement to be valid on vessels with
  this equipment [46 CFR 11.317(a)(3)(vii)].

  tasks for both OICNW and Master.

  d.  Assessments must be signed by a qualified assessor approved by the Coast Guard in

  specific endorsement. Until June 30, 2024, the Coast Guard will accept assessments for
  these endorsements signed before January 1, 2024, by mariners who have at least 1 year
  of experience on seagoing vessels of at  least 50 GRT. To assess candidates for Mate, this
  experience should be as Mate or Master, and to assess candidates for Master, the
  experience should be as Master. Af ter December 31, 2023, QAs must be approved by the

6.  RENEWAL OF ENDORSEMENT . To renew this limited endorsement, mariners should
  meet the applicable requirements in 46 CFR 10.227 to renew their national endorsement(s)
  and provide evidence of maintaining the standard of competence for Basic Training as
  specified in 46 CFR 11.302(b). Mariners may meet this requirement with BT courses that
  were approved for vessels of less than 200 GRT or 500 GT on near-coastal voyages.

### 7. REMOVAL OF LIMITATION.

  a.  Regulation II/3, paragraph 2 of the STCW Convention specifies that masters  on seag oing
  vessels of less than 500 GT that are not engaged on near coastal voyages must meet the
  competency standards for vessels of 500 GT or more.

  b.  To remove the limitation to vessels carrying not more than 12 passengers on voyages in
  the Virgin Islands not to  exceed four hours and 12 miles from shore and to qualify for an
  STCW endorsement as OICNW or Master of Vessels of Less Than 500 GT Limited to
  Near-Coastal Waters, mariners should meet all requirements specified in 46 CFR 11.321
  (for OICNW) or 46 CFR 11.317 (for Master) that were not met previously, including, but
  not limited to completion of the assessments noted in paragraph 5.a, above, and approved
  training for Advanced Firefighting and Proficiency in Survival Craft or Proficiency in
  Survival Craft-Limited  .

  c.  If the mariner had qualified for this limited endorsement by completing Basic Training
  that was limited to vessels of less than 500 GT (200 GRT), the mariner must provide
  evidence of having completed BT that was not limited. If this training was completed
  more than 5 years before the date of application, the applicant must provide evidence of
  maintaining the standard of competence as specified in 46 CFR 11.302(b).
