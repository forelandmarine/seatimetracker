# NMC: Options for military officers

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/professional_qualifications/officers.pdf

> Military officer pathways to MMC officer endorsements.


National Maritime Center
Serving Our Nation  ’s Mariners

  Officers

•  Underway deck watch officers’ (DWO) and engineering watch officers’ (EWO) duties are
  considered equivalent to the watchstanding duties performed by licensed mates and
  engineers, respectively, aboard merchant vessels.  Therefore, this qualifying sea service
  (when accompanied by a designation letter) may be used to satisfy the experience
  requirements for an original or raise of grade of a master, mate, or assistant engineer
  license.

•  Service experience obtained as a junior officer of the deck, when accompanied by a
  designation letter, is considered equivalent to able-  bodied seaman time.

•  Graduates of the United States Coast Guard Academy or United States Naval Academy may
  qualify for 3rd mate or 3rd assistant engineer, respectively, with the qualification as
  underway officer of a navigational or engineer watch.

•  Executive officers (XO) and commanding officers (CO):  Since the XO and CO do not
  receive Professional Qualification Standards letters, a copy of the orders may be submitted
  in lieu of designation letters.

•  Applying for upper level ratings requires a mariner to meet the requirements of each lower
  level license, in order of progression.  This means the military sea service will be evaluated
  sequentially over the course of a military career, to simulate the progression for a merchant
  mariner.  Tonnage and horsepower limitations, if any, shall be calculated for each license
  level through the progression.  For example:  If the application is for an  officer’s
  endorsement as master, any gross tons, oceans.  The applicant must first qualify for a 3rd
  mate, then a 2nd m  ate, and then a c  hief mate.

•  Often, service stated in a History of Assignments, Transcript of Sea Service, or Abstract of
  Operations report for military officers lists the rank but not the capacity (duties/ jobs) of
  the officer.  Supporting information must be given to verify the capacity of the officer
  showing deck or engine room attachment.
