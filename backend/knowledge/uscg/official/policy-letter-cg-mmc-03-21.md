1.  PURPOSE. This policy letter provides guidance for  the issuance of a national officer
  endorsement for master of self-  propelled vessels of less than 100 GRT for mariners who hold
  national endorsements as mate of inspected self  -propelled vessels of 200 GRT or more  .

2.  ACTION. The Coast Guard will use this policy  to  evaluate whether mariners may be issued
  an endorsement for master of self-propelled vessels of less than 100 GRT.

3.  DIRECTIVES AFFECTED. None  .

### 4. BACKGROUND.

  a.  As is specified in 46 CFR 15.901(a), an individual holding a merchant mariner credential
  (MMC) endorsed as mate or pilot of inspected self -propelled vessels of 200 gross
  registered tons (GRT) or more  is authorized to serve as master on inspected self -
  propelled vessels of less than 100 GRT  within any restrictions on the MMC , without
  further endorsement.

  b.  The Coast Guard is aware that there is confusion amongst mariners and the maritime
  industry concerning the authority of a mariner holding a national endorsement for mate
  authorizing service on inspected self  -propelled vessels of 200 GRT or more to serve as a
  master on a vessel of less than 100 GRT.  In accordance with this policy, the Coast Guard
  will issue endorsements as master of self-propelled vessels of le  ss than 100 GRT to
  mariners holding an endorsement that authorizes service as a mate on an inspected vessel
  of 200 GRT or more.

### 5. DISCUSSION.

  a.  Mariners holding a nMMC endorsement that authorizes service as mate on inspected self  -
  propelled vessels  of 200 GRT  or more , are not required to have the endorsement as master
  in their MMC in order to serve as master on an inspected vessel of less than 100 GRT.
  With this policy t  he Coast Guard  may  issue a national MMC endorsement as m  aster of s elf-
  propelled vessels of l  ess t  han 100 GRT if they holdany of the following national
  endorsements:

  1)  Chief Mate of Self-Propelled Vessels of Unlimited Tonnage;
  2)  Second Mate of Self-Propelled Vessels of Unlimited Tonnage;
  3)  Third Mate of Self-Propelled Vessels of Unlimited Tonnage;
  4)  Mate of Self-Propelled Vessels of Less Than 1,600 GRT;
  5)  Mate of Self-Propelled Vessels of Less Than 500 GRT;

  6)  Chief Mate (OSV);

  7)  Mate (OSV); and

  8)  Mate (Pilot) of Towing Vessels.

  b.  Mariners seeking to add the master less than 100 GRT  endorsement to their MMC, must
  submit an application to the Coast Guard to add the endorsement to their credential .

  c.  Mariners holding one of the endorsements listed in paragraph 5.a above authorizing
  service on either near-coastal waters or oceans will be issued an endorsement as Master
  of Near  -Coastal Self-Propelled Vessels of Less Than 100 GRT. Mariners holding one of
  the endorsements listed in paragraph 5.a above for inland waters or for Great Lakes and
  inland waters will be issued a master less than 100 GRT endorsement with the same route
  as their mate endorsement.

  d.  An examination is not required to add the endorsement for Master of Self-Propelled
  Vessels of Less Than 100 GRT for the same route as the mariner’s endorsement for mate.

  e.  Mariners holding endorsements as Chief Mate (OSV) and Mate (OSV) will be issued
  endorsements as master of less than 100 GRT that are not restricted to offshore supply
  vessels. Mariners holding endorsements as Mate (Pilot) of Towing Vessels will be issued
  endorsements as master of less than 100 GRT that are not restricted to towing vessels.

  f.  All restrictions on the mariner’s mate endorsement  , other than those discussed above,will
  also apply to the endorsement for master of  less than 100 GRT. For example, if a mariner
  holds an endorsement as Mate of Inland Self-Propelled Vessels of Less Than 500 GRT
