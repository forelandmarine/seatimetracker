Subj:  QUALIFICATION FOR NATIONAL ENDORSEMENTS AS TANKERMAN-
  ASSISTANT AND STCW ENDORSEMENTS FOR BASIC TANKER OPERATIONS

1.  PURPOSE. This policy letter provides guidance for mariners holding or qualifying for
  national endorsements as Tankerman-PIC (Barge) to qualify for an endorsement as
  Tankerman-Assistant. This policy letter also provides guidance for mariners holding an
  STCW endorsement for advanced oil tanker operations, advanced chemical tanker
  operations, or advanced liquefied gas tanker operations with a limitation to non-self-
  propelled vessels to qualify for an endorsement for basic oil and chemical or basic liquefied
  gas tanker operations without a limitation to non-self-propelled vessels.

2.  ACTION. The Coast Guard will use this policy to evaluate whether mariners are qualified to
  hold national endorsements as Tankerman-Assistant and STCW endorsements for basic oil
  and chemical tanker operations or basic liquefied gas tanker operations.

3.  DIRECTIVES AFFECTED. None.

4.  BACKGROUND. At its Fall 2022 meeting, the National Merchant Marine Personnel
  Advisory Committee (NMERPAC) recommended changes to how mariners may qualify for
  endorsements as Tankerman-Assistant to increase the ability of mariners to obtain
  employment on tank ships and to advance their careers. NMERPAC noted that otherwise
  qualified mariners who are authorized to serve as the person in charge of transfers of
  dangerous liquids or liquefied gases aboard tank barges are not authorized to assist with
  cargo transfers aboard tank ships.

### 5. DISCUSSION.

  National Endorsements

  a.  The Coast Guard has determined that the training required to qualify for a national
  endorsement as Tankerman-PIC (Barge) meets the training required for an endorsement as
  Tankerman-Assistant for the same cargoes. The course content specified in 46 CFR 13.121

[p. 2]

  for Tankerman-PIC (Barge) is more comprehensive than that required for an endorsement
  as Tankerman-Assistant valid for the same cargoes.

  b.  A mariner who applies for and is issued an endorsement for Tankerman-PIC (Barge) will
  also be issued an endorsement for Tankerman-Assistant valid for the same cargoes without
  meeting additional requirements. The mariner is not required to add the Tankerman-
  Assistant endorsement on their application for the Tankerman-PIC (Barge) endorsement.

  c.  Mariners who already hold a Tankerman-PIC (Barge) endorsement who wish to add an
  endorsement as Tankerman-Assistant for the same cargoes should submit a CG-719B,
  Application for a Merchant Mariner Credential, to the National Maritime Center (NMC) to
  add the endorsement. Alternatively, the Tankerman-Assistant endorsement will be added to
  their MMC at the next MMC renewal if they meet the requirements to renew the
  Tankerman-PIC (Barge) endorsement.

  STCW Endorsements

  d.  Mariners meeting the requirements for an STCW endorsement for Advanced Oil Tanker
  Operations or Advanced Chemical Tanker Operations with a limitation to non-self-
  propelled vessels may also be issued an endorsement for Basic Oil and Chemical Tanker
  Operations without a limitation to non-self-propelled vessels. The mariner is not required to
  include the basic endorsement on their application for the advanced endorsement.

  e.  Mariners meeting the requirements for an STCW endorsement for Advanced Liquefied Gas
  Tanker Operations with a limitation to non-self-propelled vessels may also be issued an
  endorsement for Basic Liquefied Gas Tanker Operations without a limitation to non-self-
  propelled vessels. The mariner is not required to include the basic endorsement on their
  application for the advanced endorsement.

  Renewal of Endorsements

  f.  Mariners who hold national endorsements for both Tankerman-PIC (Barge) and
  Tankerman-Assistant will meet the requirements for renewal of their Tankerman-Assistant
  endorsement if they meet the requirements for renewal of a Tankerman-PIC (Barge)
  endorsement for the same cargoes.

  g.  Mariners who meet the requirements to renew their endorsement as Tankerman-PIC
  (Barge) will have an endorsement as Tankerman-Assistant for the same cargoes added to
  their MMC at the time they renew their Merchant Mariner Credential (MMC). The mariner
  is not required to add the Tankerman-Assistant endorsement on their application for
  renewal of the Tankerman-PIC (Barge) endorsement.

  h.  Mariners who hold STCW endorsements for both Basic Oil and Chemical Tanker
  Operations and Advanced Oil Tanker Operations and/or Advanced Chemical Tanker
  Operations with a limitation to non-self-propelled vessels will meet the requirements for

[p. 3]

  renewal of their basic endorsement if they meet the requirements for renewal of their
  advanced endorsement.

  i.  Mariners who hold STCW endorsements for both Basic Liquefied Tanker Operations and
  Advanced Liquefied Gas Tanker Operations with a limitation to non-self-propelled vessels
  will meet the requirements for renewal of their basic endorsement if they meet the
  requirements for renewal of their advanced endorsement.

  j.  Mariners who meet the requirements to renew their STCW endorsement for advanced
  tanker operations with a limitation to non-self-propelled vessels will have an endorsement
  for basic tanker operations for the same cargoes without a limitation to non-self-propelled
  vessels added to their MMC at the time they renew their Merchant Mariner Credential
  (MMC). The mariner is not required to add the basic tanker operations endorsement on
  their application to renew their advanced endorsement.

  Service as Tankerman-Assistant

  a.  On vessels that are not subject to STCW, and those on a domestic voyage, mariners
  holding an endorsement Tankerman-PIC (Barge) that do not have a Tankerman-
  Assistant endorsement in their MMC may serve as Tankerman-Assistant for the same
  cargoes as their Tankerman-PIC (Barge) endorsement if they also carry a copy of this
  policy letter.

  b.  On international voyages on vessels subject to STCW, mariners will need to have their
  MMCs contain both a national endorsement for Tankerman-Assistant, and an STCW
  endorsement for basic tanker operations appropriate for the cargo of the vessel.

  Course Approval

  k.  The National Maritime Center (NMC) will include in the approval of Tank Barge
  Dangerous Liquids and Tank Barge Liquefied Gases courses that the course also meet the
  training requirements of 46 CFR 13.401(e)(1) for a Tankerman-Assistant endorsement for
  the same cargoes. Existing approved courses will have this added to the course approval at
  the next course renewal.

  of DHS Directive Instruction Manual 023-01, Rev. 1. Our rationale for selecting these
  CEs is as follows. “Promulgation of rules, issuance of rulings or interpretations, and the
  development and publication of policies … and other guidance … that implement,
  without substantive change, statutory or regulatory requirements” or “…that interpret or

  Coast Guard CE #L5 applies. In addition, the policy letter includes elements that pertain
  to training, qualifying, licensing and disciplining maritime personnel. These latter aspects
