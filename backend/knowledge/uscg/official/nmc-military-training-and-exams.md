# NMC: Training and examinations (military to mariner)

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/professional_qualifications/training_and_exams.pdf

> Using military training toward MMC requirements.


National Maritime Center
Keep ’em Safe, Keep ’  em Sailing

  Training and Examinations

•  Military training and courses may qualify toward the evaluation of various ratings and
  officers endorsement.  Training and courses must be approved by the National Maritime
  Center (NMC) and applicable to the endorsement requested.  These courses will only be
  credited within the parameters of the existing approval and may cover any combination of
  experience, training, assessments, or examinations.

•  For additional information, visit the NMC  Courses & DE/QA page and the NMC
  Examinations page.
