Per CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-20 and 46 CFR 10.229,a request for a duplicate credential must include an
affidavit. Typically,  affidavits are sworn statements and may require notarization, but Title 28 U.S. Code Section
1746 allows for an unsworn statement to be accepted “with like force and effect” as a sworn statement if the
applicant signs the statement under the penalties of perjury.

An example of a document that will meet the requirements of an affidavit or statement is located on the NMC
website,Forms then Miscellaneous Documents.  Notarization is not required and can be submitted via mail or
e-mail. Documentation of loss  must include a statement describing the circumstances of the loss, your printed and
signed name, mariner reference number or  last 4 of your SSN ,and be dated.

The statement must also include: I am affirming that all the information I provided is true and correct to the best
of my knowledge. If the Coast Guard determines I have submitted materially false information within this
affidavit, the Coast Guard may refer the matter to the U.S. Attorney for action under Title 18 USC Section 1001.
I declare (or certify, verify, or state) under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States of America, that
the foregoing is true and correct.

In submitting this request, applicant understand s s/he is  acting under the authority of the  credential /s consistent
with Title 46 Code of Federal Regulations Section 5.57(b). Any fraudulent action taken as part of this request for
a duplicate credential may subject to the suspension or revocation of my credential. .

Per Executive Order 14168 signed January 20, 2025, the National Maritime Center will not  process requests for
change of sex on a Merchant Mariner Credential.

Note:  MMCs may have to be recorded as duplicate in MMLD due to no other option when a new booklet is
required to be issued.  Examples are when removing a negative limitation such as Not Valid on ECDIS, GMDSS,
Limited to NC water, and/or STCW Tonnage limitation raised.  These are not actual duplicate transactions.
