### Info

**IMPORTANT – READ FIRST BEFORE SUBMITTING ANY DOCUMENTATION!**

- **First Class Pilot** applicants **ONLY**:
  - When applying via [Navita TM](https://navita.uscg.mil/), enter the details of your route(s) in Section II of the 719B, please also indicate the REC that services your route(s). This will help us ensure your application is assigned to the correct REC for review.

- **All other MMC applicants** – proceed to [Navita TM](https://navita.uscg.mil/).

### Original

- Step 1. Apply for your [TWIC](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/nmc/TWIC/) and complete the application forms **719B**, **719C** (if applicable), [CG-719S Sea Service](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/nmc/#myModal8) (if applicable), and [719P](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/nmc/#myModalp) using [NMC’s Checklist Page](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/nmc/checklist/) to select the type of credential you want.
- Step 2. Obtain other documented [Sea Service](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/nmc/#myModal8) and any relevant training certificates and/or assessments, if applicable.
- Step 3. Pay your fees at **[pay.gov](https://www.pay.gov/public/form/start/4795779/)** and [Apply](https://navita.uscg.mil).

### Duplicate

Duplicate MMC definition: A replacement credential issued containing the same authority, wording, and expiration date as a the **lost, stolen, or destroyed** credential.

#### Steps to Apply for a Duplicate MMC

- Step 1. Complete the **719B** and [affidavit](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/medical/declaration_in_lieu_of_affidavit_for_duplicate_document_request_061120.pdf).
- Step 2. Pay your fees at **[pay.gov](https://www.pay.gov/public/form/start/4795779/)**.
- Step 3. [Apply](https://navita.uscg.mil).

### Replacement MMC

Replacement MMC definition: A replacement credential issued as the result of a **request to change the name, address, or physical attributes on the credential.**

#### Steps to Apply for a Replacement MMC

- Step 1. Complete the **719B**.
- Step 2. Pay your modification fees at **[pay.gov](https://www.pay.gov/public/form/start/4795779/)**.
- Step 3. [Apply](https://navita.uscg.mil).

### Document of Continuity

Document of Continuity definition: A document issued to keep a mariner eligible to renew their credential while they are not actively sailing, but it does not allow them to work as a merchant mariner.

- Step 1. Complete the **CG-719B**.
- Step 2. [Apply](https://navita.uscg.mil).

### Course Submission Expectations

1. Your request will be entered into our system within 10 business days from when we received it. You will receive an email once it has been entered. If any of these are missing with submission, you will receive an Awaiting Information email notice: Cover Letter, Site Information, Instructor List & Résumés, Framework, Schedule, Syllabus, Outline, Instructor Manual & Lesson Plans, Exams/Assessments, Critique and Sample Certificate. You will have 30 days to respond with the requested information.
1. Once your submission is complete, the request will be awaiting assignment for evaluation by a subject matter expert. This waiting period is approximately 60-90 days after the submission was entered.
1. When a subject matter expert evaluates your request and needs more information, you will be sent a detailed Awaiting Information email notice that explains what the NMC needs in order to provide you an approval. You will have 90 days to respond with the requested information.
1. If your submission results in an Approval, Awaiting Information or Denial, the correspondence will be emailed to the POC provided in the submission.
