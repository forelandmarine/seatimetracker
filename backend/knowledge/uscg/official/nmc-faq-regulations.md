# NMC FAQ: Policy and regulations

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/regulations_faq.pdf

> National Maritime Center frequently asked questions on policy and regulations.


[p. 1]

  Frequently Asked Questions:
  Policy and Regulations

1.  Do I have to meet the requirements for Standards of Training, Certification, and

### Watchkeeping (STCW) in order to receive a domestic officer endorsement?

No.  If no STCW is held, employment may be limited.  For vessels and person s subject to
STCW,  see Title 46 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Part 15, Subpart K  .

2.  May I use the previous policy letters for STCW as Officer in Charge of a Navigational
  Watch (OICNW), Master or Chief Mate 3000 Gross Tons or More, Ratings Forming
  Part of a Navigational Watch (RFPNW), Ratings Forming Part of a Engineer Watch
  (RFPEW), and  Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Boats Other than Fast

### Rescue Boats (PSC)?

No.  The January 1, 2017,  deadline was extended once but i s now expired.  All applicants could
use the previous model assessments if they were signed not later than December 31, 2019 ,  AND
were submitted to the Coast Guard as part of a complete application no later than June 30,
2020.  For any updates or changes,  visit the applicable Navigation and Vessel Inspection
Circulars (NVICs  ) at the Coast Guard NVIC webpage.

3.  I applied for an STCW endorsement in the past and my application was not approved
  because I did not take all of the courses required at the time.  My application has
  now “timed out” or expired.  Do I need to re-  apply for the STCW endorsement?

Yes.  A mariner whose application has “timed out” or who was denied must re-apply by
submitting a new application.  New requirements in effect as of January 1, 2017,  must be met
that may require a new R ecord of A ssessments and updated Coast Guard approved training.
NVIC 03-  14 Guidelines for Approval of Training Courses and Programs provides information
for the validity period of courses.The assessments from the current NVICs do not expire  .

4.  I previously held an STCW 1978 and would like to apply for an STCW 1978,  as
  amended.  What do I need to do?

You must meet the current STCW requirements for the international endorsement sought.

[p. 2]

### 5. Where can I find the requirements to obtain an STCW?

Title 46 CFR Part 11,  Part 12,  and Part 13 contain sea service, training,  and required Standards
of Competence for all STCW officer and rating capacities.  Additional guidance for each
capacity, including the Record of Assessments to document competencies,  are located in
separate NVICs  .

The United States Coast Guard periodically posts new policy, NVICs,  and announcements
concerning credentialing.  In order to keep up to date you may sign up to receive notifications
on the NMC List Server  .

The following lists are not all-  inclusive.  Be sure to review all  current NVICs, including NVICs
covering topics such as Towing Vessel Endorsements or High Speed Craft.
