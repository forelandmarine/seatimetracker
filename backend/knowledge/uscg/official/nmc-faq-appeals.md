# NMC FAQ: Denial, reconsideration and appeal

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/appeals_faq.pdf

> National Maritime Center frequently asked questions on denial, reconsideration and appeal.


[p. 1]

  Frequently Asked Questions:
  Denial, Reconsiderations, and Appeals

1.  What is the National Maritime Center’s (NMC) process if I disagree with a decision
  the NMC has made regarding  my application and/or credential?

If you disagree with an action or decision of the NMC, you may submit a written request to the
NMC via our online application portal .  The request must be dated within 30 days of the date of
the decision letter or action and explain in detail that you disagree with an  action or decision
and are seeking reconsideration of such decision or action.  You will be notified by the division
of their findings.  If the division  agrees, the error will be rectified.  If the division is not  in
agreement, you will be notified of the decision, and the original decision will  remain in effect.

For course approvals,  submit reconsideration  request  s by  e-mail to NMCCourses@uscg.mil.

### 2. I requested reconsideration. When will a decision will be rendered?

All reconsiderations are processed on a first in/first out basis,  and the amount of time to review
a request depends on the complexity of the evaluation.  Once the information is reviewed and a
decision is reached, you will be contacted by the NMC,  usually within 90 days.  In some cases,
if the request is approved, you may be notified via telephone or e-  mail if the decision results in
the issuance of an MMC.

### 3. My request for reconsideration was denied . What are my options?

If you do not agree with the results of the reconsideration, submit a signed and dated request
for appeal within 30 days of the date on the reconsideration letter.  This request should be sent
via e-mail to Coast Guard Headquarters at MMCPolicy@uscg.mil  ,  or by mail to:

U.S. Coast Guard
Director, Commercial Regulations and Standards (CG-5PS)
2703 Martin Luther King Jr. Ave. SE
Stop 7509 Attn: CG-MMC-2
Washington, DC 20593-7509

### 5. Can I file an appeal before I file a f ormal request for r econsideration?

No.  The first step prior to an  appeal is to request that the NMC reconsider their decision or
action.  You MUST request reconsideration and receive a decision prior to requesting an
appeal.  If you submit an appeal prior to reconsideration, it will be processed as a request for
reconsideration.

### 6. My appeal is at Headquarters being reviewed. When will a decision be rendered?

All appeals are processed on a first in/first out basis and the amount of time to review a request
depends on the complexity of the evaluation.  If you would like an update on your status, please
contact Headquarters using the number listed in the letter you received from them or by e-mail
to MMCPolicy@uscg.mil.

[p. 3]

7.  Does the Coast Guard address requests for reconsideration or appeals regarding
  decisions made by the Transportation Security Administration?

The Coast Guard will not review decisions made by the Transportation Security Administration
to suspend, revoke, or deny a mariner’s TWIC.
