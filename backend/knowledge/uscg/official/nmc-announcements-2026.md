## 2026-10-19: Electronic submission of course completion data delayed to 25 Nov 2028

Status: CURRENT.
Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/announcements/2026/implementation_of_electronic_submission_of_mariner_course_completion_data_101926.pdf

United States Coast Guard
Merchant Mariner Credentialing Program

  Implementation of Requirements for Electronic
  Submission  of Mariner Course Completion Data

The Coast Guard issued a final rule titled “Electronic Submission of Mariner Course
Completion Data” on November 25, 2024.  The final rule requires Coast Guard-
approved training providers to electronically submit mariner course completion data
listed in 46 CFR 10.403(b)(7)(i)  through (v) to the National Maritime Center within 5
business days after a student completes a Coast Guard-approved course or program.
The 2024 final rule stated Coast Guard-approved training providers were required to
comply with this regulation by November 25, 2026.

On September 14, 2026, the Coast Guard announced the initial release of Navita™  , a
new digital platform designed to streamline the application and issuance of merchant
mariner credentials and mariner medical certificates.  A future release of Navita  ™ will
include a mariner training provider module that will facilitate electronic submission of
mariner course completion data as required by 46 CFR 10.403(b)(7).  The Coast Guard
expects the Navita™ mariner training provider module to become fully operational in
January 2028.  Accordingly, the Coast Guard expects to begin the requirement for
electronic submission of mariner course completion data on November 25, 2028.

Additional information regarding the updated implementation date can be found  in this
Federal Register Notice or by searching on www.regulations.gov under Docket Number
USCG-2026-0376.

## 2026-09-25: Bulletin: CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-26, violence and harassment training in STCW Basic Training from 1 Oct 2029

Status: CURRENT. (The bulletin footer shows 9/25/2025; the policy letter is dated 25 Sep 2026.)
Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/announcements/2026/mcp_bulletin_mmc_policy_letter_01-26_092526.pdf

United States Coast Guard
Merchant Mariner Credentialing Program

  Amendments to STCW Basic Training Requirements
  for Personal Safety a  nd Social Responsibilities

The Office of Merchant Mariner Credentialing has published CG-MMC Policy Letter
01-26 titled Amendments to STCW Basic Training Requirements for Personal Safety
and Social Responsibilities.

This policy letter provides guidance for evaluating compliance with the amended
competency requirements of the Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping
for Seafarers (STCW) Code concerning Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities
(PSSR).

Beginning October 1, 2029, applicants for original STCW Basic  Training (BT)
endorsements and for renewal of an STCW BT endorsement must demonstrate the
competencies added to Table A-VI/1-4 of the STCW Code through successful completion
of a Coast Guard approved Prevention of Violence and Harassment, including Sexual
Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault course.  Mariners currently holding an STCW
BT endorsement will need to complete the training on or before the first renewal of their
MMC that occurs after September 30, 2029.

The Coast Guard will not issue separate MMC endorsements for Prevention of Violence
and Harassment, including Sexual Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault.

The policy letter provides guidance for training providers considering developing and
seeking approval for this course.  Training providers wishing to obtain approval for a
course may develop and submit their course for approval at any time.

This policy letter is available on the Coast Guard Merchant Mariner Credentialing policy
website.

Mariners and other interested parties should contact the Mariner Credentialing
Program Policy Division at MMCPolicy@uscg.mil  or (206  ) 815-6890 with any questions
or feedback.

## 2026-09-11: Navita launch on 14 Sep 2026: ASAP and IASKNMC email discontinued, paper applications to be phased out

Status: CURRENT.
Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/announcements/2026/nmc_steps_before_navita_launch_11SEP2026.pdf

National Maritime Center
Mariner Ready, Mission Steady

  U.S. Coast Guard NavitaTM - Planned Actions for Launch

NavitaTM is the Coast Guard’s new digital system for mariner credentialing. It represents a
significant modernization of the mariner credentialing process and is designed to provide
mariners with a more intuitive, guided application experience.  The system will he  lp applicants
identify applicable requirements, provide required information and documentation, and
submit more complete applications.  These improvements are expected to reduce delays
associated with incomplete submissions and streamline application proce  ssing over time.
  NavitaTM will launch on September 14, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. EST

Beginning September 14, mariners will submit applications electronically through NavitaTM,
which will provide guided workflows intended to simplify the application process and help
mariners submit complete applications. To prepare for the launch, the National Maritime
Center (NMC) is taking several steps to facilitate applications processing.
Key Information:

•  Navita™ User Guide:  A system user guide will be available on the NMC website
  homepage to aid in the use of Navita™.

•  Application Submission and Additional Information Portal (ASAP): The
  Application Submission and Additional Information Portal (ASAP) will be discontinued and
  will no longer be available .

•  IASKNMC@uscg.mil email: The IASKNMC e-mail address will be discontinued for
  submitting inquiries to the NMC.  Mariners and other stakeholders will submit inquiries to
  the NMC directly through NavitaTM using the Case function. This transition will provide a
  centralized method for submitting and managing inquiries and will ensure correspondence
  is associated with the appropriate applicant or application.

•  Paper Application Submission: Although paper applications will still be accepted, the
  Coast Guard intends to phase out this submission method within the next 12 months.
  Mariners are strongly encouraged to submit credential applications via NavitaTM to ensure
  they receive timely, first  -in, first-out processing and status of their application. Paper
  applications, whether submitted by mail or in-person, will take significantly longer to
  process.

•  REC assistance with applications for submission. REC personnel will continue to
  review application documents presented during an appointment for completeness;
National Maritime Center
Mariner Ready, Mission Steady

  however, applicants are encouraged to submit through Navita™ after the review. REC
  personnel may assist with questions pertaining to the use of Navita™ and submissions, but
  computers or internet access at the RECs for mariner use will not be provided.
While NavitaTM will improve the application and credentialing process, its launch will not
immediately reduce current processing timelines.  Applications received  prior to 14 September,
as well as applications submitted through NavitaTM, will continue to be processed on a  first-in,
first-out basis.  Applications should not be submitted through Navita™ if previously submitted

expedited processing will be considered only for applications demonstrating a clear and

Mariners should continue to apply early using Navita™.  Renewal applications may be
submitted up to 8 months before expiration and may be postdated up to 8 months, allowing
mariners to apply early without losing validity on their existing credential.

Mariners and maritime employers should continue to plan accordingly and submit applications

## 2026-08-27: Relief for mariners with pending renewal applications (120-day temporary authorization letters)

Status: CURRENT for eligible applications (renewal filed before expiry and no later than 26 Aug 2026; credential expired 1 Jan to 31 Jul 2026).
Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/announcements/2026/corrected_nmc_relief_policy_for_pending_MMC_applications_082726.pdf

National Maritime Center
Mariner Ready, Mission Steady

  CORRECTED
  Relief for Mariners with Pending MMC Applications

The National Maritime Center (NMC) continues its efforts to reduce the elevated inventory of
mariner applications and mitigate  the impact of extended processing timelines on mariners.

Temporary credential extensions and other mitigation measures outlined in Update #3:  Mitigation
Efforts, dated April 22, 2026, will expire August 31, 2026.  However, the  NMC recognizes that
some mariners submitted renewal  applications but, due to extending processing timelines, had
their credentials expire while awaiting evaluation.  Without delays, these mariners may have
received an evaluation determination and completed remaining renewal requirements before their
credentials expired.

To address the impact of processing delays on this specific  population, the NMC is implementing
case-specific relief for mariners with qualifying renewal applications.  This relief is separate from
the temporary extensions expiring August 31, 2026, and applies only to mariners meeting eligibility
criteria below.

Eligibility for  Relief

A mariner may qualify for a temporary 120-day authorization to continue sailing under the
authority of an expired credential as follows:
•  Merchant Mariner Credential (MMC  )  (n  ational endorsements only) that expired between
  January 1, 2026, and July 31, 2026;

•  a renewal application submitted to the NMC  before the credential expired, but no later than
  August 26, 2026; and
•  the NMC evaluation results in a letter requesting additional information to complete the
  evaluation, or the NMC issues  an Approved to Test letter.

For eligible applications, the NMC will issue a temporary authorization letter in conjunction with
the applicable request for additional information or approval to test letter.  This  letter allows  the
mariner to continue operating under the authority of the affected national endorsements for 120
days from the date the NMC issues the letter.  Mariners must carry their expired credential and
authorization letter while sailing under the authority of the affected national endorsements.  The
NMC will not issue separate or replacement MMCs reflecting the temporary authorization.

National Maritime Center
Mariner Ready, Mission Steady

The temporary authorization applies only to national endorsements and does not extend the
validity of any Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers (STCW)
endorsements or medical certificates.  Additionally, the authorization is only valid for the specific
application for which the NMC  issued it.

Mariners should carefully review all correspondence from the NMC and promptly provide all
requested information  or complete required testing.  Failure to satisfy the requirements of the
pending renewal application will not result in any additional temporary authorization.

The Coast Guard designed this relief to mitigate the impact of extended application processing
timelines on qualifying mariners whose credentials expired while their renewal applications were
pending evaluation.

The NMC continues to process applications on a first-in, first-out basis as expeditiously as
possible, and mariners are strongly encouraged to submit  renewal applications early to allow
sufficient time for evaluation and resolution of any additional requirements.

United States Coast Guard
Merchant Mariner Credentialing Program

  Guidelines for Coast Guard Approved Training

The Office of Merchant Mariner Credentialing has published Navigation and Vessel
Inspection Circular (NVIC)  01-26, Guidelines for Coast Guard Approved Training.

This NVIC will streamline the process of obtaining Coast Guard approval  of required
training for a mer  chant mariner credential and will reduce the administrative  burden on
maritime training providers.  This NVIC make  s several changes to the current process
and requirements, including:

•  Reducing the documents required to be submitted with a request for course or
  program approval.

•  Reducing the records a training provider is required to maintain.

•  Reducing the documents required to be submitted when requesting renewal of a
  course or program approval.

•  Reducing the number of internal audits a training provider  must conduct.

•  Avoiding lapses in course or program  approval if a  timely request is made and the
  original approval expires before a final decision on the renewal request is made.

National Maritime Center

  National Maritime Center –  U.S. Postal Service (USPS)
  Shipping Labels and Extended Processing Timelines

The National Maritime Center (NMC) is providing this advisory regarding the use of customer  -
provided, prepaid U.S. Postal Service (USPS) shipping labels for credential delivery.

Due to current application inventory levels and extended processing timelines, applications
may not be completed for several months after submission.  In accordance with USPS shipping
policy,  prepaid shipping labels should be used within 7 days of purchase and no more than 30
days after purchase.  In most cases,  mariner credentials mailed using prepaid USPS shipping
labels older than 30 days will not be delivered.

Therefore, the NMC will apply the following procedures when processing and shipping
credentials:

•  To ensure delivery, USPS shipping labels older than 30 days will not  be used.

•  Credentials associated with expired labels will be shipped via USPS First-Class Mail.

•  The NMC will not request replacement labels or delay issuance pending receipt of a new
  label.

Mariners are strongly encouraged  not to submit USPS shipping labels with their
applications and to consider current processing timelines when choosing an alternate shipping
method.  Mariners are also encouraged to monitor the NMC website for updates regarding

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/announcements/2026/lapse_in_appropriations_and_government_shutdown_021726.pdf

National Maritime Center
Mariner Ready, Mission Steady

  Lapse in Appropriations and  Government Shutdown

Due to the government shutdown, the National Maritime Center (NMC) and all Regional
Examination Centers (RECs) are closed until further notice.  Customer walk-in service at the
RECs is suspended.  Examinations and other REC appointments are cancelled.

The following applies during the government shutdown:

•  Our Customer Service Center  (CSC) will be open from 8:00 a.m. to 5:30  p.m. EST, Monday
  through Friday.  You can reach the CSC via the NMC online chat system , by e-mailing
  IASKNMC@uscg.mil or by calling 1-888-  IASKNMC (427-5662).

•  The Application Submission Portal (ASAP) for submission of applications and supporting
  documentation will remain active for the duration of the government shutdown.  Processing
  will resume once appropriations are restored.

•  The NMC website will provide updates on NMC and REC operating status.

•  For questions regarding credentialing issues related to National Defense,  marine employers
  are advised to contact  our Customer Service Center.

The NMC understands the shutdown will  affect our industry customers and stakeholders,  and
we apologize for any potential inconvenience.

Sincerely,

/P.  A. Drayer/

Patrick A. Drayer
Captain, U.S. Coast Guard
Officer in Charge, Marine Inspections


## 2026-02-04: Course approval and renewal timeline expectations (for training providers)

Status: CURRENT unless replaced by NVIC 01-26 practice.
Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/announcements/2026/course_approval_and_renewal_timeline_expectations_020426.pdf

National Maritime Center
Mariner Ready, Mission Steady

  Course Approval and Renewal Timeline Expectations

The National Maritime Center (NMC) is committed to supporting the maritime industry and
maintaining transparency regarding course approvals and renewals.  At this time, we are
experiencing extended processing times for course evaluations due to ongoing staffing and
workload challenges.
What You Need to Know

•  Processing Times
  Providers should anticipate extended waiting periods for course approvals and renewals.  In
  many cases, processing may take months to complete.  This applies to all request types:  new
  approvals, renewals, modifications, and corrections, even when they appear straightforward.
•  No Expiration Due to NMC Delays
  Courses will not expire due to delays resulting from NMC operational circumstances.  If you
  have submitted a renewal request and have not received an official reply regarding approval
  status or extension and your course is within 7 days of expiration, please contact
  NMCCourses@uscg.mil.

•  Renewal Requests – Submit Early
  Please continue to submit renewal requests no less than 90 days prior to your course expiration
  date.  Early submission helps us support your planning needs and reduces the risk of disruption
  to your training schedules.
•  Communication and General Inquiries
  If you have questions or concerns about course approval or renewal processes, you may contact
  us at (206) 815-6893 or NMCCourses@uscg.mil.  While we are unable to provide detailed
  timelines for individual requests, we will continue to share general updates and guidance to
  help you plan.
We appreciate your understanding, patience, and continued partnership as we work to reduce
processing times and restore normal service levels.
Sincerely,
/P. A. Drayer/
Patrick A. Drayer
Captain, U.S. Coast Guard
Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection
