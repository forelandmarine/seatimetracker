# NMC: Military recency credit

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/professional_qualifications/recency.pdf

> Recency rules for officer endorsements, written for military applicants.


National Maritime Center
Serving Our Nation  ’s Mariners

  Military Recency Credit

•  Recency is required for ALL original and raise of g  rade officer endorsements.

•  For deck officer endorsements up to 200 gross registered tons (GRT), there is no minimum
  tonnage required regarding recency.(Limitations are 25, 50, 100, and 200 GRT.)

•  Recency is NOT required for renewals or qualified ratings.

•  Recency is required for towing officer endorsements, but the 90 days can be on any type or
  tonnage vessel in the last 7  years.

•  An applicant for any officer endorsement must have at least 3  months of qualifying service
  on vessels of appropriate tonnage or horsepower within the 7  years immediately preceding
  the date of application.

•  Recency required for Master or Mate 500/1600 applicants:

  o  All 90 days must be on vessels > 50 GRT for 500 GRT limitations.

  o  All 90 days must be on vessels > 100 GRT for 1600 GRT limitations.

•  Recency and unlimited tonnage for deck officers:

  o  All 90 days must be on vessels > 10 0 GRT, and

  o  At least 45 days must be on vessels > 1600 GRT

  o  If recency requirements are not met, a tonnage limitation will be calculated.

•  Recency and unlimited horsepower for engineer officers (except designated duty
  engineers):

  o  All 90 days in the last 7 years , with at least 45 days on vessel s of 4,000 horsepower or
  more

  o  If recency requirements are not met, a horsepower limitation will be calculated.
