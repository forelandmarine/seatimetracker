# NMC FAQ: Abbreviations

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/abbreviations_faq.pdf

> National Maritime Center frequently asked questions on abbreviations.


[p. 1]

  Frequently Asked Questions:
  Abbreviations

### 1. What do the abbreviations on the National Maritime Center ( NMC) website stand for?

Most of the abbreviations used are discussed in the relevant portions throughout the website;
however, the list below puts them in one convenient location.

AAMRO – American Association of Medical Review Officers

AI – Awaiting Information

ATT – Approval to Test

ATBs – Articulated Tug(s) and Barge(s)

CFR – Code of Federal Regulations

CSC – Customer Service Center

DE – Designated Examiner

DUI – Driving Under the Influence

FAQs – Frequently Asked Questions

FR – Federal Register

ID - Identification

IMO – International Maritime Organization

ISO – International Organization for Standardization

MED – Medical Evaluation Division

Med Cert –  Medical Certificate

MEDMAC – Merchant Mariner Medical Advisory Committee

MERPAC – Merchant Marine Personnel Advisory Committee

[p. 2]

MMC – Merchant Mariner Credential or Merchant Mariner Credentialing Program/Policy

MMS – Mission Management System

MRO – Medical Review Officer

MROCC – Medical Review Officer Certification Council

MSM – Marine Safety Manual

NDR – National Driver Registry

NMC – National Maritime Center

NOAA – National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration

NVICs – Navigation and Vessel Inspection Circulars

OMB – Office of Management and Budget

OUPV or 6 Pack –  Operator of Uninspected Passenger Vessel

PQE – Professional Qualifications Evaluation

QA – Qualified Assessor

REC – Regional Examination Center

SAMHSA – Substance Abuse and Mental Health Service Administration

SSE – Safety and Suitability Evaluation

Stats – Statistics

STCW – International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification,  and Watchkeeping
for Seafarers

Tankerman PIC – Tankerman Person In Charge

TOARs – Towing Officer Assessment Records

[p. 3]

TSA – Transportation Security Administration

TWIC – Transportation Workers Identification Credential

US – United States

USC or US Code – United States Code

USCG or US Coast Guard –  United States Coast Guard

WW II –  World War II
