1.  PURPOSE. This policy letter provides guidance for evaluating compliance with the amended
  competency requirements of the Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for
  Seafarers (STCW) Code concerning Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities (PSSR).

2.  ACTION. The National Maritime Center will use this policy letter to evaluate whether
  applicants for STCW Basic Training (BT) endorsements have demonstrated the
  competencies required by the STCW Code. The Coast Guard will also use this policy letter
  and Navigation and Vessel Inspection Circular (NVIC) 01-26, Guidelines for Approval of
  Training, when evaluating requests for approval of courses to meet requirements for PSSR.

3.  DIRECTIVES AFFECTED. None.

### 4. BACKGROUND.

  a.  Violence and harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying and sexual assault, in the
  maritime environment have known detrimental effects on seafarers including depression,
  poor performance, unhealthy coping mechanisms, and isolation. Moreover, seafarers who
  experience or witness violence and harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying
  and sexual assault, are more likely to make errors at work and provide compromised
  levels of service. In the maritime industry, these experiences lead to negative effects on
  recruitment and retention of seafarers and pose significant risks to the safe and secure
  operation of maritime vessels.

  b.  The International Maritime Organization (IMO) has recognized the importance of
  developing a maritime workplace culture that is safe and inclusive for all seafarers and
  maritime professionals as a means of improving operational safety and security in the
  maritime industry.

  c.  The IMO Maritime Safety Committee (MSC) recognized that training and education
  would provide the necessary foundation to empower seafarers to challenge the

  longstanding culture that has been tolerant of violence and harassment, including sexual
  harassment, bullying and sexual assault. The MSC instructed the Sub-Committee on
  Human Element, Training and Watchkeeping (HTW) to prioritize the development of
  mandatory training provisions addressing these issues in the STCW Code.

  d.  The ninth session of the HTW met in February 2023 and considered proposals on
  mandatory training requirements to address violence and harassment, including sexual
  harassment, bullying and sexual assault. The United States submitted a proposal
  developed in consultation with the National Merchant Marine Personnel Advisory
  Committee (NMERPAC) and the National Merchant Mariner Medical Advisory
  Committee (NMEDMAC). The sub-committee agreed to use this proposal as the basis of
  the draft amendments to the required personal safety and social responsibilities training.
  The U.S. submission proposed amending the standards of competence for PSSR in Table
  A-VI/1-4 of the STCW Code by adding a new competence on the prevention of violence
  and harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying and sexual assault, with associated
  knowledge, understanding and proficiencies (KUPs) addressing prevention, awareness,
  bystander intervention, response, and reporting of violence and harassment, including
  sexual harassment, bullying and sexual assault.

  e.  The amendments were adopted at MSC 108 in May 2024 and went into effect on
  January 1, 2026.

  f.  The amended Table A-VI/1-4 of the STCW Code, Specification of minimum standard of
  competence in personal safety and social responsibilities, is attached as Enclosure (2),
  and the new provisions for violence and harassment, including sexual harassment,
  bullying and sexual assault are highlighted.

### 5. DISCUSSION.

  a.  Beginning October, 1, 2029, applicants for original STCW BT endorsements and for
  renewal of an STCW BT endorsement must demonstrate the competencies added to
  Table A-VI/1-4 of the STCW Code through successful completion of a Coast Guard
  approved Prevention of Violence and Harassment, including Sexual Harassment,
  Bullying and Sexual Assault course. Completion of this course is not currently mandated
  by Coast Guard regulation, however since the United States is a signatory to the STCW
  Convention, vessel owners and operators should be aware that their vessels are subject to
  foreign port state control actions including detention if mariners are not compliant with
  the STCW Convention and Code. The Coast Guard will consider mariners who have
  voluntarily completed this course to be in compliance with the amendments to the STCW
  Convention and Code. Guidance on qualifying for and maintaining the standards of
  competence for BT is provided in Enclosure (1).

  b.  Mariners currently holding an STCW BT endorsement will need to complete the training
  on or before the first renewal of their MMC that occurs after September 30, 2029.

## Enclosure (1) to CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-26

#### GUIDELINES FOR QUALIFYING FOR AN ORIGINAL ENDORSEMENT AND MAINTAINING THE STANDARDS OF COMPETENCE FOR STCW BASIC TRAINING

### 1. GENERAL.

  a.  This enclosure provides guidance on compliance with amendments to the standards of
  competence for Personal Safety & Social Responsibilities (PSSR) in Table A-VI/1-4 of
  the STCW Code concerning the prevention of violence and harassment, including sexual
  harassment, bullying and sexual assault.

  b.  As specified in 46 CFR 15.1105, all mariners serving in positions that must be filled as
  part of the required crew complement or who are assigned a responsibility on the muster
  list, must produce evidence of having achieved, and maintained at 5-year intervals,
  proficiency in BT. There are four elements of BT:

    Personal Survival Techniques;
    Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting;
    Elementary First Aid; and
    PSSR.

  c.  46 CFR 11.302(b) and 46 CFR 12.602(b) include requirements for mariners to maintain
  the standard of competence for BT every five years. Mariners may demonstrate having
  maintained competence through a combination of seagoing service and Coast Guard
  approved training ashore.

  d.  Mariners may qualify for an original endorsement for BT and maintain the standards of
  competence for BT as described below.

  e.  Additional guidance on BT is provided in Navigation and Vessel Inspection Circular
  (NVIC) 08-14, Guidelines for Qualification for STCW Endorsements in Basic Training
  (Formerly Basic Safety Training). This and other NVICs are available at
  https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/NVIC/.

2.  QUALIFYING FOR AN ORIGINAL ENDORSEMENT FOR BT. Mariners may qualify for
  an original endorsement for BT by completing an approved STCW Basic Training course, or
  by completing separate approved courses for each element of BT. After September 30, 2029,
  mariners will also need to complete approved training for Prevention of Violence and
  Harassment, Including Sexual Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault.

### 3. MAINTAINING THE STANDARDS OF COMPETENCE FOR BT (RENEWAL).

  a.  Mariners may demonstrate maintaining the standards of competence for BT by
  competing an approved Prevention of Violence and Harassment, including Sexual
  Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault course and either:

  1)  Having at least 360 days of seagoing service within the past five years and
  completing approved Revalidation training for BT; or

[p. 6]

  2)  Completing approved Refresher training for BT.

  b.  Completion of an approved Prevention of Violence and Harassment, Including Sexual
  Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault course is not a one-time or “gap-closing”
  requirement. It will be required every five years to maintain the standard of competence
  for BT.

### 4. TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS.

  a.  Original: Until September 30, 2029, mariners may qualify for an original BT
  endorsement by providing evidence of meeting the previous requirements for qualifying
  for BT. Completion of an approved Prevention of Violence and Harassment, Including
  Sexual Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault course is not required until after that
  date.

  b.  Renewal: Until September 30, 2029, mariners may demonstrate maintaining the standards
  of competence for BT by meeting previous requirements. Completion of an approved
  Prevention of Violence and Harassment, Including Sexual Harassment, Bullying and
  Sexual Assault course is not required until after that date.

  c.  The Coast Guard will accept completion of the Maritime Sexual Assault and Sexual
  Harassment (SASH) Prevention and Response eLearning Program, developed by the
  Ship Operations Cooperative Program (SOCP) to meet the Prevention of Violence and
  Harassment, Including Sexual Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault training
  requirement. Mariners having completed this course should provide a training certificate
  that verifies successful completion of the SOCP course.

  d.  The Coast Guard recognizes that companies, labor organizations and other entities may
  have provided relevant training to their constituent mariners. The Coast Guard will
  consider acceptance of this training conducted prior to October 1, 2029, if it is
  determined the training meets the standard of competence in the STCW Code.
  Organizations seeking acceptance of such training should submit a request for acceptance
  of the training to the National Maritime Center with supporting documents that
  demonstrate the training meets the new standards of competence and a copy of the course
  completion certificate or letter issued to students successfully completing the course.
  Supporting documents may include course outlines, syllabi and examinations and other
  assessment instruments. The training does not need to be in the format and manner
  specified in Navigation and Vessel Inspection Circular (NVIC) 01-26, Guidelines for
  Approval of Training. The request should include the date the training was first offered.

  e.  For purposes of the above transitional provisions, a mariner will be eligible to qualify for
  or maintain an endorsement for BT under previous requirements if they had submitted a
  complete application prior to October 1, 2029.

## Enclosure (3) to CG-MMC Policy Letter 01-26

#### GUIDANCE FOR APPROVAL OF PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE AND HARASSMENT, INCLUDING SEXUAL HARASSMENT, BULLYING AND SEXUAL ASSAULT COURSES

1.  Training providers may submit courses for approval at any time. Training providers are
  encouraged to submit courses as soon as is practicable so that they will be available when
  mariners need them.

2.  As this competence for Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities (PSSR) will be required
  for both original STCW Basic Training (BT) and for demonstrating continued competence in
  BT, the Coast Guard strongly recommends that training providers develop stand-alone
  courses that can be approved for both original and renewal of PSSR.

  Mariners needing to demonstrate original proficiency would take the course in addition to
  other BT courses, while mariners previously qualified for BT who need to demonstrate
  having maintained the standard of competence for BT would take this new course in addition
  to other BT refresher or revalidation courses.1

3.  Requests for approval of a course should include the information specified in 46 CFR
  10.402(b). Guidance on course approval, including the format for courses submitted for
  approval, is found in NVIC 01-26, Guidelines for Approval of Training. This and other
  NVICs are available at https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/NVIC/.

4.  Courses should be at least 4 hours in duration and should include content substantially
  similar to module 6 of the 2026 edition of IMO Model Course 1.21, Personal Safety and
  Social Responsibilities. Courses may be delivered on-line, in-person, or in a blended format.
  A sample course outline and a sample detailed outline excerpted from the model course is
  provided as Enclosure (4).

5.  Courses should include written and/or practical assessments to ensure that mariners have
  obtained the required KUPs. The criteria for evaluating competence should be as described in
  column 4 of the amended Table A-VI/1-4 of the STCW Code. This table is provided as

## Enclosure (1) to this policy letter, and the relevant competence and KUPs are highlighted.

6.  For questions about obtaining approval for a Prevention of Violence and Harassment,
  Including Sexual Harassment, Bullying and Sexual Assault course, please contact the
  National Maritime Center at (888) 427-5662 or IAskNMC@uscg.mil.

1Revalidation courses are intended for mariners who have at least one year of seagoing service in the previous five
years. Refresher courses are for those mariners who do not have at least one year of sea going service during the
previous five years.

#### DETAILED OUTLINE

Introduction

The detailed outline correlates the knowledge, understanding and proﬁciencies deﬁned in the STCW Code,
with the speciﬁc learning outcomes that the trainees should achieve. Each speciﬁc outcome is presented
as a topic or sub-topic as a learning objective reﬂecting the knowledge, understanding and proﬁciency
(KUP) in table A-VI/1-4.

Learning objectives

The detailed outline has been developed in learning objective format in which the objective describes how
the trainee should demonstrate the speciﬁc KUP, and that the required competence has been achieved.

All learning objectives are understood to be preﬁxed by the words The expected learning outcome is that the
trainee is able to…

  Knowledge, understanding and proﬁciency (Learning Outcome)

  Course introduction

  1  Contribute to the prevention of and response to violence and harassment, including sexual
  harassment, bullying and sexual assault

  1.1  Prevention of violence and harassment
  Basic knowledge and understanding of violence and harassment, including sexual harassment,
  bullying and sexual assault, and the continuum of harm
  .1  remember basic concepts such as violence, harassment, sexual harassment, bullying, sexual
  assault, victims, perpetrators, bystanders, stakeholders, safety, health, and well-being
  .2  list causes of violence, harassment, sexual harassment, bullying, and sexual assault
  .3  identify causes of violence, harassment, sexual harassment, bullying, and sexual assault related
  to different cultures, religions, and traditions
  .4  recognize cyber-harassment and cyber-bullying

  1.2  Basic knowledge and understanding of the consequences of violence and harassment, including
  sexual harassment, bullying and sexual assault on victims, perpetrators, bystanders and
  stakeholders, and its effects on safety, health and well-being
  .1  recognize the consequences of violence and harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying
  and sexual assault on victims, perpetrators, bystanders and stakeholders, and its effects on
  safety, health and well-being on board ships
