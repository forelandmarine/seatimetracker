# NMC: ATB determinations for credit toward Tankerman PIC

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/professional_qualifications/atb_list.pdf

> Articulated tug-barge units and how their service counts for Tankerman PIC.


[p. 1]

  ATB Determinations for Credit toward Tankerman PIC

 Below are listings of Articulated Tug Barges (ATBs) that have been evaluated to determine if
 their cargo arrangements and equipment are substantially similar to that found on tank ships,
 and therefore, in accordance with 46 CFR 13.127(c), whether sea service and cargo transfers
 can be credited toward endorsements as Tankerman-PIC.  These lists will be updated as each
 new determination is made.

 For ATBs listed as being acceptable, mariners serving on these ATBs may use the service and
 cargo transfers to qualify for an endorsement as Tankerman-PIC.  Service on these ATBs may
 be used for any or all of the service on tank ships required in 46 CFR 13.203(a).  This service
 will be credited day for day (1 day of creditable service for each day served).  Cargo transfers to
 or from these ATBs may also be used for any or all of the cargo transfers required by 46 CFR
 13.203(b).  Service and cargo transfers on these ATBs may also be used for the requirements of
 46 CFR 13.120(a)(1) for renewal of a Tankerman-PIC endorsement.

 For ATBs listed as being not acceptable, service and cargo transfers cannot be used to qualify
 for any Tankerman PIC  endorsement.

 ATB equivalencies are creditable on a case-by-case basis and must have prior authorization by
 the Coast Guard.  In order to have a vessel reviewed for equivalence to a tank ship, please have
 the company that owns the vessel request a review by CGHQ via MMCPolicy@uscg.mil .

 Regulatory Reference:  46 CFR 13.127(c) Service on Articulated Tug Barges (ATBs).  Service on
 ATBs with an aggregate tonnage of 1,600 GRT or more will be creditable on a case-by-case
 basis and with prior authorization by the Coast Guard, provided the ATB equipment is
 comparable to tank vessel equipment.  The Coast Guard may issue blanket authorizations for
 classes of ATBs.
