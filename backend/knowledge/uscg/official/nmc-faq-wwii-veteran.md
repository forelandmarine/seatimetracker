# NMC FAQ: WWII veteran eligibility

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/WWII_veteran_faq.pdf

> National Maritime Center frequently asked questions on wwii veteran eligibility.


[p. 1]

  Frequently Asked Questions:
  WWII Veteran Eligibility Requests

1.  Who owns the World War II (WWII) records and where do we send a request for a
  copy of a WWII record or determination of WWII eligibility?

The U.S. Coast Guard is in the process of permanently  archiving WWII Merchant Mariner
records to the legal custody of the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) in
St. Louis.

Once the transfer is complete, WWII merchant mariner service records will become public
records and may be requested by contacting NARA at the address below.  Please be advised
that there are fees associated with the request of public records.

  National Archives and Records Administration at St. Louis
  P.O. Box 38757
  St. Louis, MO  63138-0757

WWII eligibility requests may be  submitted by:

  •  Mail:

  Attn:  Correspondence (NMC-41)
  100 Forbes Drive
  Martinsburg, WV  25404

  •  E-mail:

  To request records via e-mail, please submit a Record Request Form.

  •  Fax:

  304-433-3417, Attn:  Correspondence (NMC-  41).

2.  What documentation is needed if applying for either the DD Form 214 or

### DD Form 1300 for a deceased mariner?

Under the Privacy Act, a certified copy of the d eath certificate will be needed if you are applying
for a DD Form 214 or DD Form 1300 for a deceased mariner.

NOTE 1:  For living mariners, requests should be made using a DD 2168 and should include:

  •  Requester name and mariner’s full name
  •  Mariner reference number/social security number, Z card number, or MMC book
  number
  •  Mariner date of birth
  •  Contact information address and phone number for mariner and requester
  •  Signature and Date.

NOTE 2:  If the request is from a thi rd party, also include a death certificate, power of
attorney, letter of incapacitation from a doctor, or mariner consent.

### 4. How does a WWII merchant mariner request a copy of his/her medical records?

Merchant mariner medical records are generally not made a part of the records maintained by
the U.S. Coast Guard.  If a merchant mariner was treated at a private hospital, the records must
be obtained through that facility.  Requests for medical records for merchant mariners who
were treated at U.S. Public Health Service Hospitals should be sent to the following address:

  Gillis W. Long Hansen’s Disease Center
  Health Data Center
  1770 Physician Park
  Baton Rouge, LA  70816

5.  Can a DD Form 214 or DD Form 1300 be issued for merchant mariners who served
  during the Korean War, Vietnam Era, Persian Gulf War, and/or during the w ars in Iraq
  or Afghanistan?

No.  There is no active legislation permitting the U.S. Coast Guard to issue discharge
documents for merchant mariners who served during the Korean War, Vietnam Era, Persian
Gulf War,  and/or during the w ars in Iraq or Afghanistan.

6.  Can training time with the U.S. Maritime Service be used as evidence of oceangoing
  service during World War II to establish veteran’s benefits?

No.  Although a mariner may have participated in training courses or spent time training with
the U.S. Maritime Service, the U.S. Coast Guard is unable to use this documentation as
evidence of oceangoing service during WWII.  The U.S. Maritime Service was not a uniformed,
armed service,  and was not operated by the War Shipping Administration.

7.  What agency is responsible for issuing awards and decorations to Merchant Marines
  who have documented oceangoing  service during December 7, 1941, to December
  31, 1946?

The U.S. Department of Transportation maintains files on awards and decorations granted to
Merchant Marines.

Requests for information on awards and decorations should include the full name of the
seamen, Coast Guard Z or Book Number, and World War II home address.  Requests should be
submitted to:

  U.S. Maritime Administration (MAR-630)
  (Attn:  Mariner Medals and Ribbons)
  1200 New Jersey Avenue, SE
  Washington, DC  20590
