# NMC sample letter: original, raise of grade or modification (military)

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/professional_qualifications/sample_orig_att_letter.pdf

> Sample service attestation letter.


National Maritime Center
Providing Credentials to Mariners

  Sample Original/Raise in Grade/Modification Letter —  PQEB

A letter from the Professional Qualifications Evaluation Branch (PQEB) of the National
Maritime Center (NMC) concerning an application for an original credential, a raise in grade,
or a modification (increase in scope), will likely look similar to the following example:

Dear John Merchantmariner:

This is in response to your application,  which we received on December 7, 2012, for the following M erchant Mariner
Credential(s):

  ●  MMC – Original Third Mate Of Steam or Motor Vessels of A ny Gross Tons Upon Oceans

After a comprehensive evaluation, your application for the above M erchant Mariner Credentials(s) is approved  .  Prior to
issuance, you will be required to pass the following examinations:

  Module  Module Name  Passing Score

  054  RULES OF THE ROAD  90

  141  DECK GENERAL  70

  142  NAVIGATION GENERAL  70

  143  DECK SAFETY & ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION  70

  145  NAVIGATION PROBLEMS –  NEAR COASTAL  90

  146  NAVIGATION PROBLEMS –  CELESTIAL  80

  144  NAVIGATION PROBLEMS –  CHART PLOTTING  87

NOTE:  Exam fees will be assessed at the time of testing. This approval is valid one year from the date of this letter.  If you
have not completed the examination requirements within the approval period, you will be required to submit a new
application.  To schedule your examination date(s), please visit the REC Appointment Scheduler at
http://www.uscg.mil/nmc/; or call 1-888-  427-  5662; or as always, you can contact your local REC.  Congratulations!

  Sincerely,

  Chief, Professional Qualifications Evaluations Branch
