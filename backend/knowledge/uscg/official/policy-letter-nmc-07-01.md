1.  This policy letter provides information concerning the acceptability to the United States of
training courses approved or accepted by Parties other than the United States to the International
Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers, 1978, as
amended (STCW).

2.  This policy letter supersedes and replaces NMC Policy Letter 3-98 dated January 28, 1998.

3.  The Maritime Safety Committee of the International Maritime Organization clarified the
requirements for acceptance by one Party of the STCW of training carried out under the approval of
another party to the STCW in STCW.7/Circular 7 dated May 20, 1998.  The pertinent text extracted
from the circular reads as follows:

  “.1 training carried out under the STCW Convention which does not lead to the issue of
  an appropriate certificate and on which information provided by a Party is found by the
  Maritime Safety Committee to give full and complete effect to the Convention in
  accordance with regulation I/7 paragraph 2, may be accepted by other Parties to the
  Convention as meeting the relevant training requirements thereof.”

4.  Under the above interpretation, all training required by the STCW Code A tables in chapters II
and III must be approved or accepted by the U. S. Coast Guard if the training is part of, or leads to,
the issuance of an STCW certificate by the United States.  Because the tables in chapters II and III
often refer to training set forth in other chapters and tables, that training is also included under the
requirement for approval or acceptance by the U. S. Coast Guard.  Other training such as GMDSS
training and training for service on tankers lead to endorsements on STCW certificates and are
therefore also included in the requirement for domestic approval or acceptance.  The only
exceptions to the requirement for U. S. Guard approval or acceptance of training is that training
required by STCW Chapter V regulations V/2 and V/3 and training for persons designated to take
charge of medical care.

5.  The STCW requires that all training and assessments of seafarers be carried out in accordance
with the STCW Code A, Section A-I/6.  To ensure that the high standards of training required by
the STCW are maintained for U. S. mariners, the U. S. Coast Guard will not generally accept
training in the areas noted in the exceptions to the approval requirements.  In unique situations or to
accommodate training not available within the U. S., special consideration will be given to
acceptance of a course or program leading to proficiency in any of the excepted areas.  Course
