# NMC FAQ: Reduced sea service for Able Seaman (NDAA 2024)

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/reduced_sea_service_for_ab_faq.pdf

> National Maritime Center frequently asked questions on reduced sea service for able seaman (ndaa 2024).


[p. 1]

National Maritime Center
Keep ’em Safe, Keep ’em Sailing

  Frequently Asked Questions  :
  Reduced Sea Service for Able  Seaman (AB )

### 1. Able Seaman – What happened?

On December 22, 2023, the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) of 2024, (Pub. Law
118-31) Section 3534(j) was signed into law.  Currently this law is temporary and is set to expire
in 3 years,  on December 22, 2026.

### 2. How did this change requirements for specific Able Seaman endorsements?

It reduced overall service requirements for three Able Seaman endorsements.

•  AB-Unlimited overall service was reduced from 1  ,080 days to 540 days total service.

•  AB-Limited overall service was reduced  from 540 days to 360 total service.

•  AB-Special overall service was reduced  from 360 days to 180 days total service.

### 3. What did not change for Able Seaman- Unlimited and Able Seaman- Limited?

•  AB-Unlimited still requires all service to be on oceans or Great Lakes.

•  AB-Limited still requires all service to be on vessels of 100 GRT or more.

•  AB-Special still requires all service to be on navigable waters of the U.S.

### 4. Is this change retroactive?

No, if the application has been processed and an  MMC issued prior to December 22, 2023, the
National Maritime Center (NMC) will not go back and re-evaluate.

If a mariner previously was issued Able Seaman-OSV, Fish, or Sail due to less than 360 days
qualifying service, a new application and fees must be submitted.

5.  What if I have a current application in a  waiting information (AI) status and the
  reduction in sea service now allows me to receive or be approved to test for a higher

### AB then I applied for?

These applications have been identified and they are being re-  evaluated,  and updated AI letters
will be sent.

6.  What if I currently have an approved to test (ATT) letter for AB-OSV, Fish, or Sail and
  now meet examinations requirements for AB-Special?

For AB applications in ATT as of December 22, 2023, once examinations are successfully
passed, the requested AB-OSV, Fish, Sail,  and/or MODU will be issued as requested along with
AB-Special.

7.  Does this change impact how my AB course is applied to required service when
  applicable?

•  If the course is approved is for a specified number of days, such as 30, 60, or 180, no
  change.

•  If the course is approved for a specific AB endorsement such as AB-  Fish, no change.

•  If the course approval is for 1/3 or 2/3 of the required sea service, this will be applied to the
  new service requirements accordingly.

### 8. If an applicant believes an error was made, what steps should be taken?

The mariner may contact the  NMC at 1-888-  427-5662 or via e-mail to iasknmc@uscg.mil.  If
the mariner is still not satisfied, the reconsideration process is available.
