1.  PURPOSE. This Policy Letter  establishes policy for evaluating and crediting recent sea service for
  applicants for national officer endorsements on merchant mariner credentials (MMCs)  .The Coast
  Guard is publishing this policy in alignment with Administration priorities to reduceburden on
  industry and to provide ways to streamline mariner career progression.

2.  ACTION. The Coast Guard will use this policy  when evaluating applications for a  national officer
  endorsement ona merchant mariner credential (  MMC).

3.  DIRECTIVES AFFECTED. None. The  contents of this policy letter  will be considered for
  incorporation in the next revision of references (a) and (b) .

### 4. BACKGROUND.

  a.  Following a change to 46 U.S.C. 7101, reference (a)  was amended  on September 16,2020,[  85
  FR 57757] to include an  authorization for the Coast Guard to issue national officer endorsements
  to applicants who have three months of qualified service on vessels of the uniformed services
  within the 7-year period immediately preceding application who are otherwise fully qualified.

  b.  The period for recent sea service for mariners without service on vessels of the uniformed
  services was not changed and  remains at three years.  [ 46 CFR 11.201(c)(1)(i)]

  c.  In their April 8, 2020, meeting, the Merchant Marine Personnel Advisory Committee
  (MERPAC) recommended  an increase in the recency period for  non-  military mariners .
  MERPAC advised an increase would be appropriate  “ to promote equality amongst mariners.”

  This and other CG -MMC Policy Letters  are available from the CG  -MMC  -2 web pageor  by using
  this QR Code.

[p. 2]

### 5. DISCUSSION.

  a.  This policy letter will harmonize the period for recent sea service for national officer endorsements
  for all applicants. Mariners without service on vessels of the uniformed service will have the same
  7-  year period for recent sea service as mariners with service on vessels of the uniformed services.

  b.  All applicants for national officer endorsements who have at least three months of qualifying
  service on vessels of  appropriate tonnage or horsepower  within the seven years immediately
  preceding application will be deemed to have met the requirements for recent sea service in
  46 CFR 11.201(c)(1)(i) to (c)(1)(iii).

  c.  Mariners who had previously applied for a national officer endorsement which was denied because
  of having less than the required recent sea service  may re-  apply if they meet  the new  recent service
  provisions described in this policy letter. A new application will be necessary, including payment
  of required fees, per 46 CFR 10.219.

  d.  This policy letter is effective immediately .
