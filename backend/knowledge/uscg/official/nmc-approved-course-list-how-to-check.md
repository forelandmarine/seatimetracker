# NMC approved course list (courses.pdf): how the list works and how to check a course

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/courses/courses.pdf
Archived copy used: http://web.archive.org/web/20261004221034/https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/courses/courses.pdf

> The NMC publishes one PDF of every Coast Guard approved course, sorted by school name and updated every Wednesday. Each row gives the school, the course name, the approval wording (which exam or training requirement the course satisfies, with the course code in brackets such as BBBCAS-340), the school's main state, web page and phone number. The approval wording is the part that matters: it says exactly which CFR requirement is met, for which endorsements, and any time limit such as "within one year of application".

## How to read and use the list (summary of the layout shown on page 1)

- Header: "Course Approvals", the issue date, "This document is updated every Wednesday", a tip to search with Ctrl+F, and a note that it is sorted alphabetically by School Name. The state shown is the school's primary campus only.
- Columns: SchoolName, CourseName, Approval, State, Webpage, Telephone.
- The Approval column states what the course satisfies, quoting the CFR section (for example 46 CFR 11.201(j)(1) for original issuance or 10.227(e)(1)(iii) for renewal), the endorsements covered, the course code, and conditions (for example a one-year window, or that a certificate may be used for only one application).
- Edition size: the 30 Sep 2026 edition is 1,047 PDF pages (page footers say "of 1050").
- If a course is not in the current list, or the school's wording does not cover the endorsement you want, the NMC may not accept the certificate. The NMC courses FAQ says to contact the school directly about what a course covers.
