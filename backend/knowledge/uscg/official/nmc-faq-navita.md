# NMC FAQ: Navita (online application system)

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/navita_faq.pdf

> National Maritime Center frequently asked questions on navita (online application system).


[p. 1]

  Frequently Asked Questions:
  Navita™

### 1. What is Navita™?

Navita™ is the Coast Guard’s new digital system for mariner credentialing.  It  modernizes how
mariners, maritime training providers, and  credentialing stakeholders interact with the Coast
Guard by replacing paper-based  processes with a more efficient, secure,  and  user-friendly
digital platform.

### 2. What is the Merchant Mariner Credentialing Program?

The Mariner Credentialing Program issues merchant mariner credentials and  medical
certificates to qualified mariners, and approves commercial maritime  training providers and
required credentialing courses and  programs.  The  Mariner Credentialing Program also
conducts oversight of approved training providers such as the state and federal Maritime
Academies.  The  Coast Guard is the lead federal agency responsible  for credentialing U.S.
mariners, protecting our nation’s Marine Transportation System, and  regulating maritime
shipping.

3.  Why are mariners and the Marine Transportation  System (MTS) important to the
  nation’s economy and security?

Credentialed mariners are essential to the operation of the MTS,  our economic prosperity, and
our national security.  There  are approximately 200,000  U.S. merchant mariners operating all
types of vessels on America’s system of ports and  waterways.  They  are part of the $5.4 trillion
in economic activity and  30.8 million jobs  created  by our maritime  system.

### 4. Why is the Coast Guard making this change?

The current credentialing process involves significant paperwork,  manual processing, and
back-and-  forth communication, which is time-  consuming for both mariners and Coast Guard
personnel.  Navita  ™ improves efficiency, reduces errors,  and provides a more user-friendly
service  to mariners.

### 5. What will change for mariners?

Mariners will be able to submit  applications and supporting documents electronically through
a guided process, making it easier to provide complete and accurate information.

[p. 2]

### 6. Will this reduce processing times?

Navita™ is designed to reduce delays by minimizing incomplete applications and  streamlining
the overall process.

### 7. When will Navita™ be available?

Navita™ is being rolled out in phases, with the initial release  expected in September 2026.
Additional capabilities will be added  over time.

### 8. Is the Coast Guard replacing its workforce?

No.  Navita  ™ is designed to support the workforce by reducing manual tasks and  improving
efficiency, not replace personnel.

### 9. Will requirements for mariners change?

No.  Credentialing  standards and requirements remain the same.  Navita  ™ improves how
applications are processed, not the standards themselves.

### 10. Will mariners still be able to get help if they need it?

Yes.  Mariners  will continue to have access to support through the Coast Guard, including the
National Maritime  Center (NMC) and  regional  examination centers.

### 11. How will the Coast Guard communicate updates?

Updates will be shared through official Coast Guard channels, including the NMC website  and
direct engagement with maritime  stakeholders.

### 12. Will public mariners be able to provide feedback on Navita™?

Yes, the Coast Guard will hold public “hands-on events” before the first release  to get direct
feedback from mariners and  credentialing stakeholders.

### 13. Can mariners still use third-party services to help with their credential applications?

Navita™ is designed to be easy and  intuitive  for mariners to use on their own; however,
mariners can still choose to use a third-party service  to assist with their applications.

[p. 3]

### 14. Why do mariners have to enter information that the Coast Guard already has?

Navita™ is a new, secure digital platform that requires users to establish an account and  verify
their identity.  While  some information may already exist in Coast Guard systems, the initial
setup process helps ensure  records are accurate, secure, and  associated  with the correct
mariner.  Once your account is established, that information can be reused for future
transactions, helping reduce paperwork and  improve the overall credentialing experience.

### 15. Will mariners need a Login.gov account to use Navita™?

Navita™ uses Login.gov to provide a secure sign-in experience  and  protect user information.
Mariners will be guided through the account creation and identity verification process before
accessing Navita™  services.

### 16. Why are mariners being asked to create a new account?

Navita™ is a new credentialing platform that allows mariners to securely submit, track, and
manage credentialing transactions online.  Creating  an account ensures your information is
properly linked to your credentialing record and helps support a more efficient user experience
moving forward.
