# NMC approved course list, extract: MODU, fishing and Rivers courses (30 Sep 2026)

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/courses/courses.pdf
Archived copy used: https://web.archive.org/web/20261004221034/https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/courses/courses.pdf

How this extract was made: the 1,047-page list was converted to text and every entry that cites 46 CFR 11.459 (Rivers), 11.462 or 11.530 (fishing officers), 11.470 to 11.474 or 11.540 to 11.544 (MODU), 46 CFR 28.270 or 28.275 (fishing drill conductors), 46 U.S.C. 4502(g), or names MODU, OIM, Barge Supervisor, Ballast Control, Fishing Industry or Rivers was pulled out. Generic courses that many schools offer (firefighting, first aid, Rules of the Road, Basic Training) are not repeated here even though fishing and MODU applicants also need them. Text conversion can split or merge entries, so treat school names and course codes as a guide and confirm on the live list.

## What the list shows, in short

- MODU (OIM, Barge Supervisor, Ballast Control Operator): the list has two kinds of course. (1) Stability courses that meet the CFR stability course requirement (Gulf Coast Training Technologies, Martin International, G. R. DiZinno Consulting, Maritime Training Online). (2) Exam-substitute courses that replace the Coast Guard exam for OIM, Barge Supervisor and BCO when taken within one year of applying (Gulf Coast Training Technologies, Martin International, G. R. DiZinno Consulting). Stability and exam courses are separate approvals: several entries say the exam course does not meet the stability requirement, and the stability course does not replace the exam. Survival suit and survival craft courses that cite 11.470 to 11.474 are listed for University of Louisiana at Lafayette (Marine Survival Training Center) and RelyOn Nutec. Dutton's Navigation has a Rig Moving for Jack-Ups course that counts as two supervised rig moves of bottom bearing units plus 7 days of bottom bearing service. All MODU providers on the list are in Louisiana or Texas.
- IADC and OPITO: neither name appears anywhere in the list. Nothing on the list says an IADC-accredited or OPITO-accredited course or certificate meets a US MODU endorsement requirement. Only the Coast Guard approved courses below do.
- MODU engineers (Chief Engineer MODU, Assistant Engineer MODU, 11.540 to 11.544): no course on the list cites these sections. The exam is taken at a Regional Exam Center.
- Master or Mate of uninspected fishing industry vessels (11.462): no exam-substitute course is listed for an original or raise of grade. Crawford Nautical Training (trading as "Eat on the Wild Side", Washington) has a Master/Mate Fishing (Near Coastal) course that counts only as refresher training for renewal or reissuance, or as 6 days of sea service. Many schools' Oceans Navigation/Celestial Navigation (500/1600 GRT) courses list "Master or Mate of Uninspected Fishing Industry Vessels" among the endorsements whose scope they can raise from near coastal to oceans (for example Chesapeake Marine Training Institute, Clatsop Community College, Downeast Maritime, Crawford Nautical Training, MITAGS, Maritime Institute, Maritime Professional Training, Northeast Maritime Institute, Practical Navigator Training, Quality Maritime Training, STAR Center).
- Chief Engineer of uninspected fishing industry vessels (11.530): one exam-substitute course, at Maritime Professional Training (Florida), for holders of DDE 1,000 HP or more, limited to 4,000 HP.
- Fishing vessel operator training under 46 U.S.C. 4502(g): no implementing regulation exists (see fishing-vessel-operator-training-4502g-status.md), but the NMC has accepted three courses as meeting parts of the 4502(g)(2)(A) training content: AMSEA Fishing Vessel Stability (stability), NPFVOA Fishing Vessel Stability (stability portion of section 604 of the 2010 Act) and NPFVOA Survival, Drill, Damage Control and Fire (firefighting, damage control, survival and drills). These are "accepted" courses, not "approved" courses, and taking them is not yet required by any regulation.
- Fishing drill conductor courses (46 CFR 28.270(c)): AMSEA, NPFVOA, Seafarers Worldwide Career Training and Maine Maritime Academy are listed. The Maine Maritime entry still shows an expiry of 31 Mar 2022 in its text, so it may be out of date on the list itself.
- Rivers (Master or Mate of Rivers, 11.459): no exam-substitute course is listed. The only entry citing 11.459 is the Louisiana State Board of River Port Pilots Commissioners Apprentice Pilot program, which documents the service for master of river steam and motor vessels with a first class pilot endorsement. Radar Observer (Rivers) courses are listed at Chesapeake Marine Training Institute, Mountwest Maritime Academy, Sea School, The River School and West Kentucky Community and Technical College. Other courses that mention Western Rivers (for example The River School and Mountwest Maritime Academy Apprentice Mate (Steersman) on Western Rivers, Sea School Inland to Western Rivers) are towing vessel courses, not Rivers deck officer courses.

## MODU entries (OIM, Barge Supervisor, Ballast Control Operator)

### Dutton's Navigation Rig Moving for Jack-Ups (TX)
Any applicant who has successfully completed your Rig Moving for Jack-Ups (DUTTON-748) course will: Be credited with successfully directing, while under the supervision of an experienced rig mover, two (2) rig moves of bottom bearing units that may be applied toward the requirements of: 46 CFR 11.470(b)(3)(ii) for an endorsement as Offshore Installation Manager (OIM) Unrestricted; OR 46 CFR 11.470(j)(1)(i)(B) for an endorsement as OIM bottom bearing units underway; OR 46 CFR 11.470(j)(1)(ii)(C) for an endorsement as OIM bottom bearing units underway; OR 46 CFR 11.470(k)(2) for an endorsement as OIM bottom bearing units underway; AND Receive seven (7) days of sea service credit on bottom bearing units.
