  Objective& Background  :
  The document is for use throughout the National Maritime Center (NMC)  , forthe purpose of
  creating consistency when  determining what fees are due and whe  ther an application  requires a
  drug test. IMPORTANT  , the modification tables provide  information  to be usedwhen an
  applicant already holds a credential.  The below fee table from 46 CFR 10.219 outlines fees for
  all endorsements .
  The document includes four  tables .The tables take into consideration Marine Safety Manual
  (MSM) Volume III  Part A Chapter  1Figure  s 1-  2  and 1-  3,on pages A1-  21 and A1-  22, while
  speaking to endorsements that did not exist at the time the MSM table was created.
  For fees, refer to Fees Table from 46 CFR 10.219.
  For modification of deck officers, refer to the Deck Officer Table.
  For modification of  engine officers, refer to the Engine Officer Table.
  For modification  of  ratings, refer to the Ratings Table .
