Subj: RENEWAL OF ENDORSEMENTS AS RADAR OBSERVER
1. Purpose. This policy letter provides guidance for renewal of endorsements as Radar
Observer (Unlimited), Radar Observer (Inland), and Radar Observer (Rivers).
2. Action. The Coast Guard will use 46 CFR 11.480 and this policy as guidance when
evaluating whether mariners have qualified to renew a radar observer endorsement.
3. Directives Affected. None.
4. Background. The Coast Guard published a final rule titled "Amendments to the Marine
Radar Observer Refresher Training Regulations." The rule amends the requirements for
renewal of a radar observer endorsement for mariners who have at least one year (360 days)
of relevant service in the previous five years or have taught a Coast Guard approved or
accepted radar course at least twice within the past five years.
5. Discussion.
a. Under the new rule, to renew a radar observer endorsement, a mariner must provide
evidence of one of the following:
1) Completing an appropriate approved or accepted original, refresher, or recertification
course (46 CFR 11.480(d));
2) Meeting the requirements in 46 CFR 10.227(e)(1)(v) of having instructed a Coast
Guard approved or accepted radar course at least twice within the past five years (46
CFR 11.480(f));
3) Having at least one year of relevant sea service within the last five years in a position
that routinely uses radar for navigation and collision avoidance purposes (46 CFR
11.480(g)).
b. Mariners will be considered to have met the requirement in 46 CFR 11.480(g) for
relevant service routinely using radar for navigation or collision avoidance if they have
at least one year of service as master or mate in the previous five years that was obtained

on the following vessels that are required to be fitted with radar, tonnage thresholds
expressed in terms of "gross tons" are applied using GRT, if assigned, and GT ITC if
GRT is not assigned:
1) Tankships of 1,600 gross tons or more subject to inspection under 46 CFR
Subchapter D in ocean or coastwise service (46 CFR 32.15-30);
2) Passenger vessels of 1,600 gross tons or more subject to inspection under 46 CFR
Subchapter H in ocean or coastwise service (46 CFR 77.09-1);
3) Cargo and miscellaneous vessels of 1,600 gross tons or more subject to inspection
under 46 CFR Subchapter I in ocean or coastwise service (46 CFR 96.25-1);
4) Offshore supply vessels (OSVs) over 100 gross tons subject to inspection under 46
CFR Subchapter L (46 CFR 130.310);
5) Public nautical school ships of 1,600 gross tons or more subject to inspection under
46 CFR Subchapter R in ocean or coastwise service (46 CFR 167.40-40); and
6) Oceanographic research vessels of 1,600 gross tons or more subject to inspection
under 46 CFR Subchapter U over in ocean or coastwise service (46 CFR 195.17-1).
c. Mariners who have served on vessels other than those listed in paragraph (b) above, or
in capacities other than master or mate should provide documentation of their relevant
service on a vessel equipped with radar in a position in which they routinely used radar
for navigation and collision avoidance purposes.
d. Mariners who have served as a pilot who are seeking to renew a radar observer
endorsement should provide a letter from a pilot's association indicating that the vessels
piloted were equipped with radar, and that radar was used by the pilot for navigation and
collision avoidance purposes (46 CFR 10.232(a)(6)).
e. Service on vessels that do not get underway or occasionally get underway for short
voyages will only be credited for days the vessel was underway.
f.
Mariners who cannot provide evidence of serving as an instructor in an approved or
accepted radar course or of having at least one year of relevant service in the previous
five years must provide evidence of having completed an approved or accepted original,
recertification, or refresher course appropriate to the radar observer endorsement being
renewed (46 CFR 11.480(d)) within five years of application submission.
g. Mariners applying for renewal of an MMC who do not provide evidence of meeting the
renewal requirements of 46 CFR 11.480(d), (f), or (g) will not have a radar observer
endorsement placed on their MMC (46 CFR 11.480(h).
h. Mariners may have a radar endorsement added or restored to their MMC at any time
upon application to the National Maritime Center.
i. This rule will become effective on July 22, 2019.

j. Until July 22, 2024, a mariner may serve in a position in which they are required by 46
CFR 15.815(a), (b), or (c) to hold an endorsement as Radar Observer by presenting
evidence of completion of an appropriate Coast Guard-approved radar course within the
previous 5 years. An appropriate course will be one approved for a Radar Observer
endorsement that is valid for the waters upon which the vessel is operating (unlimited,
inland, or rivers). After July 22, 2024, all mariners must hold an endorsement as Radar
Observer in order to serve as:
1) Deck officer, including Master, on a vessel that is over 300 GRT and required to be
fitted with radar;
2) Pilot on a vessel that is over 300 GRT and required to be fitted with radar; or
3) Master or Mate onboard a towing vessel of 8 meters (26 feet) or more in length
equipped with radar.

Coast Guard, and other Federal and State regulators in applying statutory and regulatory
requirements the guidance is not a substitute for applicable legal requirements nor is it itself
a regulation.
