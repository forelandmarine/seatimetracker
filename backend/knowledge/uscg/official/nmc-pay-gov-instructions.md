# NMC Pay.gov payment instructions

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/fees/pay_gov_instructions.pdf

> How to pay MMC fees online.


[p. 1]

  Pay.gov Instructions

Please use the following instructions to submit payment for your credential via Pay.gov.  When
submitting your application, please ensure that you include a copy of your receipt.

1.  If you are using a mobile device, go to Pay.  gov website.Using the search function  ,  search
  for Merchant Mariner Credential, and click the magnify icon.  Disregard the Filter by
  Agency  results and scroll down to USCG Merchant Mariner User Fee Payment and click
  Continue.

2.  If you are using a desktop device, go to Pay.  gov website.  Using the search  function,  search
  for Merchant Mariner Credential, and click the magnify icon.  Scroll down to USCG
  Merchant Mariner User Fee Payment (usually the first search result) and click
  Continue.

  The USCG Merchant Mariner User Fee Payment form appears.

3.  Read the Before You Begin  section,  and then click Continue to the Form  (at the
  bottom right of the web page).

  The Complete Agency Form appears.

[p. 2]

4.  Enter all  * Denotes required fields information under Applicant/Mariner Details.

5.  Select the appropriate payment type from the drop-down menus under Please select
  what you need to pay for.

[p. 3]

6.  Click the box that states “I authorize NMC to apply my payment towards Evaluation,
  Examination then Issuance, in that order.”  Verify the fees are correct,  and click Continue
  (at  the bottom of the page).

NOTE 1:  By checking the authorization box, you allow us to adjust your payment(s) to ensure
the correct evaluation fee is applied.  If the fees you submitted do not include the required
evaluation fee, we will combine your payments to cover it—provided this does not result in a
refund being owed to you or leave unused funds that are not enough to fully pay the evaluation
fee.

NOTE 2:  Not all applications require an examination fee.  If a fee is applicable, it can be paid
at the same time as your evaluation fee, or you can choose to pay it prior to going to the
Regional Exam Center (REC)  to test.  If you choose to pay the examination fee at a later date,
select I will pay my exam fees later:  $0.00  from the Examination/Testing Fees  drop-
down menu.

NOTE 3:  The issuance fee can be paid at the same time as your evaluation fee, or you can
choose to pay it at a later date.  If you choose to pay the issuance fee at a later date, select I will
pay my MMC issuance fees later:  $0.00  from the Issuance Fees  drop-down menu.
Though you do not have to pay the issuance fee at the time you apply, your completed
credential cannot be mailed until this fee is paid.

7.  Once you’ve clicked Continue,  you will  be taken  to the Payment Information page.

8.  Use your bank account or debit/credit card to  submit payment.  Choose your method of
  payment and click Next  .

9.  Fill  in required account information and click  Review and Submit Payment.

10.  Once you click Review and Submit Payment,  you will  be provided  with a summary of
  your payment.  If the information is correct, click Submit Payment .

11.  You  will  be provided  with  a receipt page.  Save a copy for  your records and print another
  receipt for your application packet.

NOTE:  You MUST send a copy of your receipt with your application.
