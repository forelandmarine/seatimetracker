# NMC Medical Certificate page: CG-719K and CG-719K/E, color vision documentation

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/nmc/medical_certificate/

> How to apply for a mariner medical certificate and what colour vision evidence is accepted.


## Apply For Medical Certificate

### Medical Certificate

The medical certificate is a document that serves as proof that a mariner meets the [required medical and physical standards](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?c=ecfr&sid=ae63e8c8b8556b036cfde87e4a7bb8de&rgn=div5&view=text&node=46:1.0.1.2.10&idno=46#se46.1.10_1301). In order to receive a Merchant Mariner Credential or Medical Certificate you will need to complete the correct applications.

### CG-719K

- Step 1 - Review the [Common Errors on the CG-719K Form](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/medical/common_errors_719K.pdf) & [Medical Certificate FAQ](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/med_cert_faq.pdf)
- Step 2 - Complete the **Application for Merchant Mariner Medical Certificate (CG-719K).**
- Step 3 - [Apply](https://navita.uscg.mil).

### CG-719K/E “Entry Level Only”

- Step 1 - Review the [Medical Certificate FAQ](https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/faq/med_cert_faq.pdf)
- Step 2 - Complete the **Application for Merchant Mariner Medical Certificate for Entry Level Ratings (CG-719K/E “Entry Level Only” )**.
- Step 3 - [Apply](https://navita.uscg.mil).

### Duplicate

- Step 1 - Contact the Customer Service Center.
- Step 2 - A representative will verify your information.
- Step 3 - Your Medical Certificate will be resent as appropriate.
- Note: No affidavit or new application is required. Please allow adequate time for delivery before requesting a duplicate.

### Additional Information (Existing Medical Certificate Application)

- Step 1 - Combine all documentation into a single PDF file.
- Step 2 - [Submit](https://navita.uscg.mil)
