1.  PURPOSE. This policy letter addresses the impact the temporary reduction in sea service
  requirements enacted in reference (a) has on qualification for STCW deck rating endorsements.  It
  provides guidance on how mariner  s can work towards an endorsement as Able Seafarer  -Deck (AS -
  D) and describes several  options depending upon the amount of sea service the mariner has. It also
  provides guidance on the evaluation of sea service for applicants obtaining Ratings Forming Part of
  a Navigational Watch (RFPNW) while working towards an endorsement as AS-D.

2.  ACTION. The Coast Guard revises  the requirements of references ( b)-(d)  with this policy  when
  evaluating applications for a  merchant mariner credential ( MMC) with a n STCW endorsement as
  AS-D or RFPNW.

3.  DIRECTIVES AFFECTED. Implementation of the Guidelines on Qualification for STCW
  Endorsements as Able Seafarer-Deck (NVIC 14-14) is temporarily revised.

### 4. BACKGROUND.

  a.  Section 3534(j) of reference (a) temporarily reduces the sea service requirements for national
  able seaman (AB) rating endorsements. The Coast Guard published CG-  MMC Policy Letter 02-
  24, Temporary Reduction in Service Requirements for Certain Deck Ratings  , reducing service
  requirements for AB. This  policy letter will make a  temporary reductionand  is effective until
  December 22, 2026, consistent with Policy Letter 02  -24.

  b.  The change in sea service requirements for national AB  endorsements impacts the qualification
  process for STCW deck rating endorsements because the regulations and  reference (c) assume a

[p. 2]

  longer period of service in order to qualify for the various national endorsements as AB. This
  policy letter allows for service toward RFPNW and AS-D to accrue concurrently.

5.  DISCUSSION. The Coast Guard will review  MMC  applications for STCW deck ratings for AS  -D and
  RFPNW who have existing service towards ratings. Because holders of national endorsements may
  have qualified under reduced temporary statutory provisions, the Coast Guard will consider the total
  service toward STCW deck ratings when evaluating applications. Until December 22, 2026, t  he
  service gained towards RFPNW and AS- D will be evaluated  and concurrently counted towards those
  STCW endorsements. The evaluation will be completed as follows:

  a.  For those seeking RFPNW  with at least 2 months of service and less than 6 months of service:
  1.  Complete approved training for RFPNW, 12.605(a)(2)(ii).
  2.  Complete a ssessments for RFPNW.
  3.  May obtain endorsements as RFPNW
  b.  For those seeking RFPNW with  at least  6 months of service but less than 12 months of service in
  accordance with 46 CFR 12.605(a)(2)(i) :
  1.  Complete a ssessments for RFPNW.
  2.  Complete approved course for either PSC or PSC-Limited.
  3.  May obtain endorsements as RFPNW, and PSC or PSC-Limited.
  c.  For those seeking AS -D with at least 12 months service but less than 18 months of service:
  1.  Complete a ssessments for RFPNW and AS-D.
  2.  Meet the qualifications for PSC or PSC-Limited.
  3.  Complete approved training per 46 CFR 12.603(a)(3)(ii).
  4.  May obtain endorsement as AS-D, RFPNW,  and PSC or PSC  -Limited.
  d.  For those seeking AS -D with 18 months or more of service:
  1.  Complete a ssessments for RFPNW and AS-D.
  2.  Meet the qualifications for PSC or PSC-Limited
  3.  May obtain endorsements as AS-D, RFPNW, and PSC or PSC -Limited.
  e.  Applications in current inventory. The concurrent credit towards RFPNW and AS  -D must be
  applied to all applications that have not reached final agency action  . The NMC will apply these
  new criteria without requiring a new or amended application. A new application will be required if
  the MMC has already been issued or if the application has reached final agency action .

  f.  Crediting service for approved training  . Service credit for the approved training in paragraphs 5.a-d
  above will be credited in accordance with the Coast Guard-issued approval per 46 CFR Part 10,
  subpart D.

  g.  National endorsements. An applicant for theseSTCW endorsement  s must hold the appropriate
  national endorsements.  Applicants for RFPNW must hold either Ordinary Seaman or Able
  Seaman. Applicants for AS-D must hold Able Seaman.
