  Frequently Asked Questions:  Forms

### 1. I am filling out CG Form 719B. Can I apply for more than one endorsement?

Yes.  You can apply for multiple endorsements on one application.  When filling out this form,
make sure you check all applicable boxes under Section II  and provide all appropriate
information under the box for “Description of the Endorsement(s) Desired”.

2.  I am completing my conviction statement.  What information do I need
  to provide?

Please use Form CG-719C to disclose convictions.  If you do not have access to the form,
provide a signed and dated statement explaining the circumstances and outcome of the
conviction, including when the conviction occurred and the state in which it occurred.  If you
have already disclosed all convictions on a prior application, you do not need to disclose them
again.

### 3. I recently completed a physical. Can I submit the form the doctor used for this?

No.  The Coast Guard will only accept physicals that are completed on CG F orms 719K or
719K/E.

4.  Who can complete and sign CG F orm 719K or 719K/E?  Can more than one person
  sign it?

A U.S. licensed physician (medical doctor [MD] or d octor of o steopathy [DO]), certified
physician assistant, or nurse practitioner may sign the physical.  If you have more than one
practitioner signing off on the physical, be sure each provides their office address, office phone
number, license number, full name, date, and signature.

5.  My doctor needs to supply amplifying information.  Does it have to be on an
  approved  form?

No.  There is no form required when submitting amplifying information from your doctor.
Your doctor can use any format as long as it has the required information.

### 6. When completing the Drug Testing requirement, do I have to use CG Form 719P?

No.  Page 2 of CG F  orm 719P lists all acceptable alternatives.  You can also provide copy 2 of a
federal custody and control form.

8.  I am completing CG Form 719S.  What does it mean when it says, “seaward of the
  boundary line per 46 CFR Part 7”?

Boundary lines are defined in 46 CFR Part 7.  “Seaward”  means you are beyond the boundary
line and in Near Coastal waters.  The “boundary line” is generally a line drawn between the
most seaward points of land at the entrances to rivers, harbors, bays, etc.  This line will vary by
location.

### 9. Can I document more than one vessel on CG F orm 719S?

No.  You will need to use a different CG -719S for each vessel.

10.  When completing CG F orm 719S, why can’t I sign and attest to my own sea time?  I
  was the m  aster of the vessel.

Regulation 46 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) 10.232  and policy states if you do not own
the vessel, you may not attest to your own service.  If you do own the vessel, you may sign the
form and  show proof that you own the vessel.
