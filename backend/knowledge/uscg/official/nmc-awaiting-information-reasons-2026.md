# NMC Awaiting Information (AI) reasons and delays, September 2026 (why applications get held up)

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/reports/reasons/ai_reasons.pdf

> Counts of 'Awaiting Information' letters by reason and NMC section. The biggest single cause in Professional Qualification Evaluation is the sea service form.


## AI reasons by NMC section, September 2026

An Awaiting Information (AI) letter means the NMC could not finish evaluating an application and needs more from the mariner. The counts below are from the NMC chart 'General AI Reasons', September 2026.

### Regional Exam Centers (REC)

| Reason | AI letters |
|---|---|
| Fees | 72 |
| Drug test | 68 |
| Application | 44 |
| Documentation | 41 |
| TWIC | 11 |
| Statement | 9 |

### Safety and Suitability Evaluation (SSE)

| Reason | AI letters |
|---|---|
| Documentation | 42 |
| TWIC issues | 16 |
| Info from mariner | 6 |

### Medical Evaluation Branch

| Reason | AI letters |
|---|---|
| AI from physician | 1,429 |
| Incomplete physical | 69 |

### Professional Qualification Evaluation (PQE)

| Reason | AI letters |
|---|---|
| Sea service form (Sea Svc Form) | 1,060 |
| Certificates | 929 |
| Application | 418 |
| STCW | 357 |
| Documentation | 314 |
| Fees | 148 |
| Sea service | 86 |
| Info from mariner | 27 |

## AI delay, calendar year 2026 to September

From the NMC chart 'AI Delay by NMC Section' (https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/reports/delay/ai_delay.pdf). Delay is the average number of days an application waited for the mariner to send the missing information. It does not include time waiting to take an exam or NMC processing time.

| NMC section | AI volume (CY2026) | Average AI delay (days) |
|---|---|---|
| REC (regional exam centers) | 1,449 | 14.35 |
| SSE (safety and suitability) | 384 | 12.68 |
| PQE (professional qualifications) | 9,224 | 28.41 |
| MED (medical) | 4,058 | 37.97 |

Note: figures checked against the rendered chart. The same report shows monthly average AI delay of roughly 12 to 30 days for MMC applications and 24 to 56 days for medical certificates during 2026.

The NMC adds: "This website contains information about avoiding AI when an application is submitted. Look for links, frequently asked questions, and tips on each webpage."
