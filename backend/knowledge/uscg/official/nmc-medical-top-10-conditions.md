# NMC Medical: Top 10 medical conditions needing further review

Source: https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/NMC/pdfs/medical/top_10_medical_conditions.pdf

> Conditions that most often need extra documents.


[p. 1]

National Maritime Center
Serving  Our Nation’s Mariners

  Top 10 Medical Conditions

Listed below are medical conditions that can delay an application for credentials or cause an
application to be denied.  If you have one of these conditions, please review recommended
evaluation guidelines,  Merchant Mariner Medical Manual, Commandant Instruction Manual
(COMDTINST M16721.48), Table of Contents, Chapters 9 -  25,  with your treating medical
provider so that he/she may  address the issue.

  1.  Cardiovascular Conditions

  2.  Diabetes

  3.  Psychiatric Disorders

  4.  Sleep Disorders

  5.  Chronic Use of Impairing Medications

  6.  Impaired Hearing

  7.  Alcohol/Drug Abuse

  8.  Seizures

  9.  Vision Requirements

  10. Pulmonary Conditions

It is extremely important that you provide supporting medical documentation from your
treating medical provider if you have one or more of the above-mentioned medical
conditions.

Additional Information (AI):  An AI letter requests additional information from the
mariner in order to clarify a medical diagnosis, condition,  or specific medication use.  The
National Maritime Center (NMC) may request history, current status report, diagnostic testing,
etc., to obtain a more precise picture of the mariner’s health and fitness.

The majority (90 percent) of physical examination forms (CG-  719K) received at the NMC are
submitted with little or no information clarifying the noted diagnosis, condition and/or
medication.

[p. 2]

National Maritime Center
Serving  Our Nation’s Mariners

The NMC Medical Evaluation Division is responsible for evaluating fitness for certification  of
all merchant mariners in order to minimize safety risk to the Mariner Transportation System.
Without the requested additional information, the NMC is unable to make an appropriate
determination as to the mariner’s fitness for certification .  It is imperative that the mariner
submit a complete package of all requested information to the NMC to avoid further delay.
