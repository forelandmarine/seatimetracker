## Remaining gaps and why

1. Sample exam question PDFs (about 260 module PDFs linked from the NMC Examinations page) are not stored, by design. Exam codes, module topics, passing scores (approval letter sample and provider notes), retest rules (Marine Safety Manual) and the calculator policy are stored, so "how do exams work" is now answered; the assistant should point to the NMC Examinations page for practice questions.
2. The weekly approved course list (`courses.pdf`, 1,047 pages, 30 Sep 2026 edition) is still not stored in full (third pass: the MODU, fishing and Rivers entries are extracted in `nmc-approved-courses-rivers-fishing-modu-extract-2026.md`). `nmc-approved-course-list-how-to-check.md` now explains how the list works, so the assistant can tell a mariner how to check, but it cannot say whether a named school's course is approved today.
3. Medical: CLOSED for content. The CG-719K and CG-719K/E texts (04/17 editions) were recovered from web.archive.org. Open point: the May 2026 OMB notice says revised editions of all CG-719 forms are coming, and Navita now presents the form online; the newer layout was not found publicly.
4. Navita portal content and application status pages are behind a login. Public Navita material (NMC page, FAQ, user guide, Maritime Commons posts, launch press) is stored. No official post-launch processing time had been published by 25 Oct 2026: the NMC home page (12 Oct capture) still lists the 21 Jul 2026 estimate (about 4 months), and the 2026 monthly performance and awaiting-information PDFs return HTTP 403 with no archived copy after April 2026. No GAO report, congressional hearing or Federal Register notice on post-Navita processing was found, and the Federal Register shows no MERPAC (National Merchant Marine Personnel Advisory Committee) meeting notice since Jan 2025, so there are no recent MERPAC minutes to use. Third pass: UNOFFICIAL reports (gCaptain forum, AMO, a gCaptain editorial) are in `providers/navita-processing-reports-after-launch-2026.md`: two quick medical certificates (6 days, 3 weeks), no MMC renewal or upgrade filed in Navita reported as issued by 20 Oct 2026. Reddit could not be searched (blocked). The Navita listening session is 19 Nov 2026.
5. Rivers: still PARTIAL. No separate NMC river checklist, NVIC, policy letter or provider guidance was found; 46 CFR 11.459 points to the inland and Great Lakes tables. Fishing, MODU deck and MODU engineer endorsements: confirmed from the USCG NVIC subject lists that no officer training NVIC exists, so the CFR, checklists and exam modules are the whole official answer. 46 U.S.C. 4502(g) (fishing vessel operator training) has no implementing rule; third pass confirmed this from the 2016 NPRM (training deliberately left out), the Federal Register (no later rule) and the Fall 2024 Unified Agenda, and found NCFSAC 2024 tasks on operator competency with no published outcome (`fishing-vessel-operator-training-4502g-status.md`). IADC and OPITO: no Coast Guard acceptance found anywhere; they do not appear on the NMC course list.
6. Marine Safety Manual Vol III is CH-2 (2017) and the PDF itself warns it may not be current; only the crew-relevant chapters were stored. Use CFR and policy letters first.
7. Six scanned policy letters (CG-543 11-12, CG-CVC 11-11 and 14-03, CG-MMC 01-20 and 03-19, NMC 1-2000) are OCR text and may contain recognition errors.
8. FCC radio operator licences (GMDSS GOC, MROP) are issued by the FCC, not the USCG, and are not covered.
9. Time-limited items to recheck: the AB sea service reductions are now permanent law (Pub. L. 119-60, 18 Dec 2025), but PL 02-24, PL 04-24, the AB FAQ, the AB-Deck checklist and 46 CFR 12.403 still show the old wording, and the concurrent RFPNW/AB-Deck credit in PL 04-24 (policy, not statute) still ends 22 Dec 2026 unless the Coast Guard updates it; the Oct 2026 NPRM (91 FR 62383) may become a final rule after comments close 30 Dec 2026; violence and harassment training in STCW Basic Training applies from 1 Oct 2029 (PL 01-26); CG-MMC PL 02-21 may be cancelled now that the passenger-ship training rule took effect on 26 Oct 2026; NMC processing estimates change often.
10. Conflicts inside official text: the NMC REC/MU FAQ (Jan 2026) says one non-programmable calculator, while the Dec 2024 calculator policy linked from the Examinations page allows cleared programmable calculators. Both are flagged.
11. Provider gaps: time-and-a-half for two-watch vessels is now covered (WorkBoat). Yacht crew: covered by MPT, Dockwalk and The Log; the US Superyacht Association, The Triton and Dockwalk news search found nothing current on USCG credentials for yacht crew, and there is no USCG "Master of Yachts" endorsement (that is an MCA certificate). Still blocked or not found: SIU and MARAD career guides (HTTP 403, no usable web.archive.org copies), Maritime Commons posts after June 2026 other than the 29 Sep 2026 PL 01-26 notice, and MM&P and MEBA credentialing pages (not searched in depth; MEBA's hosted copy of the NMC rejection notice returned 404, so the AMO reprint and the archived NMC original are used).
