# Toi Ohomai: paraphrased fact notes

Notes in our own words from Toi Ohomai course pages read on 2026-10-25. Toi Ohomai is a Maritime NZ approved provider for SRL, ADH-F and STCW basic courses. Fees are indicative.

## Q: Is the SRL course the replacement for the old ILM and LLO tickets?
Yes. Toi Ohomai says its SRL course replaced the old Inshore Launchmaster (ILM) and Local Launch Operator (LLO) courses. ILM and LLO are pre-SeaCert certificates; holders who did not ring-fence or transition can no longer use them (see the official ring-fencing page).
Source: https://www.toiohomai.ac.nz/study/course/new-zealand-certificate-domestic-maritime-operations-skipper-restricted-limits-level-4

## Q: Can my sea time for SRL be on my own boat?
Toi Ohomai says you must gain your sea time and complete the Seafarer Training Record Book yourself, on a commercial or recreational vessel, and if it is a recreational vessel you must be the skipper. This matches Part 32 Table 3 (200 hours, pleasure craft only counts when acting as skipper). Toi Ohomai also says you should meet the full Maritime NZ sea time, medical and eyesight requirements before starting if you want the licence at the end.
Source: https://www.toiohomai.ac.nz/study/course/new-zealand-certificate-domestic-maritime-operations-skipper-restricted-limits-level-4

## Q: How is the Toi Ohomai SRL course run?
It is 28 weeks part-time (85 credits) from Tauranga, starting with online study and then a block course (for example online start 27 October 2026, block 15 February 2027). Topics: STCW elementary first aid, navigation, electronic navigation, machinery and systems, safety and compliance, and the MRROC radio certificate. Indicative fee about NZD 2,280 for domestic students. Entry needs 40 NCEA Level 1 credits including literacy and numeracy, or the Level 3 Domestic Maritime Crewing certificate, or an equivalent.
Source: https://www.toiohomai.ac.nz/study/course/new-zealand-certificate-domestic-maritime-operations-skipper-restricted-limits-level-4

## Q: What can I do with SRL?
Toi Ohomai describes SRL as for skippers of vessels up to 12 m (24 m with an endorsement) carrying up to 19 passengers, going up to 12 nautical miles off the coast, and lists roles such as work boats, water taxis and small ferries under 12 m.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (wording): Part 32 rule 32.43 limits SRL passenger and non-passenger work to restricted limits (enclosed and inshore limits). The 12 nm inshore fishing limit applies only to SRL fishing vessel work. A non-fishing SRL skipper cannot go out to 12 nm unless that water is within an inshore limit.
Source: https://www.toiohomai.ac.nz/study/course/new-zealand-certificate-domestic-maritime-operations-skipper-restricted-limits-level-4

## Q: How do I start as deck crew (QDC)?
The Level 3 Domestic Maritime Crewing certificate is 12 weeks full time (Monday to Thursday) in Tauranga, minimum age 16, about NZD 2,950. It includes STCW basic training (STCW-10) and MRROC. Toi Ohomai says finishing it is a prerequisite for applying for QDC and for completing a Maritime NZ Training Record Book.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (minor): Part 32 Table 2 does not require any course for QDC; the requirements are a completed approved TRB, first aid and RROC, plus medical and any exam. Skipper Training NZ also states no course is needed.
Source: https://www.toiohomai.ac.nz/study/course/new-zealand-certificate-domestic-maritime-crewing-level-3

## Q: How do I become an Advanced Deckhand Fishing (ADH-F)?
The Level 3 Fishing Vessel Crewing certificate is 50 weeks part-time (about NZD 1,540) and you must be working in the fishing industry. It covers STCW basic safety training, fishing deck work, navigation support, vessel stability and seamanship, and leads to the Maritime NZ ADH-F application.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: the page calls ADH-F an "endorsement" and a "Certificate of Competency". Part 32 rule 32.150 says ADH-F is a certificate of proficiency. The page's intake section also mentions the SRL Training Record Book and nautical and engineering oral exams, which look copied from the SRL course; the ADH-F route uses the ADH-F Training Record Book (Part 32 Table 38: 6 months on a fishing vessel beyond enclosed limits plus the TRB).
Source: https://www.toiohomai.ac.nz/study/course/new-zealand-certificate-fishing-vessel-crewing-level-3
