# IMINZ / NMIT: paraphrased fact notes

Notes in our own words from NMIT pages read on 2026-10-25. IMINZ (part of NMIT, Nelson) is a Maritime NZ approved training provider and has trained seafarers since 1976 (first as the NZ School of Fisheries). Check official/ for binding requirements. Fees are NMIT's indicative figures.

## Q: Where can I train for fishing deck certificates?
NMIT runs the full fishing ladder in Nelson: Mate Fishing Vessel Limited (Level 4), Skipper Fishing Vessel Limited (Level 5), Mate Fishing Vessel Unlimited and Skipper Fishing Vessel Unlimited (Level 6 diploma and its skipper strand). It says it works closely with Sealord and Talley's, the two largest fishing companies.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/study-areas/maritime-commercial-fishing

## Q: What do I need before the Mate Fishing Vessel Limited course?
It suits deckhands on fishing vessels. If you already hold Advanced Deckhand Fishing (ADH-F) you can go straight in. If not, NMIT says you must complete a Training Record Book at sea before attending, and it supplies the TRB free on request. The course is 24 weeks in total with a 15 to 18 week block in Nelson before your Maritime NZ final exam; 2026 fee about NZD 8,600 plus a student services fee.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (minor): NMIT describes MFV Limited as covering coastal and offshore limits "up to 200 nm". Part 32 rule 32.153 says MFV Limited is mate on fishing vessels under 45 m in inshore fishing limits and limited waters, and watchkeeper on fishing vessels of any length in any area. "Limited waters" is the Part 20 term for coastal plus offshore (out to the EEZ), so the 200 nm figure is an approximation.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/maritime-operations-mate-fishing-vessel-limited

## Q: What do I need for Mate Fishing Vessel Unlimited?
NMIT says the diploma is for people who have worked offshore on a fishing vessel of 12 m or more for at least 24 months and want to move into unlimited waters. It includes the STCW basic safety training, advanced firefighting, PISC and rescue boats, and GMDSS radio operator. Entry asks for some maths and English credits, an equivalent, evidence of ability, or an NZ Offshore Watchkeeper or NZ Offshore Master certificate. 2026 fee about NZD 12,750.
Note: Part 32 sets MFV-U sea service by reference to STCW-F regulation II/2; check the Maritime NZ MFV-U guideline for the exact figure.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/maritime-operations-mate-fishing-vessel-unlimited

## Q: How do I step up from MFV Unlimited to Skipper Fishing Vessel Unlimited?
NMIT says return after MFV-U and at least 12 months working offshore on a fishing vessel of 12 m or more, and complete the skipper strand (navigation and command, legislation, fishing vessel management and operations, command competency, medical first aid). No start dates were listed in October 2026.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/maritime-operations-skipper-fishing-vessel-unlimited

## Q: What does the Skipper Fishing Vessel Limited course need and cover?
Entry needs MFV Limited or Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT Near Coastal; an NZ Offshore Watchkeeper holder can also enrol but must add two courses (stability and cargo or fishing operations). It is 18 weeks with a 7-week Nelson block before the final exam, and includes the STCW short courses and radio certificates (with credit for ones you already hold). No start dates were listed in October 2026.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL: NMIT says SFV Limited lets you run fishing vessels under 45 m "or non-fishing vessels up to 500t" in coastal and offshore limits. Part 32 rule 32.155 gives SFV Limited command of fishing vessels under 45 m in inshore fishing limits and limited waters, and of passenger and non-passenger ships under 24 m length overall in those waters. Nothing in the rule gives non-fishing command up to 500 GT.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/maritime-operations-skipper-fishing-vessel-limited

## Q: Where can I study for Watchkeeper Deck (officer of the watch)?
NMIT's Diploma in Nautical Science (Level 6) is an approved programme for Watchkeeper Deck (STCW II/1). Duration depends on your sea time and prior qualifications; it includes a 36 to 43 week full-time block in Nelson. Intakes 15 February and 19 July 2027; 2026 fee about NZD 13,000 depending on credit. NMIT points applicants to the Maritime NZ Watchkeeper Deck guideline.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/new-zealand-diploma-in-nautical-science-level-6

## Q: Does NMIT train marine engineers for MEC 3 and MEC 4?
Yes. The Diploma in Marine Engineering Class 3 and Class 4 (Level 6) has a 30 to 36 week Nelson block, length depending on sea time and prior learning, intakes February and July 2027, 2026 fee about NZD 8,900. NMIT describes MEC 3 as an engineering officer of the watch on any ship worldwide, and MEC 4 as an STCW-F-aligned chief engineer certificate for fishing vessels up to 3000 kW. It leads on to Level 7 MEC 2 and MEC 1 study.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/diploma-in-marine-engineering-class-3-and-class-4

## Q: Does NMIT have a superyacht entry course?
Yes, a 12-week full-time Level 3 Superyacht Crewing certificate (zero fees in 2027, places limited), with STCW basic training, navigation and basic radar, tender driving, seamanship, maintenance, interior and hospitality training. It is an entry course; it does not by itself give a Maritime NZ certificate of competency.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/superyacht-crewing

## Q: What short STCW courses does NMIT run?
Its STCW page lists ECDIS, GMDSS and GMDSS refresher, advanced firefighting refresher, PISC and PISC refresher, high-voltage operational, basic tanker (oil and chemical) and medical first aid, among others.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/study-areas/maritime-offshore-operations-stcw

## Q: Where does NMIT tell students to check sea time?
NMIT's fishing pages send students to Maritime NZ's SeaCert material for how much sea time each licence needs. That material is the Part 32 rule tables and the Maritime NZ certificate guidelines in this library.
Source: https://www.nmit.ac.nz/study/programmes/maritime-operations-mate-fishing-vessel-limited
