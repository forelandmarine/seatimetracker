# SIS Training: paraphrased fact notes

Notes in our own words from SIS Training pages read on 2026-10-25. SIS is an NZQA-registered provider (since 2000) and a Maritime NZ approved provider for SRL.

## Q: Where and how can I do SRL in the South Island?
SIS runs SRL as about 19 weeks of guided self-study followed by a 4-week block course in Te Anau, Bluff or Picton, with the Maritime NZ oral exam arranged at the end. Listed 2026 to 2027 intakes include Picton (study from 23 Nov 2026, block 5 to 30 April 2027), Bluff, Te Anau and a later Picton intake. Fees shown were about NZD 1,874 to 1,968 plus GST, including the Maritime NZ exam fee; Maritime NZ application, medical and eyesight fees are extra.
Source: https://sistraining.co.nz/maritime/srl-course/

## Q: Can I do the SRL course before I turn 18?
SIS says you must be 18 to be issued SRL, but you can complete the course and the Maritime NZ oral exam before then. This matches Part 32 Table 3 (minimum age 18 to be issued).
Source: https://sistraining.co.nz/maritime/srl-course/

## Q: Does the course give me the 200 hours of sea time?
No. SIS says Maritime NZ requires at least 200 hours of sea time gained separately from the course, and that time as skipper of a recreational vessel counts. Outstanding Training Record Book tasks can be completed and signed off during the block course. SIS also prepares your documents for the Maritime NZ application and includes the passenger endorsement training module.
Source: https://sistraining.co.nz/maritime/srl-course/

## Q: How far offshore can I go with SRL?
SIS describes SRL as for vessels up to 12 m (24 m endorsed) "working up to 12 nautical miles offshore".
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (wording): Part 32 rule 32.43 limits passenger and non-passenger SRL work to restricted limits (enclosed and inshore limits); only fishing vessel work extends to the inshore fishing limit (the 12 nm territorial sea).
Source: https://sistraining.co.nz/maritime/srl-course/

## Q: How do I get the SRL <500 GT endorsement and can I choose limits?
SIS says the endorsement needs the completed Maritime NZ Training Record Book including 12 months' sea service on vessels of 12 m or more while holding SRL endorsed to 24 m, and an oral exam; you can start the online course while still gaining the service. SIS says you can choose an endorsement limited to enclosed water limits (fewer TRB tasks and no cargo work, upgradable later) or for full restricted limits. After the exam, the completed TRB goes to Maritime NZ to verify your sea service before the endorsement is issued.
Note: Part 32 rule 32.62 (to 21 Sept 2026) also allows 18 months in command of commercial ships of 12 m or more instead, and limits the 12-month option to the 5 years before applying. The enclosed-limits option is SIS's description; we did not find it in the rule text.
Source: https://sistraining.co.nz/maritime/srl-500gt/

## Q: What radio certificate do I need on a commercial vessel?
SIS says the Maritime Restricted Radio Operator Certificate (MRROC) is the minimum radio licence for commercial users, covering VHF and MF/HF, examined orally by a Radio Spectrum Examiner; the VHF Operator's Certificate is for recreational VHF only. SIS's online MRROC course costs NZD 295 plus GST including the exam fee. Part 32 lists RROC as an ancillary for QDC, S2NM and SRL.
Source: https://sistraining.co.nz/maritime/mrroc-course/

## Q: Which official documents does SIS point SRL applicants to?
The Maritime NZ SRL guideline (MS193), fit and proper person form, Part 34 eyesight test report form, the paper seafarer certificate application form and the statutory declaration for qualifying sea service. Note that since April 2026 most applications are made online, and the online form covers fit and proper person questions.
Source: https://sistraining.co.nz/maritime/srl-course/
