# MLAANZ Semaphore updates on Maritime NZ: paraphrased fact notes

Notes in our own words from two one-page MLAANZ newsletter items (March 2026 and June 2026) summarising Maritime NZ news. They repeat Maritime NZ statements and are useful for dating changes.

## Q: When did Maritime NZ start taking seafarer applications online?
MLAANZ reported in March 2026 that Maritime NZ planned to launch an online seafarer licensing application in April 2026, and in June 2026 that it had launched in April on a refreshed Maritime NZ website. At launch the digital form covered issue and renewal of Part 32 certificates and endorsements and ship's cook letters; other licences were to follow in later phases, and applicants for those should keep using the existing process.
Source: https://www.mlaanz.org/uploads/1/3/9/4/139416176/mlaanzjun2026-mnzwhatsnew.pdf

## Q: Is the online system faster?
MLAANZ passed on Maritime NZ's report that early feedback was very positive, applicants get automatic status updates and can track applications online, and applications are moving faster than before. No turnaround figures were given.
Source: https://www.mlaanz.org/uploads/1/3/9/4/139416176/mlaanzjun2026-mnzwhatsnew.pdf

## Q: Did rating requirements change in 2026?
Yes. MLAANZ noted that Maritime Rules Amendments 2025 (No 3) changed sea service options, certification pathways and training requirements for several ratings from 31 March 2026 (the vessel equipment parts took effect 28 January 2026). The official detail is in maritime-rules-amendments-2025-no-3-seafarer-extract.md.
Source: https://www.mlaanz.org/uploads/1/3/9/4/139416176/mlaanzmar2026-mnzwhatsnew.pdf

## Q: Why do some older Maritime NZ guidance links no longer work?
MLAANZ reported that the online form launched on a refreshed version of the Maritime NZ website in April 2026. Many older pages (for example the per-certificate pages under maritimenz.govt.nz/commercial/seafarer-certification/) now redirect to the new Seafarers section, although many of the old guideline PDFs are still hosted. This is our inference from the site change plus our own checks on 2026-10-25, not a statement by MLAANZ.
Source: https://www.mlaanz.org/uploads/1/3/9/4/139416176/mlaanzjun2026-mnzwhatsnew.pdf
