# Coastguard Boating Education: paraphrased fact notes

Notes in our own words. The live site showed only an "offline" notice on 2026-10-25 (online courses still at online.boatingeducation.org.nz; course enquiries to education@coastguard.nz), so these notes come from the Internet Archive copy of the commercial course page captured on 11 July 2026. Coastguard Boating Education is on Maritime NZ's approved provider list for the SRL qualification. Fees and intake dates may have changed since July 2026.

## Q: Does Coastguard run a course for Skipper Restricted Limits (SRL)?
Yes. Its "Pathway to Skipper Restricted Limits" is the NZ Certificate in Domestic Maritime Operations (Restricted Limits) (Level 4), the qualification Maritime NZ requires for SRL. Coastguard says it is part-time over 46 weeks, blended (home study, classroom and on-board practical hours), in four blocks: marine engineering, navigational aids, MRROC (radio) and maritime operations. The 2026 fee listed was NZD 1,999, with fees-free and a Coastguard scholarship as possible funding. Domestic learners only (NZ citizens or residents, and Australian citizens and residents). In July 2026 the page said the next intake was 4 May 2026 and invited registrations of interest for later intakes.
Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260711195614/https://www.boatingeducation.org.nz/commercial/

## Q: Is finishing the Coastguard course enough to get my SRL?
No. Coastguard says the qualification is a prerequisite for applying, and Maritime NZ may also require the logbook (training record book), an oral exam and ancillary certificates.
Note: the Maritime NZ online form (October 2026) lists for SRL: age 18, 200 hours on powered vessels (as skipper if on a pleasure craft), this Level 4 qualification, first aid (unit standards 6400, 6401, 6402), RROC, oral exam, TRB, a national medical and a criminal record check (see official/online-application-form-requirements-2026.md and Part 32 Table 3).
Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260711195614/https://www.boatingeducation.org.nz/commercial/

## Q: What does SRL let me skipper, according to Coastguard?
Passenger and non-passenger ships under 12 m carrying up to 19 passengers within restricted limits (enclosed and inshore), and fishing vessels under 12 m in enclosed, inshore and inshore fishing limits. Typical jobs it names are workboats, water taxis and small ferries.
Note: this matches Part 32 for the unendorsed SRL; the 24 m and 500 GT endorsements extend it.
Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260711195614/https://www.boatingeducation.org.nz/commercial/

## Q: Where can I go after SRL?
Coastguard lists the Coastal and Offshore qualification (for Skipper Coastal/Offshore), the Level 4 and Level 5 Maritime Operations certificates (fishing and watchkeeping), and the Diploma in Nautical Science, leading towards roles such as Skipper Coastal/Offshore, mate or skipper of fishing vessels, Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT, Master <500 GT and Watchkeeper Deck (unlimited). It names the Level 3 Domestic Maritime Crewing certificate as a lead-in.
CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL (minor): Coastguard calls the Coastal and Offshore qualification "Level 4"; Maritime NZ's online form names "New Zealand Certificate in Domestic Maritime Operations (Coastal & Offshore) (Level 5)" for Skipper Coastal/Offshore.
Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260711195614/https://www.boatingeducation.org.nz/commercial/

## Q: Does Coastguard offer a Skipper two nautical miles (S2NM) course?
Nothing on the archived page or the live site mentions S2NM. No provider course for S2NM was found anywhere on 2026-10-25.
