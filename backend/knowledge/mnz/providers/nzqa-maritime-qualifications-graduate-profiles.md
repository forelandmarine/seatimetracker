# NZQA maritime qualifications: paraphrased notes

Maritime NZ has published no competency framework for Skipper Fishing Vessel Unlimited, Integrated Rating, Chief Mate <3000 GT, Skipper two nautical miles or the SRL endorsements (see COVERAGE.md). The nearest official statement of what is taught is the NZQA graduate profile of the qualification that Maritime NZ names as the training requirement. Reliability: the listings are authoritative for the qualification, but a graduate profile is a summary of course outcomes, not Maritime NZ's assessment standard, and oral exam content may differ.

## Q: What competencies does the Skipper Fishing Vessel Unlimited training cover?
Maritime NZ's online form names the NZ Diploma in Fishing Vessel Operations (Level 6) with the Fishing Vessel Skipper strand (NZQA ref 2893, 180 to 245 credits, STCW-F based). The core diploma (Mate Fishing Vessel Unlimited level) covers leading safe work and emergency response, fishing vessel seamanship, coastal passage planning and position fixing, navigation in unlimited waters and watchkeeping arrangements, global maritime communications, weather instruments and forecasts, international accident, emergency and search and rescue procedures, survival craft and rescue boats, directing firefighting, and vessel construction, handling, stability and trim tables. The Skipper strand adds: voyage planning and navigation for fishing vessels in any operating limit, managing emergencies and search and rescue, medical care, forecasting weather and ocean conditions, managing propulsion and engineering systems to handle the vessel in all conditions, controlling trim, stability and stress, compliance with maritime law for fishing vessels, and catch handling and stowage. NZQA lists the next review of 2893 as 31 December 2026.
Source: https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/nzqf/search/viewQualification.do?selectedItemKey=2893

## Q: Is there an NZQA qualification for Integrated Rating?
No qualification is named for Integrated Rating itself. Since 31 March 2026 the Maritime NZ route is to hold (or meet the requirements for) both AB Deck and AB Engine. The training for those is the NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 4) (ref 2511, 60 credits), whose Deck Crew strand covers deck maintenance and repair, navigational watch at support level and berthing, cargo and stores, deck machinery, and survival craft and rescue boats, and whose Engineering strand covers engine room maintenance and repair and on-board engineering at support level. A provider listing (NMIT via StudySpy) says completing this certificate is a prerequisite to applying for AB Deck, AB Engine, Integrated Rating and Electro-technical Rating. So an Integrated Rating candidate in practice completes both the Deck Crew and Engineering strands.

## Q: What do the Level 4 and Level 5 Maritime Operations certificates cover?
- NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 4), Fishing and Watchkeeping strands (ref 2513, 120 credits): coastal passage planning, navigational watch, cargo monitoring and legislative compliance; Fishing strand adds supervising fishing seamanship tasks (Mate Fishing Vessel Limited, vessels under 45 m, coastal and offshore); Watchkeeping strand adds firefighting, survival craft and medical first aid (Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT near coastal).
- NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 5) (ref 2512, 60 credits): command level for skipper of a fishing vessel under 45 m or master of a commercial vessel up to 500 GT (coastal or offshore passage planning, watch management, emergency response, cargo, legislative compliance).

## Q: Did the NZQA maritime qualifications change recently?
Yes. The Workforce Development Council (Hanga-Aro-Rau) reviewed seven Marine Craft Operation qualifications from November 2024, working with Maritime NZ, industry and providers; the advisory group endorsed them in August 2025 and NZQA recorded the outcome in January 2026. All seven got a new version (category B, moderate change): 2506 (Maritime Crewing L3, including the Superyacht Crewing strand) to version 3, 2508 (Maritime Crewing L2) to v3, 2511 (Maritime Crewing L4) to v3, 2512 (Maritime Operations L5) to v3, 2513 (Maritime Operations L4) to v3, 2516 (Diploma in Yacht Operations L6) to v4 and 2895 (Domestic Maritime Crewing L3, the QDC course) to v3. Changes were wording and currency updates to graduate profile outcomes and pathways "to reflect Maritime NZ requirements"; no new S2NM, Integrated Rating or Chief Mate <3000 GT qualification was created. Old versions can be assessed until 31 December 2027 (2508, 2513) or 31 December 2028 (the others). Next review January 2031.
Source: https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/nqfdocs/summaries/2026/jan26/revsumjan26-01.doc

## Q: Is there an NZQA qualification or unit standard for Skipper two nautical miles?
None found on 25 October 2026. The January 2026 review did not add one, and no provider lists an S2NM course.
