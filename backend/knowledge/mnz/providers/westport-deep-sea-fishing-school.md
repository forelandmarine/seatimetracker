# Westport Deep Sea Fishing School: paraphrased fact notes

Notes in our own words from the school's pages read on 2026-10-25. Maritime NZ lists Westport Deep Sea Fishing School as an approved provider for ADH-F, MEC 5, MEC 6, SRL, SCO and STCW basic courses.

## Q: What licence courses does the Westport school run?
Programmes leading to Advanced Deckhand Fishing (NZ2891, Level 3), Skipper Restricted Limits (Level 4), Skipper Coastal Offshore (NZ2892), MEC 6 and MEC 5, plus a Specified Activity Endorsement certificate, STCW basic safety courses and radio operator courses. It also runs a pre-employment programme for new entrants to the seafood industry and seafood processing qualifications.
Source: https://deepsea.co.nz/maritime-licences

## Q: How often do courses start and how are they delivered?
The school says programmes start monthly or every two months and mix on-site training (workshops and its training vessel "Legionaire II") with online study to cut time away from work. It offers a student hostel in Westport (stated at NZD 350 a week including an evening meal).
Source: https://deepsea.co.nz/maritime-licences

## Q: Who sends my licence application to Maritime NZ?
The school says that in most cases final Maritime NZ exams can be held at the end of the programme and it gives you the certificates and documents for your licence application, but you are responsible for applying to Maritime NZ and paying Maritime NZ's fees yourself.
Source: https://deepsea.co.nz/maritime-licences

## Q: Can I finish my SRL training record book on the course?
The school says it offers practical training and completion of the Maritime NZ SRL record book while on the course, using its workshop and vessel.
Note: Part 32 still requires at least 200 hours of sea service for SRL.
Source: https://deepsea.co.nz/maritime-licences

## Q: I'm doing a sea survival or firefighting refresher. What do I need?
The school provides a sea service letter template for refresher courses, because you must show you took part in the required abandon-ship and fire drills during sea service. This matches Maritime NZ's rule for STCW refresher training (12 months' service in the last 5 years or 3 months in the last 6 months, confirmed by a company letter).
Source: https://deepsea.co.nz/resources

## Q: Are there practical rules for the firefighting course?
Yes. The school says you must be clean shaven for the firefighting practical so the breathing apparatus mask seals, wear sturdy closed shoes, and bring clothes for the pool session of the survival course. Results are not released until fees are paid.
Source: https://deepsea.co.nz/faq

## Q: Where can I find the Maritime NZ guideline and TRB for SRL, SCO and ADH-F?
The school's resources page links copies of the Maritime NZ training record books and certificate guidelines for SRL, SCO, ADH-F and MEC 6. Some of these links are copies stored by the school and may not be the latest versions; use the Maritime NZ originals in this library (guideline-*.md and trb-*.md).
Source: https://deepsea.co.nz/resources
