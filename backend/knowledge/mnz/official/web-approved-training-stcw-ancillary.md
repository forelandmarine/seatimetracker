# Approved training for STCW ancillary certificates (basic safety, PSC, advanced firefighting, medical, security, ECDIS, HV) and refresher rules

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/training/approved-training-for-stcw-ancillary-certificates/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

If you plan to work on a New Zealand-flagged ship, you must complete approved training for any ancillary STCW certificates you need. Use this page to find training providers approved to deliver courses that lead to ancillary (additional) STCW certificates and endorsements.

| STCW ancillary certificate | Training provider(s) |
|---|---|
| Basic Personal Survival (Table A-VI/1-1), and refresher | IMINZ; New Zealand Maritime School; Toi Ohomai Institute of Technology; Training 4 Safety; Vertical Horizonz NZ Ltd; Westport Deepsea Fishing School |
| Basic First aid (Table A-VI/1-3) | IMINZ; MedAire (APMEA) Pte. Ltd; New Zealand Maritime School; Oceania Medical; Toi Ohomai; Training 4 Safety; Vertical Horizonz; Westport Deepsea Fishing School |
| Basic Fire-prevention and fire-fighting (Table A-VI/1-2), and refresher | IMINZ; New Zealand Maritime School; Toi Ohomai; Training 4 Safety; Vertical Horizonz; Westport Deepsea Fishing School |

## Notes
- These training providers are approved by the Director of Maritime NZ to deliver the listed STCW ancillary course and issue the corresponding ancillary certificate of proficiency.
- If you attend STCW ancillary refresher training, you must provide the training provider with a company letter(s) confirming that you have actively taken part in the mandatory abandon-ship and fire drills (and fast-rescue-boat drills, if applicable) while completing approved sea service, performing functions appropriate to the certificate you hold, for at least 12 months in total during the preceding 5 years, or 3 months in total during the preceding 6 months.

Related: the nested page "Recognition of foreign certificates" (https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/recognition-of-foreign-certificates/recognition-of-foreign-certificates/, updated 1 May 2026) adds that overseas STCW ancillary certificates other than basic ancillaries must be issued by an approved training provider in one of Maritime NZ's issuing countries, that other administrations' ancillary certificates are assessed case by case, and that AMSA- and MCA-approved provider certificates are automatically accepted.
