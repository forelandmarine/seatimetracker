Caution
Providing false information or failing to disclose information relevant to the granting or holding of a
maritime document is an offence under section 406 of the Maritime Transport Act. The penalty for
this offence in the case of an individual is imprisonment for a term of up to 12 months or a fine of
up to NZ$10,000 for an individual, and a fine not exceeding NZ$50.000 for a body corporate.
