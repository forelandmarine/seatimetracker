# Seafarer Certification and Operational Limits Framework (2 of 4): certificate summaries: enclosed/inshore, coastal/offshore, fishing and unlimited-area deck certificates

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/background-to-seacert/
Source PDF: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/gggjmdsw/seafarer-certification-operational-limits-framework.pdf

Text extracted from the official PDF. Page headers, footers and page numbers removed; tables are flattened to text in their original reading order.

Certificates for enclosed waters and inshore limits
1 Qualified Deck Crew
Short name QDC
Privileges To perform the functions of a deck crew member on passenger and non-
passenger ships of less than 24 metres in length in restricted, and near
coastal limits
Minimum age 16
Minimum sea service No sea service requirement but training record book must be completed on
board a commercial ship or a warship
Qualification NZ Certificate in Domestic Maritime Crewing (Level 3)
Final examination Successful completion of any examination the Director may require
assessed by an approved Maritime NZ examiner
x Current first aid certificate
x RROC
Endorsements The following endorsements can be applied to this certificate:
x Passenger
Career progression To Skipper Restricted Limits (SRL)

2 Skipper Restricted Limits
Short name SRL
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master on:
(a) passenger and non-passenger ships less than 12 metres in length
carrying 19 passengers or less within restricted limits; and
(b) fishing vessels less than 12 metres in length within enclosed water, inshore
and inshore fishing limits.
Minimum sea service 200 hours
x on a powered vessel of any kind (may include pleasure craft or sailing
vessels) (required)
x completion of approved training record book on board
Sea service may be achieved on a pleasure craft, but only if the applicant
has been acting in the capacity of skipper at the time.
Qualification NZ Certificate in Domestic Maritime Operations (Restricted Limits) (Level 4)
x Current first aid certificate
x RROC
Endorsements The following endorsements can be applied to this certificate:
x up to 24 metres
x less than 500 GT
x 500 to 3000 GT
x passenger
x Cook Strait limit
x high-speed
x square-rigged sailing
x specified activity
Career progression To Skipper Coastal / Offshore (SCO)

Certificates for coastal and offshore limits – non-fishing
3 Skipper Coastal/Offshore
Short name SCO
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master within restricted, coastal and
offshore limits on a ship of less than 24 metres length, carrying 19
passengers or less within restricted limits and 12 passengers or less within
coastal and offshore limits
Prior certification SRL endorsed to 24 metres length overall or equivalent
Minimum sea service 12 months
x on vessels of 12 metres or more within any operational limit (required)
Qualification NZ Certificate in Domestic Maritime Operations (Coastal & Offshore) (Level 4)
x First aid certificate
x Fire fighting
x Survival craft
x GROC
Alternatively complete STCW basic training and GROC
Endorsements The following endorsements can be applied to this certificate:
x Passenger
x Square-rigged sailing
x High speed ship
Career progression If you hold a Skipper Coastal/Offshore, you can progress to the following:
Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT Near-Coastal
Watchkeeper Deck
Mate Fishing Vessel – Limited

4 Master Yacht less than 24 metres
Short name Master Yacht <24 metres
Privileges To perform the functions and duties as a master on sailing ships of less than 24
metres in length in restricted, coastal and offshore limits
Prior certification Satisfactory completion of RYA Yachtmaster Offshore course, or equivalent
Minimum sea service 50 days
On a sailing ship (required)
x including total distance of 2,500 nautical miles
x including at least 5 passages over 60 nautical miles including two overnight
passages and two acting as Skipper
Sea service may be achieved on a pleasure craft, but only if the applicant has
been acting in the capacity of skipper at the time.
Qualification Royal Yacht Association (RYA) Yachtmaster offshore course or equivalent
x First aid certificate
x Fire fighting
x Survival craft
x GROC
Alternatively complete STCW basic training and GROC
Endorsements The following endorsements can be applied to this certificate:
x Passenger
x Square-rigged sailing
Career progression If you hold a Master Yacht <24 metres you can progress to Chief Mate Yacht

5 Watchkeeper Deck on ships less than 500 gross tonnage in near-coastal area
Short name Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT NC
Regulation Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT is an STCW certificate under Regulation II/3.3
Privileges To perform the functions and duties as officer in charge of a navigational watch
on ships less than 500 GT within coastal and offshore limits
Minimum sea service Option 1 – 36 months
x on vessels 12 metres or more (required)
x includes a minimum of 18 months operating beyond restricted limits in a
deck capacity on non-fishing vessels
Option 2 – 12 months
x on vessels of 12 metres or more (required)
x including a minimum of 6 months beyond restricted limits in a deck capacity
on non-fishing vessels
Qualification NZ certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 4) – Watchkeeping strand
Final
examination
Successful completion of any examination the Director may require, assessed by
x Proficiency in survival craft and rescue boats (other than fast rescue
boats)
x Medical first aid
x GROC
Career progression If you hold a Master Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT NC you can progress to
Master <500 GT Near-Coastal or Skipper Fishing Vessel (Limited)

6 Master on ships less than 500 gross tonnage in near-coastal area
Short name Master <500 GT NC
Regulation STCW Regulation II/3.5
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a Master on ships less than 500 GT in
the coastal and offshore limits
Prior certification Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT NC or equivalent
Minimum sea service 12 months
x as an officer in charge of a navigational watch on ships 24 metres or more in
length or 80 GT or more operating beyond restricted limits (preferred)
Qualification NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 5)
Final examination Successful completion of any examination the Director may require, assessed
by an approved Maritime NZ examiner
x Medical first aid
x GROC
Career progression If you hold a Master <500 GT NC you can progress to Watchkeeper Deck or
Master <500 GT

Deck certificates for fishing vessels – limited and unlimited waters
7 Advanced Deckhand – Fishing
Short name ADH-F
Regulation STCW-F Resolution 4
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a Deckhand aboard fishing vessels of
any length in any operating area
Minimum age 16
Minimum sea service 6 months
x on fishing vessels operating beyond enclosed water limits
x includes completion of an approved training record book
Qualification NZ certificate in Fishing Vessel Crewing (Level 3)
x STCW-F basic safety training
Career progression If you hold ADH-F, you can progress to MFV – Limited or MFV – Unlimited

8 Mate Fishing Vessel – Limited
Short Name MFV
Regulation STCW-F Regulation II/4
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of:
(a) a mate on fishing vessels less than 45 metres in length operating in inshore
fishing limits and limited waters and;
(b) a watchkeeper on fishing vessels of any length in unlimited waters.
Minimum sea service Option 1 – 24 months
x in a deck capacity on fishing vessels of 12 metres or more (required)
x including at least 12 months outside restricted limits
Option 2 – 18 months
x in a deck capacity on fishing vessels of 12 metres or more (required)
x including at least 12 months outside restricted limits while holding ADH-F
Option 3 – 12 months
x in a deck capacity on fishing vessels of 12 metres or more (required) while
holding SCO
x including at least 6 months outside restricted limits
Qualification NZ Certificate in Marine Operations (Level 4) with fishing strand
x STCW-F basic safety training
x GROC
Career
progression
If you hold a MFV you can progress to the following:
x Skipper Fishing Vessel – Limited
x Mate Fishing Vessel – Unlimited

9 Skipper Fishing Vessel – Limited
Short name SFV
Regulation STCW-F Regulation II/3
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of:
(a) a master on fishing vessels of less than 45 metres in length operating in
inshore fishing limits and limited waters; and
(b) a master of passenger and non-passenger ships of less than 24 metres
length overall in inshore fishing limits and limited waters
Prior certification MFV or equivalent
Minimum sea service 12 months
x on fishing vessels of 12 metres or more beyond restricted limits (required)
as officer in charge of navigational watch
Qualification NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 5)
x STCW-F basic safety training
x GROC
Career progression If you hold a Skipper Fishing Vessel – Limited you can progress to Skipper
Fishing Vessel – Unlimited

10 Mate Fishing Vessel – Unlimited
Short name MFV-U
Regulation STCW-F Regulation II/2
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a mate on fishing vessels of any size in
any operating area
Minimum sea service  24 months
x on fishing vessels of 12 metres or more operating outside restricted limits
(required)
x while in a deck capacity
Qualification NZ Diploma in Fishing Vessel Operations (Level 6)
Final examination Successful completion of any examination the Director may require assessed by
Ancillary or other
x STCW-F basic safety training
Career progression If you hold a Mate Fishing Vessel – Unlimited, you can progress to Skipper
Fishing Vessel – Unlimited

11 Skipper Fishing Vessel – Unlimited
Short name SFV-U
Regulation STCW-F Regulation II/1
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master on fishing vessels of any size
operating in any operating area
Prior certification Mate fishing vessel – unlimited or equivalent
Minimum sea service 12 months
x on fishing vessels 12 metres or more operating outside restricted limits
while holding MFV-U and operating as Skipper or Officer in charge of
navigational watch
[Note – up to 6 months may be substituted from sea service as Officer in Charge
of Navigational Watch on vessels covered by STCW.]
Qualification NZ Diploma in Fishing Vessel Operations (Level 6) with fishing vessel skipper
strand
Final examination Successful completion of any examination the Director may require assessed by
x STCW-F basic safety training
x Medical first aid
Career progression This is the most senior certificate for the command of fishing vessels

Deck certificates for the unlimited area
12 Deck Watch Rating
Short name DWR
Regulation STCW Regulation II/4
Privileges To perform the functions and duties as a deck crew member forming part of a
navigational watch on ships of any gross tonnage in any operating area
Minimum age 16
Minimum sea service Option 1 – 2 months (training required)
x on vessels of 500 GT or more (preferred)
x associated with navigational watchkeeping functions and involve the
performance of duties carried out under supervision of the Master or
qualified rating
Option 2 – 6 months (training not required)
x on vessels of 500 GT or more (preferred)
x associated with navigational watchkeeping functions and involve the
performance of duties carried out under supervision of the Master or
qualified rating
Qualification NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 3) with deck crewing strand (for Option
1 only)
x Steering certificate
Career progression If you hold a Deck Watch Rating you can progress to AB Deck or
Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT NC or Watchkeeper Deck

13 Able Seafarer Deck
Short name AB Deck
Regulation STCW Regulation II/5
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a deck rating forming part of a
navigational watch on ships of any gross tonnage in any operating area
Prior certification Deck watch rating (DWR) or equivalent
Minimum sea service

Option 1 – 12 months sea service (training required)
x in the deck department on vessels of 500 GT or more operating beyond
restricted limits (preferred)
x while holding a Deck Watch Rating (DWR)
x completion of an approved Able Seafarer Deck course
Option 2 – 18 months sea service (training not required)
x in the deck department on vessels of 500 GT or more operating beyond
restricted limits (preferred)
x while holding a Deck Watch Rating (DWR)
Qualification NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 4) Deck Crew strand (for Option 1
only)
Final examination Successful completion of any examination the Director may require, assessed
by an approved Maritime NZ examiner
x Proficiency in survival craft and rescue boats (other than fast rescue
boats)
x Security awareness (required for seagoing ship which is required to
comply with ISPS code)
Career progression If you hold an AB Deck, you can progress to Integrated Rating or Watchkeeper
Deck<500 GT NC or Watchkeeper Deck

14 Integrated Rating
Short name IR
Regulation STCW Regulations II/5 and III/5
Privileges To perform the functions and duties (at a support level) as:
(a) a deck crew member on ships of any gross tonnage in any operating area;
and
(b) a crew member in a manned engine room or a periodically unmanned
engine room on passenger or non-passenger ships powered by main
propulsion machinery of any propulsion power in any operating area
Prior certification Meet the requirements of AB Deck and AB Engine or equivalent
Minimum sea service No sea service required
Qualification None
Final examination Final examination not required for this certificate
Ancillary
and other
x Proficiency in survival craft and rescue boats (other than fast rescue
boats)
x Security awareness (required for seagoing ship which is required to
comply with ISPS code)
Career progression If you hold an Integrated Rating, you can progress to Watchkeeper Deck<500
GT NC or Watchkeeper Deck or MEC 3

15 Watchkeeper Deck
Short name Watchkeeper Deck
Regulation STCW Regulation II/1
Privileges To perform the functions and duties as officer in charge of a navigational watch
on ships of any gross tonnage in any operating area
Minimum sea service
Accept
Option 1 – 12 months
x on vessels of 500 GT or more operating beyond restricted limits (preferred)
x includes at least 6 months of supervised bridge watchkeeping
x includes evidence of cadet programme
Option 2 – 36 months
x on vessels of 500 GT or more operating beyond restricted limits (preferred)
x includes at least 6 months supervised bridge watchkeeping
Qualification NZ Diploma in Nautical Science (Level 6)
Final examination Successful completion of any examination the Director may require assessed by
Ancillary and
other certificates
x Medical first aid
Career
progression
If you hold a Watchkeeper Deck, you can progress to Chief Mate or Master or
Chief Mate <3000 GT or Master <3000 GT or Master <500 GT NC

16 Chief Mate on ships less than 3000 gross tonnage
Short name Chief Mate <3000 GT
Regulation STCW Regulation II/2.3
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of
(a) a Chief mate on ships of less than 3000 GT in any operating area
(b) an officer in charge of navigational watch on a vessel of any gross tonnage
in any operating area
Prior certification Watchkeeper Deck or equivalent
Minimum sea service No sea service requirements but must hold Watchkeeper Deck or higher
certificate
Qualification Diploma in Masters and Chiefs Foreign Going Programme – Chief Mate Foreign
Going strand
x Medical care on board ship
Career progression If you hold a Chief Mate <3000 GT, you can progress to Chief Mate or Master
<3000 GT or Master

17 Master on ships less than 500 gross tonnage
Short Name Master <500 GT
Regulation STCW Regulation II/2
Privileges

To perform the functions and duties of:
(a) a Master on ships less than 500 GT in any operating area and
(b) a Chief Mate on ships <3000 GT within restricted, coastal and offshore
limits, if 6 months of approved sea service was on a commercial ship of
500 GT or more that is not a fishing vessel and
(c) a Master on passenger and non-passenger ships <3000 GT within
restricted, coastal and offshore limits, if 24 months of approved sea service
was on commercial ship of 500 GT or more that is not a fishing vessel and
(d) an officer in charge of a navigational watch on ships of any gross tonnage
in any operating area
Prior certification Master <500 GT Near-Coastal or equivalent
Minimum sea  service Option 1 – 36 months
x as an officer in charge of navigational watch on ships of either 24 metres in
length or more, or 80 GT or more (required)
x includes at least 24 months on voyages over 24 hours while holding
Watchkeeper Deck <500 GT NC (or equivalent) or higher and 12 months
while holding Master <500 GT NC (or equivalent)

Option 2 – 24 months
x as an officer in charge of navigational watch on ships of either 24 metres in
length or more, or 80 GT or more (required) while holding Master <500 GT
NC (or equivalent)
x includes a minimum of 12 months as Chief Mate or Master or their
equivalents
Qualification NZ Diploma in Nautical Science (Level 6) and applicable sections of the NZ
Diploma in Yacht Operations (Level 6) optional strand in Master Yacht
Final
examination
Successful completion of any examination the Director may require, assessed
by an approved Maritime NZ examiner
Ancillary
and other
x Medical care on board ship
Career progression If you hold a Master <500 GT, you can progress to Chief Mate, Master <3000
GT or Master

18 Chief Mate
Short name Chief Mate
Regulation STCW Regulation II/2
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a chief mate on ships of any size in any
operating area
Prior certification Watchkeeper Deck or equivalent
Minimum sea service 12 months
x on board vessels of 500 GT or more operating beyond restricted limits
(preferred)
x as an officer of in charge of a navigational watch
Qualification Masters and Chiefs Foreign Going programme – Chief Mate Foreign Going
strand
Completion of an accepted/approved training course
x Medical care on board ship
Career progression If you hold a Chief Mate, you can progress to Master or Master <3000 GT

19 Master on ships less than 3000 gross tonnage
Short name Master <3000 GT
Regulation STCW Regulation II/2.3
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of:
(a) a master on ships less than 3000 GT in any operating area; and
(b) an officer in charge of the navigational watch on ships of any gross tonnage
in any operating area
Prior certification Watchkeeper Deck or equivalent or Chief Mate <3000 GT or equivalent
Minimum sea service Option 1 – 36 months
x on ships 500 GT or more (preferred) as an officer in charge of navigational
watch while holding Watchkeeper Deck certificate (or equivalent)

Option 2 – 24 months
x on ships 500 GT or more (preferred)
x includes at least 12 months as Chief Mate while holding Chief Mate <3000
(or equivalent) or higher certificate
x remaining months as officer in charge of navigational watch while holding
Watchkeeper Deck (or equivalent) or higher certificate
Qualification Master and Chiefs Foreign Going programme – Foreign Going strand
Ancillary
and other
x Medical care on board ship
Career progression If you hold a Master <3000 GT, you can progress to Master

20 Master
Short name Master
Regulation STCW Regulation II/2
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master on a ship of any gross tonnage
in any operating area
Prior certification Watchkeeper Deck or Chief Mate or equivalent
Minimum sea service
Accept
Option 1 – 36 months
x on ships of 500 GT or more (required)
x as an officer in charge of a navigational watch while holding a Watchkeeper
Deck certificate (or equivalent) or higher certificate

Option 2 – 24 months
x on ships of 500 GT or more (required)
x includes at least 12 months as a Chief Mate while holding a Chief Mate
Certificate (or equivalent)
x remaining 12 months as an officer in charge of a navigational watch while
holding a Watchkeeper Deck certificate (or equivalent) or higher certificate
Qualification Masters and Chiefs Foreign Going programme – Master Foreign Going strand
x Medical care on board ship
Career progression This is the most senior command certificate

21 GMDSS Radio Operator
Short name GMDSS Radio Operator
Regulation STCW Regulation IV/2
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of:
(a) a navigational watchkeeper at operation level, or
(b) a radio operator on board a GMDSS ship and operate its specialised
GMDSS equipment
Minimum sea service Sea service is not required
Qualification x General Operator’s Certificate (GOC)
x Training leading to a GMDSS certificate
Career progression Not applicable
