# Maritime Rules Parts 31A, 31B and 31C: revoked, history only

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/rules/all-rules/maritime-rules-part-31/

This is a short history note written for this library from the "History of Part 31" section of the Maritime NZ Part 31 page (page updated 21 September 2026). The revoked rule texts are still downloadable from that page but were not copied here, because they no longer have legal effect.

## What the old parts covered

- Part 31A: Crewing and Watchkeeping, Unlimited, Offshore and Coastal (non-fishing vessels). Signed 28 June 1998, in force 1 August 1998. Amended 2001, 2011 and 2012 (Part 31A and Part 32: Amendment 2012). The last version on the site is the 2012 version: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/ls4hibea/part31a-maritime-rule.pdf
- Part 31B: Crewing and Watchkeeping, Offshore, Coastal and Restricted (non-fishing vessels). Signed 18 December 2000, in force 1 February 2001 (rule 31B.8(4) and Tables 2, 4 and 8 from 1 February 2002). Amended 2006 (Certificated Deckhand). Last version on the site is the 2006 version: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/rqspzzr4/part31b-maritime-rule.pdf
- Part 31C: Crewing and Watchkeeping for fishing vessels. Signed 18 December 2000, in force 1 February 2001. Original rule: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/ir4nsebc/part31c-maritime-rule-original.pdf

## What replaced them

- Parts 31A, 31B and 31C were revoked and replaced by a new, single Part 31 (Crewing and Watchkeeping), made on 27 January 2014 and in force on 1 April 2014, at the same time as the new SeaCert Part 32.
- The advisory circulars describing Parts 31A, 31B and 31C are marked on the Maritime NZ page as "SUPERCEDED 13/12/2019".
- Maritime NZ says it is currently amending the advisory circulars for Part 31; no current Part 31 advisory circular was published on the page at 2026-10-25.

## How this matters to crew today

- Old certificates issued under the pre-2014 framework (for example NZOM, NZOW, NZ Coastal Master, Inshore Launch Master, Local Launch Operator) were either transitioned to SeaCert certificates or ring-fenced under Part 32 Subpart F. The ring-fencing deadline has passed (see web-ring-fencing.md and part-32-07-subpart-f-transition-and-ring-fencing.md).
- Crewing questions today are answered by the current Part 31 (part-31-01 to part-31-05) and by any Minimum Safe Crewing Document for the vessel.
