Privileges table for ring-fenced certificates

The privileges listed below relate to the named ticket only.  They outline the area of operation (i.e inshore, enclosed) that the ticket can be used in,
and the size of the vessel that can be operated ( i.e .>20m = more than 20 meters LOA, <20 = less than 20 meters LOA).

A minimum safe crewing document issued to a specific vessel may give additional privileges to ticket holders on board that vessel.
