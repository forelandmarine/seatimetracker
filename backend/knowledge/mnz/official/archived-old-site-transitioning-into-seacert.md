# Transitioning into SeaCert (old Maritime NZ page, archived January 2026)

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/2026/https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/commercial/seafarer-certification/transitioning/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Captured from the Internet Archive copy of the old Maritime NZ site. The live URL now redirects to https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/.

## Transitioning into SeaCert

The deadline for transitioning certificates has now passed, please contact us if you wish to discuss this further.

## Contact us for more help

If you have any questions about transitioning, please contact our Personnel Certification team.
- New Zealand (toll free): 0508 669 734
- Calling from outside New Zealand: +64 4 494 1559

Note for this library: the deadlines themselves are in Part 32 rule 32.206 (part-32-07-subpart-f-transition-and-ring-fencing.md). Old certificates ceased to be valid on 2 September 2017 unless the holder had told Maritime NZ by 1 September 2017 whether they would ring-fence or transition. Late notice could be sought by 31 December 2019 (ring-fence, or transition to national or STCW-F) or 1 April 2018 (transition to STCW). The certificates listed in Table 46 ceased on 31 December 2016 unless an STCW certificate had been issued. Ring-fenced certificates are valid for life but NZOM, NZOW and NZCM holders must meet currency requirements every five years to use them.
