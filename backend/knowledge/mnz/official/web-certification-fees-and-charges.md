# Certification fees and charges (seafarer certificates)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/supporting-information/certification-fees-and-charges/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Find current seafarer certification fees, other related certification charges and how you can pay them.

## Current seafarer certification fees
- These fees are effective from 1 July 2019.
- All amounts are in New Zealand dollars and include goods and services tax (GST).

### Certificates of proficiency or competency and all endorsements: fixed fee $368

- All national certificates of proficiency, including: Qualified Deck Crew.
- All national certificates of competency, including: Skipper Restricted Limits; Skipper Coastal/Offshore; Master Yacht less than 24 metres; Marine Engineer Class 6; Marine Engineer Class 5; Marine Engineer Class 5 steam; Marine Engineer Class 5 motor and steam; Marine Engineer Class 4.
- All STCW (including STCW-F) certificates of proficiency, including: Deck Watch Rating; Engine Room Watch Rating; Able Seafarer Deck; Able Seafarer Engine; Electro-Technical Rating; Integrated Rating; Advanced Deckhand - Fishing.
- All STCW (including STCW-F) certificates of competency, including: Watchkeeper Deck; Watchkeeper Deck of ships less than 500 gross tonnage in near-coastal area; Chief Mate on ships less than 3000 gross tonnage; Chief Mate; Master on ships less than 500 gross tonnage in the near coastal area; Master on ships less than 500 gross tonnage; Master on ships less than 3000 gross tonnage; Master; Chief Mate Yacht; Master Yacht less than 500 gross tonnage; Master Yacht; Electro-technical Officer; Marine Engineer Class 3; Marine Engineer Class 2 on ships less than 3000 kW; Marine Engineer Class 2; Marine Engineer Class 2 endorsed chief engineer; Marine Engineer Class 1; Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS) radio operator; Radio Officer; Mate Fishing Vessel limited; Mate Fishing Vessel unlimited; Skipper Fishing Vessel limited; Skipper Fishing Vessel unlimited.

### Endorsements on certificates of proficiency or competency: fixed fee $368

- All national endorsements, including: Passenger endorsement; Cook Strait endorsement; Less than 24m endorsement; Less than 500 gross tonnage endorsement; 500 to 3000 gross tonnage endorsement; Square-rigged endorsement; High-speed vessel endorsement; Steam endorsement; Specified Activity endorsement.
- All STCW endorsements, including: Basic Tanker; Advanced Tanker; Basic Polar Water Code; Advanced Polar Water Code; Advanced IGF Ship; Basic IGF Ship.
- Other certificates: Pilot licence; Pilotage exemption certificate.

### Other services (hourly rate $245 where applicable)

| Service | Fixed fee |
|---|---|
| Foreign certificate recognition (under MOU) | $368 |
| Foreign certificate recognition (outside MOU) | $368 |
| Foreign certificate recognition (TTMRA) | $368 |
| Ships Cooks Letter of Confirmation | $245 |
| National certificate of competency or certificate of proficiency transition | $368 |
| STCW or STCW-F certificate of competency or certificate of proficiency transition | $368 |
| National certificate of competency or certificate of proficiency renewal | $368 |
| STCW certificate of competency or certificate of proficiency renewal | $368 |
| STCW-F certificate of competency or certificate of proficiency renewal | $368 |
| STCW or STCW-F endorsement renewal | $368 |
| National endorsement renewal † | $368 |
| Replacement certificates and licences, Seafarer | $245 |

**† The renewal fee does not apply to these endorsements:** Less than 24m Endorsement; Less than 500 Gross Tonnage Endorsement; Specified Limit Endorsement.

## Paying your fees

Maritime NZ has digitised the application and renewal of most seafarer certificates. You must pay the fee for your certificate or endorsement when you submit your online application form.

You can pay by: credit or debit card; bank-to-bank transfer.
