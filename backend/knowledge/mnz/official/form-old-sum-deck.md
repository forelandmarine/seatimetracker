Last updated: 2 May 2014
About this form
This form is to help keep track of your sea service in a deck capacity. (There is a separate
form for yacht deck certificates.)
- You must provide documented proof of your sea service with your application.
- Do not include time on leave. Extended time in port is generally excluded.
- For navigational (bridge) watchkeeping, tick in the box provided to show whether it was under
supervision of a qualified navigating officer (where relevant for your certificate).
- For watchkeeping, a day is eight or more hours in a 24-hour period.
- List the vessel length where the guideline indicates a minimum length for sea service to be relevant.
