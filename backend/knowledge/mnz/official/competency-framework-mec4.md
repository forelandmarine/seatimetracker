Describes the cycle of events in both 2 and 4 stroke engines.

including construction and uses of each.

main engine.

2. Fuel system Assessment of

Describes and sketches a diesel supply system from fuel tank to injectors.
Identifies key components and describes their purpose including filters,
pumps and bleed points, for both common rail and jerk type systems.
Describes:
- The construction of system components.
- The safety features of the fuel system.
- The operation of the diesel injector, electronic and mechanical.
- How fuel pumps are timed and calibrated.
- The symptoms, causes of and remedies for typical fuel pump and
injector defects.
- Priming the diesel fuel system and removal of air.
- The importance of maintaining a clean fuel supply.
- The significance of fuel oil viscosity, relative density and flashpoint.
- The dangers of fuel contamination and describes action to take to
correct.
- Modern high pressure fuel syst ems and associated safety issues.
- How fuel consumption may be reduced in an emergency.
3. Lubrication system Describes and sketches the layout of a typical lubricating oil system
including the flowpath.  Identifies key components of a lubricating oil system
and describes their purpose.
Describes types of oil and testing of oil.
Describes:
- The construction of system components.
- The purpose of lubricating oil and states its main desirable properties.
