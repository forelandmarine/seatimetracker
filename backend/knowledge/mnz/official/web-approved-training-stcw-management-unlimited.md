# Approved training for STCW management and unlimited certificates (Chief Mate, Master, MEC 2, MEC 1, GMDSS)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/training/approved-training-for-stcw-management-unlimited-certificates/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Use this page to find training providers approved to deliver programmes that lead to STCW management-level and unlimited certificates. If you plan to work on a New Zealand-flagged ship at management level (for example, chief mate, master, chief engineer), you must complete approved training for the certificate you are applying for.

| Certificate | Training (national award) | Training provider(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Chief Mate | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Chief Mate on Ships less than 3000 GT | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| GMDSS Radio Operator | General Operator's Certificate (GOC) endorsed for operation under GMDSS | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand (NMIT); New Zealand Maritime School |
| Master | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Master on Ships less than 500 GT | New Zealand Diploma in Nautical Science (Level 6), and applicable sections of the NZ Diploma in Yacht Operations (Level 6) optional strand in Master Yacht | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Master on Ships less than 3000 GT | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Marine Engineer Class 1 | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Marine Engineer Class 2 | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Marine Engineer Class 2 endorsed Chief Engineer | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Marine Engineer Class 2 on ships less than 3000 kW | Diploma in Foreign Going Master or Chief (Level 7) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Radio Officer | General Operator's Certificate (GOC) endorsed for operation under GMDSS | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand (NMIT); New Zealand Maritime School |
