About this form
This declaration form is for applicants who have obtained their sea service on vessels where
they are the owner/operator.
In exceptional circumstances, it may be used as a last resort by applicants for other types of seafarer
certificate, if they are not able to obtain a sea service record or testimonial from a Master or owner.
This form cannot be used to document sea service for an STCW certificate.
The signature on this form must be witnessed and countersigned by a person authorised to take a statutory
declaration. In New Zealand, authorised persons include Justices of the Peace (JPs), solicitors, Notary
Publics, registrars of the District or High Court and certain Police Officers.
− Do not sign the declaration unless an authorised person is present to witness it.
− Ensure each page of the statutory declaration (and any additional pages attached) is also
signed, dated and witnessed by the person authorised to take a statutory declaration .

2. Sea service details
Required information for sea service
When recording details of claimed sea service, please note the following.
Table heading Requirement
Capacity/Duties R ecord the duties and the capacity in which
service was performed – for example, master,
skipper, mate, or engineer.
Include the grade – apprentice or cadet
For roles with combined duties, please specify
the percentage of time spent on engineer and/or
deck-related duties in relation to your signed-on
capacity.
Period of sea service claimed For voyages of 24 hours or more, record these
individually including the start and finish dates.
Voyages of less than 24 hours, record these
individually including each date and hours per
day. A period of eight (or more) hours worked
within each period of 24 hours is generally
counted as one day’s service.
Consult the ‘sea service’ section of the relevant
seafarer certificate guideline form for further
information on calculating sea service.
Note you should not include periods of leave as
sea service. Generally extended periods in port
are also excluded.
