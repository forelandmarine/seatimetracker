About this form
This form can be used to declare that you satisfy currency requirements for ring-fenced
NZOM, NZOW and NZCM certificate holders.
Maritime Rules Part 32 requires holders of ring-fenced NZ Offshore Matser, NZ Offshore Watchkeeper and
NZ Coastal Master to satisfy currency requirements every five years. Use this form to declare that you
meet the competence standards required and provide evidence that you meet the medical requirements.
Medical requirements
You will need to provide a copy of your certificate of medical fitness for seafarers. The medical category
must be A, A(T) or B. The certificate must be less than two years old.
Competence
The level of competence required will depend on what privileges you are using. These are set out for
seafarers using STCW privileges, and for those using National or STCW-F privileges. You will need to
provide evidence in support of your declaration.
Note
You must satisfy the Director that you meet currency requirements five years from the date your
verification of status (VOS) card was issued.
This form sets out the minimum information we need from you. You are required to self-declare
that you meet these requirements. If when processing your application we require further detail we
will contact you.
