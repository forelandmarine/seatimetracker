Specified activity endorsement application form Page 1

Maritime Rule Part 32.67 provides for holders of a Skipper Restricted Limits (SRL) and ring-
fenced certificates of competency to obtain a specified activity endorsement to enable them
to operate beyond restricted limits to 100 nautical miles (NM) from the coast, for the purpose
of conducting a specified activity.

The following certificate holders may apply for the specified activity endorsement:
- Skipper restricted limits <24m
- Skipper restricted limits <500 GT
- Commercial launchmaster
- Inshore fishing skipper
- Inshore launchmaster
- Local launchmans licence (LLL)
- Local launch operator (LLO)
- Master restricted limit launch
- Master river ship
- Master small home trade ship
- NZOW + ILM endorsement

Endorsement requirements and restrictions
To gain a specified activity endorsement, certificate holders need to demonstrate extra knowledge and
competence (see below). They also need to comply with rule requirements and MNZ guidelines.
This endorsement is subject to the condition that the privileges under the endorsement are only exercised:
- with a minimum of two crew at all times
- with at least one crew member who holds a current MEC 6 or equivalent or higher certificate
- for the purposes of the specified activity to which the endorsement applies
- on a ship of less than 24 metres in length.

This document is uncontrolled if printed. Please refer to the Maritime New Zealand website for the latest version.
Specified activity endorsement application form Page 2

Extra knowledge and competence
Successful applicants for a specified activity endorsement are able to operate in coastal and offshore limits
up to 100NM from the coast for significant periods of time. For this reason, they must gain the following
extra competencies and successfully complete an oral exam conducted by an MNZ-approved examiner.
These modules are taken from the Skipper Coastal/Offshore (SCO) framework.
- passage planning
- coastal and offshore meteorology
- preparation for coastal and offshore voyages
- crew organisation
- heavy weather precautions
- maintenance of vessel stability.

To demonstrate you have gained these competencies you must complete and pass an oral exam.

Ancillary certificates
The following ancillaries must be completed

Ancillary certificate Document(s) you must provide
First aid certificate First aid certificate (two-day course) (must be less than
two years old from the certificate issue date)
Either Unit Standards 6400, 6401 and 6402 or Unit
Standards 6400, 26551 and 26552 or equivalent revised
standard
Fire fighting Unit Standard 12310 or equivalent revised standard
Survival craft Unit Standard US 12309 or equivalent revised standard
MGROC certificate Maritime General Radiotelephone Operator’s Certificate
issued under Radio Communications Act 1989 or an
equivalent under International Radio Regulations

Contact an approved training provider for information about the available training.
Recognition of prior learning in these competencies is possible if the holder of a certificate with a specified
activity endorsement subsequently undertakes an approved training course for the SCO certificate.
To demonstrate that you have met these requirements, you will need to provide copies of your ancillary
certificates.

Statement about operational need for the endorsement
You will need to provide a summary of the operational activity you need the endorsement for. Include the
type of activity (eg albacore trolling or scientific research) and the sea area you will be working in.

Applicants should complete the application form at the back of this document.
The application fee for this certificate is $368, which includes goods and services tax (GST).
You will need to provide all the information we require. If any items are missing, we will not be able to
process your application.

3.  When and how to apply
We recommend you apply as soon as you have completed all of the certificate requirements,
to allow enough time for your application to be processed.
Make sure you include all of the documents we have asked for.
Refer to the checklist at 3.4 to make sure you have everything you need for your application.
3.1 Fees and payment options
The application fee for this certificate is $368, which includes goods and services tax (GST).
You will be invoiced once we have formally received your application. A reference number and
instructions explaining how to pay will be sent with the invoice.
You can pay online using a credit card or debit card, or by internet banking or bank deposit.
