# Latest rule updates (seafarer-relevant extract: Amendments 2026, 2025 No 3, 2025 No 2, 2025 and SeaCert future work)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/about-us/rules/latest-rule-updates/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

View the latest changes and amendments for maritime and marine protection rules.

## Maritime Rules Amendments 2026 and Marine Protection Rules Amendments 2026

These amendments were signed by the Associate Minister on 4 August 2026 and come into force on **21 September 2026**.

The amendments:
- enable a nationally consistent approach to ship fuel changeover
- remove mandatory carbon-intensity requirements for domestic ships
- update New Zealand's implementation of international conventions
- make technical improvements to the rules

### Other work underway: SeaCert

As part of RAP 3, Maritime NZ also consulted on the future direction of SeaCert.

This included proposals relating to:
- operating limits for seafarer certificates
- recognising fishing (STCW-F) sea service towards some non-fishing (STCW) certificates

Feedback was broadly supportive. Maritime NZ is considering that feedback and progressing the next phase of work. Further updates will be provided as this work develops.

### Technical improvements

The amendments also make a number of technical changes to improve the clarity, consistency, and administration of the rules.

## Maritime Rules Amendments 2025 (No 3)

These amendments were made on 10 December 2025.

These Maritime Rules amendments replace 9 expired General Exemptions. They make changes to Maritime Rules Parts 32, 41, 40A, 40C, 40D, 40E, 42A, and 43.

Changes relating to the following topics are in force from 28 January 2026: GE-01-17 and GE-06-17 (egress or means of escape for passenger accommodation); GE-02-17 (means of launching the rescue boat on ships operating up to restricted coastal limits); GE-07-17 (breathing apparatus set and firefighters' outfit); GE-13-20 (anchor chain cables and accessories, testing and marking); GE-20-20 (MF/HF radio in respect of Narrow-band Direct Printing).

Changes relating to the following topics are in force from 31 March 2026:
- GE-05-20: Sea Service and prior certification for Integrated Rating Certificate
- GE-06-20: Training Record Book requirement for a MEC 3 certificate
- GE-08-20: Training requirements for Deck watch rating, Able seafarer deck, Engine room watch rating, Able seafarer engine, and Electro-technical rating.

Maritime Rules Amendments 2025 No 3, 3.78 MB, 24 pages: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/azsclv1w/maritime-rules-amendments-2025-no-3.pdf

## Maritime Rules Amendments 2025 (No 2) and Marine Protection Rules Amendments 2025

These amendments were made on 8 October 2025. The amendment instruments outline the rules that will come into force on 1 December 2025.

Maritime Rules Amendments 2025 (No 2) and Marine Protection Rules Amendments 2025 make up the International Omnibus Amendments. These changes update New Zealand's rules to reflect mandatory changes to international maritime conventions. They also correct minor and technical errors. The listed changes include limits on Heavy Fuel Oil use in Arctic waters, noxious liquid substances, UNSP barge exemption certificates, the Ballast Water Management Systems code, electronic record books, and expansion of service providers and accepted satellite equipment for the Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS).

Maritime Rules Amendments 2025 (No 2), PDF: 3.44 Mb, 32 pages: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/qfwdrtsw/maritime-rules-amendments-2025-no-2.pdf

Note for this library: this instrument also amended Part 32 rule 32.21 (renewal sea service), Tables 4 (SCO), 9 (MEC 5 motor and steam), 27 (Master Yacht), 44, 47 and 48, rules 32.110 (Master Yacht <500 GT) and 32.210, and the Part 32 Schedule (contents page of the scanned instrument). These changes are in the 21 September 2026 Part 32 consolidation.

## Maritime Rules Amendments 2025

These amendments were made on 20 March 2025. The amendment instrument outlines the rules that will come into force on 21 April 2025, 1 September 2025, and 1 December 2025.

### SeaCert
- Introducing a new entry-level skippers certificate,
- Amendments to the Skipper Restricted Limits <500 GT endorsement,
- Some minor and technical improvements to rules.

### Non-commercial use of commercial vessels
- Clarifying the requirements for commercial vessels when being used non-commercially for recreation, and
- Introduce minor conditions on securing commercial equipment.
