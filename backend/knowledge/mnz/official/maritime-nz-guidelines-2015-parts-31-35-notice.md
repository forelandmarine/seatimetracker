# Maritime New Zealand Guidelines 2015: guidance notice for Parts 31 to 35

MARITIME NEW ZEALAND GUIDELINES
GUIDANCE NOTICE                                  FEBRUARY 2015

Use of industry-specific training certificates
on ships of up to 15 metres
Purpose
This information is for organisations holding or applying for approval of their industry training plan and
approval to issue industry-specific training certificates, and for holders of industry-specific training
certificates. It addresses an inconsistency between Maritime Rules 31 and 35.
The purpose of this advice is to clarify that industry-specific training certificates issued under Rule 35
may meet the crewing requirement for vessels of 15 metres (m) or less, and are not limited to use on
vessels of 6m or less (as is currently indicated in Tables 14 and 15 of Rule 31).
Maritime New Zealand proposes to incorporate this clarification in a future amendment to Part 31. In
the meantime, holders of industry-specific training certificates are able to exercise the privileges of
their certificates on vessels of up to 15m, in accordance with the conditions of their certificates.
Background
Rule 35.70(4) states that a person who holds an approval under 35.70 may issue industry-specific
certificates for the operation of craft that are 6m or less in length overall, or non-passenger boats that
are not fishing boats, are 15m or less in overall length, and operate only within restricted limits.
Tables 14 and 15 in Rule 31 set out the crewing requirements for non-passenger ships operating in
restricted limits and show that, for ships less than 6m, an industry-specific certificate is permitted as an
alternative to a Skipper Restricted Limits (SRL) certificate. However, the tables do not indicate that
industry-specific training certificates may be used for ships between 6m and 15m.
Planned changes to Maritime Rules 31
MNZ will seek to amend Tables 14 and 15 in Maritime Rule 31 to give effect to Rule 35.70(4)(b). The
entry for master of vessels less than 24m will read “SRL or, for vessels less than 15m, industry-
specific training certificate issued under Part 35”. The entry for master of vessels less than 12m will
read “SRL or industry-specific training certificate issued under Part 35”. The intended changes are
shown in the updated versions of Tables 14 and 15 reproduced below.
