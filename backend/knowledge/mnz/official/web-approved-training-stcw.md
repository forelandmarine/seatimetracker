# Approved training for STCW certificates (ratings, OOW, yacht, ETO, MEC 3)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/training/approved-training-for-stcw-certificates/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Use this page to find training providers approved to deliver programmes that lead to New Zealand STCW certificates. If you plan to work on a New Zealand-flagged ship, you must complete approved training for the STCW certificate you are applying for.

| STCW certificates | Training (national award) | Training provider(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Able Seafarer Deck | NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 4) with Deck Crew strand | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand; New Zealand Maritime School |
| Able Seafarer Engine | NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 4) with Engineering strand | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand; New Zealand Maritime School |
| Chief Mate Yacht | NZ Diploma in Yacht Operations (Level 6) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Deck Watch Rating | NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 3) with Deck Crewing strand; Maritime Crewing Deck Watch Rating (Micro-credential) | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand; Skipper Training NZ Limited |
| Electro-technical Officer | NZ Diploma in Marine Electro-Technology (Level 6) | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Electro-Technical Rating | NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 4) with Electro-technology strand | New Zealand Maritime School; International Maritime Institute of New Zealand |
| Engine Room Watch Rating | NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 3) with Engineering strand | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand |
| Integrated Rating | NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing (Level 4) with Engineering strand and Deck Crew strand | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Master Yacht less than 500 GT | NZ Diploma in Yacht Operations (Level 6) with Master Yacht strand | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Master on Ships less than 500 GT in the near coastal area | NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 5) | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand; New Zealand Maritime School |
| Master Yacht | NZ Diploma in Yacht Operations (Level 6) with Master Yacht strand | New Zealand Maritime School |
| Marine Engineer Class 3 | NZ Diploma in Marine Engineering (Level 6) | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand; New Zealand Maritime School |
| Watchkeeper Deck | NZ Diploma in Nautical Science (Level 6) | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand; New Zealand Maritime School |
| Watchkeeper Deck on Ships less than 500 GT in the near-coastal area | NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 4), watchkeeping strand | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand; New Zealand Maritime School; Skipper Training NZ Ltd |
