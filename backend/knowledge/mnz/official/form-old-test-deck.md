About this testimonial

This testimonial should be completed when a Deck Officer/ Cadet/ Rating leaves a
commercial vessel. It may be submitted with any application for a Certificate of Competency
or Certificate of Proficiency issued by Maritime New Zealand.
Sea time on private yachts may be used as long as documented evidence is provided
confirming that the yacht is operated to commercial standards.

Note:
This testimonial must be completed and appropriately signed off to be valid.

The above service includes:

Actual sea service days

Actual Sea Service is time spent at sea, which may include time at anchor or river
and canal transits associated with a privilege.

Stand-by sea service days

Stand-by sea service is time immediately following a voyage while the vessel is
under preparation for a subsequent voyage. A maximum of 14 consecutive days
may be counted at one time, but on no occasion may a period of standby service
exceed that of the previous voyage. Therefore, unde r no circumstance s can
your total standb y service excee d your actual se a service.   If you wish to claim
stand-by service you must complete the Stand-by Sea Service table on the
following page.

Yard service days

Yard service is time when standing by a build, refit or repair.

Caution
Providing false information or failing to disclose information relevant to the granting or holding of a
maritime document is an offence under section 406 of the Maritime Transport Act. The penalty for
this offence in the case of an individual is imprisonment for a term of up to 12 months or a fine of
up to NZ$10,000 for an individual, and a fine not exceeding NZ$50.000 for a body corporate.
