# How to apply for other seafarer certificates (paper forms)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/how-to-apply-for-other-seafarer-certificates/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Applications for the following certificates are currently out-of-scope for digitisation, and you should continue to use the paper application forms.
- Pilot and pilot exemption certificates, and
- New Zealand Commercial Jet Boat Driver (River) Licences
- recognition of foreign STCW or STCW-F certificates, and
- recognition of Australian domestic certificates under the TTMRA
