# Training record books (which TRBs Maritime NZ issues or accepts)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/training/training-record-books/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Some seafarer certificates require you to complete a training record book (sometimes known as TRB) as part of the required training.

Maritime NZ training record books help make sure New Zealand trainees gain the competencies, skills, and experience needed to meet education standards and international obligations. The Director of Maritime NZ has also approved other training record books for specific certificates.

## Training record books in use

Training Record Books available for download from Maritime NZ include: Skipper Restricted Limits (SRL); Skipper Restricted Limits (SRL) endorsed to less than 500 gross tonnage; Marine Engineer Class 6 (MEC 6); Qualified Deck Crew (QDC); Advanced Deckhand, Fishing (ADH-F).

Maritime NZ will accept Training Record Books for the following certificates:

Marine Engineer Class 3 (MEC 3):
- on board training record book for engineering cadets, International Shipping Federation (ISF)
- training and assessment record book for assistant engineer officer, GlobalMET
- engineering training record book, Merchant Navy Training Board (UK)
- on board training and assessment record book "foreign-going voyages" (officer in charge of an engineering watch), Director General of Shipping (India)
- PSA TAR Book, Port of Singapore Authority

Watchkeeper Deck (WD):
- on board training record book for officers in charge of a navigational watch (deck cadets), International Shipping Federation (ISF).

Check with your training provider to confirm which training record book you must use for your programme.
