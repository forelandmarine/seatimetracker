# Regulatory Amendments Package 3 (RAP 3) consultation page (SeaCert in-principle proposals and S2NM changes)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/about-us/consultations/regulatory-amendments-package-3-rap-3-consultation/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Maritime NZ consulted on Regulatory Amendments Package 3 (RAP 3) from 9 April 2026 to 15 May 2026. We will review all submissions and consider the feedback as we refine the proposals. We will publish a summary of submissions.

The proposals aim to improve safety, support the maritime workforce, and make the Rules clearer and easier to apply in practice.

## Overview

RAP 3 includes in-principle changes to Seafarer Certification (SeaCert), and proposed changes to the Maritime and Marine Protection Rules.

## Proposed changes and who will be impacted

The proposed in-principle changes to SeaCert are at an early stage and we are not proposing changes to the Rules in the short-term. Instead, we are testing a proposed approach, including possible future changes to operating limits and how sea service is recognised. Your feedback will help shape the direction of the work.

| In-principle changes to SeaCert: proposal area | What we are seeking input on | Who is impacted |
|---|---|---|
| Operating limits for seafarers | We are seeking input on using the flexibility provisions in the relevant international maritime conventions New Zealand is signed up to (STCW and STCW-F) to revise or create new national certificates with privileges within Coastal and Offshore operating limits, which map approximately to ~12 to 200NM from shore. | Seafarers, vessel operators, maritime schools, and training providers operating in near-coastal or limited waters. |
| Recognising STCW-F sea service | We are seeking input on creating pathways for fishing (STCW-F) sea service to be recognised for specific non-fishing (STCW) certificates. | Seafarers working on fishing vessels, vessel operators, maritime training providers, and maritime schools. |

| Proposed rule change area | What we are proposing | Who is impacted |
|---|---|---|
| Updates to the Skipper Two Nautical Miles (S2NM) certificate | We propose minor changes to the S2NM certificate to make the certificate easier to implement. The changes are small and do not alter the S2NM certificate's intent. | Seafarers interested in the S2NM certificate, training providers, and maritime schools. |
| Fuel changeover requirements for ships entering NZ waters | Multiple options for a national foreign ship fuel changeover requirement; input on scrubbers. | Harbourmasters, councils, ports, pilots, foreign ship operators and others. |
| Carbon intensity requirements for domestic ships | Domestic voyaging ships to use the NZ ETS to meet carbon intensity requirements. | Operators of domestic voyaging ships (400 GT and over) and others. |
| Implementing updates to international maritime conventions | Update NZ Rules to reflect conventions already in force globally. | SOLAS ship operators, recognised organisations, surveyors. |
| Minor technical rule fixes | Minor technical and formatting fixes across several Maritime Rules. | Domestic operators, seafarers, pilots, foreign ship operators. |
