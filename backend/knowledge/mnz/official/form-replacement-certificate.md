About this form
Use this form if you are applying for a replacement for lost, stolen or destroyed certificate(s).
When applying for a replacement certificate, you do not have to send additional supporting documents.
Send your completed form to Maritime NZ by email to seafarers@maritimenz.govt.nz.
Privacy information
Personal information collected in this form will be used for the purpose of assessing your application.
Maritime NZ will not disclose personal information other than when permitted or required by law to do so.
You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be
corrected if you think it is wrong. If you’d like to ask for a copy of your information, or to have it corrected,
please contact the Maritime NZ Privacy Officer at the email address below or phone 0508 22 55 22.
Ministerial.services@maritimenz.govt.nz

3. Fees and payment
Application fee
The fee for a replacement seafarer certificate is $245.00 (including GST).
Invoice
You will be invoiced once we have formally received your application. A reference number and instructions
explaining how to pay will be sent with the invoice.
If another company or individual(s) are paying the invoice, or if you want the invoice made out to a different
