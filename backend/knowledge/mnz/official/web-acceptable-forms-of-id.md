# Acceptable forms of ID and trusted referees

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/supporting-information/acceptable-forms-of-id/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

When you apply online for a seafarer certificate, you must upload proof of identity and have it verified by a trusted referee. This page explains which ID documents you can use, who can act as a trusted referee, what your referee must do, and how to upload your verified documents.

## Acceptable photo identification

You must provide a clear copy of one of the following photo ID documents:
- New Zealand or overseas passport
- New Zealand driver's licence
- New Zealand refugee travel document
- New Zealand emergency travel document
- New Zealand certificate of identity
- New Zealand firearms licence
- Kiwi Access Card (formerly 18+ Card).

## Who can be a trusted referee?

Your trusted referee must be one of the following:
- Justice of the Peace
- Notary Public
- barrister or solicitor of the High Court of New Zealand
- staff member of an approved maritime training provider
- Maritime Transport Operator Certificate (MTOC) holder
- a member of the police
- registered teacher
- registered doctor
- ship's master (must hold a current master certificate of competency)
- recognised maritime surveyor
- Maritime NZ staff member.

## What your trusted referee must do

Your trusted referee must verify your identity document. They must:
- sight the original version of your identity document
- sign and date a copy of your photo ID
- write an endorsement on the document
- provide their contact details and certification on a separate sheet of paper.

### Endorsement your referee must write on your ID

"I have sighted the original version of this document, and I have compared the photographic image with [applicant's name] and confirm they appear to be the same person."

They must also sign and date each copy.

### Referee contact details and certification

On a separate sheet of paper, signed and dated: title; last name; given name(s); postal address; occupation or position; mobile phone number; alternative phone number (if available); email address; MTOC certificate number (if they hold an MTOC).

**Certification statement:** "I certify that [write your full name] is the person identified in the attached document. I confirm that I meet the requirements as a trusted referee."

## How to upload your verified identity document

After your referee has verified your identity document and provided their details: scan or photograph your verified ID and the referee's contact details sheet; upload all files with your online application.

## Scanning or photographing your documents

Make sure your scanned or photographed documents: are clear and easy to read; show all text, including the referee's signature, date, and endorsement; are in colour (not black and white); are not blurry and are not cut off at the edges; include the referee's contact details sheet in the same upload set.
