# Ring-fenced NZOW, NZOM and NZCM certificates: currency requirements information sheet

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/ring-fencing/
Source PDF: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/wjtelshk/currency-requirements-information-sheet.pdf

Text extracted from the official PDF. Page headers, footers and page numbers removed; tables are flattened to text in their original reading order.

Ring-fenced NZOW, NZOM and NZCM certificates
As it is coming up to five years since your verification of status (VOS) card was issued you now have
some decisions to make. As a holder of a ring-fenced NZOM, NZOW or NZCM certificate, you are
required to satisfy currency requirements every five years if you want to continue to exercise the
privileges of your certificate.
There are three options for you to consider:
Option one – you wish to continue to exercise the privileges of your certificate –see the guidance
below on how to satisfy the currency requirements of your certificate.
Option two – you don’t currently wish to exercise the privileges of your certificate but think you might
at a later date – see the guidance below on how to go about this in the future.
Option three – you don’t wish to use your certificate – there is nothing further required from you at the
moment.
Please note
Regardless of how you wish to proceed, you don’t ever lose your certificate. This remains
yours for life.
The requirement to satisfy currency requirements every five years only applies to those who
would like to exercise the privileges of their certificates.
Option one
If you want to continue to exercise the privileges of your ring-fenced certificate you must complete the
satisfying currency requirements application form and send it to Seafarers (via email)
seafarers@maritimenz.govt.nz.
To satisfy currency requirements you must:
- demonstrate appropriate sea service (or equivalent and acceptable continued competence)
- hold a current medical certificate (issued within the last two years) and
- continue to be a fit and proper person.
The level of currency required will depend on whether you are using STCW, National or STCW-F
privileges.
For certificate holders using or intending to use STCW privileges, you must do one of the following:
- complete 12 months of sea service within 5 years preceding your application OR
- complete 3 months of sea service within 6 months preceding your application OR
- complete 30 months in a relevant shore based role within 5 years preceding your application
OR
- pass a relevant examination.
For certificate holders using National or STCW-F privileges, you must either:
- complete 6 months of sea service within 5 years preceding your application OR
- complete 45 days of sea service within 3 months preceding your application OR
- complete 30 months in a relevant shore based role within 5 years preceding your application
OR
- pass a relevant examination.

What to do if you have not meet the minimum currency requirements
If you are using or plan to use the privileges of your certificate and have not satisfied the currency
requirements within 5 years from the date your VOS card was issued, then you can apply for an
exemption from the currency requirements for a short period to allow you more time to gain the
currency levels required.
Each exemption is treated on an individual basis, so the period the exemption is granted for will
depend on your personal circumstances. The Maritime Exemptions Officer will discuss your options
with you.
To apply for an exemption fill out the exemption request form - Seafarers.

Option two
If you are not exercising the privileges of your ring fenced NZOM, NZOW or NZCM certificate(s)
currently, but think you might want to do so in the future, then there are options for you to be able to
meet the currency requirements at a later stage.
When you decide you want to use the privileges of your ring-fenced certificate again you must
complete the satisfying currency requirements application form and send it to Seafarers (via email)
seafarers@maritimenz.govt.nz. The currency requirements you will need to meet at that time are:
- sit and pass a relevant exam as an alternative to sea service - the examiner must be
recognised by the Director under Maritime Rules Part 35 and must be able to assess for the
equivalent seafarer certificate OR
- complete 30 months in a relevant shore based role within 5 years preceding your application
OR
- apply for an exemption AND
- hold a current medical certificate (issued within the last two years) AND
- continue to be a fit and proper person.
Option three
If you are not exercising the privileges of your ring fenced NZOM, NZOW or NZCM certificate(s)
currently, and don’t think you will be in the future, then there is nothing further you need to do.
Please note, your certificate does not expire but you will not be able to exercise the privileges
associated with your certificate of competency. If at any stage you do decide you want to exercise the
privileges of your certificate(s), then you can use the options set out in Option two (above) to do this.
