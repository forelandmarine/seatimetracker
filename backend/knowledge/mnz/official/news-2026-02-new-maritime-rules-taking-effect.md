# News (18 Feb 2026): New Maritime Rules taking effect in 2026

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/news-landing-page/2026/february/new-maritime-rules-taking-effect-in-2026/

Paraphrased note of a short Maritime NZ media release dated 18 February 2026.

## What the release says

- Maritime Rules Amendments 2025 (No 3) were signed on 10 December 2025.
- They replace nine general exemptions that had expired, moving their content into the Maritime Rules.
- Maritime NZ describes the purpose as formalising practices the sector already uses, clarifying safety and technical expectations, removing outdated radio requirements and streamlining parts of the certification and training pathway.
- The changes come in two stages:
  - 28 January 2026: escape arrangements, rescue boat launching, firefighting equipment, anchor chain testing and MF/HF radio (ship and equipment items, not seafarer certificates).
  - 31 March 2026: sea service options, certification pathways and training requirements for several ratings.
- Questions about how the changes apply can be sent to regpolicyupdates@maritimenz.govt.nz.

## What the 31 March 2026 seafarer changes were (from the other library files)

The release does not list the ratings. The Latest rule updates page names the three general exemptions that became rules on 31 March 2026:
- GE-05-20: sea service and prior certification for the Integrated Rating certificate (Part 32 Table 15: holds or meets AB Deck and AB Engine).
- GE-06-20: the Training Record Book requirement for MEC 3 (Part 32 Table 31: TRB only where STCW requires it, that is on the cadetship route).
- GE-08-20: training requirements for Deck Watch Rating, Able Seafarer Deck, Engine Room Watch Rating, Able Seafarer Engine and Electro-technical Rating (Part 32 Tables 11 to 14 and 16).

The online form (October 2026) shows the resulting routes, for example:
- Deck Watch Rating and Engine Room Watch Rating: approved training plus 2 months of relevant sea service, or no training and 6 months.
- Able Seafarer Deck: training plus 12 months while holding DWR, or no training and 18 months.
- Able Seafarer Engine: training plus 6 months while holding ERWR, or no training and 12 months.
- Electro-technical Rating: NZ Certificate in Maritime Crewing plus 6 months, a Level 4 electrical engineering certificate plus 3 months, or no training and 12 months.
- MEC 3: cadetship route (12 months plus workshop skills, TRB required) or 36 months of sea service with no TRB.
