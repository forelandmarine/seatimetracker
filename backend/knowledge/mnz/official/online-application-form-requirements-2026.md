# Online seafarer application form (April 2026): requirement checklists

Source: https://licensing.maritimenz.govt.nz/#/seafarer (the "New Seafarer Certificates and Endorsements" form). Read on 2026-10-25 by choosing each certificate and endorsement in the form and recording the requirement summary it shows before "Start application". The same data is held in the form's published script, which was used to check every entry.

## How the form works (our summary)

- You first choose what you are applying for: a certificate only, an endorsement on a certificate you already hold, or a certificate together with one or more endorsements in one application. The form says doing both together is more efficient than two separate applications. Renewals are applied for through the same "certificate" route.
- After you choose a certificate, the form shows "core requirements" (age, identity, medical type, criminal record check) and "requirements" (prior certificate, sea service, training or qualification, first aid, radio certificate, ancillaries, oral exam, training record book). Where a certificate has more than one route, you must pick a pathway first. You must tick that you have read and meet the requirements before you can start.
- The form says all listed requirements must be current when you apply, and that you will have to provide documents for every requirement. The full document list appears only after you start.
- Payment: you pay when you submit; payment cannot be made later. Card (credit or debit) and bank-to-bank transfer are accepted. With a bank transfer you get a payment reference, and the application is only submitted once the payment arrives, which the form says can take 1 to 2 business days. An employer can pay (ask for their payment details first).
- Fee: NZD 368 per certificate or endorsement, including renewals (ships' cook letter of confirmation: no fee), from the form's certificate list on 2026-10-25.
- Drafts: you can save a draft by email; it is kept for 20 days, and you can extend it by resuming and saving again.
- Agents and employers: someone applying for you needs an Authority to Act, either a signed paper form MSF122 uploaded with the application, or a Digital Authority to Act that the applicant confirms online (the form calls the digital route the quickest). An application made without confirmed authority is rejected and refunded. A digital authority can be for one application, until a set date, or ongoing (it expires after 24 months).
- Applicant details must match official ID (for example birth certificate, driver licence or passport).
- Not in the form: Skipper two nautical miles (S2NM) is not in the certificate list on 2026-10-25, so it cannot yet be applied for online. Recognition of foreign certificates and TTMRA (Australian) recognition are also not in this form; they use the separate recognition forms.

## If your application is declined: Regulatory Licensing Review Panel

The licensing site also has a "Regulatory Licensing Review Panel" form for asking for a review of a licensing decision (for example on an MEC 1, Chief Mate or Master application). Our summary of what it says:
- Apply within three months of the date on your decision letter, quoting the application number on that letter.
- Explain why you think Maritime NZ should have interpreted or applied a rule differently, say what outcome you want, and upload supporting evidence (up to 5 files).
- Maritime NZ will tell you within 10 working days whether the review is accepted; an accepted review can take 6 weeks.

Note: this is the only turnaround time the licensing service states. No processing time for ordinary certificate applications is published (see news-2026-05-seafarer-licensing-goes-digital.md).

## Conflicts with the rules found in the form

- CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL RULE: SRL endorsed to 500 GT. The form lists only the route of 12 months on vessels over 12 m while holding a current SRL <24 m within 5 years, plus TRB and oral exam. Rule 32.62(2)(a) (as amended to 21 September 2026) also allows 18 months in command of commercial ships of 12 m or more with an approved TRB, and says "12 metres in length or more", not "over 12 m".
- CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL RULE: SRL endorsed to 24 m. The form describes the sea service as 6 months on vessels over 12 m without holding SRL, or 6 months on vessels of any size while holding SRL, or a mix of the two. Rule 32.61(2) says only "no less than 6 months on a commercial ship in a deck capacity".
- CONFLICTS WITH OFFICIAL RULE: renewal of Mate Fishing Vessel Limited and Unlimited and Skipper Fishing Vessel Limited and Unlimited. The form offers "3 months sea service within 6 months prior to the application date" as an option. Rule 32.21(3) gives STCW-F certificates only 12 months in the last 5 years (or 30 months of accepted shore-based equivalent). The form's renewal text for MEC 4 and MEC 5 (STCW-F renewal) follows the rule and has no 3-in-6 option.
