# Approved training for national and STCW-F certificates (which course and provider leads to each certificate)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/training/approved-training-for-national-and-stcw-f-certificates/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

Use this page to find training providers approved to deliver the programmes that lead to New Zealand national certificates, and STCW-F aligned fishing-vessel certificates. If you plan to work on a New Zealand-flagged ship, you must complete approved training for the certificate you are applying for.

| National and STCW-F certificates | Training (national award) | Training provider(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced Deckhand - Fishing | NZ Certificate in Fishing Vessel Crewing (Level 3) | Toi Ohomai Institute of Technology; Westport Deep Sea Fishing School |
| Marine Engineer Class 4 | NZ Diploma in Marine Engineering (Level 6) | International Maritime Institute of New Zealand (NMIT); New Zealand Maritime School |
| Marine Engineer Class 5, Class 5 steam, Class 5 motor and steam | NZ Certificate in Marine Engineering Class 5 (Level 5) | IMINZ (NMIT); New Zealand Maritime School; Skipper Training NZ Ltd; Westport Deep Sea Fishing School |
| Marine Engineer Class 6 | NZ Certificate in Marine Engineering Class 6 (Level 4) | IMINZ (NMIT); New Zealand Maritime School; Skipper Training NZ Ltd; Westport Deep Sea Fishing School |
| Mate Fishing Vessel - Limited | NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 4) with Fishing strand | IMINZ (NMIT); New Zealand Maritime School; Skipper Training NZ Ltd |
| Mate Fishing Vessel - Unlimited | NZ Diploma in Fishing Vessel Operations (Level 6) | IMINZ (NMIT); Skipper Training NZ Limited |
| Skipper Coastal/offshore | NZ Certificate in Domestic Maritime Operations (Coastal & Offshore) (Level 4) | New Zealand Maritime School; Westport Deep Sea Fishing School |
| Skipper Restricted Limits | NZ Certificate in Domestic Maritime Operations (Restricted Limits) (Level 4) | Coastguard Boating Education Service; IMINZ (NMIT); New Zealand Maritime School; SiS Training and Consulting Ltd; Skipper Training NZ Limited; Toi Ohomai Institute of Technology; Westport Deep Sea Fishing School |
| Skipper Fishing Vessel - Limited | NZ Certificate in Maritime Operations (Level 5) | New Zealand Maritime School; IMINZ (NMIT) |
| Skipper Fishing Vessel - Unlimited | NZ Diploma in Fishing Vessel Operations (Level 6) with Fishing Vessel Skipper strand | IMINZ (NMIT); Skipper Training NZ Limited |

Footnotes on the page: (1) these training providers are approved by the Director to issue ancillary certificates of proficiency; (2) seafarers attending ancillary refresher courses must provide the training provider with a company letter(s) that they have actively participated in the mandatory abandon ship and fire drills (and fast rescue boat drills, if applicable) while completing approved seagoing service performing functions appropriate to the certificate they hold, for at least 12 months in total during the preceding 5 years or 3 months in total during the preceding 6 months.

Note for this library: the page lists Skipper Coastal/Offshore training as Level 4, while the New Zealand Maritime School lists its Skipper Coastal and Offshore programme as Level 5 (NZ2892).
