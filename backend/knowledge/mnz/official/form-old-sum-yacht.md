About this form
This form is to help keep track of your sea service in a deck capacity.
- You must provide documented proof of your sea service with your application.
- Do not include time on leave. Extended time in port is generally excluded.
- On-board yacht service is the time signed on, from date of joining to date of leaving the vessel.
- For watchkeeping, and yard or standby service, a day is eight or more hours in a 24-hour period.
- For navigational (bridge) watchkeeping, tick the box if your watchkeeping was gained under
supervision of a qualified navigating officer such as chief mate or master yacht (for Chief Mate Yacht
certificate).
- For Chief Mate Yacht Option 2, actual sea service is time at sea, including watchkeeping.
Yacht service is on-board yacht service and includes actual sea service, and standby and yard
service.
