About this form
This form is to help keep track of your sea service in an engineering capacity.
- You must provide documented proof of your sea service with your application.
- Do not include time on leave. Extended time in port is generally excluded.
- Tick in the box provided to show whether your watchkeeping was under supervision of a qualified
engineering officer (where relevant for your certificate).
- For watchkeeping, a day is eight or more hours in a 24-hour period.
