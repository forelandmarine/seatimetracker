# Approved Medical Practitioners (STCW and STCW-F medicals in New Zealand)

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/surveyors-and-third-parties/find-a-recognised-surveyor-or-third-party-regulator/approved-medical-practitioners/

Web page text captured 2026-10-25; site navigation, cookie text and contact boxes removed.

For STCW or STCW-F (aligned) certificates of competency or proficiency, only a registered medical practitioner who is approved by Maritime NZ may conduct medical examinations and issue STCW certificates of medical fitness.

Use this list to find approved medical practitioners who can complete STCW and STCW-F medicals. Contact providers directly to book an appointment and confirm availability.
