# Seafarer Certification and Operational Limits Framework (4 of 4): endorsement summaries (national, STCW-F and STCW) and glossary

Source: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/seafarers/getting-certified/background-to-seacert/
Source PDF: https://www.maritimenz.govt.nz/media/gggjmdsw/seafarer-certification-operational-limits-framework.pdf

Text extracted from the official PDF. Page headers, footers and page numbers removed; tables are flattened to text in their original reading order.

Endorsements for National and STCW-F Certificates
39 Endorsed to 24 metres
Applies to Skipper restricted limits
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a :
(a) master on passenger or non-passenger ships less than 24 metres in
length overall carrying up to 19 passengers or less within restricted
limits
(b) master on fishing vessels less than 24 metres overall within enclosed,
inshore, and fishing inshore limits
(c) mate on commercial ships of less than 500 GT within restricted limits
(d) mate on commercial ships of less than 24 metres within coastal limits
Six months sea service on a commercial vessel of 12 metres or more in a
deck capacity (if you do not hold an SRL)
Six months sea service on a commercial vessel of any length in a deck
capacity (if you hold an SRL)

40 Endorsed to less than 500 gross tonnage
Applies to Skipper restricted limits
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a:
(a) master on passenger or non-passenger ships less than 500 GT
carrying 19 passengers or less within restricted limits, and
(b) master on fishing vessels of less than 500 GT within enclosed,
inshore and inshore fishing limits, and
(c) mate on commercial ships of less than 24 metres within coastal limits
x Twelve months sea service on vessels 12 metres or more while
holding SRL certificate <24 metres or equivalent, completed within 5
years preceding the date of application
x Completion of an approved training record book
x Successfully passed oral examination by an approved Maritime NZ
examiner

41 Endorsed 500 to 3000 gross tonnage
Applies to Skipper restricted limits
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master on vessels of 500 to 3000
gross tonnage operating exclusively in restricted limits
x Holds an SRL certificate endorsed to 500 GT or equivalent
x Completion of an approved training course or structured training
programme
x Successfully passed oral examination by an approved Maritime NZ
examiner

Note – vessels of 3000 GT or more operating in restricted limits are required to be crewed by holders of
STCW unlimited certificates.

42 Passenger endorsement – SRL, SCO & MY less than 24 m
Applies to Skipper restricted limits, Skipper Coastal/Offshore and Master Yacht <24 metres
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of the above certificates on a
passenger ship carrying any number of passengers
x Successfully complete relevant training and
x Complete at least 6 months approved sea service on a commercial
passenger ship

43 Passenger endorsement – QDC
Applies to Qualified deck crew
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of qualified deck crew on a passenger
ship carrying any number of passengers
Successfully complete relevant training

44 Cook Strait endorsement
Applies to Skipper Restricted Limits
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a skipper restricted limits within the
Cook Strait limit
Successfully pass any assessment that the Director may require

45 Square-rigged sailing endorsement (note this applies to STCW certificates also)
Applies to Any National or STCW certificate
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master or navigational
watchkeeper on a square-rigged sailing ship
Successfully complete relevant training and
Either complete at least 12 months approved sea service in a deck
capacity on a square-rigged sailing ship
Or complete at least 6 months sea service in a deck capacity on a square-
rigged sailing ship and produce testimonials from the master of the ship
confirming that the holder was assessed on board ship and found to be
competent to serve as master.

46 High-speed endorsement
Applies to Skipper restricted limits, Skipper Coastal and Offshore and Master Yacht <24
metres
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a navigational watchkeeper on a
high-speed ship
Either Successfully complete relevant training,
Or complete at least 12 months of relevant sea service as a master or
officer on a high-speed ship in the 5 years preceding the date of the
application and have a safe operating record.

47 Specified activity endorsement
Applies to Skipper restricted limits or a ring-fenced certificate
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master on vessels operating
within a uniform distance of 100 miles from the coast of New Zealand
x Demonstrate the ancillary proficiencies required for the issue of a
certificate as Skipper coastal/offshore (First aid, fire fighting, survival
craft and MGROC), and
x Show that there is a clear operational need for the endorsement and
x Successfully pass any assessment that the Director may require (or
meet the requirements of the general exemption).

48 Steam endorsement for MEC 5
Applies to Marine engineer class 5 certificate
Privileges To perform the functions and duties as an engineer:
(a) on passenger or non-passenger steam ships of any length and propulsion
power that do not proceed beyond restricted limits or
(b) on passenger and non-passenger steam ships of less than 24 metres in
length and less than 500 GT that operate beyond restricted limits but within
coastal or offshore limits
x Successfully complete relevant training and
x Complete approved sea service of no less than 6 months on a steam
ship and
x Successfully complete an approved training record book while on
board a stream ship during the 5 years preceding the date of the
application and
x Successfully complete any examination the Director may require
assessed by an approved Maritime NZ examiner

Endorsements for STCW certificates
49 Basic tanker endorsement
Short name Basic tanker endorsement
Regulation STCW Regulation V/1-1 and V/1-2
Privileges To perform designated duties related to cargo or cargo equipment on a tanker of
the relevant type (oil and chemical or liquefied gas)
Prior certification No prior certification
Minimum sea service  Option 1
If basic tanker training completed no sea service required
Option 2
If no training completed, 3 months undertaken on relevant tanker (oil and
chemical or liquefied gas)
Qualification Applies to Option 1 above - either
x Basic tanker training – oil and chemical or
x Basic tanker training – liquefied gas
Ancillary and other
certificates
STCW basic training

50 Advanced tanker endorsement
Short name Advanced tanker endorsement
Regulation STCW Regulation V/1-1 and V/1-2
Privileges To perform designated duties
(a) As part of the exercise privileges as a master, chief engineer officer, chief
mate or a second engineer officer in accordance with a certificate of
competency on a tanker of the relevant type and
(b) Generally as a person with immediate responsibility for loading, discharging,
care in transit, handling of cargo, tank cleaning or other cargo-related
operations on a tanker of the relevant type
Prior certification Applicable basic tanker endorsement for tankers (oil and chemical or liquefied
gas tankers)
Minimum sea service  3 months on relevant tanker (oil or chemical or gas) while holding basic tanker
endorsement
Qualification One or more of the following certificate courses
x Advanced oil tanker training
x Advanced chemical tanker training
x Advanced gas tanker training
Ancillary and other
certificates
STCW basic training

51 Basic polar water endorsement
Short name Basic polar water endorsement
Regulation STCW Regulation V/4-1 and V/4-2
Privileges Master – to perform the privileges of a master while in polar waters that are
open waters
Chief mate – to perform the privileges of a chief mate while in polar waters that
are open waters
A master, chief mate or a deck officer may be in charge of a navigational watch
on a ship operating in polar waters open waters
Prior certification Holds an STCW certificate as master or deck officer
Minimum sea service  No sea service required
Qualification Basic training for ships operating in polar waters
Ancillary and other
certificates
No additional ancillary or other certificates required

52 Advanced polar water endorsement
Short name Advanced polar waters endorsement
Regulation STCW Regulation V/4-3 and V/4-4
Privileges Master – to perform the privileges of a master while in polar waters
Chief mate – to perform the privileges of a chief mate while in polar waters
A master, chief mate or a deck officer may be in charge of a navigational watch
on a ship operating in polar waters open waters
Prior certification Holds an STCW certificate as master or chief mate
Minimum sea service  At least 2 months in the deck department at management level or while
performing watch-keeping duties at the operational level on a vessel operating in
polar waters
Qualification Advanced training for ships operating in polar waters
Ancillary and other
certificates
STCW basic training

53 Basic IGF ship endorsement
Short name Basic IGF ship endorsement
Regulation STCW Regulation V/3
Privileges To have responsibility for designated safety duties associated with the care or
use of the fuel on board, or with emergency response relating to the fuel
Prior certification No prior certification
Minimum sea service  No sea service required
Qualification Option 1
x Basic IGF training
Option 2
x Evidence of meeting requirements to hold a Basic or Advanced liquefied gas
tanker endorsement
Ancillary and other
certificates
No additional ancillary or other certificates required

54 Advanced IGF ship endorsement
Short name Advanced IGF ship endorsement
Regulation STCW Regulation V/3
Privileges To perform designated duties as part of the exercise of that persons privileges
as a master or engineer officer or seafarer with immediate responsibility for the
care and use of fuels and fuel systems on the ship
Prior certification Holds a basic IGF ship endorsement
Minimum sea service  Option 1
One month of seagoing service that includes a minimum of three bunkering
operations on board ships subject to the IGF Code – however, two of the three
bunkering operations may be replaced by approved simulator training on
bunkering operations as part of the training
Option 2
Three months of sea service in the previous five years on:
x Ships subject to the IGF Code, or
x Tankers carrying fuels covered by the IGF Code as cargo, or
x Ships using gases or low flashpoint fuel
AND
A minimum of three bunkering operations on board ships subject to the IGF Code
– however, two of the three bunkering operations may be replaced by approved
simulator training on bunkering operations
OR
Have participated in conducting three cargo operations on board the liquefied gas
tanker
Qualification No additional training required
Ancillary and other
certificates
No additional ancillary or other certificates required

55 Square-rigged sailing endorsement (note this applies to National certificates also)
Applies to Any National or STCW certificate
Privileges To perform the functions and duties of a master or navigational
watchkeeper on a square-rigged sailing ship
Successfully complete relevant training and
Either complete at least 12 months approved sea service in a deck
capacity on a square-rigged sailing ship
Or complete at least 6 months sea service in a deck capacity on a square-
rigged sailing ship and produce testimonials from the master of the ship
confirming that the holder was assessed on board ship and found to be
competent to serve as master.

Glossary
AB Deck Able Seafarer Deck
AB Engine Able Seafarer Engine
ADH Advanced Deckhand
ADH-F Advanced Deckhand – Fishing
Assigned inshore limit A defined section of the coastal limits not beyond the limit of the territorial
sea of New Zealand (and not more than 30 NM from a safe haven),
which a surveyor assigns to a ship as an inshore limit
Assistant engineer officer A person under training to become an engineer officer
Candidate A person who applies either to take an examination or to be issued with
a certificate of competency, proficiency or endorsement
CDH Certified Deckhand
Certificate Certificate of competency or certificate of proficiency
Certificate of competency
(CoC)
Corresponds to a maritime document that is described as a certificate of
competency in Table 1 under Rule 32.2
Certificate of proficiency
(CoP)
Corresponds to a maritime document that is described as a certificate of
proficiency in Table 1 under Rule 32.2
Chief engineer officer The senior engineer officer responsible for the mechanical propulsion
and operation and maintenance of a ship’s mechanical and electrical
installations
Chief Mate Chief Mate on ships of any gross tonnage in any operating area
Chief Mate <3000 GT Chief Mate on ships of less than 3000 gross tonnage in any operating
area
Chief Mate Yacht Chief Mate on superyachts of less than 3000 GT in any operating area
Commercial ship The same meaning as defined in Maritime Rule 32.2
Deck officer An officer certificated in accordance with the provisions of Chapter II of
STCW-78 as amended
DWR Deck Watch Rating
ECDIS Electronic Chart Display and Information System – this is a computer-
based navigation system that complies with IMO regulations and can be
used as an alternative to paper navigation charts
EEZ Exclusive economic zone: the sea zone over which New Zealand has
special rights for the exploration and use of marine resources, extending
from the baseline of the territorial sea out to 200 NM
Engineer Officer An officer certificated in accordance with the provisions of Regulation
III/1, III/2 or III/3 of the STCW Convention
ETO Electro-technical Officer
ETR Electro-technical Rating
EWR Engine Room Watch Rating
Fishing vessel A vessel required to be registered under New Zealand fisheries
legislation
GMDSS Global maritime distress and safety system
GROC General radiotelephone operator’s certificate
GT Gross tonnage
IMO International Maritime Organization

Inshore limit Inshore limits as described in Appendix 1 of Maritime Rules Part 20
IR Integrated Rating
ISPS International Ship and Port Facility Security Code - this is an amendment
to the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS) Convention (1974/1988) on
Maritime security including minimum security arrangements for ships,
ports and government agencies.
Length The same as load line length, as defined in Maritime Rules Part 47.2
Length overall Overall length of a ship as defined in detail in Maritime Rules Part 32.2
Limited waters The combined area of the coastal limits and offshore limits
Master Master on ships of any gross tonnage in any operating area
Master <xxx GT Master on ships of less than a specified size in any operating area
Master Yacht Master on superyachts of less than 3000 GT in any operating area
Master Yacht <24 m NC Master on sailing ships of less t han 24 metres in the near-coastal area
Master Yacht <500 GT Master on superyachts of less than 500 GT in any operating area
MEC 1 Marine Engineer Class 1
MEC 2 Marine Engineer Class 2
MEC 2 ECE Marine Engineer Class 2 – endorsed as Chief Engineer
MEC 3 Marine Engineer Class 3
MEC 4 Marine Engineer Class 4
MEC 5 Marine Engineer Class 5
MEC 6 Marine Engineer Class 6
MFV-L Mate of fishing vessels of less than 45m in length in limited waters
MFV-U Mate of fishing vessels of any size operating in any operating area
Month Equivalent to 30 days
MOSS Maritime transport operator safety system
Near-coastal area The combined area of the coastal limits and offshore limits
Near coastal voyage A voyage within the near coastal area
NM Nautical miles
On-board yacht service Time spent signed on a superyacht, regardless of vessel activity
Part 32 The maritime rule for seafarer certification
Pleasure craft A ship that is used exclusively for the owner’s pleasure or as the owner’s
residence and is not offered or used for hire or reward. For a full
description, refer to the Maritime Transport Act 1994 Part 1.
QDC Qualified Deck Crew
Record book Record of sea service and vessels
Restricted limits Enclosed water limits and inshore limits
RROC Maritime restricted radiotelephone operator’s certificate
RYA Royal Yachting Association
Sailing ship A ship that is designed to be navigated under wind power alone and for
which any motor provided is an auxiliary means of propulsion
SCO Skipper Coastal/Offshore
Sea service Service on board a ship that is relevant to a certificate or endorsement

Sea service (superyachts) A combination of sea service, stand-by service and/or yard service, as
well as time spent at sea that may include time at anchor, or river or
canal transits associated with a passage
SFV-L Skipper Fishing Vessel – Master of fishing vessels of 24 metres or more
but less than 45m length operating in limited waters
SFV-U Skipper Fishing Vessel – Master of fishing vessels of any length
operating in any operating area
SRL Skipper Restricted Limits
STCW-95 International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and
Watchkeeping for Seafarers, 1978, as amended in 1995
STCW International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and
Watchkeeping for Seafarers, 1978, as amended in 2010
STCW-F International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and
Watchkeeping for Fishing Vessel Personnel, 1995
Superyacht A commercial yacht or sail training vessel of 24 metres or more load line
length and less than 3000 GT that does not carry cargo or more than 12
passengers
Territorial sea New Zealand’s territorial sea, as defined by section 3 of the Territorial
Sea, Contiguous Zone and Exclusive Economic Zone Act 1977
Training record book Maritime NZ approved book providing evidence of required
competencies
Sometimes referred to as the TARbook
Voyage A journey by water from one port either to another port, or back to the
same port without calling at any other port
Warship A ship belonging to the armed forces of a State. For a full description,
refer to the Maritime Transport Act 1994 Part 1.
Watchkeeper Deck Officer in charge of a navigational watch on ships of specified gross
tonnage in any operating area
